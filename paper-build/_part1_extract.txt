I. The photon paradox: why ds² = 0 demands a new ontology
For a photon, the spacetime interval vanishes: 𝑑𝑠 2 = 𝑐 2 𝑑𝑡 2 − 𝑑 x2 = 0. A photon accumulates zero proper
time between emission and absorption. This is standard relativity, but it raises a question standard physics
leaves unanswered: what distinguishes entities that accumulate proper time from those that do not?
The Standard Model answers in two steps. For fundamental particles the correlation is clean: those
accumulating zero proper time are exactly the massless ones (photons, gluons), and for fundamental
fermions rest mass is Higgs (Yukawa) coupling — so among them proper-time accumulation tracks Higgs
coupling (neutrinos barely, with 𝑦𝜈 ∼ 10−12 ). But the exception-free statement is the relativistic one:
an entity accumulates proper time if and only if it carries rest energy and moves sub-luminally. That
covers the composite case a Higgs-only framing would miss — a proton accumulates proper time although
$∼$99% of its mass is the QCD binding energy of massless gluons (§III). NTE takes this correlation as
structural: proper-time accumulation is e-fold embedding depth (§VII), sourced by rest energy from
whatever mechanism — Higgs coupling for fundamental fermions, confinement for hadrons — while
entities with no rest energy (photons, gluons) do not embed and accumulate zero proper time (null-time).
This reframes the standard picture without asserting that the field dynamically creates time: 𝑁 is the
geometric coordinate, 𝑡 is derived once locking completes, and Standard-Model mass generation remains
the Standard-Model Higgs sector (§2.3).

II.0 On method
The working discipline of this framework is to seek simplification before mechanism: where multiple
formalisms agree on a phenomenon, treat them as descriptions of one structure and look for that structure;
where a modification reduces the count of independent postulates, prefer it; where a phenomenon seems to
require new mechanism, seek the viewpoint from which it does not, before adopting one. This is a working
bet, not a law, and it is applied symmetrically — a framework that counts its successful simplifications while
passing over its failed ones in silence is doing advocacy, not science.
