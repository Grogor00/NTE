## Abstract

The Standard Model leaves the right-handed Majorana mass scale free; leptogenesis fixes the lightest of them, M₁, only by requiring it reproduce the observed baryon asymmetry. NTE supplies M₁ from geometry. Flux quantisation of the Hopf–Planck flux through the S² base of the gauge-birth Hopf bundle, doubled by the real (Majorana) character of the fermion and quantised in the Hubble 3-volume of S³, gives a cube-root law

  M₁ = (8π)^{1/3} (M_Pl² H_GB)^{1/3} ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV,

within a factor 1.12 of the value leptogenesis requires (≈ 6.1 × 10⁹ GeV), the residual being an O(1) gauge-birth correction rather than a free parameter. The cube root is the 3-dimensionality of the S³ Hubble volume; the factor 8π is the 4π Hopf–Planck base flux doubled by the real Spin(4) representation of a Majorana mode; the (8π)^{1/3} prefactor is selected at tree level over its nearest competitors. It is distinct from, and not benchmarked against, the heaviest scale M_R = M_Pl^{3/4} v_EW^{1/4} ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, whose pathway is identified but whose derivation is open (Part 3); the two are non-competing constructions set by different inputs (cosmological H_GB for M₁, electroweak v_EW for M_R). The baryon asymmetry that follows is an order-unity *consistency* with observation, not a zero-parameter prediction, and is stated as such.

## 1. Two Majorana scales, two inputs

The seesaw needs a heavy right-handed Majorana sector; NTE fixes two of its scales from geometry, by two different routes, and it is worth separating them at the outset because they answer different questions.

- **M_R = M₃ (heaviest).** Set by the requirement that the heaviest light neutrino mass m_ν₃ = v_EW²/M_R come out near the atmospheric scale. The corpus identifies a pathway to M_R = M_Pl^{3/4} v_EW^{1/4} ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV from the Hopf base/fibre 3:1 weighting of the Majorana mass on the S² base, with v_EW the electroweak input. Part 3 grades this *pathway identified, not derived*; the derivation of M_R is an open item. This is the σ₃ (Cartan) mode — the one aligned with the Higgs VEV (Companion 7).
- **M₁ (lightest).** The leptogenesis scale, set by the natural mass quantum of a Killing-spinor mode at gauge birth, with the cosmological Hubble rate H_GB the input. This is the subject of this paper, and it is one of the σ₁/σ₂ modes orthogonal to the Higgs direction.

The hierarchy M₁/M₃ ≈ 10⁻⁵ is then the hierarchy between the cosmological and the electroweak input scales fed through the same S³ structure — a structural feature, not a coincidence to be tuned.

## 2. The derivation

**Step 1 — Hopf–Planck flux on the S² base.** At gauge birth the Hopf bundle S³ → S² carries a U(1) fibre, with base radius set by the Hubble length ℓ_GB = 1/H_GB. The S² base has area 4π ℓ_GB², and the Hopf bundle has first Chern number one, so a Planck-weighted count of flux quanta (one per Planck cell 1/M_Pl² of the base) gives

  Φ_HP = 4π (M_Pl ℓ_GB)².

This is the topological reading, tracking the Hopf monopole directly. (An Einstein–Hilbert-action reading over one Hubble time gives a numerically nearby coefficient; the topological reading is preferred because it follows the bundle structure that the rest of the framework already uses.)

**Step 2 — Majorana doubling.** A Majorana fermion on S³ is a *real* representation of Spin(4): both chirality components, ψ and its charge conjugate ψ^c, contribute independently to the flux it carries. The effective flux per Majorana mode is therefore doubled,

  Φ_Maj = 2 Φ_HP = 8π (M_Pl ℓ_GB)².

The factor 8π is thus not a fitted constant but 4π (the Hopf–Planck base flux) times 2 (the real-representation doubling). This is the same doubling that distinguishes the Majorana normalisation from the Dirac one throughout the mass sector.

**Step 3 — Adjoint multiplicity.** The S³ ≅ SU(2) structure contributes the three components of the adjoint of SU(2) — the three gauge-field components A^a, a = 1, 2, 3 — each seeing the full flux independently (not a divided share). This is the same multiplicity-3 that appears in the cross-thickness coupling κ₀, and it is the dimension of the adjoint, not a count of Killing-spinor polarisations (the fixed-sign Killing-spinor space on round S³ is two-dimensional). The three then map onto the three generations carried by the J₃(𝕆) idempotent triple.

**Step 4 — Cube-root quantisation.** Each mode quantises against the full Majorana flux in the natural 3-volume of S³ at gauge birth, V₃ = ℓ_GB³:

  M_i³ · ℓ_GB³ = 8π (M_Pl ℓ_GB)²  ⇒  M_i³ = 8π M_Pl² H_GB  ⇒  **M_i = (8π)^{1/3} (M_Pl² H_GB)^{1/3}.**

The cube root is the 3-dimensionality of the Hubble volume — the same "three" that appears as the adjoint dimension, the su(2) generator count, and the generation count, all facets of the SU(2) ≅ S³ structure.

Numerically, with (8π)^{1/3} ≈ 2.92 and (M_Pl² H_GB)^{1/3} ≈ 1.85 × 10⁹ GeV, M₁ ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV.

## 4. Which mode is M₁

The three right-handed Majorana modes are degenerate at the cube-root level; the Higgs VEV lifts the degeneracy. The σ₃ (Cartan) mode is aligned with the VEV, couples strongly through the Higgs portal, and is lifted to M₃ = M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV — the heavy scale of §1. The σ₁ and σ₂ modes are orthogonal to the VEV, weakly coupled, and remain near the cube-root value; the residual σ₁ ↔ σ₂ symmetry (the same one that fixes maximal atmospheric mixing, Companion 7 and the μ–τ mixing sector (credited to the μ–τ literature)) keeps them nearly degenerate, with

  M₂ = M₁(1 + sin²θ₁₃) ≈ M₁ × 1.02,

so M₁ is the lighter of the σ₁/σ₂ pair — the leptogenesis scale. The aligned/orthogonal split is the algebraic content of "which mode is which": σ₃ Cartan → heavy, σ₁/σ₂ → light.

## 5. Leptogenesis: a consistency, not a prediction

With a geometric M₁ ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV landing inside the successful-leptogenesis window, the observed baryon-to-photon ratio η ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰ is reproduced at order unity given the observed light-neutrino mass scale and O(1) CP and washout factors. The honest statement is order-unity structural consistency, and η is not to be presented as a clean zero-parameter result.
