## No-numerology consistency note

The framework's stated discipline is that no quantity is assembled from measured inputs to match a measured output. Applied consistently, this **withdraws** three constructions previously carried in the derived ledger: the $\beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ characterisation, the $\theta_{13}$ formula, and the Cabibbo formula. Each took observed inputs plus an interpretive coefficient and reproduced a target observable — the signature of number-fitting. Their withdrawal removes nothing structural (each was already flagged as "not derived from first principles"); it brings the ledger into line with the discipline and is part of what distinguishes this framework from coupling-constant "derivations" elsewhere in its class (see Positioning, Part 1). What remains in the derived tier is structural; what is uncertain is labelled conditional or open.

## Falsifiability register

The framework is falsifiable on multiple independent fronts:
1. **$\kappa$ (dark matter).** CMB-S4 measures the DM:baryon ratio at $\sim2\times$ Planck precision; a value inconsistent with $5.50$ (at the homogeneous CMB epoch, unbiased by void effects) falsifies the κ derivation.
2. **DM:baryon variance.** Absence of the $\sigma(M)\propto M^{-1/2}$ scaling above $M_c$ (DESI/SDSS) falsifies the two-sector mechanism.
3. **Dark energy.** NTE's void mechanism predicts SN Hubble residuals brighter along void-dominated sightlines (testable with Pantheon+ × void catalogues). Finding the correlation tests the mechanism; it does not rescue $w=-1$ — a real void bias would partially mask an evolving-DE signal, so correcting for it would strengthen, not weaken, any measured departure from $w=-1$. DESI BAO alone does not exclude $w=-1$; the live issue is DESI–Planck concordance under $\Lambda$CDM and the frequentist-vs-Bayesian reading of DESI+CMB (arXiv:2511.10631; Dovekie). Voids do not mitigate. Verdict window 2027–2029.
4. **Hubble.** Failure of local $H_0$ to show density-correlated directional scatter, or to converge toward Planck as systematics are controlled.
5. **Inflation / early acceleration (option D open sector).** Native $\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ $(n_s,r)$ **withdrawn**. Exit (a) as **Higgs** inflation is **structurally unavailable** (doublet B2). Live tests for the open sector are pathway-dependent (e.g. Starobinsky $r$, or a future credited singlet); CMB-S4/LiteBIRD bound $r$ ($\sigma(r)\sim0.001$–$0.003$). Failure of any candidate does **not** restore $\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ as inflaton.
6. **CLFV (conditional).** If charged-lepton flavour violation is ever observed, the amplitude ratio $|A(\mu\to e\gamma)|^2/|A(\tau\to e\gamma)|^2 = \tan^2\theta_{23}$ is testable (Companion 18; unobservable at the framework's heavy scale).
7. **$0\nu\beta\beta$.** A narrow effective-mass window (a few meV; next-generation reach).
8. **Atmospheric octant / $\theta_{12}$.** JUNO first result $\sin^2\theta_{12}=0.3092\pm0.0087$ (arXiv:2511.14593) excludes TM2-class democracy ($\sin^2\theta_{12}=1/3$) at $3.5$–$3.8\sigma$. TM1-class sum-rule value $\approx0.318$ sits at $\sim1\sigma$ from JUNO and remains a commitment-conditional candidate only after the $\sigma\leftrightarrow$generation dictionary (Item 1) is ratified; solar mixing is otherwise open.
9. **Gauge-birth gravitational waves.** A stochastic background distinct in shape from inflationary and phase-transition spectra.
10. **Mathematical.** $a_4 \neq 0$ for the untwisted Dirac operator on $S^3\times S^1$ (would contradict CC 2008), or $C^2/E_4 \neq 0$ pointwise there, falsifies the spectral substrate claims.
11. **Spatial curvature sign.** The boundary ontology requires a closed $S^3$, so in the convention $\Omega_k \equiv -kc^2/(a^2H^2)$ with $k=+1$, NTE requires $\Omega_k < 0$ **strictly**: exact flatness is excluded by the geometry, and an open universe is excluded outright. **A confirmed $\Omega_k > 0$ at significance falsifies the framework**, and does so at the level of the ontology rather than of any single derivation — there is no closed-$S^3$ reading of an open universe. Current data are consistent and impose no tension: Planck 2018 gives $\Omega_k = 0.0007 \pm 0.0019$, with a marginally positive central value well within uncertainty of zero. Three honest qualifications. (i) The falsifier is **one-sided**: confirming $\Omega_k < 0$ would not confirm NTE, since every closed-universe cosmology predicts the same sign. (ii) It is **not NTE-distinguishing** and does not join the three empirical handles above. (iii) NTE predicts the sign, **not the magnitude** — $|\Omega_k|$ is expected to be far below any achievable sensitivity — though the expansion history that would establish this is itself open (see Open work) — and curvature constraints are in any case degenerate with the dark-energy sector, so this is a falsifier that may never fire in either direction. It is recorded because a framework should state what would refute it, including where refutation is unlikely to be reachable.
