## I. The photon paradox: why ds² = 0 demands a new ontology

For a photon, the spacetime interval vanishes: $ds^2 = c^2 dt^2 - d\mathbf{x}^2 = 0$. A photon accumulates zero proper time between emission and absorption. This is standard relativity, but it raises a question standard physics leaves unanswered: *what distinguishes entities that accumulate proper time from those that do not?*

Two clarifications, since this is routinely mis-stated. First, $ds^2 = 0$ is an invariant we compute about the photon's worldline, not a description of anything the photon undergoes; there is no inertial frame at $c$, so "from the photon's perspective" is undefined rather than degenerate — the limit does not converge. Second, the photon has no four-velocity at all: $u^\mu = dx^\mu/d\tau$ is $0/0$ when $d\tau = 0$, and its four-momentum is null, $k \cdot k = 0$. The photon is not the extreme case of proper-time accumulation; it is the case outside the construction.

The Standard Model answers in two steps. For *fundamental* particles the correlation is clean: those accumulating zero proper time are exactly the massless ones (photons, gluons), and for fundamental fermions rest mass *is* Higgs (Yukawa) coupling — so among them proper-time accumulation tracks Higgs coupling (neutrinos barely, with $y_\nu \sim 10^{-12}$). But the exception-free statement is the relativistic one: an entity accumulates proper time if and only if it carries rest energy and moves sub-luminally. That covers the composite case a Higgs-only framing would miss — a proton accumulates proper time although $\sim$99% of its mass is the QCD binding energy of massless gluons (§III). NTE takes *this* correlation as structural: proper-time accumulation is e-fold embedding depth (§VII), sourced by rest energy from whatever mechanism — Higgs coupling for fundamental fermions, confinement for hadrons — while entities with no rest energy (photons, gluons) do not embed and accumulate zero proper time (null-time). This reframes the standard picture without asserting that the field dynamically creates time: $N$ is the geometric coordinate, $t$ is derived once locking completes, and Standard-Model mass generation remains the Standard-Model Higgs sector (§2.3).

## II.0 On method

The working discipline of this framework is to seek simplification before mechanism: where multiple formalisms agree on a phenomenon, treat them as descriptions of one structure and look for that structure; where a modification reduces the count of independent postulates, prefer it; where a phenomenon seems to require new mechanism, seek the viewpoint from which it does not, before adopting one. This is a working bet, not a law, and it is applied symmetrically — a framework that counts its successful simplifications while passing over its failed ones in silence is doing advocacy, not science.

NTE's record therefore includes both. Simplifications that earned their place: the gauge group from $G_2$, and the dark-matter coupling from $S^3$ topology. Two items earlier listed as earned simplifications are, by the test just stated, better classed as *reformulations*: the Born rule as a Hopf projection on $S^3$ (§IV) is Gleason's theorem read on an imported qubit geometry — a coherent geometric restatement, but not a reduction in independent postulates unless it yields a consequence Gleason does not; and $t = i\,\Delta N$ as the Euclidean saddle (§2.1.2) is Wick rotation plus the Hartle–Hawking no-boundary condition in NTE vocabulary, wholly within the unobservable pre-temporal regime. Both are kept for the coherence they supply, not counted as postulate reductions — and either is upgraded the moment it makes a distinguishing prediction. Proposals withdrawn because they did not: an early Klein-bottle topology (abandoned for $S^3$ when parallelisability, Lie-group structure, and boundary parity all converged on $S^3$); a "single manifold without thickness" reading (superseded by the N-thickness reading); an ontological rebuild that would have withdrawn the dark-matter derivation (withdrawn after audit showed the corpus had already committed to the relevant structure). A modification that merely re-describes phenomena already covered, without simplifying or making a distinguishing prediction, fails the test.

**Graded honest-scope.** The framework reports every result against three explicit gradations rather than a binary "predicted / not":
- *Derivation status* — **derived** (from NTE's geometric structure), **inherited/passthrough** (from underlying structure with no NTE freedom), **conditional/observational** (constrained by observation pending derivation), or **imported** (an established external result NTE adopts and credits).
- *Force of a structural claim* — **forced** by deeper structure vs **consistent** with the framework's commitments without being uniquely forced.
- *Explanatory uniqueness* — **explains uniquely** vs **re-describes** something already explained at the level observed.

This discipline, together with the explicit no-number-fitting rule, is what the framework offers in place of foundational depth it does not claim (see Positioning).

## III. Three coupling regimes

The Higgs coupling sets a particle's geometric relationship to the expanding boundary; the three regimes are post-temporal.

**Zero coupling — null-time.** Photons and gluons ($y = 0$) accumulate zero e-fold depth and do not enter the $dt$ map. For the photon, $ds^2 = 0$: it sits on the boundary, not within the manifold. "Null-time" is null *from* time (a saturated value of an existing proper-time measure), not existence in the absence of time. The structural content of null-time is reparameterisation freedom: a massive worldline carries an intrinsic scale along it, whereas a null worldline carries only an affine parameter, fixed up to affine rescaling, so nothing along it is metrically marked. This is the relativistic reading of zero e-fold embedding depth — no embedding, no intrinsic parameter. The two are one fact in different vocabularies.

**Strong coupling — embedded matter.** Quarks, charged leptons, W/Z, Higgs ($y \gg 0$) are deeply embedded, accumulate substantial proper time per e-fold, and lock to one e-fold direction (§VII). (99% of proton mass is gluon binding energy — massless gluons, but the confined energy still contributes to embedding: same geometric effect, different mechanism.)
