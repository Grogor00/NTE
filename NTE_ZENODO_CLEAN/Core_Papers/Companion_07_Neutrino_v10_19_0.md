## Abstract

The standard Majorana description says the neutrino is its own antiparticle, ν = ν̄. NTE makes a more fundamental statement: the neutrino has never undergone the topological resolution that would *create* the particle/antiparticle distinction. It is not *both* particle and antiparticle — it is *neither*. The distinction is a consequence of the Hopf projection S³ → S² engaging the U(1) fibre; fully resolved fermions (quarks, charged leptons) are pinned to definite points on the S² base, while the neutrino, with minimal Higgs coupling (y_ν ∼ 10⁻¹²), barely engages that projection and remains smeared across the S¹ fibre — an unresolved configuration that persists as a geometric echo of the pre-temporal epoch into the post-temporal regime.

This ontology is load-bearing, and it must be stated precisely: what is unresolved is the *particle/antiparticle* status (the real-structure / charge-conjugation axis), not the neutrino's *chirality*. The active neutrino is chirally definite — left-handed under SU(2)_L — while remaining unresolved on the conjugation axis. The two are independent properties, and conflating them is the usual source of confusion about "Majorana superposition."

Three consequences follow. **Mass:** the neutrino's tiny mass is the geometric tension between its near-uniform fibre distribution (which "wants" to be null-time, like a photon) and the minimal admixture that couples it to the temporal projection — the cost of being almost, but not quite, unresolved. **Oscillation:** the unresolved configuration decomposes into three eigenmodes along the three su(2) generators, degenerate before electroweak symmetry breaking and split by the Higgs VEV; they drift in relative phase as the neutrino propagates, which *is* the oscillation, and the splitting pattern yields normal ordering. **Cross-thickness coupling:** the neutrino contributes to the κ derivation precisely because it fails to project — it couples through the isotropic Y₀₀ harmonic on the S² it never reaches. Each consequence is developed below, and the boundary between what this ontology establishes and what the quantitative papers derive is kept explicit.

## 1. The standard Majorana description, and the deeper statement

**What ν = ν̄ says.** A Majorana fermion satisfies ψ = ψ^c: particle and antiparticle are identified, the same state. In earlier framings this licensed the shorthand that a left-handed neutrino in one orientation "is" the right-handed antineutrino in the other, bridging the two CPN-conjugate orientations of the manifold's N-thickness.

**What it presupposes.** The identification ν = ν̄ presupposes that the categories "particle" and "antiparticle" have already been *applied* and then found to coincide. It is a statement within the resolved regime: after gauge birth has assigned quantum numbers and the Hopf projection has created the binary, one particular fermion happens to map to itself under charge conjugation.

**The deeper statement.** NTE locates the neutrino before that step. The particle/antiparticle distinction is a consequence of the Hopf projection S³ → S²; charge conjugation exchanges antipodal points on S³, which is the CPN transformation at the Janus boundary. A fermion that has fully projected has a definite charge and a definite relationship to its conjugate; a fermion that has *not* projected has neither — not because the two categories coincide, but because the operation that would distinguish them has no resolved target to act on. The neutrino is that unresolved configuration: the "Majorana" label is kept for continuity with the literature, but "self-conjugate" implies a resolved entity mapping to itself, whereas "unresolved" implies the conjugation has nothing to act on. This is the precise content of the corpus's neutrino ontology, and this paper supplies its geometric basis.

**Chirality is not what is unresolved.** A clarification that prevents a standing confusion. Two independent structures are in play: the *chirality* of the state (its left/right Weyl character, fixed by the SU(2)_L electroweak embedding) and its *particle/antiparticle resolution* (the charge-conjugation / real-structure axis, fixed by the Hopf projection). The neutrino is **chirally definite** — the active neutrino is left-handed — while being **unresolved on the conjugation axis**. The Majorana character lives entirely on the second axis; it does not make the chirality indefinite. So statements that the neutrino is "a superposition of left and right" are, read carefully, statements about the conjugation axis (a left-handed state and the charge-conjugate of a right-handed one are tied by the unresolved Majorana structure), not a claim that the active neutrino lacks definite chirality. The corpus seesaw retains handedness for exactly this reason, and the distinction is developed where the mass mechanism is treated.

## 2. The geometry of non-resolution

**Resolved vs unresolved on S³.** The Hopf fibration π: S³ → S² sends each point of S³ to a point of S², with fibre the circle S¹ — the orbit of the U(1) gauge action. A fully resolved fermion sits at a definite point of S³ projecting cleanly to a definite point of S², with a definite (unobservable) phase along the fibre: definite chirality, definite charge, definite relation to its conjugate. Its engagement with the projection is complete.

The electron (y_e ∼ 3 × 10⁻⁶) is fully resolved: its Higgs coupling pins it deeply into the e-fold structure, locking its projection. It cannot slide along the fibre — the temporal embedding is too strong. The neutrino (y_ν ∼ 10⁻¹²), six orders of magnitude more weakly coupled, is not pinned: its configuration remains spread across the S¹ fibre rather than collapsing to a definite projected point. It slides.

**The fibre distribution.** Characterise the neutrino's state by its distribution along the S¹ fibre over a point of S². A fully resolved state is a delta function on the fibre — all amplitude at one phase. The neutrino's state is a broad distribution. Functions on S¹ decompose into Fourier modes e^{inφ}; the n = 0 mode is the constant — uniform, phase-independent, maximally unresolved. A photon, at zero Higgs coupling, *is* the n = 0 mode: entirely uniform, zero engagement, zero mass — it is the boundary itself. The neutrino is dominated by n = 0 with the barest admixture of low-n modes, and that admixture is the source of both its tiny mass and its oscillation.

**Mass as residual tension.** In the framework's pinning picture, a massive particle is a location where the boundary refuses to thin at the vacuum rate, the degree of pinning set by the Higgs coupling. For the neutrino the pinning is almost negligible: its mass is the geometric tension between a nearly-uniform fibre distribution that "wants" to be null-time and the tiny higher-mode admixture that couples it minimally to the temporal projection. The neutrino's mass is not a possession in the way the electron's is — it is the cost of being almost, but not quite, entirely unresolved. This places the neutrino/charged-lepton mass gap in geometry rather than new physics: the ratio m_ν/m_e reflects the ratio of their engagements with the Hopf projection. The *absolute* scale is a quantitative matter, set by the seesaw with a heavy right-handed Majorana scale, and is carried by the mass-sector papers (Companions 10 and 13); this paper supplies the qualitative origin those papers quantify.

## 3. Oscillation as geometric sliding

**The three generators.** The Lie algebra su(2) has three generators (Pauli matrices σ₁, σ₂, σ₃), each defining an infinitesimal rotation on S³ and a U(1) subgroup, hence a different fibration of S³. The electroweak Higgs VEV selects one direction: after symmetry breaking a combination of SU(2)_L and U(1)_Y generators becomes electromagnetic U(1)_EM (conventionally σ₃), and this defines the "official" Hopf fibration — the projection that creates the binary and assigns charge. Before symmetry breaking, all three directions are equivalent; there is no preferred fibration.

*(Notation: the Pauli σᵢ here are the su(2) generators, distinct from any scalar field denoted σ elsewhere; they are algebraic objects internal to NTE's su(2) ≅ S³ structure.)*

**Three eigenmodes.** A fully resolved particle commits to the Higgs-selected direction; its projection is along σ₃, with definite charge and chirality. The neutrino does not commit, and its unresolved configuration decomposes by its relationship to all three generators — three independent ways the near-uniform fibre distribution can carry structure relative to the full su(2) algebra. Before symmetry breaking these three modes are degenerate (all generators equivalent ⇒ same geometric tension ⇒ massless). The Higgs VEV breaks the degeneracy:

- **The σ₃-aligned mode** has the strongest engagement with the selected fibration — the largest low-n admixture along the σ₃ fibre, the strongest coupling to the temporal projection, the most mass. This is ν₃.
- **The σ₁ and σ₂ modes**, orthogonal to the Higgs direction, engage the official fibration more weakly, carry less mass, and are nearly degenerate with each other because σ₁ and σ₂ are related by the residual symmetry the VEV leaves after selecting σ₃. These are ν₁ and ν₂.

**Normal ordering follows.** This produces m₃ > m₂ ≳ m₁ with m₁, m₂ nearly degenerate and m₃ distinctly heavier — i.e. Δm²₃₂ ≫ Δm²₂₁, matching the observed pattern (Δm²₃₂ ≈ 2.5 × 10⁻³ eV², Δm²₂₁ ≈ 7.5 × 10⁻⁵ eV², a ratio near 30:1). The large gap is the gap between the Higgs-aligned mode and the two orthogonal modes; the small splitting is the weak residual breaking of the σ₁–σ₂ degeneracy. **This is a prediction:** the geometry *requires* normal ordering; confirmed inverted ordering would falsify it. The same residual σ₁–σ₂ symmetry that keeps the solar splitting small also fixes the atmospheric mixing to be maximal — its quantitative consequence (θ₂₃) is treated in §5 and in the dedicated mixing paper.

**The PMNS matrix as misalignment.** The three flavour eigenstates (ν_e, ν_μ, ν_τ) are fixed by gauge birth — each the SU(2)_L partner of a charged lepton, the basis in which the weak interaction is diagonal. The three mass eigenstates (ν₁, ν₂, ν₃) are fixed by the eigenmode decomposition relative to the Higgs VEV direction — the basis in which the geometric tension is diagonal. These bases do not coincide, because the SU(2)_L coupling that defines flavour and the VEV direction that defines mass eigenmodes are independently determined. The PMNS matrix is the rotation between them, |ν_α⟩ = Σᵢ U_αi |ν_i⟩, and the large observed angles (θ₂₃ ≈ 45°, θ₁₂ ≈ 34°) record substantial misalignment between the gauge-birth flavour directions and the Higgs-eigenmode mass directions on S³.

**Phase drift is the oscillation.** The three mass eigenmodes accumulate phase at different rates because each has a different engagement with the temporal projection — a different mass — so over distance L the relative phase grows as Δφ_ij = Δm²_ij L / (2E). A neutrino produced as a flavour eigenstate is a fixed superposition of the three mass eigenmodes; as it propagates they drift in relative phase, and a detection event — a weak interaction, which projects onto the flavour basis — samples the instantaneous orientation, which may have rotated into a different flavour. In the geometric picture this drift is literal: the unresolved S³ configuration is a superposition of three eigenmodes each sliding along a different su(2) direction at a different rate, and detection projects the sliding configuration onto the SU(2)_L flavour basis.

**Why only neutrinos oscillate.** Charged leptons are fully resolved: strong Higgs coupling pins them to definite points on S², the projection is locked, there is no fibre smearing and no sliding — generation identity is a topological fixed point. Neutrinos are unresolved: minimal coupling leaves them smeared, the three eigenmodes drift because none is pinned strongly enough to prevent it, and generation identity is a sliding superposition rather than a fixed point. This is exactly the observed asymmetry — neutrinos oscillate between generations and charged leptons do not.

## 4. The role in the cross-thickness (κ) coupling

The neutrino enters the framework's cross-thickness coupling κ as the geometric object that coupling acts on, and the connection is a direct consequence of non-resolution.

**Coupling through the unreached base.** The neutrino never engages the S¹ fibre of the Hopf projection; its configuration is near-uniform on the fibre and does not project onto S². The S² that supports the relevant harmonic is therefore not a base the neutrino reaches and then has "absorbed" by an identification ν = ν̄ — it is the base the unprojected mode *leaves behind*. Gravity, being a property of the full S³ boundary geometry rather than of the projected S² physics, couples to this residual geometry through its isotropic component, the s-wave harmonic Y₀₀(S²) = 1/√(4π). The neutrino's bilateral extent across both CPN-conjugate orientations of the N-thickness is what makes this coupling cross-thickness rather than local.

**What sets the factor of three.** The bare coupling reads κ₀ = 3 × Y₀₀(S²) = 3/√(4π) ≈ 0.846. The factor of three is the dimension of the adjoint of SU(2) — the three gauge-field components A^a (a = 1, 2, 3), each contributing one isotropic s-wave amplitude — and not a count of spinor polarisations (the fixed-sign Killing-spinor space on round S³ is two-dimensional). This is the structural anchor for the "3" (an SU(2)-adjoint reading in the manner of non-Abelian monopole-projection analyses), and it is convention-independent.

A clarifying point, because two distinct "threes" both trace to su(2) and were once conflated. The three *flavour eigenmodes* of §3 and the three *adjoint gauge components* in κ₀ are different roles of the same algebraic fact, dim su(2) = 3 — the eigenmodes live on the flavour axis (their splitting gives the mass hierarchy), while the multiplicity in κ₀ is the adjoint gauge-component sum. The eigenmodes are structurally parallel to, but do not *supply*, the κ₀ count. Both rest on there being exactly three su(2) generators.

**The closure, and the fourth-neutrino exclusion.** The bare coupling closes to the physical value by the CPN-symmetric resummation κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) = 5.50, an architectural step carried by the κ-closure paper; this companion supplies only the geometric object the bare coupling reads from. The exclusion of a fourth light Majorana neutrino has its cleanest grounding here as a topological fact rather than a numerical accident: SU(2) ≅ S³ has exactly three generators, so the unresolved geometry admits exactly three eigenmodes and the adjoint admits exactly three components. There is no fourth su(2) direction for a fourth mode to occupy. (That a fourth contribution would also push the bare coupling past the convergence threshold κ₀ < 1 is a consistency check on the same fact, not an independent argument.)

## 5. Mass and mixing: status, with the quantitative work cross-referenced

This ontology fixes the *structure* of the mass and mixing sector; the *numbers* are derived in the dedicated papers, and the honest current status is as follows.

**Mixing.** The maximal atmospheric angle θ₂₃ = π/4 follows from the residual Z₂ symmetry σ₁ ↔ σ₂ transverse to the Higgs VEV — the same near-degeneracy that keeps the solar splitting small. The dedicated mixing paper sharpens this: read as a μ–τ *reflection* (an antilinear symmetry) rather than a bare exchange, it keeps θ₂₃ = π/4 while making θ₁₃ ≠ 0 natural and forcing |δ_CP| = π/2 — falsifiable at DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande. The reflection shares the antilinear *character* of the bilateral CPN structure but is a distinct operator from it (a generation-internal operation, established parallel to the inter-orientation CPN swap, not descended from it). The solar and reactor angles θ₁₂, θ₁₃ are not yet derived geometrically and remain open.

**Masses.** The absolute scale comes from the seesaw with a heavy right-handed Majorana scale M_R, m_ν ∼ Y_ν² v_EW²/M_R. Here the σ₃ (atmospheric) and σ₁/σ₂ (solar) sectors carry different Dirac Yukawas, and the honest current status is a genuine, flagged tension: with a single M_R, the solar leg is reproduced tightly while the atmospheric leg overshoots by roughly a factor 1.5. The two legs anti-correlate in the relevant flux, so no single choice fits both — this is a real structural tension, presented as such and not tuned away, with the (flux)^(2/3) *shape* of the mass ratio the genuine fallible content. The seesaw normalisation itself factorises cleanly through the two sectors — a single (un-doubled) Dirac flux on the within-particle coupling and the doubled Majorana flux on the heavy mass — which is the structure the mass-ratio paper computes. The quantitative treatment, the branch question, and the open atmospheric residual are carried by Companions 10 and 13; what this paper establishes is why there are three sectored eigenmodes for those papers to act on.

The division of labour is the point: this companion proves the ontology (three unresolved eigenmodes, sectored by the Higgs direction); the mass-sector papers prove the magnitudes and carry the open tensions. Neither claims what the other establishes.

## 6. Relation to the Born rule

The Born-rule companion establishes that the probability rule activates at gauge birth, when U(1) gauge symmetry makes the global phase unobservable and creates the Hopf fibre. The unresolved ontology adds a clarification of domain rather than a modification. The neutrino's unresolved configuration is a perfectly well-defined quantum state — it has a wavefunction and evolves unitarily — but its projection-state on the Bloch S² is not localised; the amplitude is smeared across the fibre. Between detections the neutrino is not in a Born-rule state *on S²*; it is an unresolved S³ configuration undergoing standard unitary evolution. A detection — a weak interaction projecting onto the SU(2)_L flavour basis — *forces* the projection, collapsing the smeared distribution to a definite flavour eigenstate, and the Born rule applies at that instant. The oscillation probability is therefore the Born rule applied at the detection endpoint, not a rule operating during propagation. This does not weaken the Born rule; it locates the neutrino as the entity for which the Hopf fibre is disengaged, which is the single fact that simultaneously explains its cross-thickness coupling, its oscillation, its tiny mass, and the detection-only character of its projection.

## 7. Falsifiability

The ontology makes definite, currently-testable commitments.

- **Normal ordering is required.** The three-eigenmode decomposition forces one heavier state and two nearly-degenerate lighter states (m₃ > m₂ ≳ m₁). Confirmed inverted ordering (JUNO, DUNE) falsifies the mechanism. Present data favour normal ordering at modest significance.
- **No fourth light neutrino.** Exactly three su(2) generators ⇒ exactly three eigenmodes. A confirmed fourth light neutrino with Majorana mass (a genuine fourth mass eigenstate, e.g. from the short-baseline anomalies if they survive) falsifies the framework.
- **The neutrino must be unresolved in NTE's sense.** This is deeper than the textbook Majorana condition but implies it: a genuinely Dirac neutrino, with an independent right-handed partner carrying distinct quantum numbers, would be a *resolved* particle, not an unresolved configuration. Definitive establishment of Dirac neutrinos (e.g. a firm null in neutrinoless double-beta decay across the relevant range) rules out the mechanism.
- **Charged leptons must not oscillate between generations.** Resolved fermions are pinned and cannot slide. Charged-lepton flavour violation of the oscillatory kind has never been seen (e.g. BR(μ → eγ) < 4 × 10⁻¹³). Detection of generation oscillation for a resolved fermion would falsify the geometric-sliding picture.

## 8. Summary

The neutrino is not its own antiparticle; it is a configuration for which the particle/antiparticle distinction has never been applied, because the Hopf projection that would create the binary requires an engagement with the S¹ fibre that the neutrino's minimal Higgs coupling does not provide. What is unresolved is the conjugation axis, not the chirality: the active neutrino is left-handed and unresolved at once. Its mass is the geometric cost of incomplete resolution; its oscillation is the sliding of an unresolved configuration through the three su(2) eigenmodes, degenerate before symmetry breaking and split by the Higgs VEV into the observed normal-ordered hierarchy; its role in the cross-thickness coupling is the consequence of its failure to project, coupling through the isotropic Y₀₀ harmonic on the S² it never reaches, with the factor of three fixed by the SU(2) adjoint and the closure carried downstream. The quantitative masses and mixings — including the genuine, flagged atmospheric tension — are established by the dedicated mass-sector papers; this companion proves the geometric structure they act on. The neutrino is the one Standard Model fermion that carries the null-time geometry into the resolved epoch: everything else was processed into the binary, and the neutrino was not.
