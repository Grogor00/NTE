## C.1 Where the Framework Is Currently Losing

Five results currently sit on the wrong side of observation, or below the tier the framework would want for them. They are live, they are not footnotes, and none has been tuned away.

**The atmospheric neutrino mass ratio — overshooting by ~1.5×.** The framework's flux-line structure fixes the *shape* of the ratio between the atmospheric and solar mass scales, and with a single heavy scale M_R the solar leg is reproduced tightly while the atmospheric leg overshoots by roughly a factor of 1.5. The two legs anti-correlate in the relevant flux, so no single choice fits both — a genuine structural tension, presented as such. The fallible content is the (flux)^(2/3) shape itself; if the tension hardens as oscillation data sharpen, the flux-line reading of the mass sector fails at its own load-bearing joint.

**Dark energy — the naked prediction under pressure.** The framework predicts that the dark-energy equation of state is exactly w = −1, with no freedom to adjust it: the late universe's acceleration is geometric unloading toward a de Sitter endpoint, and that endpoint has one value. DESI's second data release, combined with the microwave background, currently prefers an evolving equation of state at roughly 3.1 standard deviations from −1 in the supernova-independent combination. (The significance quoted from supernova-based combinations was higher still until a 2026 recalibration of the DES sample brought it down from about 4.2σ to 3.2σ — a reminder that the measurement side is itself moving.) This is the framework's sharpest current disagreement with data, and Chapter 13's honesty applies here too: there is no internal adjustment available. Section C.5 explains why the framework's own void mechanism cannot be recruited to rescue it — and why the framework says so rather than quietly leaving the option open. The verdict window is 2027–2029.

**The solar mixing angle — a claim withdrawn under measurement.** Until 2026 the framework carried sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3 as a structural leading-order value for the solar mixing angle, presented as awaiting correction terms. JUNO's first dedicated measurement gives 0.3092 ± 0.0087, which excludes 1/3 at between 3.5 and 3.8 standard deviations. Pressed on provenance, the value proved thinner than its billing: it followed from assuming an even-handed democracy among the generations within the plane that the μ–τ reflection leaves untouched — a natural default in neutrino model-building, but not something the deposited structure derives. So the framework has not softened the claim; it has withdrawn it, and it appears again in C.3's withdrawn ledger. Solar mixing is now an *open* problem, and a 2026 investigation sharpened how open. It was thought to be gated on one piece of structural homework — the dictionary connecting the framework's geometric labels to the three generations. That dictionary turns out to be necessary but not sufficient. It fixes which slot the solar angle lives in; it does not fix the angle. The remaining freedom is a single number in the way the symmetry is broken, with the two candidate mixing patterns sitting at its two endpoints, and **the algebra does not choose between them**. The framework fixes the atmospheric angle and the magnitude of the CP phase, and does not determine the solar angle at all.

--- A near-miss candidate value exists in the model-building literature, and the framework declines to adopt it: taking a number that happens to sit close to the measurement, before the structure that would force it has been established, is precisely the number-fitting this framework forbids itself elsewhere. Losing a prediction cleanly is better than replacing it with a fitted one.

**Primordial lithium-7 — a factor of 1.58, improved but not resolved.** The framework's treatment moves the predicted ⁷Li/H abundance from the standard picture's factor-2.9 discrepancy to 2.53 × 10⁻¹⁰ against an observed (1.6 ± 0.3) × 10⁻¹⁰ — a residual factor of 1.58. Better is not solved. The framework carries this as partial work in progress, not as a success, and the residual may yet prove to be nuclear-physics systematics rather than anything the framework can claim.

**The baryon asymmetry's magnitude — order-one only.** The *existence* of the asymmetry is a zero-parameter consequence of the maximal CP phase, and the leptogenesis scale passes its cross-check. But the precise magnitude, η ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰, the framework reproduces only at order-one structural consistency; a zero-parameter derivation of the number itself would require closing the heavy-scale derivation fully, and that has not been done. The asymmetry's *sign* is not fixed either — the phase's sign is free spectral data within our sector, correlated with the mirror's opposite sign but not pinned on its own.

One further honest entry, of a different kind: the **solar-mass branch fork**. The striking 0.993× match of the second-generation neutrino mass lands on the 8π branch of an open normalisation choice; the 4π branch fits the ratio between the oscillation scales instead, and no single choice currently fits both legs. Until the fork is closed from the geometry, the match is carried as branch-contingent — a caveat the framework states wherever the number appears.

## C.2 The Named Gaps

Open work, roughly in the corpus's own priority order. Each is a derivation the framework needs and does not yet have; each is named precisely so that failure to close it remains visible.

**Why the field couples to curvature the way it does.** For a long time the top item here was the kinetic normalisation — how the boundary field's kinetic energy varies as the field moves, written K(Φ) in the technical papers, and left as the simplest assumption rather than a derivation. Chapter 15 called it a fuse, and the two inflationary numbers were wired to it.

Part of that homework is now done. The framework fixes the field's coupling to spacetime curvature at the conformal value — a specific, standard choice, not a dial — and once that is fixed, the kinetic normalisation follows with no freedom left anywhere in the chain. The resulting predictions are the spectral index n_s = 0.9692 and the tensor-to-scalar ratio r = 0.0053, at the framework's default expansion depth.

What remains open is one level deeper, and the framework is careful not to let the closure claim more than it earned. *Why* the coupling takes the conformal value is not derived: it is adopted, and the route that would derive it from the framework's spectral machinery is still being built. One route to it has already been closed — by proof, negatively: the attempt to have the field enter the spectral operator through the metric alone was shown to be empty, which is a real result and is filed as one. Two further caveats travel with the numbers: applying the standard slow-roll formulae across the framework's Euclidean early section is itself an assumption, and the numbers move if the expansion depth is pinned differently (at 55 e-folds the pair becomes 0.9646 and 0.0070). So the pair is conditional with its inputs stated — no longer "awaiting any derivation at all," not yet zero-parameter. The expansion depth itself is under review at the time of writing, and the reader should expect these two numbers to move before they settle.

**Independent grounding of the gauge-birth temperature.** The temperature at which the gauge birth happened, T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π), was promoted to the derived tier when its width argument closed. In 2026 that promotion was partly walked back. The argument assumes a saddle point of the *constrained* system; the raw spectral trace, examined directly, turns out to be strictly monotone in overall scale, so there is no extremum there to stand on — the earlier route was an artefact of where the expansion was cut off. A candidate independent grounding remains, through a modulus of the framework's fibre geometry, and it is open. Until it closes, the temperature is conditional, and the scorecard of Chapter 15 carries it that way.

**The derivation of M_R.** The heavy seesaw scale has an identified structural pathway (M_Pl^(3/4) v_EW^(1/4) ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV) but no closed derivation. Closing it would also feed the asymmetry magnitude and the branch fork above.

**The Yukawa hierarchy.** The distribution of embedding depths — why the electron's coupling is what it is, why the generations space as they do — is not derived. The framework's own technical work confirms the point rather than obscuring it: within the current spectral-triple formulation the Yukawa magnitudes enter as free structure, not outputs. This is the deepest open front, and the framework says so.

**Three-generation hypercharges through J₃(𝕆).** The generation count and gauge group are adopted from the exceptional-Jordan programme with credit; the framework's own cross-consistency requires verifying the hypercharge assignments through that algebra in full, which remains open.

**The atmospheric mass ratio, and the dictionary that gates solar mixing.** The atmospheric tension of C.1 is listed here too, because it is also a calculation: the correction structure that would reconcile it is identified work, not mystery. Solar mixing is a different case since the withdrawal — it is not a matter of correction terms on a value the framework still holds. Establishing the generation dictionary is necessary, but it would place the angle rather than predict it: a further free parameter in the symmetry breaking remains, and nothing in the algebra pins it. No solar-angle commitment is available, and none will be until that freedom is closed from the geometry rather than fitted to the measurement.

**Technical naturalness — the non-associative programme.** The framework's proposed route to why the Higgs sector is stable against quantum corrections runs through the pre-temporal epoch's non-associative structure. The programme is underway and could refute rather than confirm.

**The numerical value of Λ.** The structural quietness stands independently; the number does not exist yet within the framework, and a previously quoted value was withdrawn when its route was found closed.

**The Ryu–Takayanagi coefficient.** The framework's boundary-entropy structure should reproduce the exact 1/4 of holographic entanglement entropy; the derivation is open.

**The ghost-free bimetric completion — open refinement.** The κ derivation's action foundation is supplied: a single bilateral action yields the coupled two-metric equations, consistent in the averaged sector where the ratio is actually evaluated, which is why the corpus carries κ as derived without an action caveat (Chapter 9 tells the story of that gap closing). What remains is the refinement beyond that arena — extending the bilateral action to a complete nonlinear ghost-free theory of two coupled metrics, in the established Hassan–Rosen class. The corpus holds that this completion would not move the averaged-sector value; the honest stress-test joint, stated for any critic who wants it, is whether anything in the full nonlinear completion could feed back into the averaged sector and shift what is computed there. That question is the derivation's remaining exposure, and it stays on this ledger until the completion lands.

## C.3 The Withdrawn Ledger

Under the framework's no-numerology discipline — no quantity may be assembled from measured inputs to match a measured output — three constructions previously carried in the framework's derived ledger were withdrawn: a characterisation formula for β_NTE, a formula for the reactor angle θ₁₃, and a formula for the Cabibbo angle. Each took observed masses plus an interpretive coefficient and reproduced a target observable — the signature of number-fitting, however pretty the coefficients. Their withdrawal removed nothing structural; each had already been flagged as not derived from first principles.

A fourth entry joined them in 2026, and it was withdrawn under a different pressure: **sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3**, the leading-order solar mixing angle, excluded by JUNO's first measurement at 3.5–3.8σ (C.1). The first three were withdrawn by discipline, before any measurement forced the issue; this one was withdrawn by data. Both routes matter, and the ledger keeps them distinguishable.

These are recorded here because a framework that quietly deletes its retractions has not made them. The discipline also runs in both directions, and in both directions on the *same* quantity: the spectral index was demoted from the chord (Chapter 12), the gauge-birth temperature was promoted to the derived tier when its derivation closed — and then demoted again to conditional in 2026 when the argument under it was found to rest on a truncation artefact (C.2). Grading that moves both ways, including backwards on something the framework had already claimed, is how the reader knows it is grading.

## C.4 Unexpected Neighbourhoods

Several of the framework's structural commitments connect it to areas of physics and mathematics traditionally unrelated to cosmology or neutrino physics. Each connection is a place where progress made by entirely different communities, for entirely different reasons, bears directly on NTE — and where NTE's structure makes commitments those communities could test.

**Holographic entanglement entropy.** The framework's boundary-only ontology must reproduce the Ryu–Takayanagi relation between entanglement and geometry, coefficient included. A structure developed for string-theoretic holography becomes a consistency requirement on a framework that never invokes strings.

**Bimetric gravity.** The mathematics of consistent two-metric theories — developed to explore massive gravity — is the consistency frame for the framework's two orientations. Results proved or broken in that programme transfer directly.

**The CPT-symmetric universe.** The closest published predecessor the framework has (Boyle, Finn, and Turok, 2018, with its dark-matter companion): a universe–antiuniverse pair from the bang, the matter–antimatter asymmetry sorted to opposite sides rather than annihilated, and dark matter stabilised by a Z₂ symmetry on the neutrino sector — architecture Chapter 14 credits in full. The live discriminators are the kind experiment can read. Their dark matter is a sterile right-handed neutrino of definite mass; the framework predicts no such particle, its dark matter being the gravitational footprint of the mirror orientation with κ fixed by geometry. Their cosmos is inflation-free with an essentially vanishing primordial tensor signal; the framework carries a conditional tensor ratio of about five parts in a thousand — within reach of the coming experiments, and a clean separation if it is seen. And their twin is genuinely time-reversed where the framework's two orientations share one forward time — a structural fork that the paired observables above are the empirical shadow of. Progress and constraints in their programme bear directly on this one, in both directions.

**Null-hypersurface quantisation.** The closest external relative on the emergent-time axis rather than the cosmological one: a programme in which time appears as a quantum effect on a null surface, and the area form has to be treated as a discrete operator (Ciambelli, Freidel and Leigh, 2024). The resemblance is real and the framework credits it as convergence rather than confirmation — that construction starts on a surface where the light-cone structure already exists, and yields a preferred clock downstream of the moment this framework is trying to describe. The two are stacked layers, not rivals. Which is the more interesting relationship: independent programmes arriving at overlapping structure from different directions is weak evidence that the structure is real, and it is the only kind of evidence available for a claim that makes no post-lock observable.

---

**The exceptional-Jordan and octonionic algebra programme.** The framework's particle content stands on algebra developed as pure mathematics a century ago and revived by a small community connecting division algebras to the Standard Model. Progress there — particularly the hypercharge verification — is progress here, and the framework credits the lineage explicitly.

**Spectral geometry.** The Connes–Chamseddine spectral action supplies the framework's instrument-and-chord machinery, and one of its established results (the vanishing fourth heat-kernel coefficient on the framework's substrate) is simultaneously the reason the cosmological-constant suppression is structural and the reason the spectral index left the chord. A theorem from noncommutative geometry doing double duty in cosmology.

**The μ–τ symmetry literature.** The maximal atmospheric angle and maximal CP phase arrive through a reflection symmetry with a long history in neutrino model-building; the framework's contribution is the geometric seat, and the credit is stated. The same literature is where any future solar-angle commitment would have to be argued, which is part of why the framework will not take one on loan.

**Charged-lepton flavour violation.** A conditional signature of a distinctive kind: if CLFV is ever observed at all, the framework fixes the amplitude ratio between channels — |A(μ→eγ)|²/|A(τ→eγ)|² = tan²θ₂₃ — even though the framework's own heavy scale makes observation unlikely. It is worth writing that ratio out, because the trigonometry disguises how sharp it is. The framework does not leave θ₂₃ free: it predicts exactly π/4, at which tan²θ₂₃ is exactly 1. So the claim is that the two channels come with *equal* amplitude, and any observed inequality between them falsifies either the maximal atmospheric angle or the structure that carries it into the flavour sector. Two facts keep that from being more than it is. The heavy scale makes observation almost certainly unreachable, so this is a prediction written for the record rather than one anyone expects to collect. And equality is less rare than it sounds: drawing the relevant mixing structures at random produces a ratio this close to one about one time in ten. That is substantial, and it is not overwhelming, and the framework says so rather than leaving the reader to supply their own odds. What the claim is finally worth is that the framework committed to a definite value of a ratio it was under no obligation to speak to at all — stated plainly rather than in disguise, because stating it costs nothing and a surprise observation would score it.

**A gauge-birth gravitational-wave background.** The locking transition should leave a stochastic background with a spectral shape distinct from both inflationary and phase-transition templates — a target for the gravitational-wave observatories of the 2030s and beyond, sitting at the intersection of the framework's pre-temporal structure and an observational programme built for entirely other purposes.

## C.5 The Experimental Decade

The instruments that will strengthen or falsify the framework, and what each one rules on:

**JUNO** (taking data now) — has already ruled once, against the framework: the solar mixing angle measurement that forced the withdrawal in C.1. Still pending, and sharper: the neutrino mass ordering. The framework requires normal; inverted falsifies outright.

**DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande** (late 2020s onward) — the CP phase (framework: maximal, |δ| = π/2, zero-parameter) and the atmospheric octant (framework: exactly π/4). Hard tests of two of the three strictest predictions.

**KamLAND-Zen, LEGEND and successors** — neutrinoless double beta decay in the few-meV effective-mass window that normal ordering implies. Observation confirms the unresolved ontology; a firm null across the window rules it out.

**CMB-S4** (early 2030s) — the dark-matter ratio at twice Planck's precision (the third strict prediction, κ = 5.50) and sensitivity reaching the framework's conditional tensor ratio, with a sharpened spectral index.

**LiteBIRD** — the tensor-to-scalar ratio from orbit, closing the same net from the other side. The framework's conditional r = 0.0053 sits inside the detection window of both experiments.

**DESI full survey** — the dark-energy equation of state, and the framework's hardest current test. Framework: w = −1 exactly, currently in ~3.1σ tension (C.1). One point deserves stating plainly, because the framework has an obvious escape route available and is refusing it. The framework's void mechanism — underdense regions expanding slightly faster, preferentially sampled by low-redshift measurements — was for a time read as possibly explaining the DESI result as an artefact. Forward-modelling in 2026 killed that reading. The bias makes low-redshift sources look *brighter*, and when a survey fitted with that bias is analysed the standard way, the inferred equation of state moves to the phantom side, today's value below −1 — the opposite quadrant from where DESI actually sits. Reproducing DESI's pattern would require low-redshift sources looking *fainter* by a few hundredths of a magnitude, which void-enhanced expansion cannot do. And the mechanism is a sampling effect on distance indicators, so it cannot shift the supernova-independent constraint at all. If a real void bias is present, correcting for it would *strengthen* any measured departure from −1, not weaken it. The same mechanism still does its original job on the Hubble tension, where the brighter direction is the correct one; it simply cannot do both, and the framework now says which. The mechanism's own test is separate and live: supernova residuals should be brighter along void-dominated sightlines, testable by crossing existing supernova catalogues with void maps. Verdict window on the equation of state: 2027–2029.

**Rubin Observatory and Euclid** — the transition in the dark-matter-fraction scatter at the neutrino free-streaming coherence mass, mapped across hundreds of millions of systems; and the void anatomy of the Hubble tension (framework: directional scatter of 2–4 km/s/Mpc correlated with line-of-sight density). Two honest qualifications on the transition. The free-streaming scale is standard neutrino physics rather than an NTE derivation — what the framework claims is its combination with the M^(−1/2) scaling and the mean of 5.50, and the scatter's amplitude within a single coherence patch is carried as open. And the threshold is not fixed: free-streaming scales inversely with the neutrino mass, so the transition sits at a cluster-scale mass of order 10¹⁴ suns for a standard reference neutrino mass and moves as that mass moves. A survey result on the transition has to be read against whichever neutrino mass turns out to be right.

**High-luminosity LHC** — the absences: no fourth generation, no W_R, with bounds rising. The WIMP direct-detection programme (XENON, LUX-ZEPLIN, PandaX) continues the third absence in parallel.

**JWST and successors,** peripherally — early-structure statistics that stress or support the framework's closed-geometry growth history.

The decade's shape is worth stating plainly: by the mid-2030s, the mass ordering will be settled, the CP phase measured, κ tightened, the equation of state resolved, and the double-beta window largely explored. The framework does not get to wait out its tests. Most of its ledger converts from "pending" to "passed" or "broken" within the working life of this book's first edition — and one entry has already converted to "broken" while the book was being written.

## C.6 Reading This Ledger

The entries above are the framework's exposure, kept current deliberately. The tensions of C.1 may resolve — the atmospheric ratio may find its correction structure, the branch fork may close on the matching branch, the equation of state may return to −1 as the measurements sharpen — or they may harden into the failures that end the framework. The gaps of C.2 may close into theorems or into refutations. That is what it means for the working bet to be a bet.

The solar angle is the worked example of what this appendix is for. It was carried as a leading-order structural value; a measurement arrived; the value was wrong; the framework checked what the claim had actually been resting on, found it thinner than advertised, and withdrew it rather than reaching for the nearest surviving number. The book you are holding says so in three places, and this is one of them.

What this appendix commits the framework to is narrower and firmer: that the ledger stays public, that losses are recorded at the volume of wins, that withdrawn claims stay withdrawn and visible, and that when the experiments of C.5 report, the scorecard of Chapter 15 gets rewritten to match the sky — whichever way the sky rules.
