## *Where Causality Begins*

> *Before time, there was no order to enforce. After time, the order is locked. What we call the gauge group is what survives the locking.*

Six chapters have built a picture you can carry: a closed expanding three-sphere; time emerging from expansion through |Φ_NTE| (not the CERN Higgs, though their values coincide today); the photon at the no-time limit; quantum mechanics sharing Hopf geometry with the cosmological boundary.

Every chapter so far assumed that picture was **already** in place — clocks ticking, forces in their familiar forms, particles with mass.

This chapter asks what those chapters skipped: **what happened at the e-folds where none of that was true yet?**

## The Strange Question

If time comes from |Φ_NTE| and expansion, what was happening while |Φ_NTE| had not yet settled into the form that gives a steady **dt/dN**? What existed before the Standard Model's gauge symmetries, before Yukawa masses, before the electroweak Higgs was a field at all?

The framework places that era at **N < N_lock** (with *N_lock* ≈ 0.1 in its counting). Call it the **pre-temporal regime**. The transition at *N* = *N_lock* is the **gauge birth** — the moment the universe we know switched on.

One caution about the counting, because this book uses **N** for more than one thing and a reader will try to put them on a single axis. **N_lock ≈ 0.1** measures the **span** of the locking transition — how much expansion the cascade takes to complete, with the heaviest modes locking around a thousandth of an e-fold and the leptons around a hundredth. It is not a depth on the inflationary counter, which measures how far back the structures we read in the microwave background left the horizon. And neither is the total run, which the framework puts near ∼140 e-folds (Part 1). Three counters, three jobs, one letter.

Your intuition will keep whispering *before*. The framework cannot use that word honestly: *before* is temporal, and time had not crystallised yet. The honest handle is **at smaller N** — a geometric fact, not a clock reading. The universe had shape and expansion; it did not yet have the coordinate we experience as time.

## Two Fields — Why This Chapter Matters

Chapter 1 introduced two scalars that **look alike today** but are not the same thing:

| Field | Job today | Locked at gauge birth? |
|-------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **SM Higgs** (CERN, 2012) | Mass via Yukawa coupling; fixed at 246 GeV | **No** — born at the birth |
| **\|Φ_NTE\|** | Expansion rate via *H* = β_NTE\|Φ_NTE\|²; varies with cosmic epoch | **Yes** — present all along |

Earlier chapters could blur them because their **numbers** coincide at our cosmic moment. Pre-temporally, the blur is impossible: there is no Higgs doublet, no Yukawa machinery, no SU(2) weak sector. What exists is **\|Φ_NTE\|** at high strength, sourcing violent early expansion, plus a population of **pre-temporal modes** — degrees of freedom on S³ with various **embedding depths** *δ* (how strongly each mode engages \|Φ_NTE\|), not yet labelled photon, quark, or neutrino.

**Hold this picture for the pre-temporal regime:** expanding closed S³; one geometric scalar driving *H*; modes waiting for identity. No Standard Model yet. Both the Higgs and the post-locking role of \|Φ_NTE\| crystallise together at the gauge birth — as **separate** identities.

(A deeper rhyme — algebra without spacetime points, patches that only later look like locations — is Chapter 14's Palatial thread. Enough here to say: pre-temporally there are no points as primitive data, only the structure points will emerge from.)

## Algebra Without an Order of Operations

Gauge birth is not a vague threshold. The framework ties it to a change in **what multiplication means**.

Pre-temporally, the state space lives in the **octonions** — an eight-dimensional algebra where (*a* × *b*) × *c* need not equal *a* × (*b* × *c*). Bracket order matters. Demanding a single unambiguous product order is demanding **time** — something not yet there.

When temporal structure switches on at the gauge birth, **associativity** becomes enforceable. The algebra drops to its associative core, the **quaternions** — and what survives as symmetry is smaller than the full octonionic symmetry.

Here the framework stands, deliberately and with credit, on other people's shoulders. That the octonions' structure carries the Standard Model's gauge group **SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1)** and its three generations of fermions is the result of an established research programme — division-algebra and exceptional-Jordan-algebra work stretching from Günaydin and Gürsey in the 1970s through Furey, Todorov and Dubois-Violette in the present day. The framework adopts that structure; it does not claim to have derived it. What the framework adds is the piece that programme left open: the *when* and the *why*. The octonion-to-quaternion reduction is not, on the framework's reading, an abstract algebraic preference — it is a **cosmic event**, the enforcement of associativity at the moment time switches on. The gauge birth is the algebra's associativity transition wearing a date.

You do not need octonion homework to hold the claim: **the Standard Model gauge group is what Hurwitz's hierarchy of division algebras leaves behind when time enforces an order of operations** — the hierarchy is 1898 mathematics (Chapter 14 names the theorem), the gauge-group correspondence is the credited modern programme, and the framework's own stake is that the leaving-behind *happened*, once, at N_lock.

## What Happens at N_lock — Four at Once

Several identities crystallise at the **same** e-fold. That is why the framework calls it a birth, not a slow dimmer switch.

**1. Time.** \|Φ_NTE\| settles so **dt/dN** is well-defined. The trajectory of \|Φ_NTE\| through *N* has a sharp kink at the birth — geometric signature of the transition. Post-locking, *t* is a coordinate the rest of the book can use.

**2. Gauge structure.** Octonions → quaternions; **SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1)** switches on. Pre-temporal modes take up their places within the structure the reduction leaves — colour, charge, their seats in the generations — not by hand but by the algebra the credited programme mapped.

**3. Mass.** Each mode's embedding depth *δ* becomes a **Yukawa coupling** *y* when it crosses its threshold. Heaviest modes lock first; lighter ones follow. Post-locking mass is *m* ≈ *y* × *v*_EW with *v*_EW = 246 GeV. The **ordering** of masses follows from *δ*; the **specific δ values** are structural inputs the framework has not yet derived from deeper principles — honest scope, and Appendix C carries the gap by name.


A word about **embedding depth**, because the term has two jobs and the difference matters later. At the gauge birth it names a property of a fundamental mode — how strongly that mode engages |Φ_NTE|, which is what fixes its place in the locking order and becomes its Yukawa coupling. That is the sense this chapter and the next one use. But what embeds a **thing** in the expansion, now, is its rest energy, and for anything composite the rest energy is mostly not Yukawa at all: a proton's mass is about ninety-nine per cent the binding energy of massless gluons. Chapter 1 made that point and it stands — rest energy is what embeds, wherever the rest energy came from. The two readings coincide for a fundamental fermion and part company for everything built out of them.
**4. The bang.** Pre-temporal matter sat at enormous density **without** gauge forces to thermalise it. When strong, weak, and electromagnetic interactions switch on, that matter is violently out of equilibrium. The energy released fills the hot radiation bath we later see as the **cosmic microwave background**.

So: the Big Bang, on this reading, was **not** the beginning of time. It was the **thermal scream** when time and the Standard Model arrived. No inflaton required — the energy was already there, locked in pre-temporal matter, released when the rules changed.

The **SM Higgs** and **\|Φ_NTE\|** acquire their distinct post-locking identities here too — same moment, different jobs forever after.

## The Cascade — and the One That Stumbled

For most modes, locking is only step one. Chapter 6's **Hopf projection** also runs: the U(1) phase on the fibre collapses, and the collapse is what *creates* the particle/antiparticle split — mints the binary, stamps each species matter or antimatter, and pins it to one face of the manifold's thickness (Chapter 3's two faces).

Strong Yukawa coupling pays for that completion. Weak coupling does not.

Almost every species pays comfortably. One species — the **neutrino** — locks with *y* ≈ 10⁻¹², acquires a tiny mass, but the projection **does not finish**. The particle/antiparticle binary is never minted for it; it commits to neither face, and its wavefunction keeps **bilateral support** across both orientations of the manifold's thickness.

That is the fast version, and the next chapter earns the slow one — including the seventy-year-old confusion it untangles about why every neutrino we detect is left-handed (a question, it turns out, about a *different* axis than the one the projection left unresolved). For now, hold only this: one species came out of the birth without a matter-or-antimatter identity, straddling both faces — the geometry Chapter 10 will read as dark matter, Chapter 9 will put a number on.

## Questions This Picture Dissolves

Taken seriously, the gauge-birth reading reframes stubborn cosmology questions:

- **"What came before the Big Bang?"** → Wrong question. Ask **what at smaller N**: geometry without *t*, modes without mass, algebra without associativity.
- **"What banged?"** → Thermalisation when gauge forces switched on, not inflaton decay.
- **"Why this gauge group?"** → It is the structure the octonions carry — mapped by the credited algebra programme — and it switched on when the reduction fired at the birth of time. The framework answers the *when*; the algebraists answered the *what*.

It does **not** dissolve everything. Matter–antimatter asymmetry, Λ, exact Yukawa magnitudes — other chapters. But three famous puzzles change category here.

One species almost failed the cascade's finishing move: locked, massive, but **unresolved** — never stamped matter or antimatter, straddling both faces of the manifold because its coupling was too small to finish the projection.

That species is the neutrino.

In the beginning — at smaller N — there was no time, no gauge structure, no mass. And what there was, the moment the rules changed, thermalised into everything we observe. One traveller on the way through almost did not finish the crossing.
