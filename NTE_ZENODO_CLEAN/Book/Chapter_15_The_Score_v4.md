## §1 What This Chapter Does

This is the line in the book where the framework puts its money down.

The previous chapters built one picture: closed boundary, two scalars, Hopf geometry, bilateral thickness, the unresolved neutrino, the instrument-and-chord arc in Chapters 11–13. The technical papers organise that picture at the gauge-birth boundary; here it's enough to say the pieces lock together — pull on one thread and the others move.

This chapter lists the framework's claims and what each one would cost NTE if it failed. Some already match observation within the quoted errors. Others wait on measurements due in the next decade. A few are bets on *absence*: no WIMP, no fourth generation, no W_R — and so far the experiments keep finding nothing.

The list is not a menu of independent guesses, and it is not a list of equals. The framework grades its own claims, and the scorecard keeps the grades visible. Three claims sit in the strictest tier — *genuine zero-parameter predictions*, numbers or structures forced by the geometry with no observational input anywhere in their chains, made about observables that were never used to build the framework: the dark-matter ratio κ, the maximal CP phase, and the maximal atmospheric mixing angle. Below them sit the chord's structural results at their own registers; a conditional tier whose test dates depend on the framework finishing named pieces of homework; consequences that follow by construction and are honestly not tests at all; the claims the framework has withdrawn; and the open tensions — the places where it is currently *losing* — which have their own catalogue in Appendix C, because a scorecard that lists only wins is advertising, not accounting.

On scope, one correction this edition makes explicit: the chord of Chapter 12 does not include the CMB spectral index. Earlier presentations seated the inflationary tilt among the chord's notes; the framework's own accounting moved it off — the heat-kernel route that would have carried it is closed by an established mathematical result, and its value rests on an input the framework has not yet derived. It appears in this catalogue in the conditional tier, where it lives. Λ likewise stands as structural quietness — the framework's account of why empty space is not catastrophically loud — without a numerical value at the framework's current state; Chapter 13 develops what the structural account claims and does not claim.

The rest of the chapter is the scorecard.

## §2 The Chord — Notes From One Source

These lines are the chord from Chapter 12 — one heat-kernel reading on the spectral triple, each note at its honest register. What each line claims, and where observation stands:

**T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) (gauge-birth temperature) — conditional.** When gauge symmetry and clocks switched on, the boundary field locked and post-temporal dynamics began. **M₁ ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV (leptogenesis scale) — cross-check, passed at order one.** Why matter won over antimatter is the leptogenesis story: the lightest heavy neutrino's mass sets the decay channel, and standard work leaves that mass free. **M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV (heavy seesaw scale) — pathway identified, not derived.** Type-I seesaw models need a heavy partner so light neutrino masses go like v_EW²/M_R; standard treatments fit M_R by hand. The framework has identified a structural pathway — three powers of the Planck mass against one of the Higgs scale, M_R = M_Pl^(3/4) v_EW^(1/4) ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, the canonical seesaw size for sub-eV neutrinos — but the derivation is not closed, and the scorecard carries it at exactly that register: pathway, not theorem. **m_ν₂ ≈ 0.0086 eV (second-generation neutrino mass) — cross-check, branch-contingent.** On the same flux line as the Majorana scale, the Dirac coupling Y_ν₂ = (8π)^(−1/3) feeds the seesaw and delivers a solar-sector mass within 0.7% of the observed oscillation scale (0.993×), with no observational input in the chain. The atmospheric leg meanwhile overshoots by ~1.5× — a live tension carried in Appendix C. Striking, honest, and graded as a cross-check.

**The 2π² volume audit — internal consistency, passed.** One geometric number — the volume of the unit three-sphere — recurs at three separate stages of the computation, doing three different jobs. No telescope measures it; its role is the instrument confirming it is one object. Listed for completeness, weighted accordingly.

**Λ — structural quietness; numerical value open.** Chapter 13: quantum field theory overshoots the vacuum energy by ~10¹²⁰; the framework's boundary-only ontology plus the trace-free treatment of gravity removes the catastrophic term rather than cancelling it. The mechanism is the claim; the number is not — a previously circulated value was withdrawn when its route was found to be closed, and the numerical Λ sits in the open-work ledger. Nor is the mechanism NTE's alone: boundary-only ontologies and trace-free treatments of gravity remove the catastrophic term in other hands too. What the framework claims here is the combination and the company it keeps, not the discovery. Success in deriving it would promote this note to the chord's numerical surface; nothing about the structural mechanism waits on that.

Notes from one calculation, none tuned to fit the sky — and no fixed tally: the count reflects where the mathematics has reached, and the accounting has moved notes in both directions as the work matured. ## §3 The Conditional Pair — n_s and r

Two inflationary observables sit in a tier of their own, and the tier's honesty is the point.

**n_s = 0.9692 (CMB spectral index) — conditional on ξ.** Under the framework's slow-roll reading of the pre-temporal phase, the tilt is 1 − 2/N_e with the e-fold depth N_e = 63.4, fixed by the kinetic normalisation K(Φ) — and K(Φ) is now closed, in the frame the framework works in. There are two anchors to compare against, not one: Planck 2018 gives 0.9649 ± 0.0042, and the P-ACT-LB combination gives 0.9743 ± 0.0034. The corpus shows both rather than choosing, and the reason is plain in the numbers — the two measurements disagree with each other about the tilt by more than the framework's value differs from either of them. No significance figures are quoted here, deliberately: the framework's value depends on the e-fold depth, that depth is under review, and a σ printed today would be wrong by the time the review closes. The register: closing K(Φ) took a conformal coupling, ξ = 1/6, and the *spectral origin of ξ* is open — the framework selects that value on frame-closure grounds, not from the spectral triple. The slow-roll formula's applicability to the pre-temporal section is itself unestablished. So n_s stays a *conditional survivor*: consistent under a named open input, holding reduced predictive credit, explicitly not advanced as a zero-parameter prediction.

**r = 0.0053 (tensor-to-scalar ratio) — conditional on the same input.** The same sector, the same open coupling, delivering a primordial gravitational-wave amplitude below every current bound — but not by much, and not for long.

The conditional tier is not a parking lot; it is a fuse. If the framework derives ξ from the spectral side instead of selecting it, both numbers convert overnight into hard zero-parameter tests — no escape hatch — and the instruments are already coming for them. CMB-S4 and LiteBIRD reach sensitivity near r ≈ 10⁻³ and δn_s ≈ 0.001 in the early 2030s, which puts r = 0.0053 inside the window rather than below it: this is the nearest hard date the framework has. A measurement of r far above the framework's value, even now, would mean the inflationary reading has missed something at the dynamics level; a firm null well under 10⁻³ would do the same from the other side. The test date depends on the framework's own homework, and Appendix C carries the spectral origin of ξ at the top of the open-work list for exactly that reason.

## §4 Cosmology Beyond the Chord

Further cosmological claims follow from the framework's broader structural commitments — the closed three-sphere and the relation H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² — with their grades stated.

**The Hubble parameter H₀ = 67.6 km/s/Mpc — by construction, not a test.** The framework's cosmic-mean expansion rate follows once β_NTE is fixed from Planck-era inputs; the sub-percent closeness to the CMB-derived value (Planck, ACT, SPT: ≈ 67–68) is structural bookkeeping, and the scorecard declines to count it as a passed prediction. The *genuine* prediction in this sector is the tension's anatomy: late-universe direct measurements (SH0ES: ≈ 73) should show a **directional scatter of σ(H₀) ~ 2–4 km/s/Mpc correlated with line-of-sight void fraction** — void enhancement, not new physics. Supernova catalogues and the Rubin Observatory's void mapping test this directly: scatter that correlates with density as predicted supports the reading; local values that fail to converge toward the cosmic mean as systematics are controlled, or scatter with no density correlation, break it.

**The dark-energy equation of state w = −1, exactly — naked, and currently losing.** A clean cosmological constant: structural quietness, not a dynamical field, and no dial anywhere in the framework to turn. DESI DR2 — baryon acoustic oscillations with the CMB, supernova-independent — prefers evolving dark energy over w = −1 at about 3.1σ. The framework has no answer to that. The void mechanism that carries the Hubble tension is signed the wrong way here: it works *against* the DESI preference rather than explaining it, so it cannot be borrowed to soften this. w = −1 is exposed exactly as a prediction should be. Test: DESI's full survey and what follows it. If the preference firms up, w = −1 fails in the open, and the reading of Λ as structural quietness rather than dynamics fails with it.

**The dark-matter variance scaling σ(M) ∝ M^(−1/2).** Above the coherence mass, system-to-system scatter in the dark-to-ordinary ratio must fall off as the square root of mass — a central-limit consequence whose NTE-specific content is its combination with κ = 5.50 and the coherence scale itself. Chapter 10's companion claim: the transition to tight convergence near ~40 Mpc. Rubin's Legacy Survey, Euclid, and the spectroscopic surveys map dark-matter fractions across hundreds of millions of systems; absence of the scaling, or a transition at a substantially different scale, breaks the cross-thickness mechanism's empirical signature.

## §5 Particle Physics

The particle-physics lines carry two of the framework's three zero-parameter predictions, its hardest near-term falsifiers, and its bets on absence.

**The CP phase |δ_CP| = π/2 — zero-parameter, under active test.** The framework's Janus geometry forces maximal CP violation, and the μ–τ reflection symmetry of the neutrino sector carries the same value into the mixing matrix. This is the second of the three genuine zero-parameter predictions, and it is being measured right now: DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande are built to pin the leptonic CP phase within the decade. A measured phase well away from maximal breaks it — no adjustment available. What the geometry fixes is the magnitude, and only the magnitude: |δ_J| = π/2. The sign is free — nothing in the Janus construction selects it. So the framework underwrites maximal CP violation, and with it the *existence* of a baryon asymmetry; it does not underwrite the direction. That matter won rather than antimatter is a fact the framework accommodates, not one it predicts, and the distinction is worth keeping because the opposite sign would have been just as comfortable. The asymmetry's magnitude it carries only at order-one consistency (Appendix C).

**The atmospheric mixing angle θ₂₃ = π/4 — zero-parameter, inherited and credited.** Maximal atmospheric mixing follows from the μ–τ reflection symmetry — a result the framework *inherits from the established μ–τ symmetry literature and credits accordingly*, its own contribution being the geometric seat that symmetry occupies (the residual twin-degeneracy of the eigenmodes transverse to the Higgs direction, Chapter 8). Global fits: 45.0° ± 1.0° — consistent. The third of the three. What the reflection does *not* fix belongs in the same breath, because it is the reason the framework uses reflection rather than the simpler μ–τ permutation: reflection leaves θ₁₃ free, and θ₁₃ is measured nonzero, near 8.6°. A permutation symmetry would have forced θ₁₃ = 0 and been dead before it started. A symmetry that declines to fix an angle is doing work too. Tightening octant data will eventually settle whether exact maximality holds.

**Normal ordering — required, verdict imminent.** The eigenmode geometry of Chapter 8 forces one heavier neutrino above a near-degenerate light pair. Inverted ordering has no home in the framework — not a stretched fit, no fit. JUNO, and DUNE after it, settle this within the decade; present data lean normal at modest significance. This is the framework's sharpest near-term falsifier.

**No fourth light neutrino.** Exactly three su(2) directions, exactly three eigenmodes; there is no fourth slot. A confirmed fourth light mass state falsifies the framework outright.

**Neutrinoless double beta decay — expected, in the few-meV window.** The unresolved conjugation axis permits the process; a genuinely Dirac neutrino forbids it. Because the framework requires normal ordering, its prediction lives where normal ordering puts it: an effective Majorana mass in the narrow few-meV window, at next-generation reach. KamLAND-Zen, LEGEND, and successors are pushing into exactly that range. Observation there confirms the ontology's central claim; a firm null across the motivated window rules it out. (Earlier presentations placed this test in the inverted-hierarchy band; that band is where the framework's own required ordering says the signal is *not*, and this edition corrects the framing.)

**Exactly three generations of fermions — imported, credited; the absence bet stands.** The three-generation count and the Standard Model gauge group come to the framework from the established octonionic and exceptional-Jordan-algebra programme — Günaydin–Gürsey, Furey, Todorov–Dubois-Violette — and the framework claims them as *adopted structure, credited*, not as NTE derivations; NTE's own contribution is the cross-consistency of its 90-species Majorana accounting with that structure. The commitment, however, is fully owned: the algebra permits three generations and forbids a fourth. High-luminosity LHC continues to push fourth-generation bounds higher; an unambiguous fourth-generation fermion breaks the algebraic reading. As of 2026, none has been seen.

**No right-handed W boson (W_R).** The framework's reading places SU(2)_R in the mirror sector — the other orientation of the bilateral manifold. At gauge birth, the electroweak vacuum selects a direction in the imaginary octonions, and CPN combined with parity assigns SU(2)_R to the mirror sector; the observable gauge group in our orientation is SU(3)_C × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y, and parity violation is built into the boundary. SU(2)_R is not a gauge symmetry of our observable sector; therefore W_R is not an observable particle in our orientation. Test: LHC searches for W_R signatures continue to push the lower mass bound higher. If a W_R is unambiguously detected at any mass, the framework's assignment of SU(2)_R to the mirror orientation is wrong. As of 2026, no W_R has been seen.

**No WIMP direct detection.** The framework's dark matter is the gravitational register of the mirror sector's matter through the cross-thickness coupling — no particle in our orientation to find. XENON, LUX-ZEPLIN, PandaX and successors keep finding nothing, with the standard WIMP parameter space now definitively ruled out and the remaining theory space progressively less natural. Each null tightens the framework's bet; one unambiguous detection breaks it.

The catalogue pulls together, with the grades kept visible.

**Passed, at the strictest tier:** κ within 0.83σ of Planck, zero parameters, now backed by the Janus action. **Consistent, at the strictest tier:** θ₂₃ maximal within current precision (inherited, credited); |δ_J| = π/2, with the sign free and the asymmetry's direction therefore not claimed. **Cross-checks passed:** M₁ within a factor 1.1 of the leptogenesis requirement; m_ν₂ within 0.7% of the solar scale on one branch of an open fork — with the branch and the atmospheric overshoot stated in the same breath. **Conditional, fuse lit:** n_s sitting between the two CMB anchors, and r = 0.0053 under every current bound, both resting on ξ = 1/6 with its spectral origin open; and T_GB, pending the fibre-modulus grounding. **By construction, not counted:** H₀ at the cosmic mean; standard BBN recovered (N_eff = 3.044, Y_p = 0.247). **Absences holding:** no WIMP, no fourth generation, no W_R, decade after decade. **Currently losing:** w = −1 against DESI DR2 at about 3.1σ, with nothing internal to absorb it; the atmospheric mass ratio; lithium-7; the asymmetry's magnitude. **Withdrawn — a gap, not a defeat:** the leading-order solar angle. The framework once offered sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3 and no longer does; the algebra does not choose between the candidate values, and on the present reading it does not determine the solar angle at all. That is a hole in the derivation, not a failed test, and a ledger that blurs the two is no use to anyone. The catalogue's claims are not independent test cases. They are pressure points on the same architecture.
