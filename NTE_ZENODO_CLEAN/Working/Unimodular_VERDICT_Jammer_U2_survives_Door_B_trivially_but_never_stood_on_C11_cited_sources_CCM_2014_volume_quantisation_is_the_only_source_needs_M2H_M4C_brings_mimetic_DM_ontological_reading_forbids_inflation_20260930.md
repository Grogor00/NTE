## 5. **C11's two readings are not equivalent, and the difference bites the existence gate**

**5.1 The formal reading** (§3): trace-free equations plus the Bianchi identity make only the *constant* part of the vacuum energy an integration constant. A **time-varying** vacuum energy gravitates through its variation. **Inflation therefore works in unimodular gravity** (standard; also my no-inflation verdict §2.7).

**5.2 The ontological reading** (§4): "a vacuum energy that scales with four-volume is simply the wrong object … the extensive contribution has nothing to multiply."
- Taken literally, this forbids **any** extensive vacuum energy from gravitating, **including an inflationary plateau**.
- An inflationary plateau is a four-volume-scaling vacuum energy, V ≈ const over ~60 e-folds.

**5.3 Why it matters now.** Under Door B, existence requires an accelerated epoch at the handover (C9 §3; my G4 and existence-gate verdicts). **The ontological reading, if kept as "the same fact", forbids the epoch the corpus needs to exist.** The formal reading permits it.

**Repair (minimal):** C11 §4 should say the boundary-only ontology explains why the **constant, extensive** mode does not gravitate, and that time-dependent vacuum energy still gravitates under the trace-free equations. Delete "the two readings are the same fact" and replace it with "the ontology motivates the formal restriction, which is narrower". **The Dark Sector draft's §4.1 needs the same narrowing** before it cites C11.
