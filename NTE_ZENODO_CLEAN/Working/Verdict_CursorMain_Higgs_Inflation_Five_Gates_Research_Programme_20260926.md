## G-1 — Existence. Does it stop the recollapse?

### Recollapse bound (independent check of the banked G4 fact)

Closed FRW: \(\ddot a/a = -(4\pi G/3)(\rho+3p)\).  
At the no-boundary / real-tunnelling junction, \(\dot a = 0\) (Gibbons–Hartle: vanishing extrinsic curvature on the joining surface — see owed lit check below).

- If \(\rho+3p>0\) at that surface → \(\ddot a < 0\) → **maximum** → recollapse.  
- If \(\rho+3p<0\) → \(\ddot a > 0\) → **minimum** → expansion from rest.

Banked G4: hot gauge-birth bath (\(\rho+3p>0\)) at handover ⇒ Planckian recollapse. Cursor Main accepts that qualitative result (radiation at \(T\sim 0.4\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) yields Planck-scale \(a_{\max}\) and lifetime). Exact \(0.073\,\ell_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) numerics were Jammer/Scratch’s; this lane does not re-litigate the third digit.

**Therefore:** if the Lorentzian content at a′=0 is **Higgs-plateau vacuum energy**, the junction is a **bounce minimum**, not a recollapse maximum. **G-1 kinematics PASS** under that content assignment.

### The hinge (must not be papered over)

## G-2 — Unitarity (state of the dispute)

### Flat-space / low-field reading (Burgess–Lee–Trott; Barbón–Espinosa)

Non-minimal \(\xi_H\sim 10^4\) induces a tree-level unitarity cutoff  
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{vac}} \sim M_{\mathrm{Pl}}/\xi_H \sim 10^{14}\,\mathrm{GeV}\) (order of magnitude).  
Inflationary Hubble for the NTE-relevant band is \(H_{\mathrm{inf}}\sim (1.4\text{–}1.6)\times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{GeV}\) (G-4).  
**On this reading, \(H_{\mathrm{inf}}\) sits near or below \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{vac}}\)** — the EFT is under stress; some formulations put the dangerous operators at \(\sim M_{\mathrm{Pl}}/\xi_H\).

### Background-dependent cutoff (Bezrukov–Magnin–Shaposhnikov–Sibiryakov 2010; later field-redefinition analyses e.g. JHEP 06 (2022) 132)

During inflation the cutoff **rises with the Higgs background**, approaching \(\sim M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) (Einstein frame) while \(H_{\mathrm{inf}}\ll M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\). On this reading, **semiclassical inflation is inside the EFT**.
