## Verdict (one paragraph)

The textbook Hartle–Hawking measure problem is a property of the *raw gravitational-action* no-boundary saddle: the semiclassical no-boundary weight ∝ exp(+3M_Pl⁴/8V) is largest at the *smallest* inflationary V, favouring histories with the least inflation (the classic measure problem), and — per Feldbrugge–Lehners–Turok (2017) — the Lorentzian path-integral definition of that same saddle leaves perturbations unsuppressed and is contour-dependent, i.e. arguably ill-defined. **NTE does not carry that raw saddle.** Companion 9 §7 killed the raw-trace saddle outright (the 2b monotonicity theorem: Tr f is monotone in the scale, so it has no interior extremum → no nucleation saddle from the bare trace), and stated explicitly that *"the nucleation saddle, if any, belongs to the constrained system (μ(N) + Φ sector + matter)."* Companion 9 §1–2 further shows the NTE Euclidean problem is a **one-DOF** system: the constraint H = β|Φ|² collapses the two gravitational DOF to one before any saddle is counted. A measure is a weighting *over the saddles of a given action*; NTE has, at present, **removed the textbook saddle and not yet exhibited its replacement.** Therefore NTE neither inherits the textbook HH weighting (so the two standing criticisms do not attach in their textbook form) **nor** possesses a well-defined no-boundary measure of its own yet. The honest register is: **the measure question is OPEN and UNDEFINED, downstream of the same constrained-system saddle question that P1 and P2 already flagged as open.** Both textbook criticisms must nonetheless be NAMED on the open list, because any future constrained-system saddle will have to show it evades them rather than silently inheriting them.

## 1. What the textbook measure problem actually is (two distinct criticisms — do not conflate)

The literature carries **two separate** standing objections to the Hartle–Hawking no-boundary proposal. They are independent; NTE must clear both, or name both.

**(i) The classic measure problem (Euclidean, semiclassical).** The no-boundary wavefunction's semiclassical weight for a de Sitter–like history goes as
  P ∝ exp(+S_E^cl) = exp(+3 M_Pl⁴ / 8V),
which is *maximised as V → 0⁺*. Read as a probability measure, it favours the *least* amount of inflation — the opposite of what is needed for a large, flat universe. This is the original Hawking–Page / Hartle–Hawking–Hertog tension. HHH's own later programme (top-down / "classical-history" weighting, volume weighting, the 2008 and 2018 constructions) is an attempt to reweight around exactly this. It is *not* settled; it is a live construction choice.

**(ii) The Feldbrugge–Lehners–Turok criticism (Lorentzian, non-perturbative).** Defining the no-boundary amplitude as a *Lorentzian* path integral over metrics and evaluating it by Picard–Lefschetz theory, FLT (2017, arXiv:1708.05104) found that (a) the relevant saddle makes fluctuations *grow* rather than being suppressed (the perturbation weight has the *wrong sign*), and (b) the result is contour-dependent — different, equally-defensible contours give different answers — so the proposal is arguably **ill-defined** as a path integral. Diez–Feldbrugge–Lehners and the Halliwell–Hartle–Hertog reply (2018–2019) dispute the choice of contour and boundary conditions; the 2023 Physics Reports review records the dispute as **unresolved** — a genuine open disagreement in the field, not a closed error.

**Both are properties of the raw gravitational-action saddle.** That is the object NTE has removed.

## 2. Why NTE does not inherit either in textbook form (the physics)

Two structural facts from Companion 9, both already in the corpus:

- **The raw-trace saddle is dead (Companion 9 §7, 2b theorem).** Tr f is monotone in the scale factor / N, so there is no interior stationary point of the bare trace action → no nucleation saddle from the raw trace. The textbook exp(+3M_Pl⁴/8V) weight is precisely the semiclassical evaluation *at* such a saddle. No saddle ⇒ that weight is not defined in NTE. Companion 9 states the surviving saddle "belongs to the constrained system (μ(N) + Φ sector + matter)" — i.e. a *different* action from the one both criticisms target.
- **The problem is one-DOF (Companion 9 §1–2).** The NTE constraint H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² reduces the two gravitational degrees of freedom to one *before* the saddle count. The textbook measure problem, and the FLT contour ambiguity, both live in the *unconstrained* metric integration (the lapse contour in FLT is exactly the DOF the NTE constraint removes/reparametrises). A one-DOF constrained saddle is not guaranteed to reproduce either pathology — but is equally not guaranteed to evade them. It has to be *computed*.

**Net:** NTE has changed the action whose saddles define the measure. The textbook weight and the textbook criticisms are statements about the *old* action. They do not transfer automatically — that is the substance of Fork B. But NTE has not yet produced the *new* saddle, so it has no measure of its own — that is Fork C.

Reading: the no-boundary *measure* is not a settled input anyone can silently "inherit." A construction that wants to use a no-boundary saddle must specify its contour and show suppression; the debate over whether that can be done consistently is live. NTE, by killing the raw-trace saddle, has *side-stepped the current form* of the debate but inherits the obligation: whatever constrained-system saddle NTE eventually exhibits must be checked against **both** (i) the V→0 weighting tension and (ii) the FLT contour/suppression test.
