## The distinction the whole item turns on

Two different quantities have both been written "N_e ≈ 63":

- **N_HH ≡ −ln(β_NTE·M_Pl) ≈ 63.4.** The e-fold **span of the Hartle–Hawking de Sitter sub-phase** (equivalently an **energy-scale ratio**: H falls from ≈ M_Pl at the instanton to the gauge-birth scale). This is **correct and legitimate** — it is C8 §2's own definition. It is a property of where the sub-phase begins and ends, not of where observable modes cross.
- **N_\* (the inflationary pivot depth).** The number of e-folds **before the end of inflation at which the observable pivot mode** (k_\* = 0.05 Mpc⁻¹) **crossed the horizon** — the depth at which n_s and r are evaluated. It is fixed by **horizon-matching**, not by −ln(β M_Pl), and lands in a **band ≈ 55–60**, reheating-dependent.

**The error (item a):** four Companions (C8 §6, C9 §5/§6, C12 §2/§4, C17 §3), Part 1 (abstract + §2.4) and Part 3 (three sub-loci) evaluate (n_s, r) / n_s **"at N_e ≈ 63.4"** — feeding the sub-phase span / energy-scale ratio into the pivot slot. They are different physical quantities; 63.4 is not the pivot.

**The band, not a point (AC V1 — binding constraint).** Horizon-matching gives N_\* ≈ 60.2 for instantaneous reheating (AC, independent), ≈ 55–56 for a matter-dominated reheating epoch (Jammer ≤ 56; Scratch ≈ 55.5). All agree the direction: **N_\* is well below 63.4, and 63.4 is outside the plausible range on any reheating assumption.** But 55–56 and 60 are both defensible. **Replacing 63.4 with a single point (e.g. 55.5) would repeat the original error in a new place.** The headline must be **N_\* ≈ 55–60 with the reheating assumption stated**.

**The (n_s, r) band, read off C17's own α=2 map** (no new arithmetic asserted): C17 §3 gives (0.9692, 0.0053) at N_e=63.4 and (0.9646, 0.0070) at N_e=55. Restricting to the pivot band N_\* ∈ [55, 60] and interpolating C17's two endpoints to 60 gives **(n_s, r) ≈ (0.965–0.967, 0.0060–0.0070)** across the band. These should be **recomputed on the α=2 map at the chosen band at patch time**; I assert them only as the interpolation of C17's stated points, still fully conditional (Euclidean-section applicability; ξ spectral origin open). Neutral consequence, stated without spin: at the lower band n_s ≈ 0.965 sits essentially on Planck 2018 (0.9649 ± 0.0042) and moves ~2.7σ from P-ACT-LB (0.9743 ± 0.0034) — the two-anchor tension shifts toward Planck; this is a fact to record, not a win to claim.

**Anti-re-smuggling anchor (the thing most likely to go wrong later, per AC).** 63.4 is not withdrawn — it is **mis-slotted**. The one device that stops it sliding back into the pivot slot is to **give it its own label, N_HH, at its legitimate home (C8 §2)**, and to state once, there, that N_HH ≠ N_\*. Every other locus then points at N_\* for the pivot and at N_HH for the span, and neither can be silently swapped for the other.
