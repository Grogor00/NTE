## Abstract

NTE's dark sector is not two dark components but one boundary described two ways. **Dark matter** is the cross-thickness gravitational coupling of mirror-orientation stress-energy through the Z₂-even neutrino; its ratio κ = κ₀/(1−κ₀) ≈ 5.50 (Planck: 5.38 ± 0.15, 0.83σ) is fixed by S³ topology and, as shown separately, is independent of the boundary's thickness magnitude — a topological number, not an evolving coupling. **Dark energy** is the same boundary's geometric unloading as |Φ_NTE| thins, with equation of state w = −1 (the de Sitter asymptote). This companion establishes what the surface-only ontology delivers for the dark-energy *magnitude*. Three results: (i) the volume vacuum-energy catastrophe (the 10¹²⁰ problem) is **removed structurally** — a boundary functional has no bulk domain in which a volume vacuum energy can live; (ii) the surviving vacuum energy, computed from de Sitter horizon thermodynamics, is an **infrared, horizon-scale quantity ρ_Λ = 3M_Pl²H² with no reintroduced ultraviolet term**, because the surface degrees of freedom carry the Gibbons–Hawking temperature T = H/2π rather than a UV cutoff; (iii) the cosmological-constant problem is thereby **converted from a 120-order magnitude problem into a single-number problem** — the asymptotic boundary-field value |Φ_∞| — with the dark sector introducing **no new parameter** beyond the single input β_NTE that already fixes H₀. We show that the conformal-reset condition does *not* pin |Φ_∞| (it is a scale-invariant trigger), so the number itself remains underived; and we state precisely what is derived, what is inherited, and what is open. NTE does not predict ρ_Λ; it removes the ultraviolet half of the problem outright and shows the dark sector costs no additional parameters.

## 1. One boundary, two dark phenomena

The standard dark sector is two unrelated puzzles: a pressureless component that clusters (dark matter, ~27% of the energy budget) and a smooth component that accelerates expansion (dark energy, ~68%). NTE's surface-only ontology (Part 2 §XVI) reframes both as behaviours of a single object — the S³ boundary carrying the field |Φ_NTE| — seen through two different geometric features. This companion collects the two results under one thesis and resolves how far the surface picture reaches on the dark-energy *magnitude*, the corpus's standing open item (Part 2 §XII: "the numerical value of ρ_Λ is not derived").

The organising fact is the boundary length identity (Part 1 §VI): the boundary's proper-length thickness and the Hubble radius are the same object,
$$\\ell(N) = \\frac{1}{\\beta_{\\mathrm{NTE}}|\\Phi_{\\mathrm{NTE}}|^2} = \\frac{1}{H(N)}.$$
Everything below follows from taking this, and the surface-only ontology, literally.

**Result up front (the two knobs).** Dark matter is set by a **topological** number (κ, no free parameter). Dark energy's scale is set by the **single input** β_NTE that already fixes H₀. The dark sector adds no parameter that the framework did not already carry.

## 2. Dark matter: the κ ratio (summary; derivation in Part 2 §IX and Companion 7)

The Z₂-even (unresolved) neutrino is bound to the curvature of both CPN-conjugate orientations of the N-thickness; its stress-energy appears in both Einstein equations, so mirror-orientation matter is gravitationally coupled to ours across the thickness — invisible except gravitationally. This is the dark matter (Part 2 §IX).

The coupling strength is fixed by S³ topology, not fitted:
$$\\kappa_0 = 3 \\cdot Y_{00}(S^2) = \\frac{3}{\\sqrt{4\\pi}} \\approx 0.846, \\qquad \\kappa = \\frac{\\kappa_0}{1-\\kappa_0} \\approx 5.50,$$
with the factor 3 the dimension of the su(2) adjoint and Y₀₀ = 1/√(4π) the monopole s-wave harmonic on the S² the unresolved neutrino never projects onto (Companion 7 §4). Convergence (κ₀ < 1) requires exactly three su(2) modes, which forces the Majorana condition — a Dirac neutrino would give κ₀ = 6/√(4π) > 1. Planck's Ω_DM/Ω_b = 5.38 ± 0.15 sits 0.83σ from 5.50.

**Two features matter for the dark-sector thesis.** First, κ is **topological** — no free parameter enters. Second, κ is **independent of the thickness magnitude**: the coupling is a which-sheets statement (the neutrino couples to both orientations), not a how-far-apart statement, so κ does not evolve as the boundary thickness (equivalently cosmic time) grows. This is established in the N-thickness verification (Scratch/Jammer, 2026-09-27) and is what lets κ = 5.50 be compared, as a fixed number, against a fixed observation.

We do not reproduce the κ derivation here; it is carried in Part 2 §IX, Companion 7, and the κ pedagogical note.

## 4. The magnitude from surface geometry

### 4.1 The volume catastrophe is removed, not cancelled

The cosmological-constant problem is usually posed as a cancellation: a bare Λ must cancel a QFT vacuum energy ~M_Pl⁴ to 120 decimal places. In a boundary-only ontology the problem does not arise in that form. The post-temporal ontology has *only* the surface (Part 2 §XVI: "the 4-ball interior is not a place for observers on the S³ surface; the radial direction is not spatial — it is the e-fold accumulation N"). The gravitational action is a functional on the boundary; **there is no bulk four-volume over which a term ∫√g Λ can be integrated.** The M_Pl⁴ volume vacuum energy is therefore not cancelled — it has no domain. This is the precise content of the "category error" reframing already asserted in §XII; §4.2 supplies the computation that was missing.

### 4.2 The surviving vacuum energy is an infrared horizon quantity — Gibbons–Hawking–York / horizon thermodynamics

What vacuum energy does remain is the energy of the de Sitter horizon itself. Take the boundary at the de Sitter stage, horizon radius L = 1/H (reduced Planck units, 8πG = M_Pl⁻²):
$$A = \\frac{4\\pi}{H^2}, \\qquad S = \\frac{A}{4G} = \\frac{8\\pi^2 M_{\\mathrm{Pl}}^2}{H^2}, \\qquad T = \\frac{1}{2\\pi L} = \\frac{H}{2\\pi}.$$
The horizon carries N ~ A/G ~ M_Pl²/H² surface degrees of freedom, each at the Gibbons–Hawking temperature T = H/2π. Their energy density is
$$\\rho \\sim \\frac{(A/G)\\,T}{V_H} \\sim M_{\\mathrm{Pl}}^2 H^2,$$
and a crude equipartition (E = ½NT, V_H = 4π/3H³) gives ρ = 6M_Pl²H² — the right scale with an O(1) coefficient. The rigorous horizon-thermodynamic derivation (Jacobson 1995: dQ = TdS ⇒ the Einstein equations; Padmanabhan's holographic equipartition) reproduces the de Sitter Friedmann relation exactly:
$$\\boxed{\\rho_\\Lambda = 3\\,M_{\\mathrm{Pl}}^2 H_{\\mathrm{dS}}^2}, \\qquad \\text{coefficient fixed} = 3, \\text{ given } S = A/4G.$$

**The decisive point is the scale, not the coefficient.** The vacuum energy is set by the **infrared** horizon temperature T = H/2π, never by a ultraviolet cutoff. So it comes out ~M_Pl²H² — **not** M_Pl⁴ and **not** M_Pl²Λ_UV². This is the Cohen–Kaplan–Nelson (1999) infrared–ultraviolet relation realised: the horizon length L = 1/H caps the vacuum energy at M_Pl²/L². The entire 120-order gap between M_Pl⁴ and the observed density is the single replacement M_Pl⁴ → M_Pl²H²: numerically (H₀/M_Pl)² = 3.5×10⁻¹²¹ against the observed ρ_Λ/M_Pl⁴ = 7.2×10⁻¹²¹, the residual ratio being 3Ω_Λ (the Friedmann coefficient times the not-yet-fully-de-Sitter factor).

**The one imported ingredient** is the entropy normalisation, the 1/4 in S = A/4G (standard Bekenstein–Hawking). NTE does not yet derive the 1/4 from its own bilateral boundary action (Part 2 §XVI flags this). Given the 1/4, the coefficient is fixed; deriving the 1/4 is a stated residual.

### 4.3 The reset does not pin the scale — it is scale-invariant

With the coefficient fixed and the ultraviolet term removed, the number reduces to a single quantity: H_dS = β_NTE|Φ_∞|², i.e. the asymptotic boundary field |Φ_∞|. The natural candidate to pin it is NTE's conformal reset (Part 1 §V), which triggers at one photon per Hubble volume, n_γ V_H = 1. But with n_γ ∝ T³ and V_H = 1/H³, this condition fixes only the *ratio*
$$\\frac{T_{\\mathrm{reset}}}{H_{\\mathrm{dS}}} = \\left(\\frac{\\pi^2}{2\\zeta(3)}\\right)^{1/3} \\approx 1.60,$$
not the absolute scale: the reset fires at one photon per horizon for *any* H_dS. **The reset is a scale-invariant trigger and does not determine H_dS, |Φ_∞|, or ρ_Λ.** (It determines *when* the universe resets — at its de Sitter thermal state — not the *value* of the de Sitter scale.)

### 4.4 What is reached: no new dark-sector parameter

The only sub-Planckian scale in the framework is β_NTE, an input (Part 1 §2.4), which already fixes H₀ = β_NTE v_EW². Since ρ_Λ ~ M_Pl²H_dS² ~ M_Pl²(β_NTE v_EW²)² up to an O(1) factor, **the dark-energy scale is β_NTE — the same input that fixes H₀ — and no new parameter is introduced.** With κ topological (§2), the entire dark sector is parameter-free beyond β_NTE. The residual O(1) factor (equivalently Ω_Λ ≈ 0.7 today, the "why now" coincidence) is genuinely open and is not improved over ΛCDM.

### 4.5 Not holographic-fluid dark energy (the Hsu objection)

A holographic dark-energy *fluid* with infrared cutoff L = 1/H has w = 0, not w = −1 (Hsu 2004). This does not afflict NTE, because NTE's dark energy is not a posited ρ ∝ 1/L² fluid; it is the de Sitter fixed point of the actual expansion, at which H = H_dS is constant and w = −1 is trivial. The Hsu concern applies only to the *approach* to the asymptote (the matter era), whose effective w is set by the |Φ|(N) profile — a dynamical calculation flagged here as not done.
