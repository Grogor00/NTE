## §1. The phrase

> *Everything, everywhere, all at once.*

Greg's recognition: the phrase carries a pattern + voice he likes that the framework might use. Candidate associations:

- **Strange Geometries** (Ch 14) — mathematical structural rhymes appearing across multiple regimes simultaneously
- **The pre-temporal epoch** — where the framework's octonionic algebra operates before the gauge-birth cascade differentiates time and space
- **Other** — wherever the structural picture's all-at-once-ness wants linguistic embodiment

The phrase's modern-cultural reference (the 2022 Daniels film of the same name) is multiverse-themed but the phrase itself is older as a pattern. Integration should land naturally without explicit film attribution; the phrase as pattern carries the meaning without needing the citation.

## §2. Greg's observation about the self-referential tension

Greg's exact note: *"obviously, 'where' and 'once' are more post temporal ['once' explicitly]."*

This is a load-bearing observation. The phrase uses temporal language ("once") and spatial language ("where") to describe a state that has neither. The pre-temporal regime, in the framework's reading, has no temporal ordering and no spatial differentiation in the post-temporal sense. To describe it, language has to borrow from the post-temporal categories that don't yet exist in the regime being described.

This is not a flaw in the phrase. It is a feature. The phrase performs, at the linguistic level, what the framework asks the reader to do conceptually: use post-temporal categories to gesture at a pre-temporal state that those categories don't strictly apply to. The reader is invited to feel the strain of "once" and "where" being deployed for a regime that does not host them, which is precisely the strain of trying to imagine the pre-temporal epoch from inside the post-temporal one.

If the phrase is integrated into a chapter that engages the pre-temporal regime, this self-referential tension should be acknowledged — not at length, but as a flicker of the kind that does engage/explain work in one move.

## §3. Candidate placements

### §3.1 Strange Geometries (Ch 14 v1) — Greg's explicit suggestion

The chapter develops mathematical structural rhymes across Connes-Rovelli, twistor, and Palatial structures. The phrase could land in a section that points at the all-at-once-ness of these rhymes — the same Hopf-fibration shape appearing in quantum mechanics and cosmology simultaneously; the same su(2) showing up in the spin algebra, the unresolved neutrino's eigenmodes, and the κ derivation; the Connes-Rovelli structural rhyme operating across non-commutative geometry and the framework's spectral triple.

Suggested integration register: a single sentence or compact paragraph in §3 or §4 of Ch 14 v1, threaded with the "structural rhymes converge" theme. Not as the chapter's central image — Strange Geometries has its own structural arc — but as a phrase that catches the multiplicity of the rhymes.

Possible cadence: *"It is, in a strange sense, the same structural object showing up everything, everywhere, all at once — the Hopf fibration in quantum mechanics, the same fibration in the cosmological boundary, the same su(2) algebra in the spin structure and in the dark-matter ratio."*

This integration is the most natural per Greg's suggestion. Awaits Ch 14 v1 audit response cycle — when PC next has natural reason to revise Ch 14, the phrase has a slot.

### §3.2 Pre-temporal aside (Ch 7 v3.2 candidate)

The gauge-birth cascade chapter (Ch 7) is where the pre-temporal regime is most explicitly engaged. The phrase could land in the chapter's pre-temporal section — perhaps as the way the chapter gestures at what the pre-temporal regime is like before the cascade differentiates space and time.

Suggested integration register: a paragraph that uses the phrase to describe the pre-temporal state, with the self-referential tension acknowledged in a phrase or two. The post-temporal reader needs the post-temporal language to imagine a regime that doesn't host it.

Possible cadence: *"Before the gauge birth, the universe was — to borrow phrasing that does not quite work and is closer to true for the failure — everything, everywhere, all at once. The 'where' and the 'once' are post-temporal categories that the pre-temporal regime does not yet host; the reader has no choice but to gesture at the regime using language native to its successor. The phrase is closest to true precisely where it strains."*

This integration is the most evocative but the most register-disruptive. The pre-temporal regime is one of the chapter's careful exposition challenges; introducing a phrase that performs its own paradox could either lift the exposition (engage + explain in one move) or distract from the chapter's existing rhythm. PC discretion required, with calibration against Ch 7 v3.2's current register before drafting.

### §3.3 Ch 4 (time emerges from expansion) — alternative pre-temporal slot

Ch 4 is the chapter where the framework's time-emergence framing crystallizes. If Ch 7 already carries the pre-temporal heavy lifting, Ch 4 could host the phrase in a lighter touch — as a parenthetical that catches the pre-temporal regime's strangeness before time emerges.

Possible cadence: *"Cosmic expansion is the fundamental process; time is its derivative. Before time emerged — in the pre-temporal regime that hosts the framework's octonionic algebra — everything was, in a useful if strained sense, everywhere all at once."*

Lighter touch than §3.2 but possibly more natural for Ch 4's register. PC has not read Ch 4's current version this session; integration depends on register fit.

## §4. PC's pedagogical-discipline call

Per PC stream notes §4.3 (audit-queue speed-running risk) and §§2-3 (pedagogical pivot active at every sentence): the phrase should integrate when the natural drafting opportunity arises, not when forced into an existing chapter that does not have a structural slot for it.

**The integration register PC will hold:**

- Phrase should do at least two of engage/explain/connect/simplify (the engage floor). A phrase that only decorates would violate §2.3.
- Self-referential tension acknowledged briefly when the phrase is deployed; not at length, but enough that the reader feels the strain instead of glossing past it.
- Earned-term discipline: phrase doesn't require new technical vocabulary, but the surrounding context should make clear what regime the "everything/everywhere/all-at-once" applies to. The pre-temporal regime is the framework's, not the multiverse-film's.
- Schema-attachment trade: the phrase is loose at the corpus-rigorous level (the pre-temporal regime is not literally "everywhere" because there is no "where" yet) but true at the intuition level (the regime has no spatial differentiation, so "everywhere" in the post-temporal sense is closer to true for the regime's structure than "somewhere" would be).

**The natural drafting opportunity:** the next time PC has reason to revise one of the candidate chapters (Ch 14 v1 audit response, Ch 7 audit response cycle, possible Ch 4 revision), the phrase becomes a candidate sentence-level edit during the pedagogical-pivot pass on that section. PC should not produce a chapter revision *primarily* to integrate this phrase; the revision should have an independent reason, and the phrase should land as one of the available improvements.
