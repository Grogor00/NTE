**Discipline applied:**

1. **Engage floor** — concrete lay anchor at every chapter opening, not abstract opening
2. **Fleeting-glimpse** — visualisable schema lands cleanly within first 2-3 paragraphs
3. **Schema-attachment** — H2 / chapter title carries the schema forward; reader has something to hold
4. **Earned-term defer** — technical terms built up from the picture, not dropped as jargon
5. **Honest scope** — limitations named explicitly; no overclaiming
6. **Density (Greg flag)** — no redundant restatements; every sentence must add new information
7. **Forward-momentum (Greg flag)** — paragraphs build forward, do not circle back and rephrase themselves
8. **Engagement (Greg flag)** — no filler sentences that read like rephrasing for emphasis when emphasis isn't earned

Verdicts use three bands:
- **PASS** — discipline clean; no patches required
- **PASS WITH PATCHES** — substantively sound; specific tightenings recommended
- **NEEDS REVISION** — discipline drift requires substantive rewrite of named sections

## Prelude — *The Forty-Million-Year Instant* v5

File: `Book/Prelude_The_Forty_Million_Year_Instant_v5.md`

**Verdict: PASS WITH PATCHES.**

The photon's null-time framing is strong — concrete lay anchor (your retina, tonight, looking up), Einstein hook earned, schema (the bridge) lands cleanly, destination statement with H(N) equation honestly framed ("If the equation reads as opaque, that is fine — Chapter 4 develops it").

**However, two distinct density issues + one engagement softness:**

### Issue P1 — section 2 restatement chain (density / forward-momentum)

Section 2 opens with the bridge metaphor:

> If a photon experiences no time on its journey, then the photon's path is a kind of bridge.

This is a strong opener. The next three sentences then restate it without adding:

> From one frame, it crosses cosmic distances. From its own frame, it has no length. Two events — emission and absorption — are connected by the photon, but the photon itself does not register them as separate. The bridge is real; the traversal is not.

Sentences 2-4 each rephrase the same observation ("the photon connects without traversing"). The fourth ("the bridge is real; the traversal is not") is the strongest of the four and could replace all three preceding it.

**Recommended patch P1:** collapse to two sentences total:

> If a photon experiences no time on its journey, then the photon's path is a kind of bridge — connecting emission and absorption without registering them as separate events. The bridge is real; the traversal is not.

Net saving: ~30 words. Forward momentum recovered into the "this bridge is the seed observation" paragraph that follows.

### Issue P2 — "Several pieces of the framework now carry specific numbers" insertion (forward-momentum / placement)

The short paragraph sandwiched between section 2 and the destination statement reads as if patched in:

> Several pieces of the framework now carry specific numbers — the dark-matter-to-baryon ratio derived from a single geometric action, the spectral index n_s from two converging structural routes, the cosmological constant Λ at roughly 1.05× the observed value on the same e-fold depth as n_s. Chapters 12 and 13 say what landed and what remains. The chord, in plainer language, is no longer promise alone; several of its notes now carry numbers.

Three problems:
- The reader doesn't yet know what "the chord" is (Ch 11/12 territory)
- The technical references (κ, n_s, Λ) drop without earning their place
- "no longer promise alone" carries self-promotional undertone uncharacteristic of the Prelude's voice elsewhere

**Recommended patch P2:** delete this paragraph entirely. The relevant content lives in Ch 12-13 where it's earned. The Prelude should hand the reader to the destination statement without a numerical preview that hasn't been pedagogically built.

Alternative if deletion isn't acceptable: move it to AFTER the destination statement (so the reader has at least seen |Φ_NTE| and H(N)) and rewrite to remove "the chord" reference:

> Several pieces of the framework now carry specific numbers — the dark-matter-to-baryon ratio at 1.03× the observed value, the primordial spectral index n_s at 0.9685, the cosmological constant Λ within 5% of measurement. Chapters 12 and 13 say what landed and what remains.

(Removes the "the chord… promise alone" line and front-loads observable comparisons rather than internal-framework vocabulary.)

PC preference: delete entirely (cleaner Prelude flow).

### Issue P3 — closing softness (engagement)

The last two paragraphs:

> What survives scrutiny is what still holds when enthusiasm fades — and when the preferred picture is set aside. The photon's null-time has been there for a hundred and twenty years. It hasn't gone away. The framework's bet is that it has been waiting all along, with a story to tell, for someone to listen.

The "what survives scrutiny" line is strong and earned. "The framework's bet is that it has been waiting all along, with a story to tell, for someone to listen" verges on motivational/preachy register — not the discipline of the rest of the Prelude.

**Recommended patch P3:** end the Prelude one sentence earlier, after "It hasn't gone away." The reader doesn't need the bet framed in personified terms; the previous 1,500 words have established the framework's stance.

Alternative if the close needs forward-momentum, replace the last sentence with something concrete:

> What survives scrutiny is what still holds when enthusiasm fades — and when the preferred picture is set aside. The photon's null-time has been there for a hundred and twenty years. The chapters that follow are what taking it seriously, for that long, looks like.

Bridges to "the chapters that follow" without anthropomorphising the framework.

### Net impact

Applying P1+P2+P3 trims roughly 130-150 words from the Prelude (~2.5% reduction) and removes three density/engagement drift points. Prelude reads tighter, no content lost.
