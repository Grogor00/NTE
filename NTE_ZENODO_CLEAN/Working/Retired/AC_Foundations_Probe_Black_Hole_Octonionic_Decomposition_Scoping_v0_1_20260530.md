## §1 — Thesis (current sharpest form)

A black hole is a region of **local temporal de-locking**: the emergent-time structure that holds in the bulk reverts, locally, toward the pre-temporal (octonionic) phase the universe locked *out of* at N_lock. Consequences as currently framed:

1. **No interior.** There is no interior volume of degrees of freedom — not because it is hidden, but because an interior is a time-ordered region and the time-ordering is absent. This is a **strong-holography ansatz**: all structure is on the surface.
2. **S³ projecting to an observed S².** The fundamental object is the octonionic/internal **S³**; what we observe embedded in 4D spacetime is its **S² projection** — the horizon. The natural map is the **Hopf projection S³ → S²**, which collapses the S¹ fibre. The fibre-collapse is the proposed route to the area law: the S¹ is "internal," unobserved, and the entropy count lands on the S² base (2D), not the S³ (3D).
3. **Infaller (Greg).** With no interior, "what does the infaller experience inside" has no referent; at the surface, time-ordering halts, so nothing further "happens"; the infaller's degrees of freedom are stored on the surface and re-emitted as the hole evaporates ("happening" resumes on release).

The thesis is **kinematic** (what a black hole *is*). It contains no dynamics (how it forms or evaporates).

## §3 — Recognition gates (make-or-break, priority order)

**None of these is cleared. The thesis is not viable until at least GATE 1 and GATE 2 are.**

### GATE 1 — Area-law coefficient (the make-or-break)
- *Coarse part (plausibly free):* "no interior ⇒ dof on the surface ⇒ entropy ∝ area, not volume." The Hopf-fibre-collapse mechanism (§1.2) gives a candidate reason the count is 2D (S² base) not 3D (S³).
- *Dimension trap:* the count **must** land on the S² projection. If octonionic dof are counted on the S³, the result is a 3-volume law and the thesis is **falsified on contact**.
- *Fine part (hard, unsolved):* reproduce **S = A/4G exactly** — the coefficient 1/4. This is a genuine microstate count (cf. Strominger–Vafa for strings; the Immirzi parameter fixes it by hand in LQG). NTE must count the octonionic→S² degrees of freedom and obtain A/4G. **This is the first derivation a v0.1 attempt should target.** Qualitative "area-law-shaped" ≠ derived.

### GATE 2 — Equivalence principle / firewall-avoidance (the AMPS crux, inherited)
The infaller resolution (§1.3) must thread a needle:
- A large BH horizon is locally unremarkable (tidal ~1/M²); GR says the infaller feels nothing locally at crossing.
- To "halt and store" the infaller requires either (a) something local at the surface — which is **a firewall**, contradicting the no-firewall intent — or (b) nothing local — which leaves the infaller's smooth-crossing description intact, i.e. an **interior exists**, contradicting §1.1.
- The intended escape is a **third option**: the infaller's continuation past the surface has **no referent** (malformed, like "before N_lock") — neither wall nor interior. This is genuinely distinct from firewall and from complementarity. **The gate:** make "no referent" *operational* — state precisely why the proper-time geodesic continuation GR provides is not the answer, while preserving local no-drama as the infaller approaches the surface, and without smuggling an interior back in. Until this is precise, "you stay at the surface" reads as external/complementarity description and carries the equivalence-principle violation it was meant to avoid.

### GATE 3 — Dynamical sector (Hawking + Page) — wholly open
- The thesis is kinematic; "stored until evaporation re-emits you" (§1.3) is **parasitic on an evaporation dynamics that does not exist** in NTE. Greg has explicitly offered nothing here (honest concession on record).
- Benchmarks any unitarity-respecting framework must eventually hit: the **Hawking spectrum** (thermal at T = ℏκ/2π) and the **Page curve** (radiation entanglement entropy rising then falling, peaking at the Page time; reproduced by the islands/replica-wormhole programme from semiclassical gravity, 2019–20).
- The storage-and-release picture is qualitatively Page-shaped — encouraging, not derived.

### GATE 4 — Internal NTE consistency
- Temporal locking is, in the corpus, a **cosmological** phenomenon (N < N_lock, the bifurcation). The thesis requires it to be **local** (horizons as de-locking loci). NTE must supply a principled reason horizons are loci of local de-locking, not assert it by analogy. Without this, the central mechanism is borrowed, not derived.
