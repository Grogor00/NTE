## §1 — Purpose

NTE's σ_t / KMS framework gives a structural account of *how t emerges* from operator-algebraic structure (modular flow of a KMS state on the spectral-triple algebra). The quantum time-of-arrival literature provides the formal QM machinery for treating *t as an observable* — the Aharonov-Bohm TOA operator (1961) and the Kijowski (1974) POVM. The two have been developed in independent traditions but address structurally adjacent questions. This note articulates how they correspond in NTE's framework, and what (if anything) the correspondence buys us.

## §2 — The two pieces

### §2.1 NTE's σ_t / KMS framework (Charter v1.3 §2.5)

In NTE, the emergent post-gauge-birth state ω_{Φ_NTE} on the spectral-triple algebra A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 is a KMS state at inverse temperature β_GB. By Tomita-Takesaki, this state has a canonical modular automorphism group σ_t acting on the algebra. The Connes-Rovelli thermal-time hypothesis identifies *physical time evolution* with this modular flow:

**Time, in NTE, is what σ_t does to the algebra at state ω_{Φ_NTE}.**

The KMS condition (state is σ_t-invariant; correlation functions periodic in imaginary t at β_GB) is the algebraic characterisation of thermal equilibrium. The σ_t framework is *more general* than Wick-rotation framings: σ_t exists for any state on any von Neumann algebra (per Tomita-Takesaki); the KMS condition picks out one state as "thermal equilibrium" and one σ_t as "physical time evolution."

Pre-temporal regime: state on A_F is generic (not KMS); σ_t exists but doesn't align with any Lorentzian-time evolution (because there is no Lorentzian time pre-temporally). Gauge birth: C¹ kink in dz/dy IS the geometric signature of the state-type transition from generic → KMS at β_GB.

### §2.2 The Aharonov-Bohm quantum time-of-arrival POVM

Standard QM treats t as a c-number parameter, not an observable. There is no self-adjoint "time operator" canonically conjugate to a bounded-below Hamiltonian (Pauli's theorem, 1933). Aharonov & Bohm (1961) addressed this by *symmetric quantisation* of the classical expression t = m·x/p, producing the operator:

$$\hat{T}_{AB} = -\frac{m}{2}\left(\hat{p}^{-1}\hat{x} + \hat{x}\hat{p}^{-1}\right)$$

This operator is *maximally symmetric* but **not self-adjoint** — its deficiency indices are non-equal. It admits no projection-valued spectral measure. Kijowski (1974) showed axiomatically that a quantum TOA *probability distribution* for free particles can be derived from natural conditions (positivity, normalisation, symmetry, asymptotic limit recovering classical TOA). The Kijowski distribution is generated by a POVM — a positive operator-valued measure — rather than a projection-valued measure.

Subsequent work has explored:
- The connection to the *tunnelling time* problem (Galapon group; Magadan-Galapon 2025) — TOA-approach explanations of "zero tunnelling time" observed in attosecond experiments
- Multi-particle interference in arrival time (Ayatollah Rafsanjani et al. 2024 atoms; Ono et al. 2024 and Yu et al. 2025 photons)
- Bohmian variants (Tumulka; Tilloy)
- Recent rigorous mathematical-probability reformulation (*Phil. Mag.* accepted Jan 2026)

For ~50 years the AB TOA / Kijowski POVM has been the accepted answer to "what is the quantum operator for time-of-arrival?" — accepted as a POVM, not as a self-adjoint observable.

## §3 — The correspondence

### §3.1 Structural identification

In NTE's post-temporal regime, the operational time parameter is σ_t (the modular flow of the KMS state ω_{Φ_NTE}). The Aharonov-Bohm TOA operator $\hat{T}_{AB}$ is a candidate "t-as-observable" object acting on the system's Hilbert space. The correspondence is:

> **NTE's emergent t at the operational level = σ_t (modular flow).
> The Aharonov-Bohm TOA POVM = the algebra's view of "when does this transition happen in the σ_t coordinate?"**

The TOA POVM is not in competition with the modular flow; it is the formal expression of *t-as-observable* within the algebraic framework whose canonical t-flow is σ_t. Operationally, when a detector clicks at "time t" in the lab, the t-value is sampled from the Kijowski distribution generated by the AB POVM, with σ_t providing the underlying coordinate.

### §3.2 Why the TOA non-self-adjointness reads naturally under NTE

The historical "defect" of the AB TOA operator — that it is symmetric but not self-adjoint, generating only a POVM rather than a projection-valued measure — has been read in conventional QM as either a technical wart or as evidence that "time isn't quite an observable." Under NTE this reading sharpens:

**Time as an observable is *structurally derived* — derived from the modular flow of a KMS state, not from a fundamental self-adjoint operator on the Hilbert space.** That t-as-observable comes out as a POVM rather than a PVM is *exactly* what one expects when t isn't fundamental. A POVM is the right algebraic object for a quantity that is operationally meaningful (probability distributions can be computed, expectation values exist) but not built into the algebra as a primitive.

Pauli's theorem (1933) — no self-adjoint t-operator canonically conjugate to a bounded-below Hamiltonian — becomes, under NTE, *evidence* that t is emergent rather than fundamental. The thing Pauli ruled out is what an "elementary t-operator" would have to look like if t were on the same footing as position and momentum. NTE's σ_t / KMS framework explains why no such operator exists: t isn't on the same footing as x and p; it's derived from algebraic structure those operators live in.

### §3.3 What this buys NTE

Three things:

1. **Operational definiteness for "the time at which X happens" under emergent-t**. NTE no longer needs to handwave about "time-of-event" questions in the post-gauge-birth regime. The AB TOA / Kijowski POVM is the formal machinery, inherited from a mature 50-year literature.

2. **Structural reading of historical "defects"**. The TOA operator's non-self-adjointness and Pauli's theorem become *expected features* of emergent-t, not pathologies to explain away.

3. **A potential bridge to tunnelling-time interpretations**. The TOA literature has substantial spadework on attosecond-tunnelling experiments (Galapon group; Magadan-Galapon 2025). NTE's emergent-t framework gives a structural account compatible with "tunnelling happens at σ_t-zero" within the TOA-operator reading. *Speculative; flagged.*

## §4 — Honest scope

This articulation establishes a **structural correspondence**, not a unique NTE prediction.

What this note *does* establish:

- The AB TOA POVM is naturally readable as the algebraic expression of t-as-observable within NTE's σ_t / KMS framework
- Pauli's theorem and the AB TOA non-self-adjointness are expected, not pathological, under NTE
- The TOA literature provides formal QM machinery that NTE can absorb without modification

What this note *does not* establish:

- A unique NTE prediction distinguishable from textbook QM with emergent-t
- A new derivation of the Kijowski distribution from NTE first principles
- A novel resolution of the tunnelling-time controversy (the cross-link is speculative)
- A modification of the TOA literature's mathematical content

The correspondence is a structural fit, not an empirical advance. It strengthens NTE's positioning as having "natural homes" for established QM machinery rather than requiring add-ons.

## §5 — Open questions worth flagging

Three structural questions worth recording for future Connes-Programme work:

1. **Generalised TOA on the spectral-triple algebra.** AB TOA / Kijowski POVM is developed for ordinary QM (Schrödinger Hilbert space, free particle). Does it generalise to operators on the spectral-triple algebra A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆? In particular, what is the "time-of-arrival" of a fermionic mode in the octonion-graded structure? Not investigated.

2. **σ_t in the pre-temporal regime and TOA non-applicability.** Pre-temporally, the state on A_F is generic (not KMS); σ_t exists but doesn't align with Lorentzian t. There is no operational "time-of-arrival" pre-temporally because there's no t. Does the AB TOA framework simply not apply pre-temporally, or is there a generalised pre-temporal analogue? Likely the former (no t → no TOA) but worth recording.

3. **TOA-cross-link to entanglement-as-foliation (Companion 1).** Both σ_t and entanglement-as-foliation are operator-algebraic concepts. Companion 1's entanglement-as-foliation story may have a natural TOA-corollary: "when does decoherence happen" might be a TOA-type observable on the entanglement foliation. Speculative cross-link; flagged.
