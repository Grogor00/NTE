## §2. The mechanism — chirality superposition in N + vantage-point-biased perception through t

This is the structural core of Track 2. Greg's proposal expanded into a mechanism that ties to NTE's existing structures.

### §2.1 The fundamental N-structure: chirality superposition for the neutrino

For a charged fermion with strong Higgs coupling y >> 0 (electron, muon, tau, quarks, W/Z):
- The Higgs VEV breaks U(1) phase symmetry of the Hopf S¹ fibre
- Strong Yukawa coupling drives completion of the projection onto a definite chirality eigenstate
- The chirality eigenstate (LH or RH) commits the fermion to one sector (+N for LH, −N for RH) via the OctonionExtension §3.6 mechanism (CPN parity)
- Outcome: charged fermions are LH-in-+N (gauge-coupled via SU(2)_L) or RH-in-−N (gauge-coupled via SU(2)_R in the mirror sector)

For the neutrino with minimal Higgs coupling y_ν ~ 10⁻¹²:
- The Yukawa coupling is too small to drive completion of the Hopf projection
- The U(1) phase of the Hopf S¹ fibre does not lock to a definite chirality
- The neutrino remains in a chirality superposition: |ν⟩ = α|ν_L⟩ + β|ν_R⟩ in N, with both α and β nonzero
- Because chirality is not committed, sector commitment (per §3.6) is also not completed
- The neutrino configuration is smeared across both +N and −N orientations (the bilateral temporal extent of corpus §3.3)

This is the fundamental N-content of the neutrino under Track 2: a chirality superposition that has not undergone the Hopf-projection-driven commitment that produces definite chirality and definite sector commitment.

### §2.2 The t-emergent perception: vantage-point bias

t emerges from N via temporal locking (the gauge birth, per OctonionExtension §3.4 + corpus §8.7.4). Post-locking, each sector (+N and −N) has its own t-direction (along its respective e-fold accumulation). Within +N, t is the +N-emergent time; within −N, t is the −N-emergent time.

Observation of the neutrino happens through t-emergent processes (gauge interactions, detection apparatus). From the +N vantage point:
- The +N sector has SU(2)_L as its active gauge symmetry (per §3.6)
- SU(2)_L couples to LH content
- The neutrino's superposition is projected by the gauge interaction: only the LH-amplitude component is gauge-coupled in +N
- Observation in +N perceives the neutrino's LH content as the gauge-coupled object; the RH content is gauge-inactive in +N and is not detected as a "weakly interacting" entity
- Net: +N vantage perceives the neutrino as predominantly LH (because LH is what gauge-couples and is therefore detectable through gauge interaction)

From the −N vantage point (the mirror sector):
- The −N sector has SU(2)_R as its active gauge symmetry (per §3.6 + §9.4)
- SU(2)_R couples to RH content
- The same neutrino's superposition is projected by the −N gauge interaction: only the RH-amplitude component is gauge-coupled in −N
- −N vantage perceives the neutrino as predominantly RH

**The vantage-point bias is the gauge-projection in each sector.** The underlying chirality superposition is the same; the projection-via-vantage-active-gauge-symmetry is what produces the apparent chirality assignment in each vantage.

### §2.3 The "very slight tendency" — Reading B with SM-projection reconciliation

Greg's wording: *"the neutrino is not fully right-handed or fully left-handed — it's something in the middle. A tendency for it to be left or right rather than a hard definition."*

The literal reading: the underlying chirality superposition has comparable LH and RH amplitudes (the neutrino is "in the middle"). The "tendency" is the gauge-projection asymmetry from each vantage point.

In Reading B (the literal reading recommended here):

- The asymmetry observed in SM weak-interaction phenomenology in +N (LH neutrino interacts; RH neutrino is sterile) is a PROJECTION effect: SU(2)_L gauges only the LH component, and only the LH component is therefore weakly interacting from the +N vantage

Reading B preserves Greg's "in the middle" language and is fully consistent with SM weak-interaction phenomenology — the SM weak interaction is a projector, not a chirality assignment.

Reading A (AC's hypothesis) treated the vantage-bias as additive on top of a dominant chirality determined elsewhere. This reading is structurally workable but introduces an unnecessary step — it requires positing an "underlying dominant chirality" plus a "small vantage-bias addition," whereas Reading B says the chirality is genuinely a superposition and the asymmetry is the gauge-projection in each vantage.

**Reading B is the cleaner reading and matches Greg's literal wording.** Reading A is recoverable as a limit of Reading B (if |α|² and |β|² are very unequal, the projection-asymmetry resembles "dominant chirality"). Under Reading B, that limit is not the generic case for neutrinos — generic is "in the middle" per Greg.

Under Track 2 + Reading B:
