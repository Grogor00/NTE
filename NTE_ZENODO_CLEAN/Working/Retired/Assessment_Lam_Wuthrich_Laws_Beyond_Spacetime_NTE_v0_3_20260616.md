## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal, *freely recombinable* base. A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory is **"nomically charged"** — the theory constrains recombination, so it isn't modally innocent. Reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — a temporal notion. Without fundamental time, "production" becomes elusive; the fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** that may not survive QG.

### §2.5 Nearest cosmological competitor — USMEG-EFT (full paper now read)
Chishtie, *Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality* (arXiv:2502.18524v5, **physics.gen-ph**; full text now in NTE root). The closest framework in NTE's lane, worth knowing in detail.

**Shared territory.** Pre-temporal state = a 3D spatial manifold Σ with an *energy functional* E[φ] = ∫d³x[½(∇φ)²+V] — no time, no action, static configurations; pre-temporal quantum state a timeless Gaussian wavefunctional; time *emergent*; Big Bang reframed as a phase transition avoiding the singularity (finite invariants R/M_P⁴ ~ 4.9×10⁻⁴⁴, K/M_P⁸ ~ 4.0×10⁻⁸⁸); "parameter-free" rhetoric; explicit Wheeler–DeWitt / problem-of-time and entanglement (Van Raamsdonk) hooks; H₀-tension engagement.

**Corrections to v0.2 (two numbers there came from the stale v4 abstract):**
- Non-Gaussianity is **f_NL ∈ [0.8, 2.5]** (local), **not** ~20–50. Small (~1–2); CMB-S4 (σ~1) testable.
- **No observable Lorentz violation.** §7.7 washes the naive ξ~10⁻² out to ξ_eff < 10⁻⁴⁵ via ~100 e-folds of isotropisation (GRB bound |ξ|<10⁻²⁰ easily met). v0.2's "ξ~10⁻³ discriminator" is withdrawn.

**Mechanism — the real difference.** USMEG-EFT generates time by **one-loop quantum corrections producing a (∂φ/∂τ)² kinetic term** absent at tree level: time "switches on" when the loop-generated coefficient A ∝ [V'''(φ)]² exceeds A_crit = 1/M_P² (hence their added quartic, to supply V'''≠0); the lapse is identified as N²=A and FLRW emerges. NTE generates t from a **geometric boundary constraint** H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² + octonionic/Hopf structure. Entirely different machinery.

**Where they do / don't separate observationally:**
- n_s, r do **not** cleanly separate them — both Starobinsky-family: USMEG n_s=0.964, r=0.004 (N_e=55) vs NTE n_s=0.9685, r=0.0030 (N_e=63.4). Do **not** claim n_s/r as the discriminator.
- **Real separators:** (a) **f_NL** — USMEG predicts local ~1–2; NTE currently states **no f_NL**. This is both a differentiator *and a gap NTE could close*: worth a derivation pass on whether the gauge-birth cascade predicts an f_NL, since it is the one axis where USMEG has a testable prediction NTE lacks. (b) **NTE's particle sector** — SM gauge group, mass–proper-time correlation, Born/Hopf — USMEG has none; NTE's strongest separation. (c) **NTE's primordial CPT/parity signature** (C3: cosmic birefringence / chiral-GW chirality) — USMEG has none. (d) USMEG leans hard on "**exactly two tensor GW polarizations**" to exclude string/LQG/Einstein-Cartan — but that is standard GR, shared by NTE post-locking, so it does **not** separate USMEG from NTE.

**Status / priority calibration.** Largely single-author program (physics.gen-ph; supporting papers in Can. J. Phys. and Symmetry/MDPI) making a strong, non-mainstream-accepted claim ("first successful QG–SM unification"). A competitor in the *idea space*, not an established result — priority worry is modest. **Adversarial catch:** the stochastic GW background is quoted as peaking in the **LISA band, f~10⁻⁴ Hz** (abstract/§9) but computed at **f_peak~10¹⁰ Hz, ΩGWh²~10⁻¹⁵–10⁻¹⁶** in §7.5 — abstract and body disagree by ~14 orders in frequency and ~9 in amplitude. Internal inconsistency, not a NTE concern, but useful calibration on the paper's polish.

**Action:** cite for differentiation; do **not** adopt the loop-induced mechanism; flag the **f_NL gap** to the physics stream as a possible NTE derivation target.
