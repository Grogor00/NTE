## §1. Methodology

**Standalone defensibility** here means: a claim that survives publication review at a relevant math/physics venue WITHOUT requiring the reviewer to first accept NTE's foundational axioms (N fundamental + t emergent, surface only S³, H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² Friedmann coupling, Janus pair / CPN-conjugate orientations bilateral structure, geometric unloading interpretation of dark energy).

A standalone-strong claim either:
- Reduces to pure mathematics (tensor algebra, differential geometry, NCG) verifiable independent of physical interpretation, OR
- Is a clean physics identification using standard QFT / NCG / cosmology machinery with NTE-flavoured motivation but framework-independent content.

A claim with framework-flavoured *motivation* but framework-independent *content* counts as standalone-strong. A claim whose *content* presupposes framework axioms is NOT standalone.

**Four tiers:**
- **Tier S** — pure math, framework-independent: anyone with a pen can verify
- **Tier A** — standard NCG/QFT framework: presupposes Connes spectral-triple programme (standard math-physics) but no NTE-specific axioms
- **Tier B** — clean structural form with phenomenological match; physical interpretation framework-dependent but content defensible
- **Tier C** — closure candidates and confirm-tests; framework-heavier; defensibility moderate
