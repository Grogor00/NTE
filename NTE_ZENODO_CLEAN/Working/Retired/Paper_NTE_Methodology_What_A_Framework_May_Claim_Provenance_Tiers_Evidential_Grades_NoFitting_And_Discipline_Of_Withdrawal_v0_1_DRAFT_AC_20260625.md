## Abstract

A framework that extends the Standard Model invites a specific and fair challenge: that known mathematics is being re-presented as novel physics, and that numerical coincidences are being dressed as predictions. This note answers that challenge not with a defence but with an explicit method. Every claim in NTE is placed on two axes: a **provenance tier** — *new*, *re-derived*, or *imported* — and an **evidential grade** — *prediction*, *cross-check*, or *input*. Two disciplines govern what may stand. First, **no-fitting**: nothing enters the corpus as a result by numerical fit; numerical patterns may be *investigated* freely, but a pattern becomes a claim only when a physical mechanism derives it, and otherwise stays flagged as open or is discarded. Second, **withdrawal**: a claim that fails these tests is removed, not defended. We demonstrate the disciplines with teeth — by cataloguing cases where the framework has retracted or downgraded *its own* results (a withdrawn coupling-constant formula, a retired cosmological-constant numeric chain, a uniqueness claim pulled as a known corollary, a "zero-parameter" prediction reframed as an honest tension, a unification downgraded after an operator computation disproved it). The aim is a framework whose every claim a referee can locate, classify, and check — and whose discipline is a feature, not an apology.

## 1. The challenge, taken seriously

The sharpest criticism a Standard-Model extension can receive is not that a particular number is wrong but that the *category* of its claims is inflated: that re-statements of known geometry are presented as discoveries, and that quantities near a "nice" value (π/2, 2/3, a small integer ratio) are presented as derived when they are fitted. This criticism is correct as a *risk*, and no amount of individual-result defence answers it. What answers it is a method that, applied uniformly, makes the category of every claim explicit and removes the claims that cannot earn their category.

This note states that method. It is descriptive of how the NTE corpus is already maintained, and prescriptive for how it must continue to be. It deliberately includes the cases where the method has *cost* the framework results, because a discipline that only ever ratifies is not a discipline.

## 2. The provenance axis: new, re-derived, imported

Every structural element of NTE is one of three things, and the corpus is required to say which.

- **Imported.** Machinery taken from existing literature and used as-is, credited. The octonionic route to the Standard-Model gauge group, the Connes spectral-triple apparatus, the Hopf-fibration geometry, the seesaw mechanism, μ–τ reflection symmetry — these are imported. NTE's obligation here is citation and honest positioning relative to the nearest existing work (e.g. octonionic Standard-Model programmes), not a claim of originality.
- **Re-derived.** A known result reached by NTE's own route. This has value (it shows the framework is consistent with established physics and that the result sits naturally in it) but it is *not* a discovery, and must not be presented as one.
- **New.** A genuinely original identification or prediction. NTE's new content is narrow and specific — the e-fold count N as the fundamental pre-temporal parameter; the closed-S³ surface ontology; the bilateral CPN/Janus structure; and a small number of identifications built on these. The corpus is stronger, not weaker, for keeping this set small and explicit.

The governing instruction: **the architecture may be new while almost every component is imported or re-derived, and the paper must say so.** A framework is allowed to be a new arrangement of mostly-known parts; it is not allowed to relabel the parts as new.

## 3. The evidential axis: prediction, cross-check, input

Orthogonal to provenance is the grade of evidential commitment.

- **Prediction.** Forced by the structure with no freedom to absorb the result — and therefore falsifiable. Examples in the corpus: the spectral index n_s and tensor ratio r (forced, CMB-S4/LiteBIRD-testable); the neutrino mass-ratio *shape* m(ν₃)/m(ν₂) = (flux)^(2/3); θ₂₃ = π/4.
- **Cross-check.** A known empirical quantity the framework reproduces. Reproducing H₀ or recovering the Schwarzschild factor is a consistency cross-check, not an independent prediction — and is labelled as such.
- **Input.** A quantity inherited and disclosed, not derived. β_NTE is an input (the governing constraint is self-consistent across nine orders of magnitude, so self-consistency cannot select it); the heavy Majorana scale M_R is an input set by the heaviest light-neutrino mass. Inputs are legitimate; *undisclosed* inputs are not.

The failure mode the corpus guards against is an **input dressed as a prediction**, or a **cross-check dressed as a prediction**. Section 5 lists cases where exactly this was caught — in the framework's own pages.

## 4. The no-fitting discipline

The rule is stated precisely because its scope is often misunderstood: **no-fitting governs ratified additions, not investigation.**

- A numerical pattern — a recurring offset, a value near a simple constant — *may and should be investigated*. Exploration is how one learns whether a pattern has a physical cause or is a coincidence.
- A pattern becomes a **claim** only when a physical mechanism derives it. Until then it remains explicitly flagged as investigation, and if no mechanism is found it is **discarded**, not retained as a suggestive coincidence.
- The test that separates the two is mechanism-first: the number must fall out of structure, and the structure must have been fixed for independent reasons. A constant chosen *because* it matches is fitting; a constant *forced* by a structure fixed elsewhere is a prediction.

A worked instance: a cluster of neutrino-sector residuals near 1.5 was investigated as a possible shared factor. Traced to their derivations they proved to be three *different* closed forms — 1/(2/3), 2^(2/3), and a nucleosynthesis remainder — clustering by coincidence. The pattern was investigated in full and then **discarded**, with one genuine residual tension isolated and routed for a first-principles test. This is the discipline operating exactly as intended: explore, then keep only what a mechanism earns.

## 5. The discipline with teeth: withdrawals and downgrades

A method is only credible if it removes claims. The following are cases where NTE has retracted or downgraded its own results.

- **The β_NTE characterisation — withdrawn.** A closed-form "characterisation" of β_NTE was retracted once it was recognised that the governing constraint is self-consistent for any β_NTE across nine orders of magnitude; β_NTE is therefore an *input*, and H₀ = β_NTE v_EW² holds by construction once β_NTE is fixed, not as an independent prediction.
- **The cosmological-constant numeric chain — retired.** A numeric Λ closure was retired when a heat-kernel coefficient (a₄) was found to vanish on S³×S¹, collapsing the chord that supported it. Λ is now handled structurally (unimodular closure, w_true = −1), with the numeric claim gone.
- **A trace-anomaly uniqueness claim — pulled.** A standalone asserting a uniqueness result on S³×S¹ was withdrawn when its body was found to be a corollary of an existing (Lafontaine-1988) result: the title claimed novelty the mathematics did not support.
- **A "coupling constant" — reframed.** A quantity treated as a coupling (3/√(4π)) was reframed as what it is: a normalisation constant. The category error was corrected even though the number was unchanged.
- **A "zero-parameter" neutrino prediction — reframed.** A neutrino mass result presented as zero-parameter was retracted to its honest content: the (flux)^(2/3) *shape* is the prediction, while a ~1.5× solar/atmospheric tension is foregrounded as a live, fallible feature rather than buried.
- **A unification — downgraded after computation.** A proposed identification of two maximal-CP phases (θ₂₃'s reflection phase and the bilateral CPN phase) was held conditional and then downgraded when an independent operator computation proved the two operations are distinct (parallel, not descended): the claim was reduced to "shared antilinear character," and the stronger "shared operator" reading dropped.

None of these was forced by an external referee finding the error first. Each was caught and acted on internally. That is the property the method is for.

## 6. The conditional-result standard

Where a result is partly derived and partly open, the standard is to **state exactly what is derived and openly bracket what is not** — and to prefer a flagged-open result to an overclaimed closed one. Two current examples: n_s and r are forced *conditional on* an open kinetic normalisation K(Φ), and say so; the θ₂₃-reflection standalone presents a real upgrade (forced reflection, natural θ₁₃, |δ_CP| = π/2) while flagging the operator-descent and the high/low-scale phase identification as open. A conditional result is not a weaker result; it is an honestly scoped one, and it is the form most of a live framework's claims should take.

## 7. The programme-level standard

Two rules bind the corpus as a whole.

- **No standalone claims a tier above what the corpus grants the scale beneath it.** A companion may not assert as derived something the master treats as open; a prediction may not rest on an input presented elsewhere as a prediction. Tiers propagate downward, not upward.
- **Results propagate.** When a result changes the status of a quantity a published paper depends on, the dependent paper is updated — promptly — rather than left to drift. A framework's honesty is measured partly by the lag between a result and the correction of everything it touches.

## 8. What the discipline buys

The pay-off is concrete. A referee encountering NTE can, for any claim, locate its provenance tier and evidential grade from the paper itself, and check it against the stated open steps; the framework's genuinely new content is small, explicit, and falsifiable; and the catalogue of withdrawals (§5) is evidence that the method is real rather than rhetorical. The intended posture is not defensiveness about the "known mathematics" charge but its pre-emption: yes, most of the machinery is known and credited; the new content is the architecture and a few identifications; and here is exactly what each claim is and how it could fail. A discipline of withdrawal is not an admission of weakness. It is the reason the remaining claims can be trusted.
