NTE inherits most Yukawa couplings from observation, and is honest that it does so. The exception developed here is the second-generation neutrino Dirac Yukawa, which the framework fixes structurally. The Majorana mass on the σ₁/σ₂ Killing-spinor sector carries the cube-root flux normalisation (8π)^{1/3} (Companion 10); a Dirac Yukawa on the *same* Hopf–Killing-spinor flux line carries one fewer volumetric power, hence its inverse:

With the heavy scale M_R from the σ₃ Cartan mode, the seesaw gives the solar-scale mass

which is 0.993 × √(Δm²_solar). The prediction uses only v_EW and the same M_R that fixes the heavy-leg check; it is not fitted to the solar splitting, and it has no free parameter. The result is genuinely zero-parameter on the **solar leg**; the atmospheric leg, with the same single M_R, overshoots by a factor ≈ 1.5, and that limitation is stated rather than hidden. A critical point of seesaw bookkeeping is made explicit: the denominator for the light ν₂ is the Cartan-scale M_R, not the Pathway-α M₂; the M₂-only reading is wrong by orders of magnitude and is ruled out.

## 4. The seesaw, and a bookkeeping that must be right

The light ν₂ mass is the seesaw of this Dirac Yukawa against the heavy Cartan-scale Majorana propagator:

  **m_{ν₂} = (8π)^{-2/3} v_EW² / M_R**,  with M_R = 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, v_EW = 246 GeV,

giving m_{ν₂} = 0.0086 eV, against √(Δm²₂₁) ≈ 0.0087 eV — a ratio of 0.993, within current 1σ precision, with the same M_R and v_EW used for the heavy-leg check and no input fitted to the solar splitting.

The bookkeeping point is essential and easy to get wrong. The denominator is the **Cartan-scale M_R**, not the light σ₁/σ₂ Majorana M₂. Using Y_{ν₂} = (8π)^{-1/3} against M₂ alone gives a mass of order a keV — wrong by five orders of magnitude and decisively excluded. (An equivalent, algebraically tied form writes m_{ν₂} = Y_{ν₂}² v_EW²/M₂ with Y_{ν₂} = (8π)^{-1/3}√(M₁/M_R), landing at 0.972 × solar; the small difference from the canonical route is the sin²θ₁₃ split, not a free parameter.) That the correct denominator is M_R, derived ab initio from the σ₃-mixing structure, is itself an open programme item; here it is the established seesaw reading.

**The atmospheric cross-check, stated honestly.** With Y₃ = 1 on the σ₃ Cartan mode and the same M_R, the heavy leg gives m_{ν₃} = v_EW²/M_R = 0.074 eV against √(Δm²₃₁) ≈ 0.050 eV — a factor ≈ 1.49, an overshoot, not a sub-percent match. The predicted mass ratio m_{ν₃}/m_{ν₂} = (8π)^{2/3} ≈ 8.57 against the observed √(Δm²₃₁/Δm²₂₁) ≈ 5.8 differs by the same factor ≈ 1.5: a single M_R cannot fit both legs at the percent level. The zero-parameter genuine-test status therefore applies to the **solar leg only**; the atmospheric leg is an open tension, and the (flux)^{2/3} shape of the ratio is the genuine, falsifiable content. (Any earlier "0.997× atmospheric" reading used an incorrect comparand and does not stand.)
