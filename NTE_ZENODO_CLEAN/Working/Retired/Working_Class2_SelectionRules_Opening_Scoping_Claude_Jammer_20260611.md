## §1 — The structural claim and what it predicts (recap)

Per the doubly-ratified AC framing (2026-06-10 23:28Z + 2026-06-11 05:52Z):

What selection rules could discriminate this from a fundamental-CPT picture?

The selection-rule question reduces to: **does the unitary-fundamental-CPN structure leave fingerprints on transitions that a fundamental-antiunitary-T picture would not leave (or vice versa), in any observable channel?**

Three a priori paths:

**Path A — Direct probe of unitary-vs-antiunitary fundamental structure.** Hard to see how this could be tested at lab energies (emergent CPT is exact in IR; any test we run is on the emergent layer). Possibly accessible via quantum-information protocols that probe the antiunitary/unitary character of a symmetry directly (e.g., Kramers degeneracy tests).

**Path B — Transitions that cross the EWSB transition.** Selection rules at the EWSB boundary could carry the fingerprint of the CPN lift (real-orthogonal → unitary). Probably manifests as constraints on Higgs-sector physics, since EWSB is where the lift happens. LHC Higgs measurements as candidate venue.

**Path C — Fundamental-layer-sourced asymmetries that survive to IR.** Analogous to C3b (baryogenesis) but for non-cosmological observables. Examples: EDMs sourced at gauge birth that survive to present epoch; specific Majorana CP phases forced by the unitary-CPN structure; LFV rates set by the σ₁/σ₂/σ₃ Cartan-generation structure.

All three paths are speculative until concrete derivations are attempted. The scoping below identifies which are worth attempting.

## §2 — Six candidate sub-categories

### §2.1 Sub-category 2A — Lepton sector (LFV; Majorana CP phases; neutrinoless double-beta decay)

**The candidate.** NTE has Majorana neutrinos via the seesaw structure (M_R, M_1, M_2, M_3). Three generations from σ₁/σ₂/σ₃ Cartan modes. The unitary-CPN structure at fundamental level dictates the chirality assignment that emerges to give Majorana mass. Does this force specific selection rules or asymmetries in lepton-sector processes that fundamental-CPT would not force?

**Specific testable observables.**
- **Lepton flavor violation:** μ → eγ (current bound: BR < 4.2×10⁻¹³, MEG II target ~6×10⁻¹⁴); μ → eee (Mu3e); μN → eN (Mu2e, COMET). NTE's three-generation σ_i Cartan structure should predict relative rates / forbidden channels.
- **Neutrinoless double-beta decay (0νββ):** KamLAND-Zen, LEGEND, nEXO, CUPID. Effective Majorana mass m_ββ depends on PMNS Majorana phases. NTE's unitary-CPN structure plausibly forces specific phase combinations.
- **PMNS CP phase δ_CP:** DUNE, Hyper-K, T2K. Current best-fit ~−π/2 (NOvA/T2K disagreement notwithstanding). NTE has Paper P4 with θ_23 standalone derivation; possibly extends to δ_CP.

**Honest assessment.** The σ_i Cartan structure is genuinely distinctive to NTE and not present in generic seesaw frameworks. **Plausibly forces selection-rule content.** The challenge: deriving specific predicted rates without smuggling. Existing corpus has neutrino-sector machinery (Paper P4, P5; Companion 7; v_NTE Stage work) that can be leveraged.

**Priority: HIGH.** Most promising candidate of the six.

**Discrimination from fundamental-CPT:** if NTE's σ_i Cartan structure forces, say, BR(μ→eγ) within a specific range, and that range differs from generic seesaw expectation under fundamental CPT, that's a clean discriminator. Requires careful operator-level work — Scratch (Connes) and Cursor Jammer lanes for explicit derivation.

### §2.2 Sub-category 2B — Quark/meson sector (B-meson triple-product asymmetries; top CP; charm CP)

**The candidate.** Standard CKM CP violation is well-characterised. The unitary-CPN structure could predict specific CKM-extending effects — additional CP phases, modified mixing, or asymmetries in triple-product correlations.

**Specific testable observables.**
- **B-meson triple-product asymmetries:** LHCb, Belle II. T-violating but CPT-respecting; probes T-violation directly.
- **Top quark CP violation:** ATLAS, CMS. Particularly tt̄ spin correlations.
- **D-meson CP violation:** LHCb. Direct CP-asymmetry in D⁰ → K⁺K⁻ etc.

**Honest assessment.** Quark-sector CP violation is dominated by CKM at low energies. NTE doesn't obviously have a distinctive prediction in the quark sector — the σ_i Cartan generation structure is lepton-favoured in the existing corpus (Hopf-Born / chirality bifurcation gives the lepton hierarchy more directly than the quark hierarchy). Without a clean NTE-specific mechanism, this sub-category risks number-dialing (post-fitting to existing CP-violation data).

**Priority: LOW.** Bin unless a specific NTE-distinctive quark-sector mechanism is identified.

### §2.3 Sub-category 2C — EDM sector (electron, neutron, atomic)

**The candidate.** EDMs are CP-violating observables. Current bounds: electron EDM < 4.1×10⁻³⁰ e·cm (JILA 2023); neutron EDM < 1.8×10⁻²⁶ e·cm. Future: ACME III, n2EDM. Any new CP-violating physics generally contributes to EDMs.

**Does the unitary-CPN structure predict a specific EDM contribution?** If yes, and that contribution is in the testable range without smuggling, this is a clean discriminator.

**Honest assessment.** EDM contributions from BSM physics are typically loop-suppressed and depend on details. NTE's CP-violation comes from the chirality bifurcation at gauge birth (per chirality-superposition framing) — does that generate IR-surviving EDM contributions, or is it all primordial (per the Scratch C3 lock)?

**Honest prior: probably primordial only,** by the same logic that gives β ≈ 0 (CPT exact in IR; CP-violating effects that need active coupling during 0 < z < 1100 forbidden). EDMs in IR should follow standard CKM-like patterns. **But this needs a concrete derivation** — if there's an IR-surviving CP-violating contribution from the gauge-birth structure, it would be testable.

**Priority: MEDIUM.** Worth a Scratch (Connes) check: does the unitary-CPN structure source any IR-surviving CP-violating term that contributes to EDMs?

**Discrimination from fundamental-CPT:** if NTE forces a specific electron-EDM ceiling that differs from generic BSM expectation, that's a discriminator. Likely outcome: NTE predicts the same as fundamental-CPT for EDMs (both IR-exact), no discrimination.

### §2.4 Sub-category 2D — Higgs sector

**The candidate.** EWSB is where the CPN lift to unitary occurs in NTE. The Higgs sector is the operator-theoretic locus of that transition. Does NTE force specific selection rules in Higgs physics that fundamental-CPT does not?

**Specific testable observables.**
- **Higgs CP structure:** LHC measurements of H → ττ, H → ZZ, H → γγ angular distributions.
- **Higgs portal:** the Necessity paper closes the Φ_NTE–FF̃ portal; this is a *constraint* the corpus already commits to. Worth checking what selection-rule content this implies (e.g., suppression of Higgs invisible decays beyond Standard Model expectation).
- **HH production:** di-Higgs production rate (LHC HL-LHC and beyond).

**Honest assessment.** EWSB is the locus where the CPN lift occurs, so plausibly there are selection-rule fingerprints in Higgs sector. But the Necessity paper already heavily constrains the Φ_NTE / Higgs portal. Any NEW selection rule must be derived without re-opening the closed portal.

**Priority: MEDIUM.** Worth a scoping check: does the EWSB transition (where CPN lifts) imply specific Higgs-sector selection rules beyond what Necessity already commits to?

### §2.6 Sub-category 2F — Quantum-information / direct unitary-vs-antiunitary probes

**The candidate.** Specific quantum-information protocols (Bell-test variants, weak measurement schemes, etc.) can probe the antiunitary/unitary character of a symmetry directly. Kramers degeneracy and its variants are classical examples.

**Specific testable observables.**
- **Kramers-like degeneracy tests in spin systems:** can specific experimental setups distinguish whether T-symmetry of a system is fundamentally antiunitary or emergently antiunitary?
- **Bell-test variants probing T:** can the antiunitarity of T be tested directly via entanglement protocols?

**Honest assessment.** This is the most speculative sub-category. Quantum-information protocols generally probe the *emergent* symmetry (which is antiunitary CPT in NTE). Whether they can distinguish emergent-antiunitary from fundamental-antiunitary depends on subtle operational definitions.

**Priority: LOW-MEDIUM.** Worth a brief scoping pass to identify whether any direct probe of fundamental-vs-emergent symmetry structure exists in literature. If yes: investigate. If no: bin.
