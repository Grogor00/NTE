**A pathway-survey of NTE's single new field**

NTE extends the Standard Model and General Relativity with one new field, the boundary expansion field **Φ_NTE**, defined through **H(x,N) = β_NTE|Φ_NTE(x,N)|²**. We subject it to a destruction test. We find: (i) Φ_NTE is **necessary and genuinely new** — not the Higgs and not the noncommutative-geometry σ (both eliminations fail, the latter categorically via NTE's no-right-handed-neutrino ontology); (ii) it is a **geometric, constraint-bound** scalar that is a ghost if unconstrained, hence carries **no propagating particle** — signatures purely cosmological; (iii) its necessity **reduces to the single relation H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²**, whose coefficient β_NTE the corpus classifies as a fundamental constant; (iv) a survey of the derivation pathways *we have found and checked* shows each is **proven-impossible, searched-and-failed, wrong-target, or a relabelling** — with exactly one route **not yet closed**: a self-consistency (conformal-cyclic-style) fixed point, whose status is the last unlocked door of our search, not an endorsement and not a result. The honest position: **one new field, one foundational constant, no pretence of deriving the constant** — pending one open dig that could give it a floor and a survey of competing cosmologies that could change the count.

Φ_NTE is a real scalar on the S³ boundary of geometric origin — the boundary expansion field of the surface-only ontology. Established roles: it sets H via **H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²** (β_NTE = 2.38 × 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁻¹); carries emergent time (t = iΔN, dt/dN = 1/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²)); is the inflation-sector field (Role B → n_s = 0.9685, r ≈ 4×10⁻³); is the dark-energy sector (w_true = −1 + void enhancement); and is the order parameter of the temporal-locking (Euclidean→Lorentzian) transition (|Φ_NTE|: 0 at Janus → M_Pl at gauge birth). Is positing it forced?

## 2. Elimination of alternatives

**Not the Higgs.** H ∝ |Φ|² is cosmological (~10⁻⁴² GeV), not microphysical; the §2.3.1 trajectory forces |Φ_NTE| ~ 10^8.5 v_EW at BBN, catastrophic under a Higgs identification. The SM Higgs sector is untouched. |Φ_NTE(today)| = v_EW is a trajectory property, not an identity.

**Not the NCG σ — categorically.** The Chamseddine–Connes σ is generated by a Majorana coupling between ν_R and ν_R^c; NTE has no such entities as distinct fundamental objects (the neutrino has not undergone the Hopf projection creating the particle/antiparticle distinction). The mechanisms are categorically incompatible → identification rejected (`Sigma_PhiNTE_Confluence_Verdict_v1`). What survives is **structural confluence**: both reach the same effective-action form (ξ conformal + quartic + Higgs portal), NTE independently via Wick-invariance at gauge birth + 4D conformal discipline. Φ_NTE is NTE's own field.

**Not a fermion condensate.** The composite route is the rejected σ mechanism. Φ_NTE is geometric — the boundary's expansion degree of freedom — not a composite of the S⁷ spinor sector.

## 3. Ontological status and the absence of a new particle

Φ_NTE is **constraint-bound, not freely dynamical**: H = β_NTE|Φ|² is imposed by a Lagrange multiplier μ(N); V_NTE is consistency-only. A dynamically rolling realisation is impossible — it requires (Φ′)² ≈ −10⁸⁸ — and the ghost scan shows the unconstrained mode is a **ghost**, so the μ-constraint is structurally required for a healthy theory. The would-be propagating degree of freedom is thereby removed: **Φ_NTE carries no physical particle.** Not a collider state, not the σ/dilaton ("no dilaton extension required," K(Φ)=1); on-trajectory slaved to H. Its only signatures are the cosmological ones NTE already predicts. It **unifies** the boundary-expansion, inflation, and dark-energy roles into one constrained geometric field rather than adding to the spectrum.

## 4. The irreducible core

Via the Friedmann identity the relation is equivalent to ρ ∝ Φ⁴ with β_NTE = √(8πGλ/3): a **form** (H ∝ |Φ|²) and a **coefficient** (β_NTE). The form is conformally natural (the quartic is the unique conformally-invariant 4D scalar potential) but is realised in NTE only as a kinematic constraint, its dynamical realisation being the impossible/ghost case (§3). The coefficient β_NTE is, in the corpus's §10 classification, a **"Fundamental Constant (Not Derivable)."** The whole "NTE requires a new field" claim thus reduces to one relation, H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|². §5 asks whether it has a floor.
