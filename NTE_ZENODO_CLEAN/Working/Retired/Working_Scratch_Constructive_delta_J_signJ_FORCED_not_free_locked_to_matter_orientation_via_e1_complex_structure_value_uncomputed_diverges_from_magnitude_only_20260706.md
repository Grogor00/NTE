## §2 — ±e₁ is the matter/antimatter orientation, not a free convention (the crux)

The paper hands this over in §5.3: under J = L_{e₁}, Im(𝕆) = 1 ⊕ 3 ⊕ 3̄ — colour triplet and **anti**triplet. Establishing sign(J) is *forced* is not the same as computing *which* sign. To state "+π/2" or "−π/2" in PDG convention requires two things the corpus does not contain:

1. **The octonion-orientation → PDG-δ dictionary** — the explicit map from the fixed e₁ orientation to the sign of δ in the standard PMNS parameterisation.
