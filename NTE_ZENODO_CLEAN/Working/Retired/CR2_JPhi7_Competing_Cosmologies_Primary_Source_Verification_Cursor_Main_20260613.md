## Load-bearing checks (KSIP lesson applied)

### 1. Penrose CCC — does not derive Λ
**Scratch claim:** Penrose uses CCC for low-entropy Big Bang / Hawking points, not Λ derivation.  
**Cursor check:** Penrose CCC field equations include **positive cosmological constant Λ** as input; Λ "same constant value from aeon to aeon" (Foundations of Physics 2018 CCC review). Matching across aeons can demand **Λ̂ = Λ̌** but does not fix the numerical value. **PASS** — Scratch scope claim holds.

### 2. Smolin CNS — cosmological constant
**Scratch claim:** CNS fixes particle parameters at BH-production attractor; bearing on foundational cosmological scale β_NTE kind is **unestablished**.  
**Cursor check:** Smolin (Class. Quantum Grav. 9, 1992) explicitly states inability to explain Λ smallness via selection; later work (Physica D 2004) offers **conjecture** that present Λ may optimize BH formation — competition between galaxy formation and mergers — not a fixed-point equation for a specific Λ. CNS focuses on low-energy particle/chemistry parameters. **PASS** — Scratch's "unestablished for β_NTE" stands; CNS is genuine second fixed-point *kind* but not demonstrated rival for β_NTE scale.

### 3. Weinberg anthropic Λ
**Scratch claim:** Pre-diction of order-of-magnitude window; dissolution not derivation rival.  
**Cursor check:** Well-established reading of Weinberg 1987 — observer window over distributed vacua. **PASS**.
