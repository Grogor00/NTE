## The clarification

The **no-numerology / no-number-dialing** rule applies to **ratified additions** — what enters the corpus as established. It does **not** prohibit **investigation pathways**.

- If a numerical pattern worth investigating is seen, **exploring it is permitted and sometimes necessary.** It may lead to a physical pathway, or it may turn out to be numerological mumbo-jumbo — and the only way to know which is to explore.
- The discipline lives at the **ratification gate**, not at the looking stage: a pattern enters the corpus **only when it earns a physical derivation**, never by fit/coincidence alone. A discovered pattern that lacks a mechanism stays in the **investigation lane**, clearly labelled as such, until it either earns a physical route or is discarded.
- **Especially worth exploring:** a **pattern of offsets**, or a **pattern in the required offsets/residuals**. A recurring offset structure across independent results is itself a potential physical clue — chasing it is legitimate investigation, not number-dialing.

So the correct reading of the rule is: **"no unratified number-dialing in the corpus,"** not **"no examining numerical patterns."** The two were never the same; this note makes that explicit so the rule isn't over-applied.
