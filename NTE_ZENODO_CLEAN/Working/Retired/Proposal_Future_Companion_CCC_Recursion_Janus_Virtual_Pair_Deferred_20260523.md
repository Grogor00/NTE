## §1. The musing

Greg's question, verbatim framing: "If our universe/anti-universe pair is analogous to a virtual particle pair, then at ~10¹²⁰ years in the lone photon epoch, what is the N-thickness? Does it effectively reduce to zero at the same time the lone photon is completely cut off and transitions?"

Three threads being pulled together:

1. **CCC + fractal foam end-state** (corpus §XV): the dying boundary at the 1-photon-per-Hubble-volume limit where the boundary fragments into new Janus N=0 nucleation seeds.
2. **Virtual particle pair analogy**: virtual pairs come into existence transiently from vacuum and annihilate back; pair total quantum numbers = 0; finite lifetime by uncertainty.
3. **N-thickness** (F1, V_NTE Stage 2): the bilateral geometric structure of the manifold, ℓ = 1/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²) = 1/H per the corpus's §6.2 ℓ = 1/H structural identification.

## §2. Structural reading using core axioms

(N fundamental + t emergent; surface only S³; H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²; F1 ℓ = 1/H; CPN-conjugate orientations of one N-thickness.)

### §2.1 What's actually committed in the corpus

- **|Φ_NTE|² asymptotes finite and constant** (§12.2-§12.3): |Φ_NTE(N→∞)|² → v_EW²·√Ω_Λ. NOT zero.
- **N-thickness asymptotes finite and constant**: ℓ_dS = 1/H_dS where H_dS = α·v_EW²·√Ω_Λ. Approximately 1/H_0 ≈ 1.4 × 10²⁶ m ≈ 10⁶¹ Planck lengths. NOT zero.
- **w = -1 exactly at the asymptote** (§12.3): no continuing thinning past the de Sitter plateau.
- **Foam transition at 1-photon-per-Hubble-volume** (§XV): boundary fragments; every point with a local photon becomes a new N = 0 nucleation seed. "Geometric tension reaches zero" reads as standing-wave structural failure, NOT as |Φ_NTE|² → 0 literally.

### §2.2 Proposed structural identification (the proposal's central claim)

Distinguish three notions of "N-thickness":

- **Proper-length N-thickness** ℓ(N) = 1/H(N) per F1. Asymptotes to ~10⁶¹ Planck lengths. Does NOT collapse at foam transition.
- **|Φ_NTE|² field value**. Asymptotes to v_EW²·√Ω_Λ. Does NOT collapse at foam transition.
- **Effective causal N-thickness** — proposed concept, not corpus-canonical: the thickness over which the manifold supports coherent causal/quantum exchange between the two CPN-conjugate orientations through the unresolved-neutrino bridge.

**Proposal**: effective causal N-thickness collapses to zero at the foam transition, simultaneously with the lone-photon causal severance. Both are the same structural moment — the moment the manifold can no longer hold itself together as a coherent two-orientation object, and fragments. The proper-length thickness 1/H_dS persists in each new fragment as that fragment's initial geometric scale at its own new N = 0 Janus seed.

The lone-photon severance and the effective-causal-N-thickness collapse and the foam fragmentation event are three descriptions of one structural moment. Under §II.0 register, this would be a candidate structural simplification — multiple separate-seeming endings of cosmic structure resolve to one geometric event.

### §2.3 The virtual-pair analogy: where it holds, where it breaks

**Holds** at the birth and content level:
- Total quantum numbers across the Janus pair = 0 by CPN (B_total(+N) + B_total(-N) = 0, §8.4.1). Parallel to virtual pair's vacuum-quantum-number content.
- Pair born at N = 0 from |Φ_NTE| = 0 (Janus point, conformal interface). Parallel to virtual pair nucleating from vacuum.
- Pair has finite N-fold extent — bounded above at N_foam. Parallel to virtual pair's finite Δt lifetime.
- De Sitter thermal time ℏ/k_B T_dS = 2π/H ~ Hubble time. Suggestive of a Δt × ΔE ~ ℏ relation at the cosmic scale (though this would require explicit derivation, not analogy).

**Breaks** at the termination:
- Virtual pairs annihilate back to vacuum (single-event termination, vacuum restoration).
- Janus pair under §XV fragments into many new N = 0 seeds (reproductive event, new aeons).
- Daughter aeons inherit ℓ_dS as their initial geometric scale. Per Appendix A.4 forward pathway item 10, whether α (the framework's fundamental constant) is preserved across the recursion or admits a fixed-point structure α = F(Ω(α)) is open.

### §2.4 The structural moment: three descriptions of one geometric event

Under the proposal:

| Description | Operational content | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| Lone-photon causal severance | Last photon per Hubble volume; null-time exchange structure fragments (§XV) | Null-time / entanglement geometry |
| Effective causal N-thickness collapse | Unresolved-neutrino bridge loses coherence-length support at the standing-wave failure point | Cross-thickness coupling / §9 bridge geometry |
| Janus pair "termination" | CPN-pair's bilateral N-thickness loses its coherent two-orientation manifold structure | Janus / surface-ontology geometry |

These three are proposed to be the same geometric event. The corpus is compatible with this identification (no commitment forbids it) but does NOT yet make it explicit. Promotion to §II.0 register would require demonstrating that the identification is **forced by deeper structure** (rather than consistency between independent commitments). The most likely route: the daughter aeon's initial ℓ inherits the parent's ℓ_dS not by accident but by the geometric structure of the fragmentation event itself — each fragment's "starting size" is precisely the parent's Hubble radius because that's the maximum coherent geometric scale the parent could support at termination.

## §3. Timescale honest accounting

- **Standard 1-photon-per-Hubble-volume crossing**: ~10²²-10²³ years (Krauss & Scherrer 2007; Adams & Laughlin 1997). N-foldings past today: ~200 e-folds. This is the foam-transition timescale per §XV literal reading.
- **Proton decay completion**: ~10³⁴-10⁴⁰ years (depending on GUT scale + SM extension).
- **Final black hole evaporation**: ~10¹⁰⁰ years.
- **Penrose CCC reset**: ~10¹⁰⁰⁺ years (all matter has thermalised; "thermodynamic death").
- **Greg's ~10¹²⁰ years**: likely the final stelliferous-to-degenerate-to-black-hole-evaporation timescale (Dyson 1979; Adams & Laughlin 1997 "Five Ages of the Universe").

Different physics anchors different timescales. The structural reading proposed in §2.2 does not depend on which timescale anchors the foam transition; it depends on the structural moment when manifold coherence fails. NTE's §XV uses the photon-limit framing; whether this can be sharpened (e.g., to a structural identification with proton decay completion, or with black hole evaporation completion, or with a different moment) is itself part of the open work.

## §4. Open questions for future development

If/when this Proposal graduates to active work as a Companion paper on CCC recursion, these are the substantive open questions:

1. **Structural derivation of the foam-fragmentation event**. The corpus at §XV gives a qualitative reading. A first-principles derivation from H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² + the standing-wave structure of the boundary + the unresolved-neutrino bridge would convert "geometric tension reaches zero" from descriptive to derived.

2. **Entropy bookkeeping across the foam transition**. Do daughter Janus seeds inherit the parent's de Sitter horizon entropy S_dS? Is there an entropic discontinuity (e.g., generation of new entropy at the foam fragmentation)? The relation to Bekenstein-Hawking area scaling and §XVI's boundary-only Ryu-Takayanagi conjecture would need explicit treatment.

3. **α preservation under CCC recursion** (Appendix A.4 forward pathway item 10). Whether α is invariant under the foam-fragmentation event, or whether it admits a fixed-point structure α = F(Ω(α)) — would make CCC recursion either a discrete dynamical system on α-space (with possible attractors) or a trivially constant function.

4. **Daughter aeon initial conditions**. Specifically: does each new N = 0 seed inherit ℓ_dS (the parent's deep-time Hubble radius) as its initial ℓ? If so, the recursion is geometrically self-similar at the scale level. If not, the recursion involves a scale jump (smaller / larger?) at each fragmentation event.

5. **Virtual-pair analogy formalisation**. Is there a precise sense in which the Janus pair satisfies a cosmological-scale Δt × ΔE ~ ℏ relation? The de Sitter thermal time ℏ/k_B T_dS = 2π/H gives the Hubble time as a candidate "lifetime" — but the actual N-fold lifetime to foam transition is much longer (~200 e-folds in the photon-limit reading). The two scales don't immediately reconcile; this is a research question, not an established identification.

6. **CPN charge conservation under recursion**. Per §8.4.1 the Janus pair has B_total = 0. Do daughter aeons each have their own B = 0 (each fragment is its own CPN-balanced pair), or does the recursion preserve a global B = 0 across all daughter aeons (one universe-wide CPN constraint)? The corpus default would seem to be the former (each fragment is a new N = 0 with its own CPN structure), but this is not explicit.

7. **Connection to §17.5.13 Born rule scope**. The Born rule's complex Hilbert space input is currently flagged as research target. Whether the CCC-recursive structure provides a route to deriving the complex Hilbert space from the recursion dynamics itself (rather than as an input) would close a long-standing scope question.

8. **Connection to §17.5.6 g_eff × α_em ≈ √6/π coincidence**. If α has fixed-point structure under CCC recursion, the values of g_eff and α_em that emerge in any given aeon might be selected by the fixed-point conditions. This would convert the §17.5.6 coincidence from "unexplained" to "selected by recursion fixed point."
