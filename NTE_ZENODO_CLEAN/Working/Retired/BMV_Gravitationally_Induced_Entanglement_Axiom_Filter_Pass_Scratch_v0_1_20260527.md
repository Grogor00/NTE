## §1 — The phenomenon

**The proposal.** Bose et al. (2017) and Marletto-Vedral (2017) independently proposed that two massive systems in spatial superposition, allowed to interact only gravitationally, will become entangled *if and only if* the gravitational field is non-classical. The argument is information-theoretic: a strictly classical (LOCC) mediator cannot entangle two systems that start unentangled. Therefore, observation of gravitationally-induced entanglement (GIE) would constitute experimental evidence for the quantum nature of gravity.

**Experimental status (2025–2026).** Not yet performed. Several proposals in advanced development:
- **Nanodiamond NV-centre interferometry** (Di Pietra et al. 2024 *arXiv* 2410.19601; Bose-Geraci-Mazumdar groups; UCL + INRIM + Oxford collaboration). Magnetic trapping; optical readout.
- **Diamagnetic microchip traps** (Elahi et al. 2025 *Phys. Rev. A* 112, 063508) for levitated-nanoparticle entanglement.
- **Spin-based pathway** (Morley et al. white paper, *arXiv* 2509.01586, Sep 2025) — medium-sized consortium required; major decoherence-suppression challenge.
- **Review** (Bose, Fuentes, Geraci, Khan, Qvarfort, Rademacher, Rashid, Toroš, Ulbricht, Wanjura 2025) *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 97, 015003 — "Massive quantum systems as interfaces of quantum mechanics and gravity."

**Theoretical state (2024–2025).** The original BMV inference — "GIE ⇒ quantum gravity" — has been substantially weakened by two recent published results:

- **Howl & Aziz (2025)** *Nature* (DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09595-7) — "Classical theories of gravity produce entanglement." Demonstrates that classical gravity *can* produce entanglement, undermining the canonical inferential link. Establishes that the scale of the classical-gravity effect differs quantitatively from quantum-gravity predictions, providing a parameter for discriminating designs.
- **Vidal et al. (2025)** *arXiv* 2506.21122 — "BMV experiment without observable spacetime superpositions." Three explicit toy models (fermionic systems with parity superselection; non-Abelian anyonic systems; bit-anti-bit model) generate BMV-like entanglement without requiring spacetime superpositions, via non-locally-tomographic couplings.

The current consensus: **observing GIE remains experimentally desirable**, but the interpretive inference is now known to be more delicate. The clean "GIE ⇒ gravity is quantum" framing is wrong; the correct reading is that the BMV experiment discriminates *between classes of gravity-matter coupling*, not between "classical" and "quantum" simpliciter.

Related result worth noting: the parameter-free **Diósi-Penrose collapse model** has been experimentally ruled out by Donadi et al. 2021 (underground X-ray emission) and follow-ups through 2024–2025. DP-class theories predict different GIE behaviour from quantum gravity (Großardt 2025 *Phys. Rev. D* L121101).

## §4 — Axiom-by-axiom check

### A1 (Spectral action as unified principle)

NTE's spectral action emerges from a single functional. The output includes a tensor mode (graviton) that propagates on the emergent spacetime exactly like the textbook graviton at low energies. The Chamseddine-Connes 2010 calculation on S³ explicitly produces Einstein-Hilbert + cosmological term + scalar potential — *the full standard gravitational sector* — at leading order in the heat-kernel expansion.

The BMV setup operates at energy scales ~10⁻²⁰ eV (gravitational coupling between ~10⁻¹⁴ kg masses at ~100 μm). This is many, many orders of magnitude below any spectral-action UV cutoff. At these scales, the spectral action *is* Einstein-Hilbert gravity plus its perturbative graviton quantisation.

**Verdict on A1: compatible; spectral-action-emerged gravity reproduces textbook gravitational dynamics at the BMV scale.**

### A2 (Graviton as tensor mode)

NTE's graviton has the standard properties: spin-2, two helicities, universal coupling. It mediates gravitational interactions including the Newtonian potential between BMV masses. Two masses in spatial superposition each source graviton-mediated metric perturbations; the cross-coupling generates entanglement at the same rate as in textbook quantum gravity.

**Verdict on A2: compatible; NTE's graviton mediates BMV entanglement at standard rate.**

### A3 (|Φ_NTE| as separate scalar mode)

At the BMV scale, |Φ_NTE| is at its full electroweak-era asymptotic value with negligible spatial gradients on the experiment's scale (~100 μm). |Φ_NTE|'s contribution to the inter-mass interaction is therefore approximately constant, contributing to the masses' rest energies but not to a spatially-varying potential between them. The dominant inter-mass channel is the graviton-mediated Newtonian potential.

The interesting question: does |Φ_NTE| contribute *any* additional inter-mass coupling beyond the graviton? In principle, two massive particles each perturb |Φ_NTE| locally (through their stress-energy sourcing); these perturbations could couple between the particles. But at the BMV scale (~10⁻¹⁴ kg, ~100 μm, ~seconds), the |Φ_NTE| coherence length and the strength of |Φ_NTE| sourcing by laboratory masses are both negligible. The |Φ_NTE| contribution to BMV entanglement is structurally present but quantitatively negligible.

**Verdict on A3: compatible; |Φ_NTE| adds a negligible correction at BMV scale; graviton mediation dominates.**

### A4 (r ≈ 0.003 — existing graviton-sector commitment)

NTE's r prediction commits it to a quantum graviton sector with primordial gravitational-wave content at calculable amplitude. Any framework with this commitment must accept graviton-mediated quantum effects, including BMV entanglement. The two predictions (r ≈ 0.003 from CMB; BMV-positive from tabletop) are *consistent commitments of the same underlying NTE graviton sector*.

**Verdict on A4: structurally consistent; r prediction and BMV prediction belong to the same graviton-sector commitment.**

### A5 (N fundamental, t emergent)

The BMV experiment operates at lab scales far above any |Φ_NTE| coherence boundary. The emergent t is operationally exact. Quantum mechanics on the emergent (3+1)-d surface includes gravitational entanglement just as in textbook QFT. No friction at this axis.

**Verdict on A5: compatible.**

### A6 (Surface ontology)

Gravitational interactions and the masses participating in BMV live on the emergent (3+1)-d S³ × S¹ leaf. No bulk degrees of freedom are required for BMV — the spectral-action graviton lives on the surface. Clean.

**Verdict on A6: compatible.**

## §6 — Honest scope

What this filter pass does *not* establish:

- It does **not** derive the BMV entanglement magnitude from NTE first principles. The claim is that NTE's graviton sector (Connes-emergent) reproduces the standard low-energy graviton-matter coupling, hence reproduces standard BMV predictions, to within negligible corrections.
- The |Φ_NTE| contribution to BMV inter-mass coupling has **not been explicitly calculated**. The claim that it's negligible is dimensional-analysis-based, not derived. A proper derivation would compute the |Φ_NTE| field perturbation sourced by lab-scale masses and the resulting inter-mass coupling channel.
- The connection between NTE's spectral-action graviton and the "non-locally-tomographic mediator" class of Vidal et al. 2025 has **not been formalised**. Conceptually NTE sits in this class (or possibly a related one), but the exact structural correspondence is open theoretical work.
- NTE's r ≈ 0.003 CMB-tensor prediction depends on spectral-action calculations (Chamseddine-Connes 2010; Marcolli-Pierpaoli-Teh 2010) that are themselves not fully closed for NTE's specific G₂-octonionic spectral triple. The corpus has Boyle-Farnsworth 2013 as the closest reference; the literal NTE calculation is partially open (per `Exploration_Graviton_vs_PhiNTE_v2.md` derivation queue).

What this filter pass *does* establish:

- BMV-positive outcome is **structurally consistent** with NTE's spectral-action-emergent gravity sector. The graviton tensor mode mediates entanglement at the BMV scale at standard rate.
- BMV-negative outcome (if observed under controlled decoherence) would create **real tension** for NTE, since it would imply NTE's graviton sector is "more classical than expected" — inconsistent with the corpus's r-prediction commitment.
- The recent theoretical literature (Howl-Aziz; Vidal et al.) has weakened the BMV inferential link in NTE's favour: the BMV experiment is no longer interpretable as "GIE ⇒ textbook quantum gravity," opening explicit room for NTE-style emergent-gravity theories that produce GIE without requiring textbook graviton-quantisation foundations.
