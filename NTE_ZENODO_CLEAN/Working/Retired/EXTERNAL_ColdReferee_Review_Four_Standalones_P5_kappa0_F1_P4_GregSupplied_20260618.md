## TL;DR
- The two "active leads" are the weakest under scrutiny, and the supposed safe outreach paper (F1) is the most damaged. F1's central uniqueness claim is provably FALSE as stated (the flat 4-torus T⁴ and the family N³×S¹ with N³ flat/hyperbolic are explicit counterexamples), so F1 is NOT re-elevatable to "prime candidate" without being rewritten into an already-known classical result. The κ₀/Hopf and P5 solar-neutrino papers rest on numerical coincidences dressed as derivations and would face major-revision-to-reject at serious journals.
- The strongest standalone on its own merits is the θ₂₃ (P4) paper — but only because its physics (Z₂ → θ₂₃ = π/4) is correct, which is exactly why it is also the most "scooped." μ–τ interchange symmetry giving maximal atmospheric mixing has been standard model-building since Harrison, Perkins & Scott (Phys. Lett. B 530 (2002) 167) and Mohapatra et al., so P4 is correct-but-not-novel.
- Across all four, the single fatal referee objection recurs: a true/known mathematical fact (dim adj SU(2) = 3; Z₂ ⇒ maximal mixing; S³×S¹ is conformally flat; m₁≈0 ⇒ m₂≈√Δm²₂₁) is re-presented as a novel derivation of physics, while the genuinely new step — the bridge to the physical coupling or mass — is either numerology or quietly assumed.

## P5 — Solar Neutrino mass ("Zero-Parameter Solar Neutrino Mass from a Yukawa Duality")
- The headline number is a retrodiction, not a zero-parameter prediction. m_ν2 ≈ 0.0086 eV ≈ 0.993×√(Δm²_solar) is essentially the textbook normal-ordering statement that for m₁≈0, m₂ ≈ √(Δm²₂₁). NuFIT 6.0 (Esteban et al., JHEP 12 (2024) 216) gives Δm²₂₁ = 7.49×10⁻⁵ eV²; JUNO first result (arXiv:2511.14593, Nature) reports (7.50 ± 0.12)×10⁻⁵ eV²; either gives √(Δm²₂₁) ≈ 0.00866 eV. Numerically forced once m₁=0 assumed; not novel.
- The "0.993×" factor is a tell, not a triumph. A genuine zero-parameter derivation gives exactly √(Δm²₂₁) in the m₁→0 limit; the ~0.7% offset shows Y_ν2 = (8π)^(−1/3) ≈ 0.232 is reverse-engineered to land near data.
- Scoop: "lightest neutrino massless ⇒ m₂ = √Δm²_sol" is decades old; geometric/number-theoretic Yukawa Ansätze are a crowded field. (8π)^(−1/3) as a specific Yukawa is not established, but a hep-ph referee reads it as numerology absent a Lagrangian.
- The honest ~1.49× atmospheric gap is the paper's most credible feature — but it is a self-admission that the framework does not predict the atmospheric leg, undercutting "structural."

## κ₀/Hopf ("3/√(4π) from monopole projection on the unit Hopf bundle")
- The two "anchors" are a number-theoretic identity and a numerical coincidence. "dim adj SU(2) = 3" is trivially true; √(4π) is the standard spherical-harmonic normalization (Y₀₀ = 1/√(4π)), in every Gaunt-coefficient / monopole-harmonic integral. Combining to 3/√(4π) ≈ 0.846 is dimensional/structural bookkeeping, not a derivation of a physical coupling.
- Sonner–Tong invoked far outside scope. The cited result (arXiv:0809.3783, PRL 102:191801, 2009) shows a Berry connection on a CP¹ target is the path-ordered exponential of the smooth SU(2) 't Hooft–Polyakov monopole; it says nothing about a coupling 3/√(4π). Using it as an "independent anchor" is an overreach a math-phys referee flags immediately.
- No prior appearance of 3/√(4π) as a coupling found — not scooped, but also no community context establishing it as meaningful. Monopole-harmonic machinery (Wu–Yang harmonics, Gaunt integrals, spin-weighted harmonics, arXiv:1403.0480) is entirely standard.
- Strongest objection: a category error — a normalization constant is not a coupling.

## F1 — trace-anomaly / S³×S¹ uniqueness (the re-elevation question)
- VERDICT: NOT re-elevatable. The uniqueness claim is false as literally stated. "S³×S¹ is the unique Weyl-flat, Euler-zero closed product 4-manifold" has explicit counterexamples: (a) the flat 4-torus T⁴ (flat ⇒ conformally flat, χ=0); (b) the infinite family N³×S¹ where N³ is any closed flat (Bieberbach) or hyperbolic 3-manifold — closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds are conformally flat (Gromov–Lawson–Thurston, Publ. Math. IHÉS 68 (1988) 27–45; cf. Akbulut–Kalafat arXiv:0807.0837), and χ(N³×S¹) = 0.
- The true underlying statement is a classical classification (J. Lafontaine, Conformal Geometry from the Riemannian Viewpoint, Aspects of Math. E12, Vieweg, 1988): a Riemannian product of dim ≥3 is conformally flat iff one factor is 1-dimensional with the other of constant sectional curvature, OR both factors have constant sectional curvature of equal magnitude and opposite sign. Restated in arXiv:2309.00466 (Prop. 2.2), arXiv:1905.09040 (Prop. 2.3), arXiv:1502.05227 (Lemma A.1). The "new theorem" is a corollary of a 35-year-old result.
- Uniqueness recoverable ONLY with extra hypotheses — positive scalar curvature forces the 3-factor to be a spherical space form S³/Γ, and demanding a simply-connected/round factor then isolates S³×S¹. Even then the rigorous literature (Chang–Gursky–Yang, Publ. Math. IHÉS 98 (2003) 105–143; Gursky, Math. Ann. 318 (2000) 417; Catino arXiv:1408.0902) phrases it as "conformal to / isometrically covered by S³×S¹," up to conformal change and finite covers, as one of several model spaces (alongside ℂP², S⁴, Derdziński metrics).
- The F₁=0 identity is plausible but unremarkable: on a conformally flat manifold the type-B (Weyl²) anomaly vanishes pointwise and the integrated type-A anomaly ∝ a·χ; since χ(S³×S¹)=0, the anomaly integral vanishes trivially. Standard heat-kernel bookkeeping, not new.

## P4 — θ₂₃ ("θ₂₃ = π/4 from a Z₂ symmetry on the chirality-superposed Majorana sector")
- Physics correct but heavily scooped. Z₂ (μ↔τ) symmetry forcing |U_μ3| = |U_τ3| hence θ₂₃ = π/4 is among the most established results in neutrino model-building — standard since Harrison, Perkins & Scott PLB 530 (2002) 167 and Mohapatra et al. (~2002–2004). The "hidden Z₂" of any μ–τ symmetric matrix is textbook (arXiv:1112.3628; review arXiv:1207.1678).
- Current data make exact π/4 contestable. NuFIT 6.0 finds θ₂₃ still suffers octant ambiguity, best fit upper octant, first-octant allowed ~2.4σ; T2K (arXiv:2506.05889) prefers upper octant NO best fit sin²θ₂₃ = 0.559; NOvA has historically disfavored maximal (~2.6σ). A hard π/4 prediction is in mild tension and will be probed by JUNO and next T2K/NOvA/DUNE rounds.
- The "chirality-superposed Majorana sector" framing is the only potentially new element, but a referee will ask whether it adds anything beyond standard μ–τ.

## Details
- P5: hep-ph reviewers benchmark against the seesaw norm where Y_ν and M_R are not independently predictable (Casas–Ibarra). A coupling fixed as (8π)^(−1/3) with no Lagrangian, no renormalization-scale, no PMNS connection reads as ad hoc. JUNO 2025 reduces Δm²₂₁ uncertainty to ~1.55% and confirms a ~1.5σ solar/reactor difference — so "0.993×" is dataset-sensitive. Strongest objection: a one-parameter fit disguised as zero-parameter.
- κ₀: the Hopf-bundle / spin-weighted-harmonic literature is mature and produces no distinguished coupling 3/√(4π). "Topological protection via Y₀₀" conflates the s-wave normalization with a protected physical quantity. Category error.
- F1: the framing (Weyl-flat + Euler-zero ⇒ S³×S¹) inverts the field's logic: the interesting direction (Chang–Gursky–Yang) is rigidity-under-curvature-positivity, not a product classification. To salvage: (i) drop "product," (ii) add positive scalar curvature + simply-connected-factor, (iii) cite existing theorems — at which point no new theorem remains.
- P4: cleanest of four. To be more than a re-derivation it must show the "chirality-superposed" construction predicts something standard μ–τ does not (correlated δ_CP, specific octant, θ₁₃≠0 correction) and confront NuFIT 6.0 / T2K-2025 quantitatively.

## Recommendations
- Triage: Promote P4 (θ₂₃) to lead, conditional on a genuinely novel prediction beyond μ–τ + explicit NuFIT 6.0/T2K-2025 confrontation. Demote F1 from any "prime" status; it cannot go out as a uniqueness theorem.
- P5: Reframe honestly as "a structural rationale for m₁≈0 normal-ordering with m₂≈√Δm²₂₁," drop "zero-parameter," and either supply the Lagrangian yielding Y_ν2 = (8π)^(−1/3) or present it explicitly as a numerical observation. Escalation: a derivation yielding the atmospheric leg without the 1.49× gap.
- κ₀: Before submission, demonstrate 3/√(4π) appears as the coefficient of an actual observable (cross-section, decay rate, RG-matching), not a normalization. Remove the Sonner–Tong "anchor" unless a direct computational link is shown. Target a modest math-phys note, not a discovery claim.
- F1: Rewrite entirely as an expository note on conformal-anomaly vanishing on S³×S¹, explicitly citing Lafontaine and Chang–Gursky–Yang, framing S³×S¹ as the positive-scalar-curvature, simply-connected-factor representative — acknowledge priority. Do not submit a uniqueness claim.
- Benchmarks that change the verdict: (a) P4 — if JUNO/DUNE pin θ₂₃ exactly maximal, vindicated; if octant resolved away from 45°, falsified. (b) P5 — a tightened Δm²₂₁ moving data >1σ from the (8π)^(−1/3) coincidence falsifies it.
