## §1 — A4 Quantum time-of-arrival

### §1.1 The phenomenon

Aharonov & Bohm (1961) introduced a quantum time-of-arrival (TOA) operator by symmetric quantisation of the classical expression t = mx/p. The operator is maximally symmetric (not self-adjoint), generating a positive operator-valued measure (POVM) rather than a projection-valued one. Kijowski (1974) derived axiomatically a TOA probability distribution for free particles; the POVM/Kijowski framework has been the standard treatment for ~50 years.

Recent activity 2024–2026 is substantial. The TOA operator's connection to the tunnelling-time problem has been articulated (Galapon group; Magadan & Galapon 2025), with TOA-approach explanations of "zero tunnelling time" observed in attosecond experiments. Multi-particle interference in arrival time has been predicted (Ayatollah Rafsanjani et al. 2024 for atoms) and observed (Ono et al. 2024 for photons; Yu et al. 2025). Tumulka, Tilloy and others have developed Bohmian variants. A 2026 *Phil. Mag.* paper (Kijowski-style mathematical-probability solution, accepted Jan 2026, published online Feb 2026) reformulates the problem with new rigour.

### §1.2 Axioms touched

- **Connes-Rovelli thermal time (σ_t / KMS framework)** — Charter v1.3 §2.5 canonical adoption
- **N fundamental, t emergent** — TOA operator is the formal QM machinery for treating *t as an observable* rather than a parameter
- **Spectral-triple algebra structure** — TOA operator acts on the Hilbert space of the spectral triple

### §1.3 Verdict

**Compatible and structurally illuminating.**

The quantum TOA operator framework is exactly the QM formalism an emergent-t ontology needs to express t as an observable. In a framework where t is a fundamental parameter, the TOA "is" something definite — the value at which the wavefunction's support reaches a detector. In an emergent-t framework, t at the operational level is the modular flow σ_t of the cosmic KMS state, and the TOA operator becomes the natural object expressing "when, in the emergent t coordinate, did this transition happen?"

The Aharonov-Bohm TOA operator's non-self-adjointness — a "defect" historically — reads naturally under NTE as expressing that t-as-observable is not a textbook self-adjoint observable. Time is operationally meaningful (via the POVM), but it is structurally derived (via modular flow), not a fundamental operator on the Hilbert space.

**Structurally**: this is *the* formal framework NTE has been pointing toward via Connes-Rovelli. The TOA literature has done substantial spadework that NTE can directly absorb.

## §2 — A5 Wigner's friend extensions

### §2.1 The phenomenon

Wigner's 1961 thought experiment imagines an observer (the friend) inside a closed lab measuring a quantum system, while an outer observer (Wigner) describes the friend+lab as a single quantum system that has not yet collapsed. The two observers disagree on the moment of collapse.

Modern extensions: Frauchiger-Renner 2018 paradox showing that single-world unitary QM with observer-applies-Born-rule leads to inconsistency. Brukner 2018, Bong et al. 2020 *Nat. Phys.* — "no-go for observer-independent facts" under local-friendliness assumption; experimentally tested with photon-friends. Wiseman-Cavalcanti-Rieffel 2023 — "thoughtful local friendliness." Baumann-Brukner 2024 — memory + no-signalling. Polychronakos 2024 *Nat. Commun.* — observed-observers consistency. Wigner's friend with Qiskit IBM 2024 (educational demonstration). Zwirn et al. 2025 *arXiv* July 2025 (recent comprehensive reanalysis). *Phys. Rev. Research* 2025 — "Wigner's friend scenarios: on what to condition" (Wigner-friend bubbles, conditioning structure).

### §2.2 Axioms touched

- **Surface ontology / S³ × S¹** — observers and their lab environments occupy regions of the emergent leaf
- **KMS state / spectral-triple algebra** — observer-relative facts limited by the local algebra structure
- **Born rule from Hopf** (Companion 2, Companion 6) — observers apply the Born rule on their local algebras

### §2.3 Verdict

**Compatible.**

NTE's surface ontology gives a natural reading where observers and their reference frames occupy *neighbourhoods* of the spectral-triple algebra. The "observer-independent facts" no-go (Bong et al. 2020) under local-friendliness translates as: facts associated with measurement outcomes are localised in the observer's local algebra, and become global only via state-update propagation through the entire spectral-triple algebra.

NTE structurally favours the **"different bubbles"** reading (Wiseman-Cavalcanti-Rieffel; Phys. Rev. Research 2025): Wigner and his friend occupy different operational regions of the spectral-triple algebra, with different information available to each, and the question "what is the *one* definite outcome" is not well-posed because the algebra is large enough to host distinct sub-algebras with consistent but different observer-relative facts.

This is not a unique NTE prediction — it's a structural fit with one of the existing interpretive lines. But the fit is clean: NTE's spectral-triple machinery + surface ontology + KMS framework have *exactly* the structural ingredients (local algebras, modular flow, observer-relative states) that the Wigner's-friend literature has been articulating.

## §3 — A6 Delayed-choice / quantum eraser

### §3.1 The phenomenon

Wheeler's 1978 delayed-choice thought experiment: arrange the apparatus so the choice between "which-path measurement" and "which-path erasure" is made *after* the photon has already passed the slits. Quantum mechanics predicts the photon's behaviour still depends on the (later) choice. Jacques et al. 2007 *Science* — single-photon delayed-choice. Manning et al. 2015 — single-atom delayed-choice. Walborn et al. 2002 — quantum eraser. **Rauch et al. 2018** *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 121, 080403 — cosmic-scale delayed-choice with quasar light, settings determined by light from quasars billions of light-years away.

### §3.2 Axioms touched

- **N fundamental, t emergent** — "moment of decision" ontology
- **Born rule from Hopf** — standard QM amplitude/probability machinery
- **Surface ontology** — measurement on the emergent (3+1)-d leaf

### §3.3 Verdict

**Compatible.**

Delayed-choice phenomena use the same Born-rule machinery as the temporal double-slit (the subject of `Working/Paper_Popular_Two_Slits_in_Time_v0_1_Scratch_20260527.md`). The "moment of decision" — when does the photon "decide" which path? — is *operational*, not ontological. In the standard QM reading, the photon doesn't decide anything; the interference pattern is determined by the experimentally-realised set of paths through the apparatus, regardless of when the "decision" is made.

In an emergent-t framework, the question becomes even more natural: there is no fundamental "moment" at which a quantum amplitude is bound to a specific outcome. The operational t is the modular flow σ_t of the emergent KMS state; the photon's wave function evolves under this t throughout the apparatus; the Born rule is applied at detection. Delayed-choice settings affect which detection structure is realised, not which decision the photon makes.

The Rauch et al. 2018 cosmic-scale variant uses quasar light from billions of years ago to set the apparatus. This nicely confirms that the "moment of decision" framing has no physical content — the experiment's outcome is determined by the geometric structure of the apparatus, including settings causally distant from the photon's path.

## §4 — B3 PBR theorem tests

## §5 — B6 Kochen-Specker contextuality

### §5.1 The phenomenon

Kochen-Specker theorem (1967): in Hilbert spaces of dimension ≥ 3, there is no consistent way to assign definite values to all observables simultaneously while preserving functional relationships. Quantum measurement outcomes depend on the *context* (which compatible set of observables is measured together), not just on the observable being measured. Cabello group and others have developed experimental tests; many confirmations through 2024.

### §5.2 Axioms touched

- **Spectral-triple algebra structure** — commutative subalgebras are "contexts"
- **Surface ontology** — measurement on the local algebra
- **Adjacent to A5 Wigner's friend** — both are observer-/context-relativity results

### §5.3 Verdict

**Compatible.**

NTE's algebraic framework treats contextuality as a structural feature: commutative subalgebras of the spectral-triple algebra correspond to "contexts" (compatible sets of observables), and KS contextuality follows from the non-commutativity of the full algebra. The state ω assigns expectation values to all observables, but the *outcome distributions* depend on which commutative subalgebra is being measured.

This is standard algebraic-QM territory. NTE inherits it directly through the spectral-triple machinery. No tension; no unique NTE content; clean pass.

## §6 — C3 Casimir effect / vacuum energy

### §6.1 The phenomenon

Casimir 1948 predicted that two parallel conducting plates in vacuum experience an attractive force from vacuum-fluctuation modes excluded between the plates. Lamoreaux 1997 — first high-precision measurement. Many subsequent measurements with various geometries through to current high-precision torsion-balance and AFM-cantilever experiments.

### §6.2 Axioms touched

- **|Φ_NTE| / Λ closure** (Companion 13 / Chapter 13) — vacuum-energy bookkeeping
- **Spectral action** — produces a cosmological term at leading order

### §6.3 Verdict

**Compatible.**

The Casimir energy is the *finite difference* in zero-point energy between two configurations (with vs. without plates). It is real, measurable, and consistent with standard QFT vacuum-energy calculations. NTE does not modify standard QFT at lab scales; the Casimir energy in finite cavities is exactly as predicted by standard QFT.

The deeper question — how Casimir energy relates to the cosmological constant Λ — is the well-known "vacuum catastrophe" puzzle, which Companion 13 / Chapter 13 addresses through the NTE-specific Λ closure (Φ_NTE-driven trajectory + locking mechanism, distinct from naïve vacuum-energy summation). The Casimir effect at lab scales coexists with the corpus Λ closure: the finite-difference Casimir energy is a perfectly legitimate QFT observable that doesn't propagate to a contribution to the cosmological constant under NTE's framework.

**Structurally illuminating mini-point**: the standard "vacuum catastrophe" arises from naïvely summing zero-point energies and identifying the sum with Λ. NTE's Λ-closure mechanism specifies that this naïve identification is wrong — Λ is determined by the corpus's specific dynamical structure, not by vacuum-mode summation. The Casimir effect's continued empirical success without any concomitant Λ catastrophe is consistent with this NTE reading.

## §7 — C4 Unruh effect / BEC analogues

The Unruh effect has not been directly observed (the temperature is extremely small for accessible accelerations). BEC analogue black-hole experiments (Steinhauer 2016 *Nat.
