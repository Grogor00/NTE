## Abstract

The cosmological constant problem has two layers. The **old** problem asks why empty space is not deafeningly loud — why the quantum vacuum energy does not gravitate at the Planck-scale level a naive field-theory estimate predicts (an overestimate of order 10¹²⁰). The **new** problem asks why the residual, after any suppression, sits near 10⁻¹²² in Planck units rather than anywhere else.

NTE addresses the **old** problem structurally. In its boundary-only ontology the physical degrees of freedom live on the expanding S³ wavefront, not in a four-volume interior; computing a *volume*-scaling vacuum energy for a system whose degrees of freedom are *surface* ones is a category error, and it is the source of the catastrophic estimate. The same content appears geometrically as a unimodular constraint: in the NTE spectral triple the metric variations that preserve the kinematical volume project out the leading extensive cosmological term in the spectral action, so the vacuum energy of that mode never gravitates — equivalently, the trace-free (unimodular) form of the Einstein equations in which a constant vacuum density does not source curvature. Empty space is quiet because the geometry forbids the loud term, not because large terms finely cancel.

This structural mechanism **stands and is independent of the subleading spectral data**. In particular it does not rest on the fourth Seeley–DeWitt coefficient a₄, which **vanishes** on the canonical S³×S¹ substrate (Chamseddine–Connes 2008). NTE does **not** derive the specific observed value of Λ: a previously explored a₄-dependent numerical estimate is **withdrawn**, because with a₄ = 0 the chain that produced it collapses. The claim of this paper is therefore the structural one — the suppression of the catastrophe — and not a prediction of the number.

## 1. The two cosmological constant problems

Every quantum field contributes zero-point energy to the vacuum. Summing modes to the Planck scale gives a vacuum energy density of order M_Pl⁴ — roughly 10¹²⁰ times the observed cosmological value. This is not a small discrepancy; it is the largest failure of an order-of-magnitude estimate in physics, and lowering the cutoff does not rescue it (even an electroweak cutoff leaves the estimate dozens of orders of magnitude too large). The failure is structural: standard field theory and standard general relativity treat vacuum energy in incompatible ways once a cosmological constant couples to total volume. Something in the geometric setting must forbid the loud version from gravitating.

That is the **old** problem. The **new** problem is separate and sharper: granting some mechanism that removes the catastrophe, why does the residual take the particular tiny value observed, rather than zero or anything else? The two are worth keeping apart, because NTE answers the first structurally and is explicit that it does not, at present, answer the second.

## 2. The mechanism: unimodular suppression on a boundary-only geometry

**The boundary-only reading.** NTE's degrees of freedom are carried on the closed S³ wavefront — the surface, not a bulk interior. A vacuum energy that scales with four-volume is then the wrong object: it assigns extensive energy to an arena the framework does not treat as physical. The catastrophic 10¹²⁰ is the numerical signature of that mismatch — a volume-scaling estimate applied to a surface-only system.

**The unimodular form.** The same content has a clean geometric statement. Chamseddine and Connes (2008) showed that spectral-triple gravity carries a built-in unimodular constraint: variations that preserve the total kinematical volume project out the leading cosmological term in the spectral-action expansion. NTE inherits this automatically from its Hopf-S³ × S¹ × A_F spectral triple. The leading extensive mode — the one that would have scaled with comoving volume and gravitated catastrophically — is removed by the constraint. This is equivalent to working with the trace-free (unimodular) Einstein equations, in which a spatially constant vacuum energy density does not appear as a source: the vacuum does not gravitate. The loud version is forbidden at the level of the geometry, before any cancellation is invoked.

**Independence from a₄.** The suppression just described acts on the *extensive* (a₀-level) contribution. It is independent of the fourth-order spectral data. On the canonical NTE substrate S³ × S¹ the fourth Seeley–DeWitt coefficient vanishes, a₄ = 0 (Chamseddine–Connes 2008; this follows because the Dirac trace-anomaly integrand −18 C² + 11 E₄ is pointwise zero there, both the Weyl and Euler densities vanishing on the round-S³ × flat-S¹ product). The structural mechanism neither requires nor is affected by this; it would stand whatever the subleading coefficients were.

## 3. What is claimed, and what is not

The honest scope of this paper is narrow and deliberate.

**What stands (structural).** The old problem is dissolved. A boundary-only geometry under a unimodular constraint forbids the catastrophic vacuum term; empty space is structurally quiet, independent of a₄ and of any fine cancellation. This is the result.

**What is not claimed (the number).** NTE does not here derive the specific observed magnitude of Λ. An earlier exploration estimated a value through a chain that depended on a nonzero a₄ (a residual spectral amplitude renormalised over a lock-scale volume); with a₄ = 0 that chain yields nothing, and the numerical estimate is **withdrawn**. It is not presented as a prediction, and no quantity in this paper is adjusted to match the observed Λ. The residual is *intrinsically* small — set by the geometry forbidding large terms rather than by tuning — but "intrinsically small" is not yet "this specific value," and the framework does not claim to bridge that gap.

This is the same discipline applied elsewhere in the corpus: a result that cannot be derived without fitting is not retained as a suggestive number. The structural mechanism earns its place; the number does not, and is withdrawn rather than defended.

## 4. Status and falsifiability

The structural claim is not vacuous. It commits NTE to a vacuum that does not gravitate through the extensive mode — a definite stance within the unimodular family of resolutions, tied to the boundary-only ontology rather than added as an independent assumption. It would be in tension with any demonstration that the NTE spectral triple fails to carry the unimodular projection, or that the boundary-only ontology requires a gravitating extensive vacuum term after all.

What the paper does **not** offer is a falsifiable numerical prediction for Λ; that would require deriving the residual's magnitude from the geometry without observational input, which remains open. Should such a derivation be found, the new problem would come into scope and the value would become a forward test. Until then the contribution is the structural one: the universe's vacuum is quiet at rest because its geometry forbids the loud term — one boundary, a unimodular constraint, no fine cancellation, and no claim on the number.
