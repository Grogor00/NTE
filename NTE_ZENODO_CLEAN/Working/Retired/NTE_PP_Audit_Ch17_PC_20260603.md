## Issue C17.1 — §5 "**Was** stays in the geometry" (RETIRED-PHRASING EQUIVALENT — alignment fix)

§5 close, second-to-last sentence:

> The shape of time, in this reading, is the shape of expansion. Massive matter accumulates proper time; the photon does not; the neutrino almost does not. **Was** stays in the geometry; **next** is still open — as the book argued from the first chapter on.

The phrase **"Was stays in the geometry"** carries the same structural claim as "stays happened" and "geometry remembers" — that the past is preserved/retained in the geometry. Per Greg's 2026-06-03 gate-setting directive, these framings are retired because they contradict the "only now exists" stance the broader alignment audit will bring the book into. The Ch 4 A10 patch (Batch 1) addressed the explicit "stays happened / geometry remembers" instance in Ch 4's close; the Ch 17 instance here is the same idea phrased differently and needs the same alignment.

**Recommended patch C17.1 (alignment-safe; goes into Batch 2 handoff):**

**FIND:**

> The shape of time, in this reading, is the shape of expansion. Massive matter accumulates proper time; the photon does not; the neutrino almost does not. **Was** stays in the geometry; **next** is still open — as the book argued from the first chapter on.

**REPLACE WITH:**

> The shape of time, in this reading, is the shape of expansion. Massive matter accumulates proper time; the photon does not; the neutrino almost does not. **Was** is the record of expansion already run; **next** is still open — as the book argued from the first chapter on.

PC view: preserves the *was/next* rhythm (which the book has used since Ch 4's epigraph, Greg KEEP-register), removes the retained-in-the-geometry framing, and aligns with the Ch 4 A10 replacement ("The geometry of expansion is the record of what has occurred"). Same alignment principle applied consistently across both chapters.

Alternative phrasings if Greg prefers:
- "**Was** is what the expansion has traced; **next** is what it has not yet generated"
- "**Was** is the trace of expansion already run; **next** is what remains to unfold"

PC preference: "record of expansion already run" — most precise structurally and closest in tone to the Ch 4 patch.

**Add to Batch 2 patch handoff.**
