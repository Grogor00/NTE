### Abstract

A photon accumulates zero proper time between emission and absorption ($ds^2 = 0$) and has zero Higgs coupling; every massive particle accumulates nonzero proper time and has nonzero Higgs coupling. Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) takes this correlation as structural and adopts the proportionality relation $H(N) = \beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}|\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}(N)|^2$ as a load-bearing constraint — not a causal claim that the field "generates" time. The boundary expansion field $\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ is a scalar on the $S^3$ boundary, distinct from the Standard Model Higgs.

The universe is not set in a pre-existing temporal background. The $S^3$ boundary of a 4-dimensional hypersphere expands through e-folds $N = \ln a$, parameterised by $N$ alone — a geometric quantity requiring no clock. Proper time is emergent: for Yukawa-coupled entities, once temporal locking completes, $t(N) = \int dN / (\beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}|\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}|^2)$. Three coupling regimes follow — null-time (zero Higgs coupling: photons, gluons), minimal (neutrinos, unresolved across the Hopf fibre), and strong (massive matter) — all post-temporal.

The cosmological coupling $\beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ is treated as a **fundamental input constant of our universe, not derived**; once it is fixed, $H_0 = \beta_{\mathrm{NTE}} v_{\mathrm{EW}}^2 \approx 67.6$ km/s/Mpc follows by construction, not as an independent prediction. (A previously circulated closed-form "characterisation" of $\beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ in terms of measured parameters is **withdrawn**: assembling a constant to match an observed value, with a residual numerical coincidence, is number-fitting, which this framework does not do.)

The framework's genuine, honestly-scoped results are: a **dark-matter-to-baryon ratio** $\Omega_{\mathrm{DM}}/\Omega_b = \kappa_0/(1-\kappa_0) \approx 5.51$ from a cross-sector coupling fixed point (Planck: $5.38 \pm 0.15$); a **maximal CP phase** $\delta_J = \pi/2$ from the bilateral (CPN) geometry; a **baryon asymmetry** consistent with a geometric heavy-neutrino scale; and **maximal atmospheric mixing** $\theta_{23} = \pi/4$ (inherited from the established $\mu$–$\tau$ symmetry literature, not claimed). The octonionic gauge group and three-generation species count **adopt the established division-algebra programme** (Günaydin–Gürsey; Furey; Todorov–Dubois-Violette) and are not presented as new. The scalar spectral index $n_s$ and tensor-to-scalar ratio $r$ are **conditional** on an open kinetic normalisation $K(\Phi)$ and are not claimed as fixed predictions. Standard Big-Bang nucleosynthesis is recovered (not NTE-distinguishing).

**Positioning.** NTE belongs to the family of approaches in which time and spacetime are emergent and the Standard Model arises from division-algebra / noncommutative-geometric structure (Page–Wootters; Rovelli; Connes–Rovelli; Chamseddine–Connes; the octonionic programme above; and, as the closest architectural relative, T. P. Singh's octonionic theory). NTE is a **distinct, leaner implementation** within that family — its own commitments are the fundamental count coordinate $N$ with $t = i\,\Delta N$, the $S^3 \times S^1$ Hopf substrate, and the bilateral CPN-conjugate cosmology — and it adheres to an explicit discipline of **no number-fitting**: every quantity is structural or honestly scoped as conditional, cross-check, or open.
