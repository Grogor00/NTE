## 1. The thesis (one paragraph)

Susskind's paper is, at root, about the **status of time**: whether time-reversal T is a real symmetry, whether it is better read as a gauge symmetry (Harlow–Numasawa), and whether it breaks spontaneously in de Sitter. NTE's central commitment is that **time t is not fundamental** — it emerges, via the Hopf construction, from the e-fold coordinate N once the surface locks (N ≥ N_lock). The proposed point of contact is therefore at the level of *what time is*, not at the level of shared projection bookkeeping. Specifically: **the discrete operation that does the structural work in NTE is not a time-reversal at all. It is a CP-type operation (corpus: CPN, the Pin⁻-orbifold operation) under which the emergent time-direction Δt is preserved in both sectors.** Because the arrow is not reversed, the operator is **unitary, not antiunitary** — which is exactly why NTE's involution was never a candidate to mirror Susskind's antiunitary T, and should never have been framed as one. What Susskind grades as **T-odd** (his Π₋), NTE grades as **chirality-odd**. The proposal to put to Susskind is that **in an emergent-time setting the chirality grading is the more fundamental one, and T-odd structure is what the chirality grading looks like after time has emerged.**

- **Claim:** Susskind's question (is T real / gauge / broken) and NTE's answer (t is emergent from N) are the *same question viewed from two sides*. His work asks what time's symmetry status is; NTE asserts time has no fundamental status to break.

### Move 2 — CPN, not CPT: the operation preserves the arrow

- **Claim:** Where Susskind breaks T, the operative NTE discrete symmetry is CP-type (CPN). Under CPN, Δt is **same-direction in both sectors**; the operation is **unitary**. This dissolves Scratch blocker #2 by reframing: NTE has no T-breaking story to mirror, so the antiunitary/unitary mismatch is not a defect — it is the correct statement that we are talking about a *different operation*.
- **CRITICAL framing — CPT is emergent, never "CPT fails":** State that the **premises of the CPT theorem (a fixed Lorentzian time) are not in force at N < N_lock**. In the pre-temporal epoch the operative discrete symmetry is CP-type (CPN); ordinary CPT is **recovered as a post-locking, emergent statement**. Frame as *"CPT is downstream of time-emergence."* **Never** phrase as a violation of CPT — that reads as crank to an expert and is not what the corpus claims.
