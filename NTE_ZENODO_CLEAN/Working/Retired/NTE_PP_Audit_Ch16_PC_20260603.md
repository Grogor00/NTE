## What works in Ch 16

**Engage floor:** "We went looking for tests that should have broken the picture. They didn't." — strong direct opener.

**Honest scope (exemplary throughout):** Multiple explicit moments:

1. §1 opener: "That is not the same sentence as: the framework predicted all of them. It did not... A theory does not deserve a medal for surviving the double slit, Bell tests, or Berry phase. It deserves, at most, permission to keep speaking."

2. Case A close: "That is a pass, but a retrospective one. The experiment was not waiting for NTE. NTE was waiting to be judged by it."

3. Case B: "Again, this is not a new prediction by NTE. These effects are established quantum physics. The framework's test is whether it can house them without special pleading."

4. Case C close: "Bell and Hardy do not prove NTE. They do something more modest and more necessary: they fail to kill it at the point where many realist pictures die."

5. Case D scope limit: "There is a scope limit. These experiments do not test NTE's gauge-birth scale. They do not reach the regime where time itself fails to have post-temporal meaning."

6. Case E: "That does not make time crystals a prediction of NTE. They are not. The chapter's discipline forbids pretending otherwise. They are a compatibility test with a strong pedagogical echo."

7. Case F (BMV): "That sentence should be read carefully. It is not a calculation of the experimental signal strength in a particular apparatus... Nor is the framework claiming that standard quantum gravity cannot also expect a positive result. Many physicists already regard a positive BMV result as the natural expectation if gravity is quantum. NTE's commitment is not unique in sign. Its importance lies in coupling."

8. §Toward the Score: "Retrospective compatibility is necessary, but it is not sufficient. The history of physics is full of beautiful pictures that could explain yesterday and predict nothing tomorrow."

This is the chapter where the framework's honest-scope discipline is most consistently visible.

**Fleeting-glimpse:** Each case provides a concrete fleeting-glimpse:

**Schema-attachment:** Greg KEEP-register epigraph (*Trying Every Way to Be Wrong* + *"The surprise of being right is sweetest when you have already exhausted every honest way to be wrong."*) carries the chapter's stance forward.

**Earned-term defer:** Spinors, holonomy, gauge potentials, Hardy's paradox, Floquet driving, KMS condition, BMV protocol — all introduced with brief lay context before being named.

**Density:** No redundant restatements found. Each case follows a consistent structure (experiment → pressure point → framework response → scope note) without restating across cases.

**Forward-momentum:** Six cases sequentially build the argument: retrospective passes (A-E) → forward commitment (F) → close (§Toward the Score).

**Engagement:** "Permission to keep speaking" — memorable phrase. "More than a strike. It would say the framework has mistaken the shape of the graviton-mediated channel" — clear stake on BMV. "The mood, then, is not triumph. It is unease of the productive kind" — earned register.

**Coupling of BMV to Ch 15 r prediction:** The two-channel framing — "Chapter 15 gave the tensor-to-scalar ratio as one of the coming cosmological tests... That prediction and the BMV expectation are not independent ornaments. They probe the same broad graviton-mediated sector from two wildly different directions" — is the structural-unification discipline the framework wants throughout. The four-quadrant scenario analysis (r-pass + BMV-pass / r-fail + BMV-pass / r-pass + BMV-fail / r-fail + BMV-fail) is exemplary honest-scope.
