**OLD:**
> The universe is not set in a pre-existing temporal background. The $S^3$ boundary of a 4-dimensional hypersphere expands through e-folds $N = \ln a$, parameterised by $N$ alone — a geometric quantity requiring no clock.

**NEW:**
> The universe is not set in a pre-existing temporal background. *Rationale:* removes the contradiction-inviting reading without changing physics; aligns the abstract with §2.1 ("only the increment $dN$") and §2.1.2 ($t = i\,\Delta N$, seam) already in the body. Stands regardless of how the CPN work lands.
