## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent, not fundamental. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal supervenience base ("mosaic") whose parts are *freely recombinable* (this is what guarantees "no necessary connections"). The classic base was spatiotemporal (Lewis). A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory (causal-set order, spin-network adjacency, Hilbert/configuration space, or entanglement) is **"nomically charged"**: the theory constrains which recombinations are allowed, so the base is not modally innocent. The Humean must either reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — laws/dispositions acting on an initial state to produce the total state. That is a temporal notion (initial-value formulation, Cauchy evolution, temporal asymmetry). Strip out fundamental time and "production" becomes elusive; their sketched fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** (kinematics = metaphysical possibility/glue; dynamics = nomic), which they concede may not survive in QG and needs a non-temporal asymmetry.

Conclusion: survival of all three into the QG era is genuinely in question.

## §2. Where NTE lands — and the one real internal finding

### §2.1 NTE as concrete instance
Unlike causal sets / LQG (pre-spacetime combinatorial structures), NTE has an explicit, *temporally-scoped* ontology: N (e-folds) fundamental and pre-temporal; t emergent at gauge birth; pre-temporal epoch undifferentiated (octonionic). So NTE supplies a definite answer to "what is the non-spatiotemporal base?" and a definite emergence event. It is the kind of concrete case the philosophers say is needed but mostly lack.

### §2.2 The load-bearing finding — constraint vs production (CORPUS ACTION)
NTE has *already taken a side* on their central question, but does so **inconsistently across carriers**:
- **Constraint/supervenience reading** (Part 1 abstract / §2.3.1): H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² is "the load-bearing mathematical relation — *not* a derived causal claim that |Φ_NTE| 'generates' time"; t(N) = ∫dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²) is "a proportionality definition, not a separate dynamical law."
- **Production reading** (Popular Full Edition): |Φ_NTE| "governs the rate at which proper time accumulates"; "the universe's expansion *produces* time."

In Lam–Wüthrich's taxonomy these are *different metaphysical commitments*: the first is Humean/best-systems (a kinematic constraint that the boundary's two properties jointly satisfy); the second is dispositionalist/primitivist (a productive power). NTE should **choose deliberately**, or hold both as explicitly-labelled complementary registers (constraint = fundamental statement; production = post-temporal effective/heuristic gloss). Recommended: lead with the **constraint** reading as canonical (it is the safer, more defensible one and matches Part 1), and mark production language as effective/pedagogical. **This is an AC corpus diff** (Part 1 §2.1/§2.3.1 footnote + a Popular/book wording pass), routed to PC for the lay carriers.

### §2.3 Two places NTE is positively advantaged (worth claiming)
1. **Their "non-temporal nomic production" gap.** They say a productive account needs a non-temporal ordering/asymmetry and can't guarantee one in QG. NTE *has* candidates: N is an intrinsic ordering parameter that is not itself temporal, and gauge birth supplies a privileged direction. If NTE ever wants the production reading, N + gauge birth is exactly the non-temporal asymmetry the gap calls for.
2. **Entanglement glue.** Their most promising Humean "world-making relation" is entanglement (Jaksland). NTE's line already reads entanglement as the projection of zero-proper-time objects via Hopf structure (Companion 2). NTE can present its entanglement-geometry as a concrete realisation of entanglement-as-glue.

### §2.4 Honest caution
This is metaphysics of laws; NTE is empirical physics. Do not claim NTE "solves" the dilemma or the problem of time. The defensible claim: NTE is a concrete test case that *sharpens* the question and *takes a position*, and (per §3) has a strong answer to the empirical-coherence objection it will inevitably face from this community.

## §3. Research lines worth engaging (curated — not a literature dump)

**Tier 1 — engage directly (positioning + a known objection NTE must answer):**
1. **Huggett & Wüthrich, *Out of Nowhere: The Emergence of Spacetime in Theories of Quantum Gravity* (OUP 2025).** The canonical monograph; central positive thesis is **spacetime functionalism** ("something non-spatiotemporal appears as spacetime by playing the spacetime role"). This is the reference frame to position NTE within. NTE's emergent t — valid locally post-locking "the same way Newtonian gravity is valid locally even though GR is deeper" — *is already a functionalist move*; NTE should name it as such.
2. **Empirical-incoherence problem** — Huggett & Wüthrich, "Emergent spacetime and empirical (in)coherence" (2013, arXiv:1206.6290); Lam & Wüthrich, "Spacetime is as spacetime does" (Stud. Hist. Phil. Mod. Phys. 64, 2018; arXiv:1803.04374). **The objection NTE will face:** a theory whose fundamental level is non-spatiotemporal seems to undermine its own empirical support, since all evidence is local in spacetime. **NTE's answer is strong and should be stated explicitly:** all observation occurs in the post-gauge-birth *temporal* epoch; emergent t functionally plays the time-role (clock readings, proper-time accumulation dt = dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²), causal ordering); only the *functionally relevant* features need recovering (their own sufficiency criterion), and NTE recovers them. NTE passes by construction — pre-empt the objection rather than wait for it.
3. **Huggett & Wüthrich, "The (A)temporal Emergence of Spacetime" (arXiv:1710.11444).** Specifically on emergence of *time*, and flags the residual "gap": a fundamentally non-temporal region has no observers, so is the theory empirically idle about it? **Bears directly on NTE's pre-temporal epoch (N < N_lock).** NTE's reply: the pre-temporal structure is not idle — it is *inferentially anchored* by its observable post-gauge-birth consequences (n_s = 0.9685, the SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) derivation, κ). Worth stating to close the gap pre-emptively.

**Tier 2 — physics line that strengthens NTE's actual construction:**
4. **Page–Wootters relational time** (time as conditioning on an internal clock within a globally static state) and the **Connes–Rovelli thermal time hypothesis** (time as the modular flow of a KMS state). These are the established *physics* routes to "time from a timeless/constraint structure." NTE's t = ∫dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²) — emergent time from the constraint H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² — is a concrete cousin, and the Page–Wootters "internal clock" maps onto NTE's "Yukawa-coupled entities accumulate proper time" (couplers are the clocks; photons, zero-coupling, are clockless). **Use as ally/contrast, not identity:** PaW/thermal time target generally-covariant (Wheeler–DeWitt) timelessness, whereas NTE's N is a genuine e-fold ordering — the analogy is structural. This is the physics-side counterpart to Lam–Wüthrich's "non-temporal production" gap and is where NTE has something concrete to offer.

**Tier 3 — the glue thread (lower priority):**
5. **Jaksland, "Entanglement as the world-making relation" (Synthese 198, 2021)** + critique **Ney (2021)**. Entanglement-as-glue; pairs with §2.3(2). Relevant if NTE develops the Humean-base framing of its Hopf/entanglement structure.
