## I. The photon paradox: why ds² = 0 demands a new ontology

For a photon, the spacetime interval vanishes: $ds^2 = c^2 dt^2 - d\mathbf{x}^2 = 0$. A photon accumulates zero proper time between emission and absorption. This is standard relativity, but it raises a question standard physics leaves unanswered: *what distinguishes entities that accumulate proper time from those that do not?*

The Standard Model answers cleanly. Every entity with zero proper-time accumulation has zero Higgs (Yukawa) coupling — photons, gluons. Every entity that accumulates proper time has nonzero Higgs coupling — electrons, quarks, W/Z; neutrinos, barely, with $y_\nu \sim 10^{-12}$. The correlation is exact, with no Standard-Model exceptions. NTE takes it as structural: the boundary expansion field $\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ parameterises the map from e-fold increments to proper-time increments, and entities without Yukawa coupling do not participate — they accumulate zero proper time (null-time). This reframes the standard picture without asserting that the field dynamically creates time: $N$ is the geometric coordinate, $t$ is derived once locking completes, and Standard-Model mass generation remains the Standard-Model Higgs sector (§2.3).

## II.0 On method

The working discipline of this framework is to seek simplification before mechanism: where multiple formalisms agree on a phenomenon, treat them as descriptions of one structure and look for that structure; where a modification reduces the count of independent postulates, prefer it; where a phenomenon seems to require new mechanism, seek the viewpoint from which it does not, before adopting one. This is a working bet, not a law, and it is applied symmetrically — a framework that counts its successful simplifications while passing over its failed ones in silence is doing advocacy, not science.

NTE's record therefore includes both. Simplifications that earned their place: the gauge group from $G_2$, the Born rule as a Hopf projection on $S^3$, the dark-matter coupling from $S^3$ topology. Proposals withdrawn because they did not: an early Klein-bottle topology (abandoned for $S^3$ when parallelisability, Lie-group structure, and boundary parity all converged on $S^3$); a "single manifold without thickness" reading (superseded by the N-thickness reading); an ontological rebuild that would have withdrawn the dark-matter derivation (withdrawn after audit showed the corpus had already committed to the relevant structure). A modification that merely re-describes phenomena already covered, without simplifying or making a distinguishing prediction, fails the test.

**Graded honest-scope.** The framework reports every result against three explicit gradations rather than a binary "predicted / not":
- *Derivation status* — **derived** (from NTE's geometric structure), **inherited/passthrough** (from underlying structure with no NTE freedom), **conditional/observational** (constrained by observation pending derivation), or **imported** (an established external result NTE adopts and credits).
- *Force of a structural claim* — **forced** by deeper structure vs **consistent** with the framework's commitments without being uniquely forced.
- *Explanatory uniqueness* — **explains uniquely** vs **re-describes** something already explained at the level observed.

This discipline, together with the explicit no-number-fitting rule, is what the framework offers in place of foundational depth it does not claim (see Positioning).

## III. Three coupling regimes

The Higgs coupling sets a particle's geometric relationship to the expanding boundary; the three regimes are post-temporal.

**Zero coupling — null-time.** Photons and gluons ($y = 0$) accumulate zero e-fold depth and do not enter the $dt$ map. For the photon, $ds^2 = 0$: it sits on the boundary, not within the manifold. "Null-time" is null *from* time (a saturated value of an existing proper-time measure), not existence in the absence of time.

**Strong coupling — embedded matter.** Quarks, charged leptons, W/Z, Higgs ($y \gg 0$) are deeply embedded, accumulate substantial proper time per e-fold, and lock to one e-fold direction (§VII). Particles are standing-wave patterns in the boundary, not objects moving through a pre-existing space; coupling sets embedding depth. (99% of proton mass is gluon binding energy — massless gluons, but the confined energy still contributes to embedding: same geometric effect, different mechanism.)

**Minimal coupling — the unresolved neutrino.** Neutrinos ($y_\nu \sim 10^{-12}$) are barely embedded and not locked to one direction. NTE reads the Majorana character geometrically: the neutrino has not undergone the topological resolution (the Hopf projection $S^3 \to S^2$, requiring engagement with the $S^1$ fibre) that creates the particle/antiparticle binary. It is neither particle nor antiparticle but an *unresolved* configuration, smeared across the fibre, with bilateral support across both CPN-conjugate orientations of the N-thickness. This bilateral support — not a mediating role — is the geometric structure on which the dark-matter cross-thickness coupling acts (§VI, and Part 2). The "Majorana bridge" label from earlier drafts should be read in this light: the bridge is geometric, not a particle mechanism.
