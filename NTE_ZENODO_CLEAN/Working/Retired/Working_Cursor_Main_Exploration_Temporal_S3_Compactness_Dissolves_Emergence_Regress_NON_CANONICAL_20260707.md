## (a) The regress critique

Greg's critique of the axiom "t emerges from N": *emergence* is a process word. A process requires a meta-ordering in which it runs — steps, before/after, iteration. If t is said to "emerge from" N, then N must already carry the operational content of time (ordering, sequence, step-structure). The claim does not derive time; it renames time's skeleton and Wick-rotates it (consistent with Part 1 §II.0 regrading `t = iΔN` as *reformulation*, not postulate reduction).

The non-vacuous residue of `t = iΔN` was never the *order* (all in N) but the *signature*: the `i` encodes that intervals along the emergent direction carry a minus sign, giving causal structure and a clock rate tied to H.
