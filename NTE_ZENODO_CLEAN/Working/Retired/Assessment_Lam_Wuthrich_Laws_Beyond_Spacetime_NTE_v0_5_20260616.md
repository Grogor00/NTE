## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal, *freely recombinable* base. A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory is **"nomically charged"** — the theory constrains recombination, so it isn't modally innocent. Reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — a temporal notion. Without fundamental time, "production" becomes elusive; the fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** that may not survive QG.

### §2.5 Cosmological cousins — USMEG-EFT and Grady's phase-boundary universe
Two single-author frameworks share NTE's core move (pre-temporal substrate → phase transition → emergent Lorentzian spacetime). Neither is mainstream-established; priority-worry modest. Worth knowing for differentiation.

**(a) USMEG-EFT** — Chishtie, *Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality* (arXiv:2502.18524v5, physics.gen-ph; full text in NTE root). USMEG's "exactly 2 tensor GW polarizations" is standard GR, shared by NTE post-locking → not a separator. Adversarial catch: GW background quoted LISA-band f~10⁻⁴ Hz (abstract) vs f_peak~10¹⁰ Hz, ΩGWh²~10⁻¹⁵–10⁻¹⁶ (§7.5) — ~14-order internal inconsistency.

**(b) Grady's phase-boundary universe** — M. Grady, *Quantum Mechanics, Quantum Gravity, and Approximate Lorentz Invariance from a Classical Phase-Boundary Universe* (Trends in Quantum Gravity Research, Nova 2006; arXiv:hep-th/0104237). A 4D Euclidean substrate with universal time; our universe = the 3D expanding **phase boundary of a liquid→solid (crystallisation) transition**; Big Bang = nucleation event (first-order, no singularity); **Minkowski emerges dynamically** (local time = coordinate normal to the moving boundary); quantum fluctuations = 4D thermal fluctuations (stochastic / Parisi–Wu quantisation); fermions = screw dislocations, gauge bosons = surface acoustic waves; **quantum measurement = SSB** (and cosmological "self-measurement" into a single vacuum); gravity via elasticity (Einstein–Cartan/torsion). ## §3. Research lines worth engaging (curated)

**Tier 1 — positioning + a known objection:**
1. **Huggett & Wüthrich, *Out of Nowhere* (OUP 2025).** Canonical monograph; **spacetime functionalism**. NTE's "emergent t valid locally like Newtonian gravity under GR" is already functionalist — name it. (Ch. 9 draft + Read's "two miracles of GR," a parallel to NTE's mass–proper-time correlation made necessary by the gauge-birth cascade.)
2. **Empirical-incoherence problem** — Huggett & Wüthrich 2013 (arXiv:1206.6290); Lam & Wüthrich 2018 (arXiv:1803.04374), both full-text read. NTE's answer: all observation is post-gauge-birth/temporal; emergent t plays the time-role; only functionally relevant features need recovering, and NTE recovers them. Reinforcements: (a) HW2013's **"physical salience flows from above"** — empirical success of a derivation, not a prior metaphysical criterion, certifies a non-observable layer as physically real (NTE's unobservable N<N_lock is licensed *because* n_s / gauge group / κ succeed); (b) L-W2018 functionalism applies to **time**, not just space, and is the natural home for NTE's constraint/supervenience reading (§2.2).
3. **Huggett & Wüthrich, "The (A)temporal Emergence of Spacetime" (arXiv:1710.11444).** Strongest single Tier-1 anchor. Touchpoints:
   - HW (§2c) call for **"some new fundamental parameter… that varies across the atemporal region, and agrees with effective time in the quasi-classical regions."** N is a concrete instance — NTE answers a stated need.
   - Their LQC case has a Euclidean, purely spatial ("no time at all") deep regime → Lorentzian at late times; the signature change *is* the emergence-of-time event. Published QG precedent for **NTE's Hartle–Hawking Euclidean section → Lorentzian-saddle gauge birth**.
   - Reply to their empirical-incoherence "gap": pre-temporal structure anchored by observable traces (n_s, gauge group, κ).
   - **Supporting analog-gravity precedent (new):** Nissinen & Volovik, *Effective Minkowski-to-Euclidean signature change of the magnon BEC pseudo-Goldstone mode in polar ³He* (arXiv:1707.00905). A condensed-matter system whose effective Nambu–Goldstone metric undergoes a **Minkowski→Euclidean signature change** as a control angle crosses threshold (tan λ_c = 2) — a *lab-realisable* instance of the same Euclidean↔Lorentzian transition. With LQC and Grady's crystallisation, signature-change-at-emergence is now **multiply-realised**, not a one-off. Caveat (structural-only): in the ³He case the Euclidean region is an *instability* (tachyonic ghost / false-vacuum decay), whereas NTE's section is a saddle — precedent for *that signature change exists and is threshold-controlled*, not a model of NTE's section.

**Tier 2 — physics anchor:**
4. **Calcinari & Gielen, *Relational dynamics and Page–Wootters formalism in group field theory* (Quantum 9:1610, 2025; arXiv:2407.03432).** Worked QG construction of emergent time from a reparametrisation constraint C = H_χ + p_χ = 0, **linear in the clock momentum**, with a scalar field χ as internal clock; Dirac quantisation ≡ Page–Wootters; "clock-neutral" trinity. Its **multi-fingered (§5.2)** extension — an independent clock χ_J per mode, Abelian constraints — is a worked template for NTE's "different entities carry different proper-time clocks (photons clockless)." Strongest single physics anchor.
5. **Connes–Rovelli thermal time** — supporting; POVM formulation in van Neerven & Portal (arXiv:2306.13774). Its closing open problems explicitly propose reading these time-POVMs in the **Page–Wootters** formalism — so both Tier-2 papers converge on the same relational-time thread. Contrast only: NTE's t-from-N is an e-fold ordering, **not** modular flow (structural-analogy register).

**Tier 3 — glue thread:**
