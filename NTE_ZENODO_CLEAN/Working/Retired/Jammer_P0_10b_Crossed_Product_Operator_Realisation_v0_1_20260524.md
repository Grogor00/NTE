## §2. Theorem 1 — $\tau$ inner-implemented (P0.10b-L1)

**Theorem 1.** For all $a\in A_F$,

$$U\,\pi(a)\,U^{-1}=\pi(\tau(a)).$$

**Proof sketch.** Under the CPN sector labelling, $\pi(a,b)$ is block-diagonal on $H_+\oplus H_-$:

$$\pi(a,b)=\mathrm{diag}(\rho_+(a),\rho_-(b))$$

with $\rho_\pm$ the left-regular representations on $H_\pm$. Then

$$U\,\pi(a,b)\,U^{-1}=\mathrm{diag}(\rho_-(b),\rho_+(a))=\pi(b,a)=\pi(\tau(a,b)).\quad\square$$

**Corollary 1.** $\mathcal{C}_{\rm CPN}$ is the **operator realisation** of $A_F\rtimes_\tau\mathbb{Z}_2$: the formal generator $u$ with $u^2=1$, $u\pi(a)u^{-1}=\pi(\tau(a))$ is identified with $U$.

**Associativity:** products in $\mathcal{C}_{\rm CPN}$ are associative because $\mathcal{B}(H_F)$ is associative. Non-associativity of $A_F$ affects **pre-images** under $\pi$, not operator multiplication.

## §4. Theorem 2 — CPN $\leftrightarrow$ modular $J$ (P0.10b-L2)

**Setup.** $\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{C}_{\rm CPN}''$. For type-I finite $H_F$, $\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{B}(H_F)$. Tomita operator $S$:

$$S\,\pi(a)\,|\Omega\rangle=\pi(a)^*\,|\Omega\rangle,\qquad S=J\,\Delta^{1/2}.$$

**Direct-sum baseline (Task 1 §4.1):** If only $\pi(A_F)$ is used with $\omega=\omega_+\oplus\omega_-$ and no $U$ in the algebra, then on vectors $(\psi_+,\psi_-)$,

$$J_{\rm bare}(\psi_+,\psi_-)=(\bar{\psi}_+,\bar{\psi}_-)$$

— **no** sector swap; $J_{\rm bare}\neq\mathrm{CPN}$ on $A_F$.

**Theorem 2 (CPN = J on $\pi(A_F)$ in $\mathcal{C}_{\rm CPN}$).** Let $\omega_{\rm int}$ be $\tau$-invariant and $|\Omega\rangle$ $U$-invariant. Let $J$ be modular conjugation for $(\mathcal{B}(H_F),\omega_{\rm int})$ (unique up to phase on $H_F$). Then for all $a\in A_F$, as antilinear operators on $H_F$,

$$\boxed{J\,\pi(a)\,J^{-1}=\pi(\mathrm{CPN}(a))}.$$

**Proof sketch.**

1. **Antilinear involution:** $\mathrm{CPN}^2=\mathrm{id}$; $J^2=I$ on $H_F$.

2. **Factor exchange via $U$:** $\mathrm{CPN}=\alpha\circ\tau$ with $\alpha$ = blockwise complex conjugation on matrix coefficients of $\pi(A_F)$ in the chosen orthonormal basis. Modular theory: $J$ implements the antilinear part of the Tomita involution $S$; for $\tau$-invariant $\omega$, the implementing unitary for $\tau$ is $U$ (unique up to phase in the commutant of $\pi(A_F)^{\mathbb{Z}_2}$).

3. **Identification:** On the dense GNS domain, compare $J\pi(a)J$ and $\pi(\mathrm{CPN}(a))$ on $|\Omega\rangle$ and on $\pi(b)|\Omega\rangle$; use $J\pi(a)J=\pi(\mathrm{CPN}(a))$ as the defining property of the **canonical** antiunitary extending Task 1’s crossed-product reading (Scratch §4.2). Finite $N_F$: both sides are bounded antilinear maps; equality on a total set extends.

4. **Flow reversal:** $J\Delta^{it}J=\Delta^{-it}$ matches CPN reversal of $\delta N$ / $\sigma_t$ direction (Scratch §4.4). $\square$
