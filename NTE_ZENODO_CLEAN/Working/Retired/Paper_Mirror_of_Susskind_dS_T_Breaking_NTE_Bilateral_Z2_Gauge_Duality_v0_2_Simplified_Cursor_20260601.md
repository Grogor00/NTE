## Abstract

L. Susskind (2026) argues that de Sitter quantum gravity requires spontaneous breaking of **time-reversal T**, with **T** naturally treated as a gauge symmetry in the Harlow–Numasawa sense: observables carry a forward/backward clock split, and physical correlators use **dressed** operators $\bar{\mathcal O} = \mathcal O \cdot \Pi_-$ where $\Pi_- = \Pi_f - \Pi_b$.

The Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) framework is built from a different premise: a bilateral $S^3 \times S^1$ surface, Hopf-fibre geometry, and an e-fold coordinate $N$ from which cosmological time emerges. At the **Janus surface** ($N=0$) it displays a **unitary chirality reflection** $M$ (not antiunitary **T**) and the same **ℤ₂ projection bookkeeping** ($\Pi_R,\Pi_L,\Pi_\pm$) on a two-branch subspace.

We **propose** a structural parallel — shared Π_− dressing / ℤ₂-gauge treatment — and list what must be shown for it to be more than bookkeeping common to any two-branch system. We do **not** claim NTE derives Susskind's result, geometric equivalence, or identity of $M$ with $T$. **Test D1** (cross-sector correlator in the symmetric Janus state) is **in progress**; until it is computed, cluster-decomposition statements are **conjecture** only.
