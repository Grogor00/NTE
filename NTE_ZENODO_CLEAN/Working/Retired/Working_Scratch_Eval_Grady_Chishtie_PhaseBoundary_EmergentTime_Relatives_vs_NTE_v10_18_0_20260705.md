## 1. Grady — classical phase-boundary universe

**What it is.** A 4D Euclidean space with universal time; a supercooled liquid nucleates a solid; our universe is the expanding 3D phase boundary (a crystalline surface) between solid (past) and liquid (future). Quantum fluctuations = 4D thermal fluctuations from the bulk (stochastic quantization; Parisi–Wu; complex Langevin; hbar proportional to 4D temperature). Minkowski space emerges dynamically by taking local time as the coordinate perpendicular to the moving boundary; Lorentz invariance only approximate (preferred crystal frame; phonon cutoff; cosmic-ray/GZK tests). Fermions = screw dislocations; gauge bosons = surface acoustic waves; gravity = elasticity of space (Sakharov/Einstein–Cartan). Measurement = spontaneous symmetry breaking; the universe "measures itself" at a cosmological phase transition.

**Parallels (reframed through NTE axioms).**
- Universe = expanding closed 3-boundary of a 4D object, born by nucleation/crystallisation — near word-for-word structural match to NTE's "S³ boundary of a 4-ball, boundary crystallisation at gauge birth" (Part 2 §8; §XVI). Both explicitly boundary-only.
- No initial singularity; finite nucleation seed — matches NTE's no-boundary / C1-kink gauge birth.
- Emergent time = the boundary-advance direction — matches NTE's t = i*DeltaN with N the radial e-fold direction.
- Euclidean-thermal to Lorentzian-quantum — matches NTE's Euclidean pre-temporal section to Lorentzian at gauge birth, with a gauge-birth temperature.
- Measurement = SSB / cosmic self-measurement — a specific conceptual predecessor to NTE Companion 6 ("Born rule activates at the gauge birth" via crystallisation). Grady's own SSB-measurement paper is also in the library.

**Distinctions (where NTE is distinct).**
- Base ontology (decisive): Grady is a literal classical ether / hidden-variable theory — a real crystal of atoms with real thermal noise from which QM emerges as stochastic quantization (deterministic-underneath). NTE is not hidden-variable; it derives QM structure geometrically (Born rule = Hopf, quantisation = compactness) with no classical deterministic substratum. NTE's "crystallisation" is a geometric/gauge event, not a lattice of atoms.
- Lorentz invariance: Grady predicts observable Lorentz violation (phonon cutoff, GZK anomalies); NTE keeps local Lorentz invariance post-lock.
- Particle content / DM: Grady's dislocations/phonons and interstitial-DM are qualitative and do not reproduce the SM; NTE has the octonionic SM route and the kappa = 5.50 CPN-DM. No bilateral/CPN structure in Grady.

**Grady verdict.** A striking crystallisation/boundary relative worth a measured citation in §XVI and Companion 6, with an explicit distinction that NTE is geometric-not-classical-ether and Lorentz-preserving.
