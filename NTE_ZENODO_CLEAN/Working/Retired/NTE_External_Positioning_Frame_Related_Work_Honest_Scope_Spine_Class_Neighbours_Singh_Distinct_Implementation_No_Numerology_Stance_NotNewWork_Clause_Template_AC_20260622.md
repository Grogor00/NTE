## A. The class NTE belongs to (name the neighbours)

NTE is one approach within a family of programs that take time and spacetime to be emergent and seek the Standard Model from division-algebra / noncommutative-geometric structure. Honest external presentation names this family:

- **Emergent / fundamentally-timeless time:** Page–Wootters (1983); Rovelli (1991) + relational QM; **Connes–Rovelli thermal time** (1994); Wheeler–DeWitt; Barbour; experimental test Moreva et al. (2013).
- **Division-algebra Standard Model:** Günaydin–Gürsey (1973); Dixon (1994); Furey (2014, 2018); Dubois-Violette (2016); Todorov–Dubois-Violette (2018); Baez.
- **Spectral action / NCG:** Chamseddine–Connes (1996, 1997, 2008, 2010).
- **Non-associativity ↔ time / causality:** Gogberashvili (2004); Köplinger–Dzhunushaliev–Gogberashvili ("emergent time from non-associative quantum theory").
- **Closest architectural relative:** **T. P. Singh's octonionic / pre-quantum pre-spacetime theory** (2019–2025) — assembles emergent time + octonionic-NCG + exceptional-Jordan SM + Connes time + Majorana + left–right symmetry into one framework.

## B. NTE's distinct position in the class (a distinct implementation, not a new class)

NTE's own choices, which differentiate it within the family:
- **N as a fundamental coordinate; t = iΔN** — a *concrete* proposal for what time emerges from (a count coordinate via Wick rotation), distinct from modular / Connes time.
- The **S³×S¹ Hopf substrate** and Hopf-flux quantisation.
- The **bilateral CPN-conjugate two-orientation cosmology**.
- Specific identifications that are NTE's own: **κ₀ = 3/√(4π)** and its closure κ₀/(1−κ₀); the **dark-matter/baryon ratio** as a cross-sector fixed point; the **two-branch neutrino mass ratio**; the **CLFV amplitude ratio tan²θ₂₃**.

Explicit contrast with Singh (the nearest relative): NTE carries **no** trace dynamics, **no** Grassmann-matrix aikyons, **no** spontaneous-localisation collapse, **no** E₈×E₈, **no** sedenions. NTE **assumes** quantum mechanics rather than deriving it, and correspondingly attempts less at the foundational level while remaining materially leaner. Its distinctiveness is the *specific* assembly and its specific predictions — not the existence of the assembly.

## C. Methodological stance — no number-fitting (state it explicitly)

NTE does not fit numbers. Every quantity is either **structural** (fixed by geometry/algebra) or **honestly scoped** as conditional, a cross-check, or open. No quantity in the corpus is assembled to land on a measured value, and no post-hoc correction is bolted on to match data. Results are tiered explicitly — **derived / re-derived (credited) / imported (credited) / open**. This is a deliberate methodological discipline, and a deliberate contrast with coupling-constant "derivations" elsewhere in the class that rely on assembled closed forms plus corrections.
