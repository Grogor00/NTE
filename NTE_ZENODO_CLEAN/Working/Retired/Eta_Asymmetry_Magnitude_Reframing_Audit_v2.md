These four components combine to make the *existence* of cosmic baryon asymmetry inevitable in NTE, with all inputs derived from S³ topology and Janus geometry. **Zero free parameters at the existence level.**

### 1.4 Precondition check: status of v_EW and Δm_eff under existing corpus commitments

**Result: Δm_eff is derived in NTE to factor 1.5; v_EW is SM-inherited under Path B with no additional NTE freedom. They have distinct status — both are not "free parameters of NTE", but only one (Δm_eff) is derivative content; the other (v_EW) is passthrough content.**

The framework's commitment that v_EW = 246 GeV at the present cosmic epoch is structural (the §2.3.1 trajectory passes through v_EW at H = H₀, which sets α's calibration); the value 246 GeV itself is SM-inherited.

**Δm_eff:** the corpus has a geometric derivation of the Majorana mass scale M_R at line 1274 (§19.7, in the orbifold + J₃(𝕆) section): *"The J₃(𝕆) structure provides a natural Majorana mass scale M_R ~ M_Pl^{3/4} · v_EW^{1/4} = 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV. The 3/4 + 1/4 split reflects the Hopf bundle S¹ → S³ → S²: the Majorana mass lives on the S² base (2 of 3 S³ dimensions), with the residual 1/4 from the S¹ fibre."* Combined with the seesaw (line 1276): m_ν³ = v_EW² / M_R = 0.074 eV (within factor 1.5 of observed √(Δm²_atm) ≈ 0.05 eV). **Δm_eff is therefore derived in NTE to factor 1.5** — derivative content, not passthrough.
