## 1. Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati (BKM) — Janus point / two futures

**Refs:** arXiv:1310.5167; arXiv:1409.0917 (PRL 113, 181101); arXiv:1507.06498; arXiv:1604.03956.

**What they construct:** Relational gravity on shape space for the zero-E–L–P Newtonian N-body problem (and GR extensions). Generic solutions pass through a **Janus point** — a configuration of minimum shape complexity — then evolve with **two emergent arrows of time** pointing in opposite directions away from that point. Complexity/information grows irreversibly on each branch; internal observers on one branch see one past and one future.

**Same-direction-in-both-halves mechanism:** **No** — the hallmark is **opposite** emergent time directions on the two halves (Roman Janus). Both branches share the Janus minimum as a common "past" in the BKM picture, not co-evolving with identical forward orientation.

**Anticipation of NTE CPN same-t reading:** **Partial structural overlap, different mechanism.** Overlap: two Lorentzian branches from a quantum-gravity-style turning point; time not fundamental. Divergence: BKM arrows are **anti-aligned**; NTE CPN lane (post-T1) treats **dt as even** under CPN with **outward |N|** on both sides — a same-orientation discipline Jammer derived from bilateral HH gauge birth, not from BKM complexity growth.

**Priority risk:** **Medium (conceptual, not claim overlap).** Reviewers may cite BKM Janus literature when NTE discusses Janus boundaries or two-branch cosmology. NTE must distinguish: Janus **geometry** (shared nomenclature) ≠ BKM **opposite arrows**. No direct priority threat to NTE's κ/η/CPN parity results if framing stays explicit.
