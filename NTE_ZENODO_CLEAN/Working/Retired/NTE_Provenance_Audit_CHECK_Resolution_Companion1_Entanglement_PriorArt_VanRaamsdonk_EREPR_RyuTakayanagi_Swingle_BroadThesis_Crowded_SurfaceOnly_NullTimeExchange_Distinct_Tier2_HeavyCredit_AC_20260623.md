## The claim under audit

Companion 1 reads entanglement as **null-time-exchange geometry**: the entangled pair is one object whose correlation lives at zero e-fold depth, "outside" the emergent manifold, on the surface-only substrate. The audit question: is "entanglement is geometry" novel, or prior art?

## Prior art [WEB ✓]

"Entanglement is geometry" is **mainstream and crowded**. The load-bearing members:

- **Van Raamsdonk (2010)**, *Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement* — arXiv:1005.3035, Gen. Rel. Grav. 42, 2323. Disentangling two regions makes them pull apart and pinch off; connectivity of spacetime *is* entanglement.
- **Maldacena–Susskind ER=EPR (2013)**, *Cool horizons for entangled black holes* — arXiv:1306.0533, Fortschr. Phys. 61, 781. An EPR-entangled pair is connected by an Einstein–Rosen bridge through the interior.
- **Ryu–Takayanagi (2006)** — hep-th/0603001. Entanglement entropy of a boundary region = area of a minimal bulk surface (holographic entanglement entropy).
- **Swingle (2012)** — arXiv:1209.3304. Tensor-network / entanglement-renormalisation (MERA) construction of an emergent holographic geometry.

The broad thesis is **not NTE-original**, and it is a well-populated field.

## The decisive distinction (why this is not a fatal collision)

Every member above builds a **bulk** geometry from entanglement: a wormhole *interior* (ER=EPR), a holographic *bulk* dual (Van Raamsdonk, RT), a minimal *bulk* surface (RT), a *bulk* tensor-network (Swingle). All require a bulk the entanglement is "in."

NTE is **surface-only / no-bulk by its core axiom** (boundary-only ontology, Part 1). It therefore **cannot be ER=EPR / RT / Van Raamsdonk** — those constructions presuppose exactly the bulk NTE denies itself. NTE's reading — entanglement as a null-time exchange at zero e-fold depth, on the surface, with no interior wormhole to thread — is a genuinely **distinct mechanism**, distinct *precisely because* it has no bulk. The single-object reading (one object appearing as two) is the NTE-native content; the broad "entanglement is geometry" framing is the class it joins.
