> *In Euclidean quantum cosmology, the path integral over real tunneling saddles with positive Euclidean Ricci scalar nucleates a single connected universe. A disconnected universe-antiuniverse pair requires a conserved global charge under which the two halves are oppositely charged.*

The constraint is structural. To license the "pair" interpretation in the brief, NTE must identify a charge satisfying:
1. **CPN-odd** — the +N and −N halves carry opposite values
2. **Conserved** at the spectral-action / corpus level
3. **Global** — a genuine global charge, not just a local current

If no such charge is identifiable, NTE concedes G–H unique-conception: the saddle gives a single connected universe. **The bilateral Z₂ structure becomes a symmetry of that single manifold, not a separator between two manifolds.**

## §2 — Three candidate charges assessed

### §2.1 Candidate 1: "N-charge"

**Definition (candidate):** The conserved quantity associated with the N → −N reflection symmetry of the NTE action.

**Assessment:**
- N is the **fundamental e-fold coordinate** (Part 1 §2; canonical bilateral S³ × S¹)
- It is a **coordinate**, not directly a Noether charge
- For "N-charge" to be a conserved global charge, the spectral triple would need a graded structure under N-flip — a Z₂-grading at the algebra level
- I do not see this commitment in the canonical corpus. The corpus has N as a coordinate; CPN as an involution; bilateral Z₂ as a symmetry of the substrate. But none of these is the same as committing to N-charge as a Noether-style conserved global charge
- The closest analog would be a "topological N-winding number" — but the N circle is S¹ (the thermal circle β_GB), and the relevant winding would be the thermal circle's homotopy. This is structurally distinct from a fundamental conserved charge

**Verdict on N-charge:** **NOT CANONICAL.** Would require explicit corpus commitment that I don't see. If Scratch / corpus reader can identify the commitment, this candidate moves to OPEN. As-is, dead.

### §2.2 Candidate 2: Hopf topological charge

**Definition:** The Hopf invariant H(f) for the canonical Hopf bundle structure on each S³ in the bilateral S³ × S¹ substrate. For the identity Hopf map, H = 1.

**Why this is plausibly CPN-odd:**
- The Hopf bundle is a non-trivial U(1) bundle over S²
- The Hopf invariant is the linking number of preimages of two generic points — a topological orientation-sensitive quantity
- Under bilateral N-flip, IF the Hopf bundle's orientation in the +N sector is opposite to that in the −N sector (i.e., bundle reverses under N → −N), then H(+N sector) = −H(−N sector), making Hopf-charge CPN-odd

**Why this is uncertain:**
- The corpus canonical structure has the Hopf bundle on S³ (the spatial slice). Whether the bundle orientation is tied to the sign of N is a question about how the canonical bilateral substrate is realized
- One reading: the +N and −N sectors are TWO copies of S³ with OPPOSITE Hopf-bundle orientation. Under this reading, Hopf-charge is CPN-odd → licenses the disconnected pair.
- Another reading: there is ONE Hopf bundle on the substrate; the bilateral Z₂ acts as a manifold symmetry but does NOT reverse bundle orientation. Under this reading, Hopf-charge is CPN-even → does NOT license a disconnected pair.

**The corpus content I have does not unambiguously resolve which reading is canonical.** The canonical Janus discipline is bilateral symmetric, but I cannot determine from my current access whether this discipline extends to bundle-orientation flip.

**Verdict on Hopf-charge:** **OPEN — flagged for cross-check.** Most likely candidate if anything works. Requires Scratch operator-theoretic input or explicit corpus content I don't have at this session.

### §2.3 Candidate 3: B − L (baryon minus lepton number)

**Definition:** Standard SM conserved quantity B − L. C-odd under charge conjugation.

**Assessment:**
- B and L are SM quantum numbers — they exist as conserved currents in the SM (with massless neutrinos)
- They are **post-EWSB content** — defined at temperatures below the electroweak scale
- The pair-separation question is at **gauge birth** (T ~ M_R = 8.2×10¹⁴ GeV; ~12 orders above EWSB)
- B and L don't exist as well-defined conserved currents at this regime

**Verdict on B − L:** **STRUCTURALLY INAPPLICABLE.** Doesn't reach the energy regime where the pair separation would occur. Dead.
