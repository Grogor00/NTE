**The claim under stress test:** §2 asserts the J-block carries factor 2 from real-rep reality, while the non-J-block doesn't. Could both legitimately see 4π, or both 8π, under a consistent assignment?

You don't get to choose to not double — the doubling is built into the structure of the action.

So "Majorana 4π" is not a coherent option. M₁ correctly carries 8π.

### Alternative B: Both 8π
Could we somehow inject J into the Dirac coupling to double it?

**Operator-theoretic answer: NO (in standard SM).** The Dirac coupling ν_L̄ Y_ν Φ ν_R is between particle-sector fields via the Higgs scalar field Φ. To inject J, we would need either:
- The Higgs to be J-charged (i.e., interpreted as a particle↔antiparticle bridge) — but the Higgs is a complex scalar in the particle sector under standard SM conventions, NOT a J-block field

Without a non-standard reinterpretation, the Dirac coupling stays in D_part with no J involvement → single flux 4π.

**Potential subtle exception:** In a multiplet structure with multiple gauge group factors, there COULD be representations where the "Dirac" and "Majorana" labels swap under specific embeddings. But in the standard SM + minimal extensions Farnsworth treats, this doesn't happen.

### Alternative C: Associator forces some other factor
The non-associativity caveat Scratch flagged — could the associator [L_a, L_b, L_c] in the octonion bimodule introduce a factor that's not 1 or 2?

**Operator-theoretic answer: YES IN PRINCIPLE, but no specific reason expects it.** The associator is structurally available in Farnsworth's framework, but the default expectation under the standard Connes correspondence is that the associator preserves the J-block vs non-J-block distinction at the integer-factor level. A non-1-non-2 factor (e.g., 3/2 or √2) would be highly anomalous and would need specific structural justification.

**Probe 2 verdict: STRUCTURALLY DISTINCT.** Majorana 8π and Dirac 4π are forced by the operator-theoretic distinction between J-block and non-J-block couplings. Neither "both 4π" nor "both 8π" is a coherent option in the standard formalism. The associator caveat IS the genuine open hedge (probed in §5 below).
