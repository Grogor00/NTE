## §3 — Five operator-level mappings (where the resonance is strongest)

### §3.1 Mapping M1 — Inverted ontology (time fundamental, space emergent)

Both frameworks invert the standard Einstein 3+1 picture in the same direction: **time has ontological priority; space is secondary.**

- **NTE:** the e-fold coordinate N is the fundamental embedding label; the Lorentzian time t emerges only post-bifurcation; the bilateral S³ × S¹ surface is the fundamental object.
- **Kletetschka:** three-dimensional time T³ is the fundamental fabric; the observed three spatial dimensions emerge as projections.

The motivation for the inversion differs (NTE: Hopf-bundle geometry + Programme S derivation; Kletetschka: re-examination of the role of time in physical equations + symmetry requirements). The *direction* of priority is identical.

### §3.2 Mapping M2 — The S³ factor

In both frameworks the spatial part is an S³. This is *not* a coincidence at the framework-architecture level; it is a constraint on what structures can host both pictures.

- **NTE:** S³ is forced by the Weyl-flatness + Euler-zero constraint (Paper F1, recently uplifted to ratified by AC; Zenodo v2 published).
- **Kletetschka:** S³ is the spatial part of M = T³ × S³; emerges from symmetry requirements (his §2 / §3).

**This is a non-trivial agreement.** Two independent paths land on S³ as the spatial geometry. The convergence is a *positive structural anchor* for both frameworks.

### §3.3 Mapping M3 — Three-mode-three-scale-three-generation structure

NTE's σ₁, σ₂, σ₃ Cartan modes operate at distinct physical roles:
- **σ₁/σ₂** charged base modes (Hopf base S² action; raising/lowering pair); seesaw → M_1 mass scale (BPS-anchored under geometric flux quantization).
- **σ₃** neutral Cartan mode (fibre-acting; Higgs-anchored); seesaw → M_R mass scale.

Kletetschka's t₁/t₂/t₃ temporal modes operate at distinct physical scales:
- **t₁** quantum/Planck-scale (mass generation).
- **t₂** interaction-scale (finite corrections, quantum/classical bridge).
- **t₃** cosmological-scale (gravitational, large-scale).

**Both frameworks have three structurally distinguished modes at three physical scales each tied to one particle generation.** Under the derivative-literature reading that Kletetschka's three temporal axes are *eigenmodes of a temporal metric operator*, the structural alignment with NTE's Cartan triad becomes very tight: in both cases we have three eigenmodes of a structurally-fixed operator, with the higher modes excluded or suppressed.

### §3.4 Mapping M4 — Topological charge

Kletetschka's companion paper (*Charge as a Topological Property in Three-Dimensional Time*, JHEP/GC 2025) derives electric charge from twisting of temporal configurations.

NTE has the topological coupling **κ₀ = 3/√(4π)** from the Hopf-bundle structure on S³ → S² (post-B3 rep-theory patch: counts dim(adj SU(2)) gauge components on the Hopf base s-wave). This is the topological origin of charge / gauge coupling in NTE.

Both: topology of an internal geometric structure → charge.

The mechanisms differ (NTE: Hopf-base gauge field component sum; Kletetschka: temporal twisting), but the **topological-origin claim for charge** is the same conceptual move. This is one of NTE's most distinctive structural claims; finding it independently in Kletetschka is significant.

### §3.5 Mapping M5 — Chirality / V–A

Kletetschka predicts left-handed neutrinos and V–A from temporal geometry. NTE predicts the same from chirality bifurcation at the Janus surface (N=0).

Both: a geometric source for the V–A asymmetry, rather than a parameter in the Lagrangian.

The geometric sources differ (NTE: bilateral mirror sector selection at gauge birth; Kletetschka: orientation/projection in the temporal sector), but the *type* of explanation — V–A as geometry-forced rather than parameter-fitted — is the same.

## §4 — Tensions (six binding flags; T-K1 through T-K6)

**T-K1.** **Three time dimensions vs one emergent t.** NTE has *one* emergent time coordinate t (post-bifurcation) derived from the e-fold N via the Hopf-bundle structure. Kletetschka has *three* time dimensions t₁/t₂/t₃ as fundamental. Whether NTE's three Cartan modes σ₁/σ₂/σ₃ can be reinterpreted as Kletetschka's t₁/t₂/t₃ under a change of variable is the deepest open question and **the load-bearing test for full integration**. If the three Cartan modes *are* the three temporal dimensions of Kletetschka under suitable identification, the frameworks may be equivalent; if not, they differ at the deepest level.

**T-K2.** **T³ vs S¹.** Kletetschka's T³ (a 3-torus) is a specific topology — three independent circles. NTE has S¹_β_GB — one thermal circle. The dimensional counting and topology differ. **Possible resolution:** Kletetschka's three temporal eigenmodes might project onto a single S¹ via a quotient or under coarse-graining; alternatively, NTE's S¹ might be enriched to a T³ structure in a Kletetschka-extension version. Either way: corpus retreat is *not* required, but reconciliation work is open.

**T-K3.** **Mass ratios 1:4.5:21 vs (8π)^(2/3) ≈ 8.57.** Kletetschka and NTE both predict three-generation mass hierarchies, but the predicted ratios differ:
- Kletetschka: 1 : 4.5 : 21 (each ratio ~4.7×)
- NTE: m_ν2/m_ν3 = (8π)^(-2/3) ≈ 0.117 (i.e., m_ν3/m_ν2 ≈ 8.57; the "structural ratio" open in Programme S, currently NEEDS-MORE / PENDING)

These are the same observable (lepton generation hierarchy) with different numerical predictions. **This is the sharpest discrimination test** — both frameworks make zero-parameter predictions for the same number, and at most one can be right. NTE's atmospheric leg honest scope is *currently 1.49× over the observed* (B1 retracted "0.997×"); Kletetschka's top-quark prediction is **173.21 ± 0.51 GeV** (vs 173.2 ± 0.9 GeV observed), claimed at sub-1% precision. **Kletetschka's mass-spectrum claims appear empirically tighter than NTE's at this moment** — but the derivation chain for Kletetschka must be adversarially verified before this is conceded.

**T-K4.** **Author credentials and journal reputation.** Kletetschka is at the Geophysical Institute, University of Alaska Fairbanks — he is a geologist by training, not a particle physicist. The journal *Reports in Advances of Physical Sciences* is open-access but not top-tier (impact factor and citation network suggest mid-tier; see derivative reception). For NTE outreach purposes, **this is a credibility flag**: a positive citation of Kletetschka in our outreach materials may attract the same scepticism that has been directed at his work in some venues (Futurism notably skeptical). **Discipline:** Cite Kletetschka with honest qualification; do not piggyback on his prestige (he doesn't have it in particle physics yet); position NTE as an independent route arriving at convergent structural conclusions, not as a derivative of Kletetschka.

**T-K5.** **Causality preservation is an unverified claim.** Kletetschka's K8 — that causality is preserved despite the multi-time structure via a chronometric form — is *claimed* but not yet independently adversarially verified (in particular, not in the Connes Programme adversarial register). Multi-time theories have a notorious history of causality difficulties (Bars's two-time physics needed gauge-fixing; Kletetschka claims his three-time avoids this, but the proof needs scrutiny). NTE's emergent-single-t structure does not have this risk; if Kletetschka's causality argument fails on adversarial verify, the frameworks may diverge sharply on this point. **Routes to Scratch Connes Programme verify** (§8 below).

**T-K6.** **Community traction is largely popular-press.** The reception in 2025 has been heavily in popular science media (Sci.News, Earth.com, Phys.org, ScienceAlert, Interesting Engineering, Sustainability Times, Medium pieces). Academic engagement is sparse — the most substantial derivative item I found is a July 2025 ResearchGate preprint proposing the "Hentsch manifold" infinite-dim time-fibre extension. This is a real flag: a framework with strong popular traction and weak academic traction is at the "interesting hypothesis, not yet established" tier. NTE's adoption posture should reflect that.
