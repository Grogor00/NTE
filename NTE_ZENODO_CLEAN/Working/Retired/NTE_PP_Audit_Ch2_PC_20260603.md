## Why Ch 2 passes (criterion-by-criterion)

**Engage floor:** Schrödinger (1935), Einstein (1947 letter to Born, "spukhafte Fernwirkung"), the "hand-waves are a sign that something is missing" line — all concrete historical anchors that pull lay readers in.

**Fleeting-glimpse:** §"One Object, Two Intersections" is one of the cleanest pedagogical-pivot moves in the book. Picture a ball; push a rod through it; the two surface circles read as "two things" to surface-dwellers but are one rod. Give the rod an orientation (arrow head/tail); diametrically opposite intersections read opposite ends. *"That is the entire content of the spin-conservation rule for an entangled pair, rendered geometric."* This is exactly what fleeting-glimpse pedagogy should look like.

**Schema-attachment:** "No Pair, One Object" (§1 title) carries the schema forward. The rod-and-ball is referenced naturally in later sections without being restated. Reader has something to hold.

**Earned-term defer:** Bell's theorem mentioned with one-sentence explanation; Tsirelson bound built up from the Bell context; Van Raamsdonk + AdS/CFT + holographic principle named only in §5 with proper historical positioning. No jargon drops.

**Honest scope:** §"The Singular-Object Picture, Carefully" is a model of honest-scope discipline:
> "We are *not* claiming that the two surface intersections are, in some metaphysical sense, the same point. They are not. They are at different locations on the surface; they are read by different detectors; they have distinct measurement outcomes..."

Tsirelson bound section explicitly:
> "The framework is not competing with quantum mechanics on the value... Tsirelson is what we should see if the framework's reading is correct; κ is what only the framework predicts."

This is precisely the kind of *what we claim and what we don't* discipline the book needs throughout.

**Density:** No redundant restatement chains found. The rod-and-ball schema is built once, then re-used (not restated) in later sections. The "one object, two intersections" language carries forward as a structural reference rather than as repeated explanation.

**Forward-momentum:** Each section builds. §"What Entanglement Actually Is" sets the puzzle. §"The Photon's Lesson, Applied" bridges from Ch 1. §"One Object, Two Intersections" delivers the schema. §"Why This Picture Is Not New" places it in the literature. §"Carefully" sharpens honest scope. §"Tsirelson" closes the geometric prediction. §"What This Means for the Photon" synthesises. Clean linear build.

**Engagement:** No filler sentences. "There is no information to move. The shape was always there." — closes §6 with rhetorical force earned by the geometric argument that preceded it. "The strangeness was not in the physics. It was in our assumption that there were two particles in the first place." — closes the chapter with the same discipline.
