## §3 — The §2.1+§2.5 merger applied to Phase I data

Restating the structural argument, since this is the load-bearing correction:

### §3.1 Mechanism (§2.1's territory)

Ch 10 v4 (PC, 2026-05-29 05:10Z) integrated the **trousers-of-time bifurcation** at gauge birth as canonical framing: the pre-temporal octonionic regime ("waist") splits along the manifold's CPN symmetry into two orientations at the moment associativity locks and time becomes real. The bifurcation event is the symmetry-breaking moment. If the bifurcation is exactly CPN-symmetric, no seed; if a sub-perturbative asymmetry ε is frozen in at the split, the bilateral structure carries a primordial directional preference.

### §3.2 Observable (§2.5's territory)

The κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) closure derivation in Paper_Kappa_AB v10.17.2 §5 assumes exact CPN symmetry: T_μν^(b,+) = T_μν^(b,−) ≡ T_μν^(b). Under weak CPN-symmetry-breaking with seed ε, the closure step is perturbed: the bilinear S_bridge and Lagrange-multiplier S_cons no longer balance exactly, and the resummation κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) acquires a directional correction δκ(direction) whose magnitude tracks ε and whose direction tracks the bifurcation-preferred axis.

The bare coupling κ₀ = 3/√(4π) (Hopf-monopole-projection Y₀₀ on S² base) is **structurally isotropic** by construction (Paper_Kappa_AB §3.3 Gaunt-coefficient argument; §3.4 topological protection). The closure step is where directional structure enters — and only through CPN-symmetry-breaking driven by an upstream mechanism. §2.1 (gauge-birth seed) supplies the upstream mechanism; §2.5 (anisotropic κ) is the closure-step manifestation.

**Without §2.1, §2.5 has no driver** — the Hopf-projection machinery actively prevents anisotropic κ in the isotropic case (per Gaunt-coefficient argument). **With §2.1, §2.5 follows automatically** through the closure step.

### §3.3 Amplitude estimate (Phase III question, preview)

The seed amplitude ε at gauge birth is bounded above by the SSB amplitude in the bifurcation effective action. SSB amplitudes in dimensionless field units are typically O(1); the observable amplitude depends on whether the seed gets diluted or frozen through post-gauge-birth expansion.

**Frozen-vs-diluted question.** Per Ch 10 v4 trousers-of-time framing, the bifurcation is a one-time event and the bilateral structure is the post-bifurcation steady state. The asymmetry between the +N and −N orientations is a property of the steady state, **not** a perturbation that decays with cosmic expansion. Per mirror metaphor (canonical): the mirror is the steady-state object; the trousers-of-time is the singular event that produced the mirror; the asymmetry between left and right mirror images is permanent.

**Phase III prediction:** δκ/κ_0 ~ O(seed amplitude ε), with ε frozen at gauge-birth value. If ε can be O(few %) at the bifurcation (SSB-natural), δκ/κ ~ few % is observable at survey-discrimination level. If ε is suppressed to vacuum-fluctuation amplitude at T_GB, δκ/κ is much smaller.

**Scratch Phase III deliverable target on this:** derive the natural ε scale from the bifurcation effective action under Ch 10 v4 trousers-of-time framing + the closure-step sensitivity ∂κ/∂ε from Paper_Kappa_AB §5 S_cons matrix inversion. Two-part computation; tractable.
