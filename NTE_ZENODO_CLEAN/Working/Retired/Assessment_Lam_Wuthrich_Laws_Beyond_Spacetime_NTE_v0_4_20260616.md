## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal, *freely recombinable* base. A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory is **"nomically charged"** — the theory constrains recombination, so it isn't modally innocent. Reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — a temporal notion. Without fundamental time, "production" becomes elusive; the fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** that may not survive QG.

### §2.5 Nearest cosmological competitor — USMEG-EFT (full paper read)
Chishtie, *Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality* (arXiv:2502.18524v5, **physics.gen-ph**; full text in NTE root). The closest framework in NTE's lane, worth knowing in detail.

**Shared territory.** Pre-temporal state = a 3D spatial manifold Σ with an *energy functional* E[φ] = ∫d³x[½(∇φ)²+V] — no time, no action, static configurations; pre-temporal quantum state a timeless Gaussian wavefunctional; time *emergent*; Big Bang reframed as a phase transition avoiding the singularity (finite invariants R/M_P⁴ ~ 4.9×10⁻⁴⁴, K/M_P⁸ ~ 4.0×10⁻⁸⁸); "parameter-free" rhetoric; explicit Wheeler–DeWitt / problem-of-time and entanglement (Van Raamsdonk) hooks; H₀-tension engagement.

**Mechanism — the real difference.** USMEG-EFT generates time by **one-loop quantum corrections producing a (∂φ/∂τ)² kinetic term** absent at tree level: time "switches on" when the loop-generated coefficient A ∝ [V'''(φ)]² exceeds A_crit = 1/M_P² (hence their added quartic, to supply V'''≠0); the lapse is identified as N²=A and FLRW emerges. NTE generates t from a **geometric boundary constraint** H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² + octonionic/Hopf structure. Entirely different machinery.

**Where they do / don't separate observationally:**
- n_s, r do **not** cleanly separate them — both Starobinsky-family: USMEG n_s=0.964, r=0.004 (N_e=55) vs NTE n_s=0.9685, r=0.0030 (N_e=63.4). Do **not** claim n_s/r as the discriminator.
- **Real separators:** (a) **f_NL** — USMEG predicts local ~1–2 (CMB-S4 σ~1 testable); NTE currently states **no f_NL** → differentiator *and* a gap NTE could close (does the gauge-birth cascade predict an f_NL?). (b) **NTE's particle sector** — SM gauge group, mass–proper-time correlation, Born/Hopf — USMEG has none; NTE's strongest separation. (c) **NTE's primordial CPT/parity signature** (C3: cosmic birefringence / chiral-GW chirality) — USMEG has none. (d) USMEG leans on "**exactly two tensor GW polarizations**" to exclude string/LQG — but that is standard GR, shared by NTE post-locking, so it does **not** separate USMEG from NTE.

**Status / priority calibration.** Largely single-author program (physics.gen-ph; supporting papers in Can. J. Phys. and Symmetry/MDPI) making a strong, non-mainstream-accepted claim ("first successful QG–SM unification"). Competitor in the *idea space*, not an established result — priority worry modest. **Adversarial catch:** stochastic GW background quoted peaking in the **LISA band f~10⁻⁴ Hz** (abstract/§9) but computed at **f_peak~10¹⁰ Hz, ΩGWh²~10⁻¹⁵–10⁻¹⁶** in §7.5 — abstract and body disagree by ~14 orders in frequency.

**Action:** cite for differentiation; do **not** adopt the loop-induced mechanism; flag the **f_NL gap** to the physics stream.

## §3. Research lines worth engaging (curated)

**Tier 1 — positioning + a known objection:**
1. **Huggett & Wüthrich, *Out of Nowhere* (OUP 2025).** Canonical monograph; **spacetime functionalism**. NTE's "emergent t valid locally like Newtonian gravity under GR" is already functionalist — name it. (Ch. 9 draft + Read's "two miracles of GR," a parallel to NTE's mass–proper-time correlation made necessary by the gauge-birth cascade.)
2. **Empirical-incoherence problem** — Huggett & Wüthrich 2013 (arXiv:1206.6290); Lam & Wüthrich 2018 (arXiv:1803.04374), both full-text read. NTE's answer: all observation is post-gauge-birth/temporal; emergent t plays the time-role; only functionally relevant features need recovering, and NTE recovers them. Two reinforcements from the full texts: (a) HW2013's **"physical salience flows from above"** — the empirical success of a derivation, not a prior metaphysical criterion, is what certifies a non-observable layer as physically real (so NTE's unobservable N<N_lock epoch is licensed *because* n_s / gauge group / κ succeed; cf. their Lewis-style move: the fundamental entities just *are* the things from which spacetime emerges); (b) L-W2018 functionalism ("spacetime is as spacetime does") applies to **time**, not just space, and is the natural home for NTE's constraint/supervenience reading (§2.2).
3. **Huggett & Wüthrich, "The (A)temporal Emergence of Spacetime" (arXiv:1710.11444).** Strongest single Tier-1 anchor (full text read). Two specific, citable touchpoints:
   - In §2c they argue an atemporal→temporal transition may require **"some new fundamental parameter… that varies across the atemporal region, and agrees with effective time in the quasi-classical regions."** N is a concrete instance of exactly this parameter — NTE *answers a need the philosophers articulated*, rather than merely overlapping with them.
   - Their LQC case has a Euclidean, **purely spatial ("no time at all")** deep-quantum regime giving way to Lorentzian signature at late times; the signature change *is* the emergence-of-time event. This is a published QG precedent for **NTE's Hartle–Hawking Euclidean section → Lorentzian-saddle gauge birth** — the same Euclidean→Lorentzian structure, arrived at independently.
   - NTE's reply to the empirical-incoherence "gap" they raise (a fully non-spatiotemporal region is unobservable in a profound sense): the pre-temporal structure is inferentially anchored by observable consequences / traces (n_s, gauge group, κ); the future of an atemporal phase leaves traces (their CMB point).

**Tier 2 — physics anchor:**
4. **Calcinari & Gielen, *Relational dynamics and Page–Wootters formalism in group field theory* (Quantum 9:1610, 2025; arXiv:2407.03432).** Worked QG construction of emergent time from a reparametrisation constraint C = H_χ + p_χ = 0, **linear in the clock momentum**, with a scalar matter field χ as internal clock; Dirac quantisation ≡ Page–Wootters; "clock-neutral" trinity. Closest published physics cousin to NTE's t = ∫dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²): N = fiducial ordering, the proportionality constraint = the reparametrisation constraint, Yukawa-coupled entities = internal clocks. Its **multi-fingered (§5.2)** extension — an independent clock χ_J per mode, Abelian constraints — is a worked template for NTE's "different entities carry different proper-time clocks (photons clockless)." Strongest single physics anchor.
5. **Connes–Rovelli thermal time** — supporting; POVM formulation in van Neerven & Portal (arXiv:2306.13774, full text read). Its closing open problems explicitly propose reading these time-POVMs in the **Page–Wootters** formalism — so both Tier-2 papers converge on the same relational-time thread NTE's cousin sits in. Contrast only: NTE's t-from-N is an e-fold ordering, **not** modular flow (structural-analogy register).

**Tier 3 — glue thread:**
6. **Jaksland, "Entanglement as the world-making relation" (Synthese 198, 2021)** + Ney (2021). Pairs with §2.3(2).
