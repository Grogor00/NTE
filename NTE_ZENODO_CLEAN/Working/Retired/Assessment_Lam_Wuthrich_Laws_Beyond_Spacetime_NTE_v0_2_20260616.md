## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent, not fundamental. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal supervenience base whose parts are *freely recombinable* (guaranteeing "no necessary connections"). A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory is **"nomically charged"** — the theory constrains recombination, so the base is not modally innocent. The Humean must reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — a temporal notion (initial-value formulation, Cauchy evolution, asymmetry). Strip out fundamental time and "production" becomes elusive; the sketched fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** that may not survive in QG and needs a non-temporal asymmetry.

Conclusion: survival of all three into the QG era is genuinely in question.

## §2. Where NTE lands — and the real internal finding

### §2.1 NTE as concrete instance
Unlike causal sets / LQG (pre-spacetime combinatorial structures), NTE has an explicit, *temporally-scoped* ontology: N (e-folds) fundamental and pre-temporal; t emergent at gauge birth; pre-temporal epoch undifferentiated (octonionic). NTE supplies a definite answer to "what is the non-spatiotemporal base?" and a definite emergence event — the concrete case the philosophers say is needed but mostly lack.

### §2.2 The load-bearing finding — constraint vs production (CORPUS ACTION)
NTE describes H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² two incompatible ways:
- **Constraint/supervenience** (Part 1 / §2.3.1): "load-bearing mathematical relation — not a derived causal claim that |Φ_NTE| 'generates' time"; t(N) is "a proportionality definition, not a separate dynamical law." (Humean/best-systems.)
- **Production** (Popular): |Φ_NTE| "governs the rate at which proper time accumulates"; expansion "produces time." (Dispositionalist/primitivist.)

Different metaphysical commitments, not phrasing. **Recommendation:** lead with the **constraint** reading as canonical (matches Part 1; safer); mark production language as explicitly effective/pedagogical. ### §2.3 Two places NTE is positively advantaged
1. **Non-temporal production gap.** They say a productive account needs a non-temporal ordering/asymmetry and can't guarantee one. NTE has candidates: N (intrinsic ordering, not temporal) + gauge birth (privileged direction).
2. **Entanglement glue.** Their best Humean world-making relation is entanglement (Jaksland). NTE already reads entanglement as projection of zero-proper-time objects via Hopf (Companion 2).

### §2.4 Honest caution
Metaphysics of laws, not empirical physics. NTE *takes a position in* and *answers an objection from* this debate; it does not *solve* the problem of time or the Humean dilemma.

### §2.5 Nearest cosmological competitor — USMEG-EFT (flag, not threat)
Chishtie, *Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality* (arXiv:2502.18524; now in NTE root) is the closest framework in NTE's lane: time and gravity emergent from a purely spatial high-energy state, Big Bang reframed as a **3D→4D phase transition** avoiding the singularity, "no additional parameters" rhetoric, explicit H₀-tension engagement. It is mechanistically and observationally **distinct** from NTE, and the differences favour NTE:
- **Mechanism:** USMEG-EFT generates time via quantum loop corrections / dimensional regularisation / bubble nucleation; NTE via a geometric boundary constraint (H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²) + octonionic/Hopf structure.
- **Inflation:** USMEG-EFT *replaces* inflation; NTE is inflation-compatible (N = ln a, N_e = 63.4, n_s = 0.9685).
- **Distinguishing predictions:** USMEG-EFT predicts large CMB non-Gaussianity (f_NL ~ 20–50) and Lorentz-invariance violation (ξ ~ 10⁻³); NTE predicts neither (n_s, r = 0.0030, w = −1, LI-preserving post-locking). **The two are observationally separable** — a point in NTE's favour.
- **Particle sector:** NTE derives the SM gauge group, mass–proper-time correlation, Born/Hopf; USMEG-EFT does not. NTE is richer on particle-sector derivations; USMEG-EFT is more developed on the loop-QFT machinery of the 3D→4D transition.

## §3. Research lines worth engaging (curated)

**Tier 1 — engage directly (positioning + a known objection):**
1. **Huggett & Wüthrich, *Out of Nowhere* (OUP 2025).** Canonical monograph; central thesis **spacetime functionalism**. The frame to position NTE within; NTE's "emergent t valid locally like Newtonian gravity under GR" is already a functionalist move — name it. (Ch. 9 draft now in NTE root; also carries Read's "two miracles of GR," a clean parallel to NTE's mass–proper-time correlation made necessary by the gauge-birth cascade.)
2. **Empirical-incoherence problem** — Huggett & Wüthrich 2013 (arXiv:1206.6290); Lam & Wüthrich 2018 (arXiv:1803.04374). The objection NTE will face. NTE's answer is strong and should be stated: all observation is post-gauge-birth/temporal; emergent t plays the time-role; only *functionally relevant* features need recovering (their own criterion), and NTE recovers them.
3. **Huggett & Wüthrich, "The (A)temporal Emergence of Spacetime" (arXiv:1710.11444).** On emergence of *time* + the "gap" (a non-temporal region has no observers). Bears on NTE's pre-temporal epoch; the paper itself frames a non-spatiotemporal→spatiotemporal transition "maybe the big bang" = NTE's gauge birth. NTE's reply: the pre-temporal structure is inferentially anchored by observable consequences (n_s, gauge group, κ).

**Tier 2 — physics anchor for NTE's actual construction (UPGRADED v0.2):**
4. **Calcinari & Gielen, *Relational dynamics and Page–Wootters formalism in group field theory* (Quantum 9:1610, 2025; arXiv:2407.03432).** The **worked QG construction** of emergent time from a reparametrisation constraint, using a scalar matter degree of freedom as an internal clock in a background-independent theory with no fundamental time. Closest published physics cousin to NTE's t = ∫dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²): N is the fiducial ordering, the proportionality constraint plays the reparametrisation-constraint role, and Yukawa-coupled entities are the internal clocks (photons, zero-coupling, are clockless). **This upgrades the time-emergence line from "philosophy ally" to "a QG construction does the same move"** — the strongest single physics anchor in the batch. Engage directly when defending that NTE's emergent t is well-posed.
5. **Connes–Rovelli thermal time** — supporting line; rigorous POVM formulation in van Neerven & Portal, *Thermal time as an unsharp observable* (arXiv:2306.13774). Time as modular flow of a KMS state. Contrast only: NTE's t-from-N is an e-fold ordering, not a modular flow — background literature, not a structural match.

**Tier 3 — glue thread (lower priority):**
6. **Jaksland, "Entanglement as the world-making relation" (Synthese 198, 2021)** + critique **Ney (2021)**. Pairs with §2.3(2).
