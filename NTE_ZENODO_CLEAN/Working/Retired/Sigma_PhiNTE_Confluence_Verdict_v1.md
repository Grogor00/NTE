## §5. The Wick-invariance + S³ argument: V_NTE form independently derived

The structural-confluence claim has bite only if NTE can derive its action form independently of CC. Here is the argument.

**Premise 1 (Wick rotation at gauge birth).** Per Stage 2 finding F2: the C¹ kink at gauge birth IS the Wick rotation event. For Φ_NTE to be consistently defined across this transition, its action must respect the conformal structure that enables Wick rotation.

**Premise 2 (4D conformal structure).** A real scalar field on a 4D manifold has a unique conformally invariant action structure:

```
S = ∫ √g d⁴x [ -(1/2) g^μν ∂_μΦ ∂_νΦ - (1/12) R Φ² - λ Φ⁴ ]
```

The coefficient ξ = 1/12 is the conformal coupling for d=4 (specifically (d−2)/(4(d−1)) in some conventions, or equivalently 1/12 with Marcolli/CC's sign convention). The quartic potential V = λΦ⁴ is the unique conformally invariant scalar potential in 4D — any other power breaks scale invariance (Φ³ has dim 3 ≠ 4; Φ² requires explicit mass).

**Premise 3 (Higgs portal at gauge birth).** When the SM Higgs activates at gauge birth (per §VIII), the natural coupling between Φ_NTE and the SM Higgs respecting all relevant symmetries (gauge invariance, conformal-respecting at the level of Φ_NTE) is the Higgs-portal quartic |H|²Φ². Any direct Yukawa coupling is excluded by Path B; the |H|² portal is the next-leading allowed coupling.

**Conclusion:** Φ_NTE has structurally forced action

```
S = ∫ √g d⁴x [ -(1/2)(∂Φ_NTE)² - (1/12) R Φ_NTE² - λ Φ_NTE⁴ - 2e |H|² Φ_NTE² - … ]
```

This is exactly the form CC derives via Yukawa traces in the spectral action — but NTE arrives at it independently from Wick-rotation invariance + 4D conformal discipline + Higgs portal genericness.

### 5.1 What this gets you and what it doesn't

**Closes:** V_NTE Stage 2 form. The action structure is forced by NTE's existing commitments; no spectral-action machinery needed.

**Doesn't close:** Coefficient values λ, e. The Wick-invariance argument fixes the action form but not the coupling magnitudes. CC's Yukawa-trace mechanism gives them (via M, Y_ν matrices); NTE has no analog Yukawa-trace mechanism on its A_{G₂} side. Stage 2 F3's "V_NTE off-trajectory under-determined" finding stands.

**Doesn't close:** H = β_NTE|Φ|² Friedmann coupling. The action structure above doesn't impose it; H = β_NTE|Φ|² is an additional NTE-specific commitment beyond the conformal-invariant scalar action. **CR's concern #2 stands** — but explicitly so, and this is the right disclosure.

**Doesn't close:** K(Φ) explicit functional form. The kinetic term -(1/2)(∂Φ)² is the canonical normalization, not a derivation of K(Φ). If the structurally-forced kinetic structure has higher-derivative or curvature corrections, those would emerge from a more complete derivation than is given here.
