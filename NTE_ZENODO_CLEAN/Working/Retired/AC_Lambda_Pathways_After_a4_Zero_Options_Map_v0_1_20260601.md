## §2 — The reframe a₄ = 0 forces (and it may be clarifying, not just destructive)

[INFER] In the spectral action S ~ f₄Λc⁴·a₀ + f₂Λc²·a₂ + f₀·a₄, the vacuum-energy/CC content lives in **a₀** (the volume term, the catastrophic bare CC) and partly **a₂** (the R term). **a₄ is the dimensionless higher-curvature coefficient** (Weyl², Gauss–Bonnet) — it is *not* a natural vacuum-energy density. Sourcing an energy-density Λ from a₄ required a renormalised-over-comoving-volume construction (Ch 13's language), which was always a strained home for the CC value.

So the honest reading of a₄ = 0: **the exact, empty, maximally-symmetric vacuum has exactly zero higher-curvature residual** — clean, and consistent with "empty space is quiet." It says the observed nonzero Λ **cannot** come from the empty symmetric vacuum; it must come from the universe *not being* that vacuum (matter, structure, a real expansion history). a₄ was arguably never the right place to look for the value — its collapse points back to where the CC actually lives (a₀, the structural term) and isolates the value as a separate question.

## §3 — Pathways for the value of Λ (honest map, ranked by defensibility)

**P0 — Anchor on the structural result (the robust core).** Lead with the volume-constraint quietness; a₄ = 0 *strengthens* it (the symmetric vacuum is exactly quiet — no residual needing fine cancellation). This is real and publishable on its own.

**P1 — Λ as a unimodular integration constant (value not predicted).** In unimodular gravity Λ enters as an integration constant fixed by boundary/initial conditions, not derived from the action. If NTE's mechanism is genuinely unimodular, the principled position is: NTE explains the *absence of the catastrophe* but does **not** predict the value — it is a boundary condition of the specific cosmological solution. **Most defensible current status.** Cost: retire the value-prediction claim rather than replace it.

**P2 — Λ from the realistic (non-empty, non-symmetric) background.** a₄ = 0 is exact only on S³×S¹. On a realistic background (matter, inhomogeneity, genuine FRW expansion) C² and E₄ need not vanish and matter stress-energy enters, so a residual can reappear. Physically the most motivated place to look for a *derived* value. Costs: no clean closed form (the "1.05×" was an artifact of the symmetric-background error and does not return); Λ would acquire dependence on cosmic content, raising constancy/coincidence issues; technically hard. A research line, not a near-term number.

**P3 — A different spectral-action term (a₂-level / scale-ratio / RG).** Tie Λ to a₂ or to a cutoff/scale-ratio structure rather than a₄. [INFER] a₂ gives Newton's constant in the standard reading, not Λ, so this needs a non-standard construction. Speculative; moderate dialing risk.

**P4 — NTE-native (e-fold / horizon / κ).** Ch 13 gestured at "the residual renormalised over the comoving volume at gauge birth." There may be an NTE-native scale (vacuum energy at gauge-birth diluted by the e-fold expansion; a horizon/projection quantity) that sets Λ. **This is the single highest number-dialing-risk route in the programme** — it is tempting to find a combination of (N_efold, κ, M_R, v_EW) that lands 10⁻¹²² and call it a derivation. That is precisely the S6-0.997× / a₄-270β_GB failure mode in a new costume. Defer or gate hard (see §5).

**P5 — Honest open gap (P0 + P1 combined).** Claim the structural quietness; mark the value as not-currently-derived. A respectable scientific position — most serious CC approaches explain the absence of the catastrophe without nailing the value.
