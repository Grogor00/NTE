## Issue C3.1 — §"Where This Leaves the Photon and the Entangled Pair" Ch 1 recap mildly redundant (density)

The recap of Ch 1's photon claim:

> Chapter 1 established that the photon is not a thing in flight — it's the geometric connection between emission and absorption, a single object with two readings: from outside, length in space and time; along the connection itself, both extents vanish together.

The "two readings: from outside... along the connection itself..." restates Ch 1's central content. Necessary as a refresher, but a few words longer than needed.

**Recommended patch C3.1:** trim the recap to one sentence:

> Chapter 1 established that the photon is not a thing in flight — it's the geometric connection between emission and absorption, a single object with two readings.

The "two readings" framing is now sufficiently load-bearing (also used in Ch 2's rod-and-ball schema closing) that it can carry the reference without expansion. The Ch 3-specific new content ("The photon is on the boundary of the expanding S³, riding the expansion as the manifold unfolds...") then takes the explanatory weight.

Net saving: ~25 words.

## Issue C3.2 — §"What Expansion Means" closing summary slightly anchor-redundant (density, minor)

The closing sentence of §"What Expansion Means" reads:

> For now, hold the picture: *N* is the geometric count of expansion, *t* is what emerges from *N* with |Φ_NTE| setting the rate, and the universe is an expanding closed three-dimensional space whose radius has grown so vast through a huge run of e-folds that it appears, locally, flat.

This sentence restates everything the section has just covered. It functions as a section-close anchor — the kind of "hold this in mind" summary that helps reader retention. Acceptable as pedagogy, but worth flagging that the |Φ_NTE| reference ("|Φ_NTE| setting the rate") hasn't been pedagogically built yet — it's introduced properly in Ch 4 and Ch 5. The reader is asked to hold a placeholder.

**Recommended patch C3.2 (optional, lower priority):** trim the |Φ_NTE| reference, keeping the structural anchor without front-loading the next chapter's notation:

> For now, hold the picture: *N* is the geometric count of expansion, *t* is what emerges from *N* with the boundary expansion field setting the rate, and the universe is an expanding closed three-dimensional space whose radius has grown so vast through a huge run of e-folds that it appears, locally, flat.

Replacing "|Φ_NTE|" with "the boundary expansion field" defers the notation to Ch 4 where it lands properly. Saves no words; cleaner pedagogical sequencing.

PC verdict: optional. If Greg prefers the symbol introduced early as forward-pointer, leave as-is.
