# Emergent time and a shared antiunitary modular conjugation

**An NTE-side reading of Susskind's spontaneous T-breaking in de Sitter space (arXiv:2601.01666v2)**

## Abstract

Susskind (2026) argues that quantum de Sitter space requires spontaneous breaking of time-reversal **T**, with **T** read as a gauge symmetry (Harlow–Numasawa). The deepest content is a claim about the status of time. The Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) framework meets this at the same level, and — once stated correctly — in the same mathematical language: **modular (Tomita–Takesaki) theory.**

In NTE, time is emergent (t = iΔN from e-fold depth on a bilateral S³ surface). Its bilateral structure is organised by **CPN**, the simultaneous reversal of charge, parity, and e-fold direction (N → −N). Crucially — and contrary to an earlier draft of this note — **CPN is antiunitary**. In the finite spectral-triple realisation it is the **modular conjugation J** of the Janus crossed product A_F ⋊_τ ℤ₂: the *unitary* object is the bare sector swap τ (the antipodal map), while CPN = (complex conjugation)∘τ is antilinear, with J Δ^{it} J = Δ^{−it}. CPN is therefore the **same operator class** as Susskind's antiunitary T, in a Bisognano–Wichmann / thermofield-double structure.

The genuine difference is sharper than "unitary vs antiunitary": **Susskind breaks his antiunitary T; NTE keeps its antiunitary CPN exact** (the fundamental symmetry relating the two Janus sectors) **and spontaneously selects an orientation** (which sector is realised) at gauge birth. CPT is recovered as an emergent, post-locking statement. We submit this for adversarial reading; we claim no derivation of either framework from the other.

## 2. Susskind, minimally

A finite-dim static-patch theory, T-invariant H, a maximally-mixed T-symmetric but cluster-violating state, spontaneous T-breaking selecting a clustering vacuum, and (Harlow–Numasawa) T as gauge with observables dressed to a forward/backward clock. ## 3. NTE: emergent time and the antiunitary modular conjugation

Time is emergent: t = iΔN, dt = dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²), H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² (Companion 5 §3). The bilateral surface carries CPN = C·P·(N→−N). In the finite spectral-triple model (A_F = 𝕆₊ ⊕ 𝕆₋, H_F = ℂ¹⁸⁰):

- The **sector swap τ(a,b) = (b,a)** is **unitary**, implemented by U (U² = I). This is the geometric "antipodal map."
- **CPN(a,b) = (b̄, ā)** is **antilinear**; it equals the **modular conjugation J** for the τ-invariant state: J π(a) J⁻¹ = π(CPN(a)), with **J Δ^{it} J = Δ^{−it}** (modular-flow reversal).

So CPN reverses modular flow **between** the two Janus sectors while, **within** each sector, emergent time runs forward (Ch 10) — a Bisognano–Wichmann / thermofield-double arrangement, structurally Susskind's static-patch-and-mirror. The arrow is preserved precisely because the antiunitarity acts between sectors, like the TFD's J, not within one.

This is the correct correspondence: Susskind's forward/backward clock split (carried by antiunitary T) and NTE's +N/−N orientation split (carried by antiunitary CPN = J) are the **same operator class in the same modular framework**.

## 4. The genuine difference (sharper than v0.3's)

Not unitary-vs-antiunitary (both are antiunitary). Rather:

- **Susskind:** the antiunitary symmetry **T is spontaneously broken** (and gauged à la Harlow–Numasawa). The feature that breaks T selects a clustering vacuum.
- **NTE:** the antiunitary symmetry **CPN is exact** — it is the fundamental conjugation relating the two sectors, never broken. What is spontaneously selected at gauge birth is the **orientation** (a ℤ₂ label: which sector is ours). CPT is the emergent local description post-selection.

In one line: *you break an antiunitary time-reflection and gauge it; we keep an antiunitary modular conjugation exact and select an orientation under it.* The shared object (antiunitary modular conjugation) makes the comparison precise; the difference (broken-and-gauged vs exact-with-orientation-selection) is the substantive question to put to an expert.

## 5. CPT is emergent, not violated

The CPT theorem's premises (fixed Lorentzian time, locality) are not in force pre-locking (N < N_lock), where only e-folds are meaningful (Part 3 §VIII.7). There the operative discrete symmetry is the antiunitary CPN; ordinary CPT is recovered post-locking. CPT is downstream of time-emergence, not broken by it.

## 6. Withdrawn (honest scope)

The v0.1/v0.2 Π₋ cluster-decomposition "mirror" is withdrawn: the symmetric pre-selection configuration lives in the pre-temporal octonionic, non-associative regime with no entanglement-as-geometry (Part 3 §VIII.7, §20.3, §22.13), so the entanglement-based cluster argument has no foundation there; and the gauge-birth instant is the selection instant, leaving no metastable symmetric phase for breaking to act on. The §3–§4 parallel does not rely on cluster decomposition.
