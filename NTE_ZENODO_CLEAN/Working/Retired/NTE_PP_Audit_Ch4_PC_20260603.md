## Issue C4.1 — §"Time Dilation as Boundary Field Weakening" structural-commitment framing restated four times (density — primary issue)

This section's structural-identity claim — that |Φ_NTE| **is** curvature, named differently, with the full derivation still open work — gets stated, then restated, then restated again, then summarised, then re-summarised. Specifically:

**First statement (early in section):**
> "Under the framework's structural commitment, |Φ_NTE| is not a separate field that responds to curvature through some additional mechanism — it *is* curvature, viewed through a different parameterization, the same surface structure named twice."

**Second statement (~5 paragraphs later):**
> "The framework now commits to the structural reading that |Φ_NTE| is not a separate field responding to curvature through some additional mechanism — it *is* curvature, viewed through a different parameterization, the same surface structure named twice."

Near-verbatim duplicate. The phrase *"same surface structure named twice"* appears in both. This is **exactly** the pattern Greg flagged.

**Third pass (next paragraph):**
> "the framework is consistent with observed gravitational time dilation in the regimes that have been tested, the structural reading is now committed, and the underlying derivation of that reading's full content is open work."

**Fourth pass (same paragraph chunk):**
> "The structural reading is now committed; whether the full derivation can be reached is open work; that the picture is consistent with what has been measured is established."

Tricolon restating the same three points in different word-order.

**Fifth pass (next paragraph):**
> "A note on where this leaves the framework's structural commitments more broadly. The structural-identity reading is committed here, though the full derivation remains open work."

Said yet again.

**Recommended patch C4.1:** the section has three substantive things to say:
(a) The structural reading — |Φ_NTE| **is** curvature, two names for one structure
(b) The framework is consistent with observed gravitational time dilation; full derivation open
(c) Time dilation is a reading the framework commits to; κ is what the framework stakes its case on

Each of these should appear ONCE. Suggested compressed version of the §"Two pieces of honesty are in order here..." through end-of-section pattern:

> Two pieces of honesty are in order here. First, the framework's account is consistent with the observed time dilation; it does not yet *derive* it from first principles. Reproducing the standard prediction at first post-Newtonian order is not the same thing as having a complete derivation of how the boundary expansion field's local configuration produces the dilation effect. On the framework's two-field reading, the Standard Model Higgs (which gives mass to electrons and quarks and would otherwise be the natural candidate for "the field that responds to mass-energy") is *not* the field involved; the boundary expansion field |Φ_NTE| is, and |Φ_NTE| does not give mass to particles. The structural identification is committed; the full derivation is technical-corpus work the framework continues to develop.
>
> Second, even with the derivation open, the structural reading does real work. Standard relativity treats gravitational time dilation as a property of the metric, with no further structural reading specified. The framework reads it as a consequence of how the boundary expansion field distributes itself across the manifold, with mass-energy's local concentration of curvature being the same fact as the local weakening of |Φ_NTE|, and the dt/dN conversion rate registering the change as clocks slowing in gravity wells.
>
> The framework picks its empirical battles deliberately. Time dilation is a reading it commits to; the dark-matter ratio κ — a later chapter's territory — is the number it stakes its case on.

Trims roughly 280-330 words from the section while preserving every substantive point. The "same surface structure named twice" phrase, used once early in the section, doesn't need to reappear.

## Issue C4.2 — §"The Pre-Temporal Epoch" multi-clause gauge-birth sentence (density / readability)

> The reason any of this matters is that the gauge birth — the structural junction at N = N_lock where the Euclidean pre-temporal geometry joins the Lorentzian post-temporal regime, where the boundary expansion field's configuration is such that dt/dN is well-defined for all N > N_lock, and where the Standard Model gauge symmetries are present (with the SM Higgs's identity as the field that gives mass to electrons and quarks among them) — is a structural feature whose content the framework places at a specific *N*.

One sentence, three nested clauses bracketed by em-dashes, parenthetical inside. Hard to parse on first read. The content is valuable but the syntax fights the reader.

**Recommended patch C4.2:** break into 2-3 sentences:

> The reason any of this matters is that the gauge birth — the structural junction at *N* = N_lock — is a feature whose content the framework places at a specific *N*. The gauge birth is where the Euclidean pre-temporal geometry joins the Lorentzian post-temporal regime, where the boundary expansion field's configuration becomes such that dt/dN is well-defined for all *N* > N_lock, and where the Standard Model gauge symmetries are present (with the SM Higgs's identity as the field that gives mass to electrons and quarks among them).

Same content, parseable on first read.

## Issue C4.3 — §"Where This Leaves the Photon (Again)" minor restatement at close (density, minor)

The closing line:
> "Time, for any physical system, is the integrated outcome of how that system's matter content engages with the universe's expansion. A different engagement pattern would give a different time. A photon, with no engagement at all, has no time."

The "A different engagement pattern would give a different time. A photon, with no engagement at all, has no time" pair restates the section's main thesis once more after it's already been stated three times in the chapter.

**Recommended patch C4.3:** drop the last sentence ("A photon, with no engagement at all, has no time") — the previous paragraphs have established this clearly. The penultimate sentence ("A different engagement pattern would give a different time") is the cleaner forward-pointing close.

Net saving: ~12 words.

## Issue C4.4 — Possible Pratchett-adjacent phrasing at chapter close (Greg directive 2026-05-31)

The chapter's final two sentences:
> "Everything that has happened along the way stays happened. The geometry remembers."

The phrase "**Everything that has happened... stays happened**" is structurally similar to the Pratchett-adjacent callback ("everything that happens, geometrically, stays happened") that was supposed to be removed in Cursor's Pratchett-removal pass. The current Ch 4 v7.2 phrasing is **softer** than the original — uses past tense, drops "geometrically" — but the rhythm and pattern are close enough to flag.

Greg's directive on the original phrase was clear (famously Pratchett, cannot use). Worth re-checking whether the softened version is far enough from the original to be safe. PC view: borderline. The geometry-remembers framing is doing real work; could be preserved with a different lead-in.

**Recommended patch C4.4 (Greg review):** if the softened phrasing is also Pratchett-adjacent for Greg's taste, replace with:

> The geometry of expansion is the record of what has occurred. The geometry remembers.

Removes the "stays happened" rhythm while preserving the "geometry remembers" close.

PC defers to Greg on this one — the call is whether the rhythm is too close to the Pratchett original or whether the past-tense softening is sufficient.
