## §2 — Verdict: not forced at v0.1; a one-parameter (direction) family with a favoured structural anchor

The Hopf fibration S¹→S³→S² has a geometrically-defined fibre direction in S³, but its **embedding in the observable cosmological frame today is not pinned by current corpus structure** (scoping §3.2). Two sub-questions, neither closed:

1. **What selects the fibre orientation at gauge birth?** The bifurcation (Ch 10 v5) freezes the bilateral asymmetry along *some* axis, but the corpus does not derive which S³ direction that is — it is fixed by the bifurcation's boundary conditions, which are not specified to this resolution.
2. **What observable axis does it project to today?** Even given a gauge-birth axis, the projection to celestial coordinates depends on post-bifurcation evolution that the corpus has not tracked.

So **n̂_pref is a fit parameter at v0.1**, with the **CMB kinematic dipole direction (l,b)=(264°,48°)** as the structurally-favoured anchor (scoping §3.3): it is the minimal commitment, matches the Phase I.5 Wagenveld-near-CMB finding, and is the natural macroscopic indicator of our "+N rather than −N" orientation. A one-parameter direction is still a tight constraint against four independent survey directions, but this is **not** a parameter-free directional prediction.
