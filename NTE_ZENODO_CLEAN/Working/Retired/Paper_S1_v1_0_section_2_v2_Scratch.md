## §2. The σ_t / KMS-compatibility argument for V_NTE form closure

**Claim.** Three structural premises rooted in NTE's existing corpus commitments — (P1) σ_t / KMS-compatibility at gauge birth, (P2) 4D conformal invariance of Φ_NTE's action, (P3) Higgs-portal genericness under Path B — together force the post-gauge-birth effective action for Φ_NTE to the form

$$
S[\Phi_{\text{NTE}}, H] \;=\; \int \sqrt{g}\, d^4x \left[ -\tfrac{1}{2}(\partial \Phi)^2 \;-\; \tfrac{1}{12} R\, \Phi^2 \;-\; \lambda \Phi^4 \;-\; 2e\, |H|^2 \Phi^2 \right]
\tag{2.1}
$$

with the coefficient `1/12` of `RΦ²` corresponding to the conformal coupling `ξ = 1/6` in NTE's ratified Mechanism B convention (via `(ξ/2)RΦ² = (1/12)RΦ²`).

This is the same effective-action form as Marcolli's spectral-action expansion `V_E(x) = λ₀ x⁴ / (1 + (κ₀²/12) x²)²` reduced to leading + subleading orders, but derived here from NTE-internal structural reasoning. **No spectral-action / Chamseddine-Connes machinery is invoked**; the argument runs purely on NTE's pre-temporal/post-temporal architecture + the algebraic constraints of 4D conformal invariance + Path B's neutrino-handling commitments. The result is **structural confluence** with Marcolli's V_E, not field-identification with Chamseddine-Connes's σ. (Confluence framing: Jammer 2026-05-10 verdict, Greg-ratified 2026-05-11; see Paper S1 §7 below.)

### §2.1 Premise P1 — σ_t / KMS-compatibility at gauge birth

Per the canonical σ_t framework adopted at v10.10 (Charter v1.3 §2.5), NTE's pre-temporal and post-temporal regimes correspond to distinct state-types on the spectral triple algebra: pre-temporally, the state on A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 is in a generic / non-KMS regime; post-temporally, the state ω_{Φ_NTE} is **KMS at β = β_GB** with respect to the modular automorphism group σ_t generated by Tomita-Takesaki on (A_F, ω_{Φ_NTE}, β_GB). The geometric event that interpolates between them is the **C¹ kink in dz/dy at gauge birth**, identified in Companion 8 Stage 2 finding F2 and integrated into the corpus at §VIII.1.4 (the Wick rotation reading, v10.6) — now retained as the post-temporal-side derived special-case route under the canonical σ_t framing.

**Structural content of P1**:

- **Pre-temporal → generic state on A_F**: σ_t modular flow exists (Tomita-Takesaki applies to any state on any von Neumann algebra, including the GNS completion of A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 per Task 1 §3.2 GNS-representation route), but it does not align with any Lorentzian-time evolution (there is no Lorentzian time pre-temporally). The state is **not KMS at any specific β** — it is generic / non-KMS-regularized.
- **Post-temporal → KMS state on A_F at β = β_GB**: the state ω_{Φ_NTE} specified by the canonical Φ_NTE-trajectory configuration is KMS at β = β_GB with respect to σ_t. Via the standard correspondence (Connes-Rovelli 1994 §3.1), σ_t can be related to Lorentzian-time evolution: t = β_GB · τ where τ is geometric time post-gauge-birth. The Wick rotation reading recovers the periodicity of Euclidean correlation functions in imaginary time as the KMS condition's standard derivation route in QFT-with-Hamiltonian settings.
- **The C¹ kink in dz/dy at gauge birth IS the geometric signature** of the state-type transition from generic (non-KMS) to KMS at β_GB. The Wick rotation reading is the post-temporal-side viewpoint of the same structural event. The σ_t / KMS framing is **more general** (applies pre-temporally and post-temporally uniformly without requiring time-like-concept extension pre-temporally) — the Wick-rotation framing is recovered as the derived special-case route at the post-temporal side.
- **The transition is structural, not a choice**: NTE's no-time-ordering pre-temporal axiom + emergent-time post-temporal axiom + v10.10 σ_t framework canonical adoption (Charter v1.3 §2.5) force it. Cross-coherence with Jammer Tier 2 Salvio 2025 reframing (gauge birth as first-order phase transition / state-type transition non-KMS → KMS at T_GB) is canonical (per AC convergence assessment §6.2).

**For the action form on Φ_NTE**: any effective action descending from pre-temporal structure must support a state on A_F that becomes KMS at β_GB post-gauge-birth — i.e., must be **σ_t / KMS-compatible** in the sense that correlation functions admit analytic continuation across the gauge-birth state-transition. (Under the Wick rotation special-case reading, this corresponds to the action being Wick-rotation-compatible with t → iτ analytic continuation of measures and signature factors applying on both sides of the gauge-birth event.)

This is the structural basis of P1. It is **necessary** (forced by NTE's axioms + v10.10 σ_t framework canonical adoption) but, by itself, **not sufficient** to fix the action form: many non-conformal scalar actions (massive scalars; scalars with arbitrary self-interactions) admit σ_t / KMS-compatibility. The remaining structural strength comes from premises P2 + P3.

**Corpus reference**: §VIII.1.4 V_NTE Stage 2 F2 integration (the Wick rotation reading at v10.6, retained as the post-temporal-side derivation route); Companion 8 Stage 2 F2; v10.10 σ_t framework canonical complement adoption (Charter v1.3 §2.5); Task 1 §3.2 GNS-representation route for non-associative A_F.

**What P1 alone forces**: any Φ_NTE action must be σ_t / KMS-compatible (equivalently, Wick-rotation-compatible in the post-temporal special-case derivation).
**What P1 alone does NOT force**: conformal invariance, specific value of ξ, specific potential form.

### §2.2 Premise P2 — 4D conformal invariance of Φ_NTE's action

[UNCHANGED from v1; reproduced for completeness]

**The separable commitment**: Φ_NTE's effective action on the post-gauge-birth manifold (closed S³ × R, with 4D Lorentzian signature post-Wick-rotation) is Weyl-invariant — i.e., invariant under local conformal rescalings `g_μν → Ω²(x) g_μν` accompanied by `Φ → Ω^((2-d)/2)(x) Φ` for `d = 4`.

This is **not** a consequence of P1; it is an additional structural commitment specific to NTE's closed-S³ cosmology + surface ontology (Companion 5). It is corpus-committed via the Mechanism B supersession ratified by Greg 2026-05-11: NTE's ξR|Φ|² non-minimal coupling (already in the action at §2.2 of the corpus) is now pinned to **ξ = 1/6** — the unique value in 4D that makes the free-scalar action conformally invariant under Weyl rescalings.

**The mathematical content of P2**:
The conformal coupling value in d dimensions is `ξ_c = (d-2)/(4(d-1))`. For `d = 4`, `ξ_c = 1/6`. Under this coupling, the free-scalar kinetic + curvature term is Weyl-invariant. Conformal invariance further restricts the allowed self-interaction and Higgs-coupling terms in 4D:
- **Quartic** `Φ⁴`: allowed (marginal under 4D conformal — `Φ` has mass dimension 1; `Φ⁴` is dimension 4 = spacetime dimension).
- **Cubic** `Φ³`: forbidden by Z_2 symmetry on Φ_NTE (see P3 below). Even ignoring Z_2, `Φ³` has dimension 3, conformally allowed only with an explicit mass scale, breaking scale invariance.
- **Sextic and higher** `Φ⁶`, `Φ⁸`, ...: irrelevant under 4D conformal — dimensions 6, 8, ... > 4. Suppressed in the deep IR; vanish under conformal RG flow. (Note: V_NTE's F3 on-trajectory form `V_NTE = (3β_NTE²/(8πG))Φ⁴ − (β_NTE²/8)Φ⁶` carries a `Φ⁶` term; this is not in tension with P2 — F3 is a trajectory expression for the running |Φ(N)|², not a fundamental Lagrangian term. The relevant Lagrangian is what P2 constrains.)
- **Mass term** `m²Φ²`: forbidden by 4D conformal invariance (dimension 2; relevant operator; breaks Weyl invariance). The non-minimal coupling `(1/12)RΦ²` plays the role the mass term would play but in a conformally-respecting way.

**Why P2 is corpus-committed**:
- The `ξR|Φ|²` term is already in the action at corpus §2.2.
- The Mechanism B supersession (Greg-ratified 2026-05-11) commits to ξ = 1/6 specifically.
- The closed-S³ cosmology + surface ontology framework (Companion 5) is structurally compatible only with conformally-invariant matter content — non-conformal terms would break the surface ontology's natural Weyl-rescaling freedom.
- This supersedes §18's Mechanism B Higgs-inflation framing (which used large ξ ≫ 1/6 — breaks conformal invariance). The n_s = 0.9685 prediction value is retained; the derivation route shifts to Companion 8 F4 closure route (γ') under the ξ = 1/6 conformally-coupled action.

**What P1 + P2 together force**: the kinetic + curvature-coupling + quartic structure
```
-(1/2)(∂Φ)² - (1/12)RΦ² - λΦ⁴
```
up to the quartic coupling `λ` (unfixed at this level).

**The leading allowed Higgs-portal coupling** under all four constraints is `2e|H|²Φ²`, with `e` a generic dimensionless coupling. By "generic" we mean: under all four constraints, this coupling is forced to be non-vanishing in general — it is the leading allowed coupling, and there is no structural reason for `e = 0` exactly. The specific value of `e` is empirical (fixable from confluence with electroweak phenomenology); the existence and quartic-portal form are structural.

**What P1 + P2 + P3 together force**: the action form (2.1) above.

### §2.4 The forced action form, restated

Combining the three premises:

- **P1 (σ_t / KMS-compatibility at gauge birth)** forces σ_t / KMS-compatibility of the action — equivalently, Wick-rotation-compatibility in the post-temporal-side derivation route (Companion 8 Stage 2 F2 + §VIII.1.4 v10.6 reading retained as derived special case).
- **P2 (4D conformal invariance)** forces kinetic + (1/12)RΦ² + quartic-only structure for Φ_NTE's self-interactions; mass terms forbidden; sextic+ irrelevant.
- **P3 (Higgs-portal genericness under Path B + Z_2 + renormalizability)** forces the leading Higgs coupling to be `2e|H|²Φ²`.

The unique action form satisfying all three is (2.1):

$$
S[\Phi_{\text{NTE}}, H] \;=\; \int \sqrt{g}\, d^4x \left[ -\tfrac{1}{2}(\partial \Phi)^2 \;-\; \tfrac{1}{12} R\, \Phi^2 \;-\; \lambda \Phi^4 \;-\; 2e\, |H|^2 \Phi^2 \right]
$$

**The structural confluence**: this is the same effective-action form Marcolli's V_E expansion produces at leading + subleading order on the Chamseddine-Connes spectral-action side, but here derived **purely from NTE's internal structural commitments** (σ_t / KMS-compatibility at gauge birth + 4D conformal invariance + Path B + Z_2 + 4D renormalizability). No spectral-action / heat-kernel machinery is invoked. No identification with Chamseddine-Connes's σ field is made (and is in fact rejected by the "no RH/LH neutrino" structural lever — see §7 of this paper).

### §2.5 Cross-check at sextic order — V_NTE F3 ↔ Marcolli V_E

[UNCHANGED from v1; reproduced for completeness]

The σ_t / KMS-compatibility argument fixes the action form at the **renormalizable + relevant** level. Sextic and higher terms in the action are irrelevant under 4D conformal RG flow, and the argument is silent about them. However, NTE's V_NTE on-trajectory expression from Companion 8 F3 carries a sextic term:

$$
V_{\text{NTE}} = \tfrac{3\alpha^2}{8\pi G} \Phi^4 - \tfrac{\alpha^2}{8} \Phi^6
$$

This is a **trajectory expression for the running scalar potential evaluated along the H = β_NTE|Φ|² Friedmann coupling**, not a fundamental Lagrangian sextic term. The sextic coefficient is set by NTE's specific cosmological coupling, not by a fundamental `Φ⁶` operator in the action.

**Matching V_NTE on-trajectory to Marcolli's V_E `V_E(x) = λ₀ x⁴ / (1 + (κ₀²/12) x²)²` at leading + subleading orders**:

- Marcolli V_E expanded: `V_E ≈ λ₀ x⁴ − (κ₀²λ₀/6) x⁶ + O(x⁸)`. (Note: Marcolli's V_E denominator carries `/12` under CC's ξ = 1/12 convention. Under NTE's ratified ξ = 1/6 convention, the matching shifts: see Charter v1.3 §2.5 and Working Note `NTE_Connes_Programme_P0_9_Marcolli_VE_Convention_v1.md` for the convention-dependence analysis.)
- Quartic match: `λ₀ = 3α²/(8πG)`.
- Sextic match (under Marcolli's ξ = 1/12): `κ₀² λ₀ / 6 = α²/8` → `κ₀² = 2π/M_Pl²` → `κ₀ = √(2π)/M_Pl` (the σ↔Φ_NTE confluence verdict §5.2 result).
- **Under NTE's own ξ = 1/6 convention** (Mechanism B supersession ratified): the sextic coefficient picks up factor 2 → `κ₀ = √π/M_Pl`. The factor `√2` shift is real and convention-dependent (per Charter v1.3 §2.5 + P0.9 working note).

**Honest disclosure (per Charter v1.3 §2.5 vocabulary discipline and Manifest Lesson #28 recognition-gate discipline)**: the `√(2π)` factor on the κ₀-from-V_E-matching leg is **convention-dependent**. Only the T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) leg (Companion 9 Session 3) carries `√(2π)` cleanly as a structural feature (via Y₀₀(S¹) on the Hopf fibre). The κ₀-from-V_E-matching leg's `√(2π)` is a coincidence under the specific convention choice ξ = 1/12; under NTE's own ratified ξ = 1/6, this leg gives `√π/M_Pl`, not `√(2π)/M_Pl`.

**What the cross-check shows**: the leading λ₀ match (which is dimensionally forced) and the order-of-magnitude sextic match (both routes give κ₀² ~ 1/M_Pl²) are **structurally meaningful**. The specific numerical factor distinguishing `√(2π)` from `√π` is convention-flexible. The structural confluence claim is preserved at the order-of-magnitude / dimensional level; the specific numerical factor is convention-flexible and depends on which side's ξ value is used to compare.

### §2.6 What §2 establishes and what it doesn't

**§2 establishes**:

Connes Programme's central deliverable (Paper S2 heat-kernel computation on `Hopf-S³ × S¹_{β_GB} × A_F`) is a **complementary** route to the same form, not a prerequisite for §2's argument.

Cross-stream coherence with Jammer Tier 2 Salvio 2025 reframing (gauge birth as first-order phase transition / state-type transition non-KMS → KMS at T_GB) achieved at canonical framing level.

**§2 does NOT establish**:

1. The specific numerical values of `λ` (the quartic coupling) or `e` (the Higgs-portal coupling). These are empirical / fixable from electroweak + cosmological data.
2. The K(Φ) explicit functional form. This is open (Companion 9 Session 5 working target; Paper S2 closure target).
3. The H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² Friedmann coupling. This is the load-bearing NTE-specific commitment that drives V_NTE's trajectory form; it is **NOT** forced by P1+P2+P3 and remains an axiomatic input.
4. The specific n_s and r prediction values. The Mechanism B supersession (Greg-ratified 2026-05-11) commits NTE to deriving these via Companion 8 F4 closure route (γ') under the ξ = 1/6 conformally-coupled action; the numerical target values (n_s = 0.9685; r ~ 10⁻³) are retained; the derivation routes are open.
5. The specific `√(2π)` factor in κ₀ — convention-dependent (§2.5 above). Resolution awaits Paper S2 heat-kernel direct computation (the Connes Programme central deliverable) or a tighter NTE-internal derivation of the κ₀ from geometric invariants.
