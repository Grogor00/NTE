## No-numerology consistency note

The framework's stated discipline is that no quantity is assembled from measured inputs to match a measured output. Applied consistently, this **withdraws** three constructions previously carried in the derived ledger: the $\beta_{\mathrm{NTE}}$ characterisation, the $\theta_{13}$ formula, and the Cabibbo formula. Each took observed inputs plus an interpretive coefficient and reproduced a target observable — the signature of number-fitting. Their withdrawal removes nothing structural (each was already flagged as "not derived from first principles"); it brings the ledger into line with the discipline and is part of what distinguishes this framework from coupling-constant "derivations" elsewhere in its class (see Positioning, Part 1). What remains in the derived tier is structural; what is uncertain is labelled conditional or open.
