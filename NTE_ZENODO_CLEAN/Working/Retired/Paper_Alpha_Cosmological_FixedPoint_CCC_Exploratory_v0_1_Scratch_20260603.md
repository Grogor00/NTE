# α as a Cosmological Fixed Point

**A conformal-cyclic self-consistency programme for NTE's foundational constant**

> **Register note.** This is an *exploratory* paper. The companion paper (`Paper_PhiNTE_Necessity_and_Irreducibility`) establishes — rigorously and at corpus-core register — that α is, by every closed pathway, a fundamental non-derivable constant. This paper takes up the *single surviving* route by which that verdict could be overturned: that α is not inherited but is the unique fixed point of a cosmological self-consistency condition. Everything here is conjecture-and-programme, clearly labelled, and could equally lead nowhere. It is written separately precisely because its epistemic status is different from the necessity result, and because — if it works — it is the seed of a new layer of physics (an inter-aeon dynamics) rather than a tidying of the existing one.

## 1. The question

NTE's single foundational constant is α in H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² (β_NTE = 2.38 × 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁻¹). The necessity paper shows α survives as irreducible against the boundary action (α-agnostic), the spectral action (a_4 = 0), naturalness (α is anti-natural), the empirical characterisation (construction, not derivation), the Hartle–Hawking bound, and the asymptotic-safety UV fixed point (closed-negative: α is a *relevant* IR scale the UV fixed point leaves free). One structure was *not* closed: a self-consistency fixed point in which the cosmological output of one cosmic epoch sets α for the next. This paper formalises that structure and asks whether it can be made into a derivation.

## 2. The latent fixed-point equation

Two corpus facts combine into a self-consistency condition.

**(i) The N → ∞ end-state.** As the boundary expansion completes, |Φ_NTE|² → v_EW²√Ω_Λ, and the fractal-foam transition (§XV) fragments the boundary into new Janus seeds — a Penrose-style aeon succession, with the dS/CFT future-infinity structure as the conformal interface.

**(ii) The characterisation circularity.** The §2.4.2 expression α = (v_EW/M_P)^{3/2} · √2 / (π M_P N_total) carries a known caveat: **N_total depends on Ω_Λ, and Ω_Λ depends on α** (via Ω_Λ = Λ(α)/(3α²v_EW⁴) and H₀ = αv_EW²). In the necessity analysis this circularity is a *weakness* — it is why the characterisation is "construction, not a test."

**The reframe (this paper's central move).** That same circularity, read not as a fit but as an equation, is a one-variable self-consistency condition:

> **α = F(Ω_Λ(α))**, with F bundling the characterisation structure and Ω_Λ(α) the cosmological feedback.

The weakness of the characterisation is, structurally, the *seed* of a derivation: a circular fit and a self-consistency fixed point are the same equation read in opposite directions. The question is whether the fixed point is **forced** (unique, attracting, landing on 2.38×10⁻⁴⁷) and whether F can be built from **independent** physics rather than from the fit.

## 3. The circularity trap (why this is hard, stated precisely)

If F is taken *from the characterisation formula itself*, then "solve α = F(Ω_Λ(α))" merely re-derives the value already built into the formula — a tautology, not a derivation. **The programme has content only if F is constructed independently** — from the actual inter-aeon nucleation dynamics (how the parent aeon's end-state seeds the daughter's α) — and then *compared* to the characterisation, with agreement being the non-trivial result. This is the single discipline that separates a genuine result from a circular one, and it governs everything below.

## 4. Two sub-cases

**Sub-case A — bare geometric seed (fixed point trivial).** If the daughter Janus seed sets α′ purely from its own boundary geometry (e.g. the S³ curvature scale at nucleation), then α′ is the *same* every aeon — a bare geometric constant, independent of the parent's Ω. The "fixed point" is trivial and the conclusion folds back into the necessity paper's: **α is an irreducible geometric constant.** No new physics; the CCC route collapses to "α is the seed scale."

**Sub-case B — genuine recursion (fixed point non-trivial).** If the daughter's α′ depends on the parent's end-state |Φ|²_∞ = v_EW²√Ω_Λ (hence on the parent's α), then α′ = F(Ω_Λ(α)) is a real recursion. A stable universe requires a fixed point; the *observed* α would be that fixed point, and its value would be *explained* (not inherited) if the fixed point is unique and attracting. **This is the case that would constitute new physics**: an inter-aeon dynamics selecting the foundational constant. It requires the §XV foam-nucleation map, which is currently undeveloped.

## 5. What must be derived (the programme)

1. **The nucleation map F.** From §XV foam dynamics: given a parent aeon ending at |Φ|²_∞ = v_EW²√Ω_Λ, what α′ does a daughter Janus seed nucleate with? This is the core deliverable and the hardest. Candidate structures to test: (a) conformal-rescaling transfer (Penrose CCC: the parent's conformal end-state sets the daughter's scale ratio, hence α′); (b) entropy/area transfer (the daughter's seed scale set by the parent's de Sitter horizon entropy S_dS, linking to N_lock = S_dS/4); (c) spectral transfer (the daughter's Dirac/heat-kernel data inherited across the foam).
2. **Fixed-point analysis.** Existence, uniqueness, and stability (attracting vs repelling) of α* = F(Ω_Λ(α*)). A repelling fixed point would *not* explain α (fine-tuning persists); only an attracting one does.
3. **Value check.** Does α* = 2.38×10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁻¹ emerge, *using F built independently of §2.4.2* (per §3)?
4. **Independence audit.** Verify no characterisation-derived quantity has leaked into F (the trap).

## 6. External anchors (why this is a real research direction, not just NTE-internal)

- **Penrose Conformal Cyclic Cosmology.** NTE's N → ∞ end-state + foam fragmentation is structurally a CCC aeon boundary; NTE supplies a concrete boundary field (|Φ|²_∞ = v_EW²√Ω_Λ) and a microphysics (Janus seeds) that CCC leaves schematic. A derived F would be a contribution *to* CCC, not only to NTE.
- **Strominger dS/CFT.** The future-infinity dual at N → ∞ is the natural home for the conformal-transfer version of F (sub-case B, candidate a).
- **Self-organised criticality / fixed-point cosmology.** The idea that a fundamental constant is selected as an attractor of a cosmological recursion is a known (if speculative) theme; NTE would give it a specific realisation in α.

## 7. Honest prospects

This could go three ways: **(B-attracting)** F is derived, the fixed point is unique and attracting, and α* matches — α gains a floor and NTE acquires an inter-aeon dynamics (major new physics); **(A)** the seed is bare-geometric — α is confirmed irreducibly geometric (the necessity paper's end-state, no new physics, but a clean closure); **(B-repelling or mismatch)** a recursion exists but doesn't explain α — interesting but not a derivation. Only the first overturns the necessity verdict. The author's prior, stated honestly: the trap in §3 is severe, and most naive constructions of F will either be circular or collapse to sub-case A. But the prize (and the connection to Penrose/Strominger) justifies the dig.

## 8. Programme milestones

- **M1:** read/develop §XV foam-nucleation dynamics; write the candidate F structures (a/b/c) explicitly.
- **M2:** for each candidate, determine whether α′ depends on parent Ω (sub-case B) or not (sub-case A).
- **M3:** for any sub-case-B candidate, do the fixed-point analysis (existence/uniqueness/stability).
- **M4:** independence audit + value check against 2.38×10⁻⁴⁷.
- **M5:** if any candidate survives M1–M4, two-Scratch convergence + AC review; this would re-open the necessity paper's α-irreducibility verdict (a ratification-level event).
- Cross-anchor throughout to Penrose CCC and dS/CFT for external legibility.

## 9. Relationship to the necessity paper

That paper stands on its own and does **not** depend on this one: α is irreducible by every closed route, and this programme is the single open route. If this programme fails (sub-case A or mismatch), the necessity paper is *confirmed*. If it succeeds (sub-case B-attracting), the necessity paper's §6/§7 are amended to "α floored by cosmological fixed point." Either way the necessity result is the secure base camp; this is the expedition.
