## 1. Penrose CCC — recursion framework, not a derivation [ASSESSMENT]

CCC connects aeons by conformal rescaling: the Λ-dominated de Sitter end-state of one aeon maps to the big bang of the next. **It does not derive Λ or any constant** — it *requires* Λ > 0 as input (to get the conformal de Sitter end-state) and propagates geometry across the join. Constants are assumed, not produced. NTE borrows only the **recursion shape**; any derivation would come from NTE's specific foam-nucleation map, not from CCC. ⟹ CCC does not floor α; consistent with NTE using its shape only. (Confirms the §6 not-endorsement framing: there is nothing in CCC *to* endorse for this purpose.)

## 2. Eternal / chaotic inflation — distributes, does not derive [ASSESSMENT]

Eternal inflation populates a multiverse of bubbles with different effective constants. Extracting a prediction requires a **measure** on the multiverse — and the **measure problem** (Guth, Linde, Vilenkin, Freivogel) is unsolved: different measures give different, sometimes paradoxical answers (youngness, Boltzmann-brain pathologies). Even granting a measure, it yields a **probability distribution** over constants, not a derivation of a unique value; a sharp prediction still needs anthropic conditioning. ⟹ **distributes/selects, does not derive.** No α-floor.

## 3. Bouncing / ekpyrotic / cyclic — inherits; one relaxation cousin [ASSESSMENT]

Ekpyrotic and cyclic models (Steinhardt–Turok) replace inflation with a contracting/bouncing phase; the constants (Λ, masses) are inputs from the underlying brane/moduli/string physics — **not derived by the cycling.** *Partial cousin worth noting:* the cyclic model includes a dynamical **Λ-relaxation** over many cycles (a slowly-rolling quintessence field ratcheting Λ down) — a recursion-relaxation idea structurally adjacent to a fixed point. But it relaxes Λ *toward small values* dynamically; it does **not** derive a specific α. ⟹ inherits constants; the relaxation cousin is about Λ's smallness, not β_NTE's value.

## 4. String landscape + anthropic selection — dissolves, does not derive (the real competitor) [ASSESSMENT]

The landscape (~10^500 vacua; Bousso–Polchinski, Susskind) hosts different constants per vacuum; **anthropic selection** (Weinberg's 1987 Λ bound is the paradigm) conditions on observer-compatible vacua. This **does not derive β_NTE — it dissolves the question**: there is no mechanism producing β_NTE's value, only selection from a distribution conditioned on observers. **AC's framing is exactly correct and goes on the record:** anthropic selection is **not a competitor *to* NTE's derivation attempt — it is the fallback *behind* even NTE's open route**, the position one adopts *if* derivation fails. A critic will raise the landscape; the honest NTE stance is "we attempt to derive β_NTE; if the one open self-consistency route closes negatively, anthropic selection is the backstop, not a rival mechanism." ⟹ no derivation; pre-empts the obvious critique.

## 5. Smolin Cosmological Natural Selection — the genuine structural cousin [ASSESSMENT]

CNS (Smolin, 1992/1997): black holes spawn offspring universes with slightly **mutated** constants; universes producing more black holes dominate the population; observed constants therefore sit near a **local maximum of black-hole production**. This *is* a recursion/attractor-determination of constants — **the closest structural cousin to NTE's self-consistency route**, and the one that could move "sole survivor" toward "one of two."

**But the mechanism differs in a way that matters:**
- CNS determines constants by **extremization** (critical point of BH production under variation) + **population dynamics** (selection) — a *statistical* statement that observed constants are near-extremal. NTE's route is a **self-consistency fixed point** α = F(Ω(α)) — a *derivation* of a unique value, if F exists and is unique.
- CNS is therefore closer to **selection-without-observers** (BH-fitness replacing anthropics) than to derivation: it predicts constants lie near a BH-production maximum, not a unique value from first principles.
- CNS is itself **contested** (whether the mutation/selection ensemble is well-defined; whether its falsifiable predictions — e.g. the neutron-star upper mass bound — hold).

**Calibration it forces on the paper:** the broader *category* "fundamental constants fixed by a cosmological recursion/selection dynamics" is **not unique to NTE** — CNS is an independent instance. So the claim must be stated as: *NTE's is the only **self-consistency-fixed-point derivation** route we are aware of; Smolin CNS is a structurally-related **selection/extremization** cousin (different mechanism, statistical rather than unique).* That earns and sharpens "sole survivor" rather than killing it — and it identifies CNS as the natural place to look for a genuine second route if the BH-fitness idea can be recast as a self-consistency condition.

## 6. Asymptotic-safety cosmology — closed-negative for α [ASSESSMENT]
