Abstract
This paper binds four strands of the Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) programme into a
single structure. (1) Within an established octonionic reading of the Standard Model —
pre-temporal time-orderlessness ⇒ non-associativity ⇒ the octonions O (Hurwitz); G2 =
Aut(O); the gauge group from G2 subgroups; three generations from the exceptional Jordan
algebra J3 (O) — we record where the surface-ontology axiom (“the universe is the shape of
the state space”) is forced to split into two sectors. (2) That split is derived independently
from a measure-theoretic floor and a least-action ceiling: the Born rule read as invariant
area on the Bloch sphere requires measurability (the sphere is non-amenable; SO(3) ⊃
F2 ), Gleason-forcing needs Hilbert dimension ≥ 3, while least-action bound-state stability
needs spatial dimension ≤ 3 (V ′′ ∝ 3 − d); no single manifold meets both, so an external
dynamical sector and an internal Hilbert sector are required — realised algebraically as the
quaternionic subalgebra H ⊂ O (space) and J3 (O) (generations). (3) The atmospheric angle
θ23 = π/4 follows from a generation-Z2 , and (4) the maximal CP phase δCP = ±π/2 with
naturally nonzero θ13 follows from upgrading that Z2 to an antilinear µ–τ reflection. The
central new result binds (3)–(4) to (1): the σ-triple on which the atmospheric symmetry
was originally posited is representationally distinct from the J3 (O) generations (gauge su(2)
adjoint, irreducible 3, versus Jordan slots, 1 ⊕ 2), but its operative content re-grounds
cleanly on J3 (O): the generation-Z2 is the Hermitian slot-swap, the antilinear reflection is
the automatic octonion conjugation of the inter-generation connector, and the CP-phase
direction is fixed — with no free parameter — to the unique colour singlet of Im(O), which
is the hypercharge direction. We are explicit about scope: the octonionic Standard-Model
facts and the dimensional theorems are established prior art; δCP = π/2 pre-exists in the
corpus; no new observable is claimed. What is new is the unification — and the demotion
of a posited symmetry to a consequence of Jordan Hermiticity.

Introduction

NTE treats time and causal ordering as emergent and reconstructs Standard-Model structure
from what survives in a pre-temporal epoch. Several of its results have been developed as standalone notes: maximal atmospheric mixing θ23 = π/4 from a discrete symmetry on the neutrino
sector; the maximal leptonic CP phase δCP = π/2 from a bilateral conjugation geometry; an
octonionic account of the gauge group and the three-generation count; and a surface-ontology
reading of quantum mechanics in which the Born rule is area on a state-space sphere. These
were written separately and, until now, linked only by shared vocabulary.
This paper shows they are one structure. The binding object is the exceptional Jordan
algebra J3 (O) sitting inside the octonions O, and the binding claim is that the neutrino mixing
symmetry — originally posited on a triple of Pauli generators — is in fact a property of the
Hermitian structure of J3 (O). Along the way the surface ontology’s two-sector character is
shown to be forced (conditionally) by the incompatibility of two dimensional requirements, and
to coincide with the octonionic split of O into a quaternionic spatial subalgebra and the Jordan
generation algebra.
What is and is not claimed. We separate, at every step, established prior art from
framework-specific increments and from genuinely new results. The octonion → StandardModel dictionary (Günaydin–Gürsey, Dixon, Furey, Todorov–Dubois-Violette) and the dimensional theorems (Hausdorff–Banach–Tarski, Gleason, Ehrenfest–Tangherlini) are not ours. The
value δCP = π/2 pre-exists in the corpus. No new observable is predicted here. The new content
is: (i) the two-sector necessity from dynamics-plus-measure (§3); (ii) the re-grounding of the
atmospheric symmetry and its CP phase onto J3 (O), in which the antilinear reflection is derived
rather than posited (§5); and (iii) the parameter-free colour-fixing of the CP-phase direction
(§5.3).

The octonionic root (established)

We recall the established structure on which everything else hangs; §2 claims no novelty and
follows Günaydin–Gürsey (1973), Dixon (1994), Furey (2018), Todorov–Dubois-Violette (2018),
and the framework’s adoption thereof.

The surface ontology is necessarily two-sector

NTE reads the Born rule as invariant area on the quantum state-space sphere (the Hopf base
CP 1 = S 2 ). We ask what constrains such an ontology and find the dimension three bracketed
from two sides by independent principles that cannot be met on one manifold.

3.1

Measure floor

The rotation group of the Bloch sphere, SO(3), contains a free group F2 (generated by rotations
through arccos(1/3) about two axes; checked numerically: 1456 reduced nontrivial words of
length ≤ 6 all sit ≥ 0.13 from the identity). A group containing F2 is non-amenable, so S 2 is
Banach–Tarski paradoxical: the invariant area defining probability is unique only on measurable
sets. Measurability is therefore a load-bearing physical constraint, non-trivial precisely because
the surface is non-amenable. Uniqueness-and-forcing of the Born measure then requires, by
Gleason’s theorem, Hilbert dimension ≥ 3; the single qubit (CP 1 = S 2 ) is doubly deficient —
first non-amenable sphere and the dimension at which Gleason fails.

Maximal atmospheric mixing and the antilinear CP phase

4.1

The generation-Z2 and θ23 = π/4

A Z2 exchanging two neutrino mass eigenstates (ν1 ↔ ν2 , ν3 fixed), mapped to the flavoursector µ ↔ τ exchange, enforces Uµ3 = Uτ 3 and hence s23 = c23 , i.e. θ23 = π/4. In the original
standalone treatment this Z2 was posited as the exchange σ1 ↔ σ2 of two Pauli generators of
SU (2)L , with σ3 along the Higgs vacuum. We return to that identification critically in §5.

4.2

Exchange versus reflection

Two discrete symmetries fix θ23 = π/4: a µ–τ exchange (P Mν P = Mν , Mν real), giving θ13 = 0
and Jarlskog J = 0 (CP-conserving); and a µ–τ reflection (P Mν∗ P = Mν , antilinear), giving
θ13 ̸= 0 allowed and |J| maximal, i.e. δCP = ±π/2. The two differ by a complex-conjugation
factor.

4.3

Why the symmetry is antilinear

With σ3 pinned to the vacuum, the swap σ1 ↔ σ2 is an improper rotation of generator space
(det = −1); no linear-unitary conjugation realises it in any basis (these are all det = +1), and the
only det = +1 alternative flips σ3 (forbidden). Hence the realisation is antiunitary. Moreover
σ2 is conjugation-odd (σ2∗ = −σ2 ), so a σ2 -polarised Majorana mode carries an intrinsic π/2
4

phase: the σ1 - and σ2 -modes differ by π/2, the linear exchange is inconsistent with this phase,
and the antilinear reflection is the consistent one. The forced π/2 is exactly δCP = ±π/2.
Remark 1. The value δCP = π/2 pre-exists in the corpus, derived from the bilateral (CPN)
conjugation geometry; the antilinear-reflection argument derives the same value and ties it to
θ23 , but is not an independent new observable. Independent operator analysis shows the bilateral conjugation (an inter-sector swap) and the generation swap (intra-sector) share antilinear
character but are not the same operator (parallel, not descended).

The re-grounding: σ-triple onto J3 (O)

The atmospheric symmetry was posited on the σ-triple. We test whether that triple is the J3 (O)
generation structure, and find: not as labelled, but its operative content re-grounds cleanly.

5.1

Gate 1: the two “threes” are inequivalent representations

In the original treatment σ1 , σ2 , σ3 are the Pauli generators of SU (2)L , and the three generations
are identified with these three gauge directions. But the gauge su(2) adjoint is the irreducible 3
(spin-1: no nonzero vector is annihilated by all three generators), whereas the J3 (O) generations
are the three Jordan slots, carrying the S3 slot-permutation representation, which is reducible,
1 ⊕ 2 (the permutation generators fix (1, 1, 1)). A reducible representation cannot equal an
irreducible one.
Theorem 1 (Representation mismatch). The posited σ-triple (gauge su(2)L adjoint, irreducible
3) is not the J3 (O) generation-3 (Jordan slots, 1 ⊕ 2). The original treatment placed the
generations in the representation occupied by the gauge group, not by the generations.
An independent soft spot confirms the tension: SU (2)L is generation-blind (it acts identically on
all three generations), so its adjoint directions cannot intrinsically distinguish the generations.

the Albert algebra with real diagonal slots (a, b, c) — the generations — and octonion connectors
x1 (2-3), x2 (1-3), x3 (1-2), Hermiticity pairing (i, j) to the conjugate of (j, i). The slot-swap 1 ↔ 2

Theorem 2 (Re-grounding). J3 (O) carries the operative content of the atmospheric symmetry: the generation-Z2 is the Hermitian slot-swap (ν3 , the vacuum-aligned slot, fixed), and the
antilinear µ–τ reflection is the automatic octonion conjugation x3 7→ x̄3 of the inter-generation
connector. The reflection is derived from Jordan Hermiticity, not posited.
This replaces the broken gauge-σ identification by a generation-distinguishing structure and
gives the CP-violating reflection a structural origin: it is the conjugation built into the Hermitian
Albert algebra.

Gate 3: colour fixes the CP-phase direction, parameter-free

The connector x3 is an octonion; its imaginary part lives in Im(O) = R7 . The neutrino is
colourless, so the phase-carrying direction must be a colour singlet. Under SU (3)C = Stab(e1 ) ⊂
G2 , Im(O) = 1 ⊕ 3 ⊕ 3̄. With the complex structure J = Le1 one finds J(e1 ) = −1 (so
e1 lies in the complex line C = {1, e1 }) while the other six units pair into three complex
lines, (e2 , e3 ), (e4 , e5 ), (e6 , e7 ), with J 2 = −1 verified on the six-dimensional complement — i.e.
3 ⊕ 3̄ = C3 . Hence:
Theorem 3 (Parameter-free direction). The colour-singlet imaginary subspace of Im(O) is
exactly one-dimensional (Re1 ). Requiring the neutrino Majorana phase to be colourless fixes
the connector direction to e1 uniquely — no free parameter. The direction e1 is imaginary
(conjugation-odd, furnishing the π/2 phase) and is the U (1)Y /complex-structure direction.
The parameter one might have feared — which of seven imaginary directions carries the phase
— is removed by colour, not posited. A resonance worth flagging (not banked): the CP phase
ends up carried by the hypercharge direction e1 , tying δCP to the electroweak/complex-structure
axis.

Falsifiers

The combined empirical content is θ23 = π/4, θ13 ̸= 0, |δCP | = π/2, falsifiable in the standard
sense: a measured θ23 inconsistent with π/4 beyond the reflection-breaking precision, or a
robustly non-maximal |δCP |, falsifies the reflection reading. Global fits currently place δCP
near −π/2 within ∼ 1σ; DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande are decisive. A sign flag is recorded:
the corpus commits to +π/2 while fits lean −π/2; this is left to data, not adjudicated here.
Structurally, if a consistent NTE formulation required a normed division algebra other than O
(Hurwitz leaves none), or if the colour-singlet of Im(O) were not one-dimensional (it is), the
re-grounding would fail.
