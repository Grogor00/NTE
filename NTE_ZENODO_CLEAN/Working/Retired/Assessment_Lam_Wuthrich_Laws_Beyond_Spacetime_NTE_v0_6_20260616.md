## §1. What the paper argues

QG's near-consensus is that spacetime is emergent. If so, the three main analyses of laws all face deep trouble:

- **Humean best-systems:** needs a non-modal, *freely recombinable* base. A non-spatiotemporal base read off a real QG theory is **"nomically charged"** — the theory constrains recombination, so it isn't modally innocent. Reject the theory's guidance (un-naturalistic) or accept necessary connections (un-Humean).
- **Primitivism / dispositionalism:** rest on **"nomic production"** — a temporal notion. Without fundamental time, "production" becomes elusive; the fix leans on a **kinematics/dynamics split** that may not survive QG.

### §2.6 The det e = 0 / signature-change / discrete-symmetry cluster
A coherent four-paper cluster centred on one physics object: **the degenerate locus det e = 0 as the only place orientation/signature can flip**, plus signature change at cosmological origin. It feeds three NTE programmes — the Euclidean-section → Lorentzian gauge birth (§3 item 3), the **C-series** CPT/parity sector (C3/C5/CPN), and the SM-from-pre-temporal-algebra cascade — and supplies the mainstream cosmological urtext the v0.5 signature-change line was missing.

**(a) Sakharov (1984), *Cosmological transitions with changes in the signature of the metric* (Sov. Phys. JETP 60, 214; repr. Sov. Phys. Usp. 34(5), 1991).** The mainstream **urtext**: the Big Bang as a quantum transition from a purely-spatial, timeless region (signature a=0, which Sakharov literally names **"P" for Parmenides** — "a world without motion") to our Lorentzian universe (a=1); uses **Hartle–Hawking** explicitly; Λ=0 or anomalously small; induced ("metrical-elasticity") gravity. The canonical physics ancestor of NTE's **Hartle–Hawking Euclidean section → Lorentzian-saddle gauge birth**, and the Parmenidean timeless-region framing is a clean bridge between NTE's pre-temporal epoch ("before is oxymoronic / N<N_lock") and the (a)temporal-emergence philosophy at the core of this note. **What NOT to import:** Sakharov's specific apparatus — compactified extra *time* dimensions, Kaluza–Klein, anthropic Λ — is **not** NTE's. Cite for the signature-change-at-origin idea and the timeless-region framing only.

**(b) Rovelli & Wilson-Ewing (2012), *Discrete Symmetries in Covariant LQG* (arXiv:1205.0733).** Mainstream (Rovelli). Internal/manifold T and P in spinfoam LQG; sign s = sgn(det e) (S_EH ∝ |det e|R vs S_tetrad ∝ (det e)R); a modified Holst action transforming coherently under (i)T/(i)P; **the relative orientation between physical manifold and internal Minkowski space can flip only across a degenerate det e = 0 region**. Closing speculation: a region of flipped internal time = **"anti-spacetime"** (Stückelberg–Feynman: backward-in-time = antiparticle). NTE touchpoint: a credible mainstream reference for the **C-series** (C3/C5/CPN) and the orientation-flip-at-degenerate-locus theme; the anti-spacetime / backward-internal-time idea rhymes with NTE's Θ/CPN machinery and the pre-temporal epoch. (Cf. Han–Rovelli, *Spinfoam Fermions: PCT*.)

**(c) Nissinen & Volovik (2017), *Transitions to anti-spacetime* (arXiv:1710.07616).** The **bridge** of the cluster: a condensed-matter realisation (polar distorted ³He-A) of the det e sign flip — a topological Lifshitz transition through a degenerate-tetrad (det e = 0) state in which right-handed Weyl fermions become left-handed (chirality flip), i.e. "spacetime → anti-spacetime." Result: the fermionic action is analytic across the transition, but the **bosonic (EM + gravity) effective action depends only on √−g = |det e|**, so it is non-analytic in det e and **does not distinguish spacetime from anti-spacetime** (their answer to the Rovelli/Diakonov gravitational-path-integral question: anti-spacetime exists but the action depends on |det e|). Explicitly cites (b) and (d). NTE touchpoints: (i) it ties the LQG (b) and lattice-spinor (d) threads to the condensed-matter signature-change line already in §3 item 3; (ii) it carries **Klinkhamer–Volovik, *Emergent CPT violation from the splitting of Fermi points*** — a concrete emergent-CPT-violation precedent that strengthens **C3** beyond Grady's hand-wave.

**(d) Vladimirov & Diakonov (2012), *Phase transitions in spinor quantum gravity on a lattice* (arXiv:1208.1254).** A third cosmological cousin (alongside §2.5) and the strongest structural rhyme yet with NTE's particle programme: a lattice QG built from **fundamental fermion + spin-connection fields — explicitly "the same type of degrees of freedom as in the Standard Model"** — with a **composite tetrad from fermion bilinears** (geometry emergent), det(e) again flipping through det(e)=0, and the **Einstein limit with cosmological constant automatically zero achieved by sitting at a 2nd-order phase transition** (spontaneous chiral-symmetry breaking → Goldstone). Spinor-gravity lineage: Akama, Volovik, Wetterich. NTE touchpoints: resonates with the **SM-from-pre-temporal-octonionic-algebra** programme and the **gauge-birth SSB cascade**; another "Einstein/Big-Bang-at-a-phase-transition, Λ→0" cousin. (NTE's gauge group comes from the octonionic algebra and its κ/Λ story is its own — do not import the KK/anthropic or 2nd-order-transition mechanism as a derivation.)

**Net:** the recurring object across (b)–(d) is **det e = 0 as the unique flip-locus** — the same seam NTE places at gauge birth — and Sakharov (a) supplies the mainstream cosmological-origin precedent the v0.5 line lacked. All four stay at **structural-precedent / positioning register**, not derivation inputs.
