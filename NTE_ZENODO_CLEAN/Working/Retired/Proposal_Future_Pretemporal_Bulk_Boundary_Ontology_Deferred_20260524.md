## §1. The musing

Greg's question, verbatim: "Just because the temporal epoch is S³ surface that doesn't mean that the pre-temporal epoch 'was'... perhaps it was all bulk and no boundary?"

The structural claim being probed: the corpus's "surface only, S³ sphere" axiom is *temporally scoped* — it applies to the post-Janus / temporal regime, not necessarily to the pre-Janus / pre-temporal regime. The pre-temporal phase may have a fundamentally different ontology: all bulk, no boundary.

## §2. Structural reading using core axioms

(N fundamental + t emergent; surface only S³ — *temporally scoped*; H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²; F1 ℓ = 1/H; CPN-conjugate orientations; Janus pair at N=0.)

### §2.1 What "surface only" actually commits

The axiom is operationally: the universe IS the S³ spatial topology with no embedding 4D bulk. The S³ is not a boundary OF anything; it's the thing-itself. The corpus uses this in the post-Janus / temporal regime where Φ_NTE has a well-defined value on the S³ slice at each N.

The axiom makes no commitment about pre-Janus structure. Pre-temporal could be:
- A 4D Euclidean closed manifold (no boundary in the HH sense) with the S³ emerging as the equatorial signature-change slice
- A higher-dimensional pre-geometric substrate with no defined topology
- A 4-bulk that the S³ subsequently *materialises out of* at gauge birth

### §2.2 §22.18 already uses a partial bulk reading

The Hartle-Hawking saddle anchoring T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) is structurally a 4-ball-like Euclidean object — closed, no-boundary in the HH no-boundary-proposal sense, with the S³ spatial topology appearing as the equatorial slice where Wick rotation crosses Euclidean → Lorentzian. **The corpus is already using bulk + boundary structure at §22.18**; the HH saddle IS the bulk; the post-Janus S³ is what emerges at the signature interface.

This Proposal's claim is that §22.18's implicit bulk/boundary distinction is the right reading for the full pre-temporal vs post-temporal ontology, not only for the saddle calculation.

### §2.3 Janus event = boundary nucleation = gauge birth (three names, one event)

Under this reading, the Janus event isn't only "the birth of t." It's the geometric crystallisation event by which:
- A 4-bulk gives rise to an S³ spatial topology
- Φ_NTE acquires nonzero value on the newly-emergent S³
- CPN-conjugate orientations differentiate (the bilateral N-thickness structure becomes well-defined)
- Gauge structure is born on the surface (Path B; SU(2) → SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R selection)

These four things are the same structural event viewed from four angles. The "surface only" axiom applies after this event; before it, there's no surface to be only-of.

### §2.4 V_NTE pre/post connection reinterpreted

Companion 9 Session 5 D1 (V_NTE smooth pre-temporal/post-locking connection) is currently framed as: find a smooth V_NTE(Φ) extending V_NTE^pre (Φ⁴ − Φ⁶ form) into V_NTE^post (on-trajectory form). Under this Proposal, that framing is potentially ill-posed:

V_NTE^pre is a potential on Φ; but if pre-Janus has no S³ for Φ to be a field on, then V_NTE^pre is a potential on... what? Either:
- (a) V_NTE^pre lives in the 4-bulk as a *bulk* scalar field, and V_NTE^post is its boundary projection
- (b) V_NTE^pre is a *modular function* or other bulk-geometric object that has no field-on-surface interpretation pre-Janus
- (c) V_NTE^pre is well-defined only in a limit as Φ → 0⁺ from the post-Janus surface, with no actual pre-Janus content

The smooth-connection problem is then a boundary-projection problem, not a function-extension problem. The right question is: what is the geometric event by which V_NTE^pre (bulk) gives rise to V_NTE^post (boundary) at the Janus nucleation, and what does "smooth" mean in that bulk-to-boundary projection? This is a different mathematical object than a smooth function of Φ.

### §2.5 CCC recursion as boundary dissolution + nucleation (re-reading of prior proposal)

The deferred CCC recursion proposal (`Proposal_Future_Companion_CCC_Recursion_Janus_Virtual_Pair_Deferred_20260523.md` ID 1-e21ZRX3S74TODA-lkMQnDIoUebTbva-) framed the fractal foam at §XV as "S³ fragmenting into new S³ Janus seeds." Under this Proposal, that reading is sharpened to:

S³ *dissolves back into 4-bulk* at the 1-photon-per-Hubble limit → new S³ *nucleates from the bulk* at each foam fragment. The bulk persists across the recursion; the boundary is what recurses. The temporal arc is then:

4-bulk → boundary nucleation event (Janus, gauge birth, S³ crystallisation, |Φ_NTE| acquires value) → temporal expansion + thermal history on S³ → boundary failure at 1-photon-per-Hubble → S³ dissolution back to bulk → new S³ nucleation event → next aeon.

This is structurally cleaner: daughter aeons inherit the 4-bulk substrate (not a copy of ℓ_dS as an initial condition), and ℓ_dS is the boundary-scale at which dissolution and renucleation occur. The α fixed-point question (Appendix A.4 forward pathway item 10) becomes: under what bulk-geometric conditions does α persist across the boundary-dissolution-renucleation event?

### §2.6 H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² as bulk-to-boundary projection remnant

The corpus repeatedly flags H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² as a load-bearing NTE-specific commitment that is NOT derived from heat kernel. Phase S2-E AC integration package §1.3 explicitly says "H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² Friedmann coupling remains load-bearing NTE-specific commitment (NOT derived from heat kernel; intrinsic NTE input rather than spectral-triple output)."

Under this Proposal, the reason heat-kernel can't derive it is structural: heat-kernel operates on the spectral triple, which is the *surface*. The α-Friedmann relation is a statement about how the boundary's expansion rate relates to the boundary's field value, but **the value of β_NTE is fixed by the bulk-to-boundary projection geometry at the Janus event** — not by anything intrinsic to the boundary's spectral triple.

The "intrinsic input" character of H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² is then because α is a *remnant of pre-temporal bulk structure projected onto the surface at boundary nucleation*. deriving β_NTE from first principles would require either: (i) a theory of the pre-temporal bulk's structure, or (ii) a derivation of the boundary-nucleation projection that fixes α as a consequence of bulk-to-boundary matching conditions. Neither route is currently scoped in the corpus.

This is a potentially significant structural insight: it explains *why* α is recalcitrant to derivation from canonical NTE machinery — because the canonical NTE machinery operates on the wrong side of the Janus event.

### §2.7 κ Pathway A bilinear bridge as bulk-shared communication

Pathway A's bilinear cross-sector S_bridge couples the two CPN-conjugate orientations. Under this Proposal, the two orientations are "two faces of one S³ surface that emerged from a single 4-bulk." The bridge between them is not communication across an arbitrary partition — it is **communication through the shared bulk origin**. The 4-bulk persists as the geometric substrate of the surface even after the surface has nucleated; the cross-sector coupling is a structural feature of how the two CPN faces remain in geometric contact through that shared substrate.

The κ closure arithmetic (κ_0/(1−κ_0) ≈ 5.50) stays the same; the *structural interpretation* of the bilinear cross-sector bridge gets cleaner. This may also be relevant to Charter v1.4 §4.5 A3 disclosure of κ derivation pathway architecture.

## §4. Open questions for future development

When this Proposal activates:

1. **What IS the 4-bulk?** A closed Euclidean 4-manifold (HH-style; S⁴ or 4-ball)? An open 4-manifold? Pre-geometric substrate (e.g., a state in some pre-topology theory)? Spectral-triple-with-no-Dirac (algebra-only)?

2. **What is the geometric event of "boundary nucleation"?** Signature change (Euclidean → Lorentzian)? Topology change (4D → 3D + 1D)? Field nucleation (|Φ_NTE| acquires value)? All three simultaneously? Is there a single mathematical object that captures the nucleation event?

3. **Bulk-to-boundary projection of V_NTE**. If V_NTE^pre lives in the bulk and V_NTE^post is its boundary projection, what is the projection mechanism? Standard AdS/CFT-style holographic projection? Some other mechanism (e.g., conformal limit of bulk → boundary)?

4. **Does the bulk persist post-Janus?** Two readings: (a) the bulk dissolves at boundary nucleation, leaving only the S³ surface (clean separation between epochs); (b) the bulk persists as a hidden substrate the boundary remains in contact with (allows §2.7 bulk-shared communication reading; allows ongoing bulk influence on boundary dynamics). The CCC recursion picture (§2.5) favours reading (b).

5. **α as bulk-to-boundary projection coefficient**. Is α a single number fixed by the projection geometry, or a parameter that depends on bulk structure? Under reading (b) above, α could vary across CCC recursion cycles, giving the α fixed-point structure (Appendix A.4 item 10).

6. **Holographic dictionary**. Is there a precise AdS/CFT-style dictionary mapping bulk operators to boundary observables? Or is the NTE bulk-boundary relation structurally distinct from AdS/CFT (different bulk-boundary geometry; different correlator dictionary)?

7. **Spectral triple in the bulk**. The canonical NTE spectral triple Hopf-S³ × S¹_{β_GB} × A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 is the boundary's spectral triple. Is there a "bulk spectral triple" that boundary-restricts to the canonical NTE one? If so, would Programme S Paper S2's heat-kernel computation extend naturally to the bulk?

8. **Connection to §17.5.13 Born rule scope**. Complex Hilbert space might emerge structurally from bulk-to-boundary projection if the bulk has natural ℂ structure (e.g., complex-Euclidean signature) that boundary inherits at nucleation. This is speculative — would need explicit construction. If it works, it closes Born rule scope as a free structural consequence of the bulk/boundary ontology.

9. **Connection to fractal foam end-state (§XV)**. The S³ dissolution back to bulk at 1-photon-per-Hubble limit needs a precise geometric statement. What is the failure mode of the boundary's coherence that causes dissolution? Is it the same standing-wave failure mechanism §XV currently describes, or a deeper geometric event?

10. **Mathematical machinery to use**. Conformal compactification / Penrose-style? Spin-foam-style discrete bulk-boundary? Spectral triple with auxiliary bulk algebra? Quantum gravity programmes like causal dynamical triangulation? Greg's intuition (and previous adversarial-review experience) will guide which mathematical machinery is structurally faithful.
