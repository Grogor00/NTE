## Abstract

The Connes spectral action encodes gravity, gauge structure, and matter in a single heat-kernel expansion on a compact manifold. NTE's claim is that *one geometry* — the Hopf bundle Hopf-S³ × S¹_{β_GB} times the octonionic doubling algebra 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 — yields a coherent set of its structural predictions from one Seeley–DeWitt computation, which must agree or the framework fails collectively. That is the chord: not arbitrary tones, but a structured relation fixed by one instrument.

A single expansion on this geometry fixes the heat-kernel coefficients a₀, a₂, a₄, and from them four cleanly-derived results: the bare cross-thickness coupling κ₀ = 3/√(4π); the gauge-birth temperature T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π); the right-handed Majorana mass scales (cross-checked against the dedicated derivation); and the volume audit in which Vol(S³) = 2π² recurs across three registers. A further clean result of the *same* computation is that the fourth coefficient vanishes on this substrate, a₄ = 0 — which is itself the reason the cosmological-constant suppression is structural rather than numeric, and the reason an a₄-based derivation of the inflaton kinetic term is not available. The paper states, for each result, exactly which register it sits at: cleanly derived, structural-only, or dependent on input the computation does not provide. It makes no claim to a fixed count of "notes"; the content is the coherence, not a tally.

## 1. One computation, not many fits

The strength of the claim matters. It is not that a list of quantities can each be computed somewhere in the framework; it is that one geometry — Hopf-S³ with fibre modulus β_GB, times the octonionic doubling algebra — produces a set of them from a single heat-kernel expansion, so they cannot be tuned independently. A chord is not a handful of arbitrary frequencies sounded together; it is a structured relation among tones fixed by the instrument. Here the instrument is the manifold M and its Dirac operator; the tones are the bare coupling, the gauge-birth temperature, the Majorana scales, the volume audit, and the structural cosmological-constant result. If any one of them required a free parameter the others did not, the coherence claim would fail; that shared dependence is exactly the framework's exposure.

"Structurally complete" is used here in a limited sense: the operator on M is specified, the coefficients a₀, a₂, a₄ are closed-form on M, and the results below follow from them. It does not mean every sub-leading correction is closed, nor that quantities outside the gravitational heat-kernel spine (the individual Yukawas) are derived.

## 2. The single spine

**Geometry and algebra.** The canonical substrate is M = Hopf-S³ × S¹_{β_GB} × A_F with A_F = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆. The spatial S³ carries the Hopf fibration; the temporal S¹ has fibre length β_GB; the finite algebra is the octonionic doubling. The Dirac operator is D_M = D_{S³} ⊗ 1 + γ_{S³} ⊗ D_{S¹}, with the S¹ taken antiperiodic (spin structure θ = 1/2). The fibre modulus is fixed, not fitted, by the Hopf identification β_GB · M_Pl = √(2π) — the single relation that also fixes the gauge-birth temperature.

**Seeley–DeWitt coefficients.** On M with this operator, the leading heat-kernel coefficients are a₀ ∝ Vol(M) (the cosmological/volume term), a₂ ∝ the scalar-curvature integral (Einstein–Hilbert), and the fourth-order coefficient

  a₄|_M = 0.

The vanishing is exact and is a clean result of the computation: the Dirac trace-anomaly integrand on this substrate — the fixed combination of Weyl-squared and Euler densities that the framework's F₁ result identifies — is pointwise zero on the round S³ × flat S¹ product, and the higher coefficients a_{2n} (n ≥ 2) are tail-suppressed. This single fact has two consequences that recur below: the cosmological-constant suppression is structural (independent of a₄), and any derivation that would have run through a *nonzero* a₄ is not available from this substrate.

## 3. The results

**κ₀ — the bare cross-thickness coupling.** The heat-kernel extraction gives the bare coupling through the isotropic harmonic on the Hopf base,

  κ₀ = 3 / √(4π) ≈ 0.846,

where the factor 3 is the dimension of the adjoint of SU(2) — three gauge-field components on the base, not three Killing-spinor polarisations (the fixed-sign Killing-spinor space on round S³ is two-dimensional). The heat-kernel route and the direct Hopf-projection route agree on this value. The closure to the physical coupling, κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) ≈ 5.50, is a separate architectural step (the Janus resummation), not a bare-heat-kernel output, and is not claimed as such here.

**T_GB — the gauge-birth temperature.** The fibre modulus relation gives

  T_GB = M_Pl / √(2π) ≈ 4.9 × 10¹⁸ GeV,

the temperature at which the temporal circle closes. This is the same √(2π) that fixes β_GB; the two are one relation. (The holographic lock width N_lock ≈ 0.1 that this temperature feeds is a distinct counter from the inflationary e-fold depth N_e ≈ 63 — the two must not be conflated.)

**The Majorana and Dirac mass scales.** The same spine carries the right-handed Majorana scales, cross-checked against the dedicated derivation: the lightest, M₁ = (8π)^{1/3}(M_Pl² H_GB)^{1/3} ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV, and the heaviest, M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, with the (8π)^{1/3} prefactor unique at tree level and the residual to the leptogenesis value an O(1) gauge-birth correction (Companion 10). The Dirac dual on the same Hopf–Killing-spinor flux line, Y_{ν₂} = (8π)^{-1/3}, feeds the solar neutrino mass m_{ν₂} = (8π)^{-2/3} v_EW²/M_R = 0.0086 eV at 0.993 × √(Δm²_solar) — a zero-parameter result on the solar leg, with the atmospheric leg an open ≈ 1.5× overshoot (Companion 13). The Majorana (8π)^{1/3} and Dirac (8π)^{-1/3} are one duality on one flux line.

**The 2π² volume audit.** Vol(S³) = 2π² recurs across three registers of the computation — as the spectral internal volume Vol(M) = 2π² β_GB, as the comoving post-lock 3-volume, and as the normalisation in the cosmological-constant structure — and these are not three coincidental appearances of the same number but one geometric object (the S³ boundary unit cell that nucleates at gauge birth) appearing at three stages. The alternative 4π (the Hopf base area) is excluded by the heat-kernel choice on M. This is a framework-internal consistency audit, not an independent observational test.

**The cosmological constant — structural, not numeric.** The cosmological-constant result is the *structural* one: the unimodular projection on this geometry removes the extensive vacuum term, so empty space does not gravitate catastrophically, and this stands independently of a₄ (Companion 11). It is not a numeric prediction. A numeric value would have required a derivation through a nonzero a₄; since a₄ = 0 on this substrate, that route yields nothing, and no numeric Λ is claimed. The structural suppression is the result; the number is not derived.

## 4. What the computation yields, and at what register

The honest accounting is by register, not by count.

**Cleanly derived on the spine** (four results): κ₀, T_GB, the Majorana/Dirac mass scales, and the 2π² audit. These follow from the one operator and the one finite algebra, and they cohere — no one of them carries a free parameter the others do not.

**A clean result of the same computation:** a₄ = 0 on the canonical substrate. It is not an observational prediction, but it is a definite output, and it is the hinge for the two items below.

**Structural-only:** the cosmological-constant suppression — the mechanism stands (Companion 11), the number is not claimed.

**Dependent on input the computation does not supply:** the inflationary observables. An a₄-based derivation of the inflaton kinetic normalisation is not available, because a₄ = 0; the n_s = 1 − 2/N_e form shares the e-fold depth N_e with the rest of the framework, but its derivation depends on the kinetic normalisation, which is open (the inflation sector's current status is carried in the master trilogy and Companion 9, presented there as conditional). It is therefore not a clean output of this computation.

**Outside the gravitational spine:** the first-generation and charged-lepton Yukawas, which enter through the finite Dirac operator and are not fixed by the curvature heat-kernel coefficients; these remain open.

The coherence claim attaches to the four cleanly-derived results and the structural Λ that share the one geometry; the inflation normalisation and the remaining Yukawas are explicitly outside it. The paper's content is that the shared results cohere — not that they number any particular total.

## 5. Falsifiability

The exposure is collective, which is the point.

- An independent determination that β_GB ≠ √(2π)/M_Pl breaks the spine, taking the temperature and the volume audit with it.
- A heat-kernel value κ₀ ≠ 3/√(4π) fails the coupling result.
- A demonstration that a₄ ≠ 0 on the canonical substrate would change the picture in the other direction — restoring an a₄-route to the inflation normalisation and to a numeric Λ, but also undermining the claim that the Λ suppression is purely structural; the framework is committed to a₄ = 0 here.
- A demonstration that the cleanly-derived results in fact require independent fit parameters — that they do not share the one geometry — would defeat the coherence claim directly.

If the cleanly-derived results hold under independent scrutiny, the framework carries a small set of structural spectral results from a single computation, in the spirit of the spectral-action programme itself. If any one of them fails a genuine test, the others fall with it.
