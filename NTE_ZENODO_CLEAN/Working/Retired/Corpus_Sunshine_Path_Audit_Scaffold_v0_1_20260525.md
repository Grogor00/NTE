## §1. What Greg named (the recurring test)

Near-fatal moments recur when:

- Numbers look good **locally** but something **uniform** or **too neat** (0.38 on all leptons; 8/3; 7/2) asks a question the prose does not answer.
- Vocabulary **inherits** from a neighbouring formalism (strict SUSY/BPS; “two pathways”; render-lane shortcuts) and **outruns** ontology.
- **Ratio closure** is mistaken for **absolute closure**, or chart-at-$G$ quantities are read as laboratory masses without a **completion step** (resolved fermion on $S^2$ vs unresolved neutrino on fibre).

**Historical outcome (Greg):** stick to core ontology → find a **sunshine path** (bulk→surface depth, existing flux line, spectral measure, resolved/unresolved distinction) — **every time** so far. Corpus today is evidence.

**This session (2026-05-24–25):** sunshine exit (ratios) + D5 interface (absolute) = latest passed test.
