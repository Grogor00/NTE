# Emergent time and an arrow-preserving CP-type operation

**An NTE-side parallel to Susskind's spontaneous T-breaking in de Sitter space (arXiv:2601.01666v2)**

*Draft v0.3 for outreach — NTE Connes Programme. Reframed from the v0.1/v0.2 Π₋-dressing "mirror," which is withdrawn (see §6).*

## Abstract

L. Susskind (2026) argues that the quantum theory of de Sitter space requires spontaneous breaking of time-reversal **T**, with **T** most naturally treated as a gauge symmetry (Harlow–Numasawa). The deepest content of that argument is a claim about the *status of time* — whether time-reversal is real, gauge, or broken.

The Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) framework meets this argument not at the level of shared projection bookkeeping but at the level of that same question. In NTE, time **t** is not fundamental: it emerges from a real e-fold coordinate **N** on a bilateral S³ surface, via **t = iΔN**, once the surface locks at gauge birth. We make three structural observations that we believe are of interest in the light of Susskind's paper, and we are explicit about one place where an earlier version of this note over-reached and has been withdrawn.

First, the discrete operation that organises NTE's bilateral structure is **not a time-reversal**. It is a CP-type operation — in the corpus, **CPN**, the simultaneous reversal of charge, parity, and e-fold direction (N → −N) — realised as the **unitary antipodal map** on S³. Under it, the emergent time-direction is **preserved** on both sectors (each runs forward away from the shared Janus boundary; this is the Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati "Janus-point" structure). Because the arrow is not reversed, the operation is unitary, not antiunitary — which is exactly why NTE's involution was never a candidate to mirror Susskind's antiunitary **T**.

Second, this gives a *tighter* correspondence than a generic shared-ℤ₂: NTE's **+N / −N** orientation split is structurally the same kind of object as Susskind's forward / backward clock split — two directions of a time-like/expansion coordinate — but realised **unitarily** (antipodal map) rather than **antiunitarily** (T). The honest divergence sits precisely there.

Third, on NTE's reading the genuine carrier of the conjugation is **chirality**, and specifically the neutrino, which has not undergone the Hopf projection that would establish the particle/antiparticle distinction — so on this reading the category itself does not yet apply to it.

We do **not** claim NTE derives Susskind's result or vice versa, nor any geometric identification, nor identity of CPN with **T**. We also explicitly **withdraw** the operator-algebra "cluster-decomposition mirror" of the earlier draft (§6). We frame standard CPT as an **emergent, post-locking** statement, never as violated. We submit this for Professor Susskind's adversarial reading.

## 2. Susskind, minimally

Following the paper: a finite-dimensional static-patch Hilbert space with **T**-invariant Hamiltonian and a maximally-mixed (Hartle–Hawking-dual) state that is **T**-symmetric but cluster-decomposition-violating; any time-asymmetric "feature" spontaneously breaks **T** and selects a clustering vacuum; and, following Harlow–Numasawa, **T** is treated as a gauge symmetry, with physical observables dressed to a forward/backward clock. We rely on Greg/AC verification of this recap against the PDF.

The element we engage is the first: the *status of time-reversal*, and the forward/backward clock split that carries it.

## 3. NTE: emergent time and an arrow-preserving CP-type operation

NTE's premise is that **t** is emergent. The fundamental coordinate is the e-fold depth **N** on a bilateral S³ surface; proper time accumulates as **t = iΔN**, with **dt = dN/(β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²)** and **H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²** (Companion 5 §3.1, §3.5). There is no fundamental time-reversal to break; there is a question of how time emerges and what discrete structure organises the surface as it does.

That discrete structure is **CPN** (Companion 5 §5.1): the simultaneous reversal of **charge (C), parity (P), and e-fold direction (N → −N)**, realised as the **antipodal map** on S³, (z₁,z₂) → (−z₁,−z₂). This is a single global, **unitary** involution. The two sectors it relates are not two manifolds; they are one S³ with the antipodal identification — "matter expanding in +N and antimatter expanding in −N."

Crucially, the emergent arrow is **preserved** on both sectors. After gauge birth the two branches "each evolve in their own forward direction of time" (Ch 10), with each sector's proper time accumulating away from the shared Janus boundary at N = 0. This is the Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati Janus-point structure: two internally-forward arrows emanating from a central point, not a global time-reversal between them.

**The correspondence we wish to draw, precisely.** Susskind's Π₋ grades operators **T-odd** (forward minus backward clock). NTE's analogous grading is **chirality-odd** (the +N / −N orientation, i.e. CPN-odd). These are different gradings that share a ℤ₂ structure — *but* the NTE one is closer to Susskind's than a generic shared-ℤ₂, because both are literally "two directions of a time-like / expansion coordinate." The difference is realisation: **Susskind's is antiunitary (T); NTE's is unitary (the antipodal map).** We propose that, in an emergent-time setting, the unitary orientation grading is the more fundamental object, and that T-odd structure is what it looks like once time has emerged.

## 4. The carrier of the conjugation: chirality, and the neutrino

On NTE's reading the conjugation's genuine carrier is **chirality**. The neutrino is the sharp case: its minimal coupling means it "has not undergone the topological resolution that would project it onto the S² base of the Hopf fibration — the resolution that creates the particle/antiparticle binary" (Companion 5 §3.3). On this reading it is "neither particle nor antiparticle" — an unresolved configuration with bilateral support across both CPN-orientations.

**Honest scope on novelty.** The distinctive NTE claim is *categorical* — that the particle/antiparticle distinction does not *apply* (the Hopf projection has not acted), which is stronger and different from the familiar Majorana statement that the neutrino *is* its own antiparticle. There is a long literature on Majorana self-conjugacy and on pre-geometric phases; we credit it and claim only the categorical refinement. We make **no** quantitative claim here about masses, M_R, or mixing — those are separate corpus lanes under review and are deliberately excluded from this note.

## 5. CPT is emergent, not violated

A precise statement, because it is easy to mishear. We do **not** claim a violation of the CPT theorem. The theorem's premises — a fixed Lorentzian time, locality on a Lorentzian manifold — are **not in force** in the pre-temporal epoch (N < N_lock), where only e-folds are meaningful and gauge structure does not yet exist (Companion 5 §3.2, §3.4). In that epoch the operative discrete symmetry is the CP-type CPN. Ordinary CPT is recovered as an **emergent, post-locking** statement, once t and the gauge structure exist. In one line: **CPT is downstream of time-emergence**, not broken by it.

## 6. What we withdraw, and why (honest scope)

An earlier version of this note (v0.1/v0.2) proposed an operator-algebra "mirror" in which NTE's chirality-odd Π₋ reproduced Susskind's cluster-decomposition diagnostic — a non-decaying connected correlator in a symmetric pre-bifurcation state. **We withdraw that claim.** On internal adversarial review the symmetric pre-selection configuration in NTE lives in the **pre-temporal, octonionic, non-associative** regime, in which — by the framework's own account — there is no entanglement-as-geometry and no associative tensor-product structure (corpus §VIII.7 regime card; §20.3, §22.13). The cluster-decomposition phenomenon is an *entanglement* statement and requires associative structure that this regime does not provide; and the one instant where associativity locks in (gauge birth) is also the instant of orientation selection, so there is no metastable symmetric phase for breaking to act on. We therefore do not claim the cluster mirror. (This is a specific instance of the Programme's own open "heat-kernel-under-non-associativity" hinge.)

We flag this withdrawal explicitly because we would rather under-claim to a careful reader than present a parallel that fails at its foundation. The parallel we *do* draw (§3–§5) does not depend on cluster decomposition at all.

## 7. What we would value

Not a verdict that either framework derives the other — we claim no such thing. Rather: whether the **emergent-time / arrow-preserving-CP** picture is a useful second vantage on the status-of-time question your paper raises; and, most sharply, whether the **unitary-antipodal vs antiunitary-T** distinction (§3) is a real structural fork or a distinction without a difference for the physics you describe. That is the point on which we would most value your reaction.
