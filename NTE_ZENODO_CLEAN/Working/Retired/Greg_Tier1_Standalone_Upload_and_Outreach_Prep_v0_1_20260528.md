## §2. Non-negotiable standalone discipline

Use this as a hard gate before upload.

- Main body stays conventional: standard physics/maths language only.
- No framework ontology in argument chain (NTE, Janus, null-time, etc.).
- Any extraction provenance appears in a short end-note only.
- Claims stay testable and bounded: no overreach language.
- Every numerical comparison cites standard reference source.

If a paragraph fails these checks, rewrite before PDF export.

## §7. Expert outreach playbook

Rule: one paper per message, one concrete reason the recipient is relevant.

## 7.1 Message structure (short)

1. Two-line contextual opener (why them).
2. One-sentence claim summary.
3. One-sentence falsifier/testability hook.
4. Link to paper (arXiv URL once live).
5. Optional ask: "Would value your technical criticism on X."

Keep under 180 words.

## 7.2 Template A (math-ph: F1 / kappa0)

Subject: Short note on [trace-anomaly uniqueness / Hopf coupling derivation]

Hello [Name],

I am sending a brief standalone manuscript in your area: [paper title].  
Core claim in one line: [insert exact claim].  
The result is structured to be falsifiable at the level of assumptions [insert one line].

If you have time, I would value criticism specifically on [assumption/proof step].

Link: [arXiv link]

Best regards,  
Greg White

## 7.3 Template B (hep-ph: P4 / P5)

Subject: Short neutrino-theory note: [theta23 Z2 / solar mass duality]

Hello [Name],

I am sharing a short standalone neutrino paper: [paper title].  
Main result: [one-line quantitative or structural claim].  
I have kept the note model-explicit and test-facing; key stress point is [one line].

If useful, I would appreciate critique on [PMNS mapping / numerical input chain].

Link: [arXiv link]

Best,  
Greg White
