**No n gives both.** Stability needs manifold dim ≤ 3 ⟹ n ≤ 2 ⟹ Gleason fails. Gleason needs n ≥ 3 ⟹ manifold dim ≥ 4 ⟹ dynamically unstable. **The two requirements are disjoint** — there is no single state-space-as-spacetime manifold that is simultaneously Born-forced and dynamically stable.

## Why the Hopf bridge cannot close the gap
The bridge proposed in DD §5 was: spatial S³ = unit sphere of the qubit ℂ², so the dynamical and quantum dimensions are "the same object." But the qubit is **n = 2** — exactly the Gleason-DEFICIENT case (row 1). So the Hopf map links the dynamical spatial-3 to Hilbert-**2**, where Born is *not* forced; it does **not** link it to the generation-3 (where Born *is* forced). The bridge connects the dynamical 3 to the wrong (Gleason-failing) side.

And the "two 3's" are genuinely different objects, not one integer seen twice:
- **spatial 3** = dim(S³) = dim(SU(2)) — the dimension of the group manifold (3 generators);
- **generation 3** = the multiplicity of the SU(3) fundamental (3 of G₂ ⊃ SU(3)).
A manifold dimension and a representation multiplicity. Their numerical equality is a coincidence, not a derivation. The "everything points to 3" intuition is, on inspection, several *different* threes of different type.

## The genuine by-product (conditional, but real)
The disjointness is itself a structural statement worth keeping:

> **No single manifold can be both dynamically stable and Gleason-forced.** Stable least-action dynamics demands dim ≤ 3 (and hence Hilbert n ≤ 2, Gleason-deficient); Gleason-forced Born demands n ≥ 3 (and hence a state-space manifold of real dim ≥ 4, dynamically unstable). The two jobs cannot be done on one manifold.

Therefore a surface ontology that wants **both** a dynamically stable space **and** a Born rule that is *forced* (not merely posited) is **compelled to split** into two sectors: an **external** dynamical manifold (spatial S³, dim 3, stable, Gleason-deficient qubit) and an **internal** Hilbert sector (the generations, ℂ³, Gleason-forced, carrying no stability requirement). NTE already has exactly this split — and the crunch shows it is not optional decoration but a *necessity* given the two demands. This reframes "why are space and the generation/internal structure separate?" as: because one manifold provably cannot carry both the stability ceiling and the Gleason floor.

**Conditionality (stated honestly):** this necessity is conditional on (i) Born-forcing being routed through Gleason (rather than taken axiomatically or forced by another principle), and (ii) the rigid QM-is-geometry reading in which the dynamical manifold is literally the qubit state-space sphere.
