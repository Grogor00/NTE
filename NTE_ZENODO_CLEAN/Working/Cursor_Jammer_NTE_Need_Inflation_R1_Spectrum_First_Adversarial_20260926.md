## R3 — Horizon / isotropy (tested; not assumed)

**NTE's proposed difference (to be tested):** at the signature change, every point of the S³ shares one Euclidean origin with no Lorentzian time between them.

**Attack:**

1. **Shared ancestry ≠ causal contact.** Every FRW cosmology has a shared bang. The horizon problem is that widely separated patches on the last-scattering surface were **never in causal contact during the Lorentzian era**, so their thermal agreement is unexplained. Saying "they share an Euclidean origin" restates shared ancestry in Wick-rotated language. It does not establish a thermalization or correlation argument.

2. **Euclidean signature has no light cones.** "No time between them" at the equator means emergent t is common at lock, not that null geodesics connected the whole S³ in a prior Lorentzian sense. Correlation across the S³ would have to be **posited or computed** as a property of the Euclidean wave function / mode structure — which is again a **spectrum / initial-condition** claim (R1), not a dissolution of the horizon problem.

The deposit does not compute (a). Without (a), "we all fit in one Hubble volume once" is a slogan that historically still needed inflation or an equivalent to match the CMB.

## ξ = 1/6 vs geometric / Ricci reheating (AC inference check)

**AC's claim:** ξ = 1/6 is conformal; geometric / Ricci / gravitational particle-production candidates in the literature require non-conformal coupling; therefore those escapes switch off.

**Jammer check:** **Holds for the stated class**, with scoped caveats — not a blanket theorem about all energy transfer.

- **Holds:** In 4D, ξ = 1/6 is the unique conformal value for a scalar. For a **massless** conformally coupled field in a **conformally flat** spacetime (FRW is), the wave equation is conformally invariant and gravitational particle production vanishes (standard QFT-in-curved-spacetime result; Birrell–Davies class). Bassett–Liberati geometric reheating and Ricci-reheating / reheaton constructions in the literature are built around **ξ ≠ 1/6** (often ξ ≪ 0 or ξ ≫ 1) or explicit non-minimal reheatons. NTE's load-bearing commitment (C9 eq. (1); C17's entire K(Φ) closure) sits where those mechanisms are designed to be off.
- **Caveats (do not overclaim):** (i) Massive fields, higher-spin non-conformal species, or departures from conformal flatness can still produce particles. (ii) **Gauge birth radiation** (Part 2 §8.2–8.3) is a different channel — free→interacting ground-state difference radiated as gauge bosons — and is **not** killed by ξ = 1/6; it is the corpus's hot Big Bang. (iii) AC correctly flagged the suppression-at-ξ=1/6 step as inference from conformal triviality rather than a source treating NTE's exact case; the inference is sound for massless conformal scalars in FRW, which is the class the cited geometric/Ricci candidates use.

**Consequence for forks:** If someone tries G3 (second inflaton), ξ = 1/6 still blocks the cheap geometric-reheating escape for *that* field unless conformality is broken somewhere — which would fight Companion 17. Gauge birth remains available only if it is **not** inflated away (the F2 reheating-gap problem from sequencing). Under G4, "what reheats after inflation?" is indeed the wrong question — but the **right** question ("what generates the coherent adiabatic spectrum?") has no answer either.
