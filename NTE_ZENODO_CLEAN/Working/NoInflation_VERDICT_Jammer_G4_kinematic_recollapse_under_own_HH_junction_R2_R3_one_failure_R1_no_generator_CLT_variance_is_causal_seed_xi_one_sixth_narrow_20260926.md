## 2. The kinematic result (R2 + R3). This is the headline, and it is stronger than the directive's framing.

**2.1 The junction condition.** A smooth no-boundary signature change occurs on a surface of vanishing extrinsic curvature, K_ij = 0 (the Gibbons–Hartle real-tunnelling condition; from background knowledge). So at the instant the Lorentzian section begins, ȧ = 0.

The deposit places itself there twice:
- **C9 §1, first placement.** "At the equator the geometry continues from Euclidean to Lorentzian." The equator of the Euclidean 4-geometry is where a′(τ) = 0.
- **C9 §1, second placement.** It says the Janus point is where CPN forcing requires |Φ_NTE| → 0 "and hence H → 0." The deposit's own constraint therefore gives H = 0 there.

**2.2 What H = 0 at birth forces.** The closed-universe Friedmann constraint, H² = ρ/(3M²) − 1/a², then fixes the birth radius at a_b² = 3M²/ρ. The acceleration equation, ä/a = −(ρ + 3p)/(6M²), decides what happens next:
- If the birth content has **ρ + 3p > 0**, the universe is born at its *maximum* radius and recollapses. This covers Part 2 §8.3's "hot Big Bang: T_GB ~ 0.4 M_Pl, not inflaton decay" and the degenerate Fermi gas at near-Planck density.
- It expands only if **ρ + 3p < 0**, which means accelerated, inflation-like content at nucleation.

This is why the no-boundary proposal is always paired with a potential. Hartle–Hawking–Hertog's abstract (read this session): classical late-time behaviour arises "if the initial scalar field is more than a certain minimum", and the proposal "selects inflationary histories."

**2.3 Numbers.** Radiation at T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) = 4.87×10¹⁸ GeV, with g* = 106.75 and reduced M = 2.435×10¹⁸ GeV:
- ρ = 562 M⁴
- a_b = 0.073 reduced-Planck lengths
- Recollapse time is a_max, i.e. **0.073 reduced-Planck times.** (For closed radiation FRW from turnaround, a = a_max sin η and Δt = a_max.)

The classical Lorentzian universe does not exist, and the birth radius is already below the scale where semiclassical treatment is valid.

**2.4 If the recollapse is waived.** Suppose the same S³ expanded adiabatically instead:
- Its radius today would be **0.37 mm**, against a Hubble radius of 1.37×10²⁶ m.
- |Ω_k| < 0.002 needs **71.2 e-folds** of extra growth from this birth radius.
- For comparison, a standard nucleation at 1/H_inf with instant reheating needs 63.7 e-folds (H_inf = 10¹³ GeV), 60.2 (10¹⁰ GeV) or 54.5 (10⁵ GeV).

The 71.2 lands near the 71.21 constant I flagged on 2026-09-25. I am calling that a numerical proximity with no claimed connection.

## 3. R1: the perturbation spectrum. Attacked hardest.

**3.1 The historical record** (from background knowledge unless marked):
- **Causal generation.** Perturbations generated causally inside a decelerating FRW universe have density power P(k) ∝ k⁴ as k → 0 (Traschen's integral constraints). Causal seeds cannot produce superhorizon correlations at decoupling.
- **Defects.** Topological-defect models were incoherent and gave no secondary acoustic peaks; the 2000 acoustic-peak data killed them.
- **The decisive test.** Spergel & Zaldarriaga (PRL 79, 2180, 1997; title and venue confirmed this session): the **TE anticorrelation at ℓ ≈ 50–150** measures superhorizon adiabatic correlations at last scattering and cannot be produced causally. WMAP detected it in 2003 (first-year implications-for-inflation paper surfaced in search; not read).
- **What the survivors share.** Every non-inflationary alternative that survives the peaks makes modes leave a causal horizon somehow: a contracting phase (ekpyrotic or matter bounce), a quasi-static Hagedorn phase (string gas), a varying sound speed, or a holographic dual. None is a causal seed in a decelerating universe.

**3.2 The candidates, one by one**
- **(a) S³ boundary modes / Euclidean-vacuum fluctuations at the signature change.**
  - At birth the S³ radius equals its curvature scale (§2), so no mode is superhorizon in the sense the TE test requires.
  - The Euclidean vacuum becomes a scale-invariant spectrum only when followed by a quasi-de Sitter Lorentzian phase that stretches modes. That phase is what has been withdrawn.
  - Verdict: no generator without the phase that §2 already shows is missing.
- **(b) The Hopf/spectral sector.** The spectral action is a static trace. C9 §7's 2b theorem shows it has no scale extremum. Nothing in it evolves, so nothing sets A_s or n_s. **R1c.**
- **(c) Φ_NTE fluctuations.** Φ_NTE has ξ = 1/6 (C9 eq. (1)).
  - As a spectator in any de Sitter phase, whether a daughter phase or the parent's late de Sitter, the effective mass is m_eff² = 12ξH² = 2H², so ν = 1/2.
  - That gives P_δΦ ∝ k^(3−2ν) = k², i.e. **n − 1 = +2 (n_s = 3)**. Computed; scale invariance needs ν = 3/2, i.e. ξ ≈ 0 and m ≪ H.

- **The parent-aeon route generally.** The parent's late de Sitter phase has H_dS ~ H_Λ ~ 10⁻⁴² GeV. Every massive Standard Model field is superheavy relative to it. Photons are conformal and are not amplified. Only gravitons are amplified, with h ~ H_dS/M ~ 10⁻⁶⁰. It fails on amplitude by about 50 orders and supplies no scalar mode.   - It is a fluctuation in the DM:baryon ratio, so it is **isocurvature**, not adiabatic.
