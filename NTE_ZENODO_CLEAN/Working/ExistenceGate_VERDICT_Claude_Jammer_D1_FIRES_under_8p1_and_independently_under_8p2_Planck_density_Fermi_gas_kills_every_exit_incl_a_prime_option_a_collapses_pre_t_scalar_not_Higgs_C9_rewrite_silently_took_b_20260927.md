**The only ways out, stated so they can be ruled:**

## 5. Pressure on option (a): **it collapses into AC's pre-t path**

**5.1 The index argument (AC's "for (a)"): correct but not sufficient.** §8.1's stated reason concerns 1-forms with a timelike index; a scalar has none. On that argument alone, "and the Higgs" does not follow.

**5.2 The group argument, stronger than AC's bundle argument.** §8.5 (read): "At gauge birth the electroweak vacuum selects a direction e₁ ∈ Im(O) whose G₂-stabiliser is SU(3) … the quaternionic S³ ≅ SU(2) ⊂ S⁷ gives SU(2)_L and the complex line gives U(1)_Y."
- **SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y itself emerges at gauge birth** from the G₂/octonionic structure. Pre-t there is no SU(2)_L, **gauged or global**.
- So nothing pre-t can be an SU(2)_L doublet with hypercharge. This is not because of the index; it is because the group does not yet exist.
- **"And the Higgs" is entailed by §8.5, not by §8.1's index argument.** Option (a) repairs the wrong sentence.

**5.3 The locking cost.** A pre-t Higgs at inflationary field values would give Yukawa masses y·h during inflation. §8.2 says pre-temporal modes are "**neither massive nor massless**" and acquire mass at locking (Part 1 §VII).
- Pre-t Yukawas also need fermion representations, which §8.2–8.3 assign only *at* gauge birth.
- So **a pre-t "Higgs" can have no gauge charges and no Yukawas**, which means it is not the SM Higgs.

**Result: A-collapses.** Option (a), pushed to consistency, *is* AC's third path: an uncharged pre-t scalar.
