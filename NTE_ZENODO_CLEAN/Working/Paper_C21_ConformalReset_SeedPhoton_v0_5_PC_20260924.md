This paper specifies the mechanism by which NTE's Janus pair nucleates, and states its relation to Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology.

### Temporal language, and two distinct reasons for it

The parent aeon is **causally disconnected** from this one. It has its own e-fold coordinate; we have ours; no shared coordinate relates them. "Before" does not appear in this paper in that sense, because there is no relation for it to name.

This is **not** the corpus's existing pre-temporal rule, and the two must not be collapsed:

- **Pre-temporal.** "Before" has no referent because time has not emerged. N < N_lock, within one universe's own history. This is the standing rule (Manifest §6).
- **Inter-aeon.** "Before" has no referent because two *emerged* times share no coordinate. Both aeons are post-lock throughout.

Same failure of language, different cause. The distinction matters because a reader who takes C21's usage as deference to the pre-temporal rule will conclude that the parent aeon *is* the pre-temporal epoch. ## §3 The trigger: one photon per Hubble cube

Matter decays to photons; photon density thins; at one photon per Hubble volume the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence can no longer close.

**Convention, stated because it matters.** The trigger is *one photon per Hubble cube*:

> n_γ · V_H = 1, with **V_H ≡ R_H³ = (c/H_dS)³**, and n_γ = (2ζ(3)/π²)(kT/ħc)³

This is the convention canonical Part 1 §V now carries explicitly (Greg-ratified 2026-09-23), and it is the one the formula below has always used. It is **not** V_H = (4π/3)R_H³; earlier working text carried that definition alongside this formula, which is inconsistent by (4π/3)^(1/3) = 1.612. The factor is a convention, not a derivation — "one photon per Hubble volume" is an order-of-magnitude criterion — but the convention and the formula must match, and here they do.

Solving:

> x ≡ kT_reset·R_H/(ħc) = (π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) = **1.60121**
>
> **kT_reset = ħH_dS(π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3)** → **T_reset = 2.213 × 10⁻²⁹ K**

**Register.** Zero free parameters *given H_dS*. This is an internal consistency result, not a prediction of anything observable. **H_dS carries an Ω_Λ dependency**, and with Λ's value open on the ledger, T_reset is exactly as determinate as Ω_Λ is and no more. That qualification travels with the number wherever it appears.

**A consistency check that passes, and could have failed.** The de Sitter horizon radiates at the Gibbons–Hawking temperature T_GH = ħH/2πk. The trigger temperature sits above it:

> T_reset / T_GH = 2π × 1.601 = **10.06**

and the Gibbons–Hawking bath supplies only about **0.001 photons per Hubble cube** — three orders below the trigger. Had T_reset fallen below T_GH, the horizon's own thermal floor would have kept the volume permanently above one photon and the trigger could never have been reached. It does not. The bath is thermal and featureless, so it carries no information about anything beyond the horizon. Register: internal consistency.

**What is not derived.** That one photon per Hubble cube is the *right* criterion, rather than a natural-looking one, is not established. §12.2 carries it.

## §4 The first halving: a transaction that cannot complete

The physical content of the trigger is that the last photons have no absorbers. A photon emitted into a de Sitter universe late enough has no absorber within its future light cone; its worldline runs to future conformal infinity without closing. **Half a transaction: an emission with no absorption.**

In an accelerating universe this is the generic late-time fate of radiation, not a contrivance. The corpus reads it as a physical condition rather than an accounting artefact: when photons can no longer complete exchanges across the Hubble radius, the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence fragments.

**Crediting.** The framing *a photon with no possible absorber is a transaction that cannot complete* is **Cramer's transactional interpretation of quantum mechanics**, and behind it **Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory**. A search of the full historical archive (341 text files, 67 .docx, 14 .pdf) returned **zero** occurrences of either; the framework has used the criterion since the NTFN era without citing it. ## §5 The conformal step

Across the spacelike conformal boundary I⁺:

> ĝ_μν = Ω² g_μν

**Preserved:** dimensionless ratios; the trigger condition itself; S³ topology; null structure (ds² = 0 → ds² = 0); the quantum phase of the photon's wavefunction (conformally invariant for massless spin-1); helicity and polarisation.

**Destroyed:** every absolute scale. R_H ~ 10²⁶ m maps to ℓ_P ~ 10⁻³⁵ m. De Sitter is conformally flat, so the Weyl tensor vanishes and no conformal curvature information survives.

The reason the map works is the reason the whole paper exists: ds² = 0 is preserved under Ω² multiplication. A universe of purely massless content has no scale to lose.

### The seed's wavelength is of order R_H, and the ratio is universal

The corpus has carried two figures, λ/R_H = 0.625 and λ = 3.92 R_H. **They are one relation**, reduced and unreduced: ħc/(kT_reset·R_H) = 1/x = 0.62453, and hc/(kT_reset·R_H) = 2π/x = 3.92402. Neither is a mistake; what was missing was a label.

Neither is the wavelength of a typical photon either — at T_reset the Wien peak sits at 0.790 R_H and a mean-energy photon at 1.453 R_H. **The defensible statement is λ ∼ R_H**, which is what canonical Part 1 §V says and what holds under any convention. Where a number is wanted it travels with its label: *the reduced thermal wavelength ħc/kT_reset is 0.62 R_H under the n_γR_H³ = 1 convention.*

What survives intact is the **universality**: x = (π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) is a pure number, so the ratio is the same for every parent aeon whatever its β_NTE.

### The mapped seed arrives at Planck scale — by construction, not by coincidence

Taking the conformal factor at its geometric value, Ω ≡ R_H/ℓ_P ≈ 10⁶¹:

> E′ = kT_reset × Ω = (x·ħc/R_H)(R_H/ℓ_P) = x·ħc/ℓ_P = **x·E_Pl ≈ 1.95 × 10²⁸ eV**

with E_Pl = ħc/ℓ_P = 1.22 × 10²⁸ eV exactly. R_H cancels identically, so the result carries no residual scale dependence.

**This is a consistency of construction and must not be read as more.** Ω ≡ R_H/ℓ_P *is* the definition of the map — the parent's horizon scale is carried to the daughter's Planck scale. The trigger condition makes the seed's energy horizon-scale by construction, since n_γ ∝ (kT/ħc)³ against a volume R_H³ forces kT ∝ ħc/R_H on dimensional grounds alone. Multiply a horizon-scale energy by a horizon-to-Planck ratio and a Planck-scale energy is **forced**. §5's Ω and §6's Planck-density halt are not two independently fixed facts that meet; they are one assumption seen twice, and the cancellation of R_H is the map doing what it was defined to do.

That residue is thinner than it first looks: x is the cube root of π²/2ζ(3), a ratio of two O(1) constants from Planck statistics, so an O(1) coefficient was itself close to forced. The honest content is that the map is dimensionally coherent and delivers a coefficient of order unity rather than an absurd one.

The photon supplies the information. The bilateral split supplies the content, at zero net cost. §12.1 records why the information reading is forced, and what it costs.

## §10 The one observational discriminator, and it cuts against NTE

Penrose's CCC makes a claim about the microwave sky that NTE's variant cannot make: **Hawking points**, anomalous features imprinted by supermassive black holes evaporating in the previous aeon.

**The reason NTE cannot produce them is the modification in §9, not any fact about when black holes evaporate.** Penrose's crossover is a *single global surface*, so every evaporation event anywhere in the parent has somewhere on it to imprint. NTE's crossover is *per-Hubble-volume*, and each volume inherits exactly one datum. The discrete-and-local modification **is** the no-Hawking-points result.

Three supports, in descending order of strength.

**1. Causal severance — decisive, and in principle rather than in practice.** At the trigger the photon is alone in its observable universe. In de Sitter, anything beyond the event horizon at ~c/H is *permanently* causally disconnected: not distant, but unreachable for the remainder of that aeon. A supermassive black hole immediately outside the Hubble boundary has no channel to the photon and none to the daughter. How many black holes the parent contains is therefore irrelevant to what a causally severed patch inherits.

One qualification, stated so a referee does not find it first: what left the horizon earlier leaves static Weyl curvature behind, and cosmic no-hair (Wald 1983) erases it as roughly e^(−3Ht) rather than exactly. At the trigger epoch Ht ≈ 5.7 × 10¹¹, so the residue is of order one part in 10^(10¹¹). **Erased, not zero** — and in-principle unobservable, which blocks the objection that "effectively" is not "actually". A completeness remark only: in CCC the Hawking-point channel is concentrated Hawking radiation, not Weyl curvature.

**2. The trigger condition excludes black holes from the patch.** A black hole radiates at T_H ≈ 6.2 × 10⁻⁸ K × (M_☉/M). Setting T_H = T_reset ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻²⁹ K gives M ≈ 3 × 10²¹ M_☉. Any black hole lighter than that is *hotter than the reset bath* and would flood its Hubble volume with photons, violating n_γ·V_H = 1. **A volume containing a black hole cannot satisfy the trigger.** The condition enforces the isolation rather than coinciding with it. Register: internal consistency.

**3. The inheritance carries no localisation.** Whatever lay in the reset event's causal past has already been diluted to one photon — that is what the trigger states. A Hawking point is a *localised* feature requiring concentrated energy at a point on the crossover surface. NTE's inheritance is a scalar and topological datum (§7): a conformal factor, a topological class, a conformal class. **None of those admits localisation at all** — which is a stronger statement than the one this section made before the recast, when the inheritance was a horizon-scale mode.

**Precision on what crosses.** It is too strong to say nothing crosses. Exactly one thing does, by construction: the scalar/topological datum of §7. The accurate statement is **nothing localised crosses; the only inheritance is a global datum** — and that phrase is itself the argument.

This is the same open node as P1–P3, the constrained-system Euclidean saddle; it does not become a separate item.

A stronger reading — that the Euclidean section is a shared container for all aeons — was considered and set aside: a shared manifold carries a metric and therefore distances between universes, which is the relation causal severance denies and on which §10 rests.

### 12.2 Open — mechanism

- Derivation of the mechanism from NTE's axioms. Absent.
- Whether one-photon-per-Hubble-cube is *forced* as the criterion or merely natural.
- A first-principles derivation of foam fragmentation — converting "geometric tension reaches zero" from descriptive to derived.
- Whether "unclosed" admits a sharp covariant definition.
- Whether S³ boundary data of the type §12.1 commits to is consistent with the no-boundary construction.
- Which moment the foam transition anchors on (§2). Ordinary open work; **not** load-bearing for §10.

### 12.3 Open — transfer, and one item now closed

**Closed.** *Does the seed fix the daughter's e-fold budget?* **No.** N_* is a horizon-matching quantity and is insensitive to N_total; only the unobservable pre-inflationary stretch absorbs the budget. Both streams concur. C21 draws no consequence from this for any inflationary parameter — see §11.

**Open:**

- Entropy bookkeeping across the transition. Do daughter seeds inherit the parent's de Sitter horizon entropy? Connects to Bekenstein–Hawking area scaling and the open boundary-only Ryu–Takayanagi item.
- Whether each daughter inherits ℓ_dS as its initial scale — so whether the recursion is scale-self-similar or involves a jump.
- Whether CPN's B_total = 0 holds per-fragment or globally across all daughters. Corpus default appears per-fragment; not explicit.

- Confirmed Hawking points (§10) — disfavours the fractal variant. Conditional on confirmation.
