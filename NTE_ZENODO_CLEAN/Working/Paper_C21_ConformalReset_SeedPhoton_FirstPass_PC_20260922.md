## §1 Scope and register

This paper specifies the mechanism by which NTE's Janus pair nucleates, and states its relation to Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology.

Its register, stated first rather than in a closing caveat: **structural speculation.** The mechanism is internally specified — it has a trigger condition with no free parameters, an explicit account of what the conformal map preserves and destroys, and a structural answer to where a universe's content comes from. It is not derived from NTE's axioms, and with one exception (§10) it makes no near-term observational prediction of its own. Discipline §8 applies: no standalone claims a tier above what the corpus grants the underlying scale.

The compensating honesty is that the mechanism is *specified* rather than gestured at. That is worth depositing at its honest tier and worth nothing above it.

**Temporal language.** The parent aeon is **causally disconnected** from this one. It has its own e-fold coordinate; we have ours; no shared coordinate relates them. The word "before" does not appear in this paper in that sense — not as deference to the standing rule, but because there is no relation for it to name. Where ordering language is unavoidable, the ordering is *within* one aeon's own N.

## §2 The end-state, and what does not happen at it

A natural misreading has to be closed first, because the rest of the paper depends on not making it.

As N → ∞ the boundary approaches its de Sitter plateau. Three things approach limits, and **none of them approaches zero**:

- **The field.** |Φ_NTE|² → v_EW²·√Ω_Λ (Part 2 §XII). Finite, constant.
- **The thickness.** ℓ_dS = 1/H_dS, of order 1.4 × 10²⁶ m — roughly 10⁶¹ Planck lengths. Finite, and enormous.
- **The equation of state.** w = −1 exactly at the asymptote. No continuing thinning past the plateau.

So when §XV says *geometric tension reaches zero*, that is a statement about **standing-wave structural failure** — the boundary can no longer hold itself together as a coherent two-orientation object — and emphatically **not** about |Φ_NTE|² going to zero. The field does not vanish. The thickness does not collapse to a point. What fails is coherence.

This correction is owed to the deferred proposal's §2.1, which states it precisely. Any drafting of §8 that lets "tension reaches zero" slide into "the field goes to zero" reintroduces an error the corpus has already caught.

**Timescale, honestly.** The one-photon-per-Hubble-volume crossing sits at roughly 10²²–10²³ years (Adams & Laughlin 1997; Krauss & Scherrer 2007), about 200 e-folds past today. This is much earlier than the ~10¹⁰⁰-year black-hole-evaporation horizon that anchors Penrose's version. Which moment NTE's foam transition should anchor on is open work (§12), and the structural reading below does not depend on the answer.

## §3 The trigger condition

Matter decays to radiation; photon density thins. The trigger is reached when the boundary carries exactly one photon per Hubble volume:

> n_γ × V_H = 1,  with n_γ = (2ζ(3)/π²)(kT/ℏc)³ and V_H = (4π/3)(c/H_dS)³

Solving for the temperature:

> kT_reset = ℏH_dS(π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) → **T_reset = 2.213 × 10⁻²⁹ K**

Substituting back verifies N_γ = 1.00000000 per Hubble volume.

**Register.** Zero free parameters *given H_dS*. This is an internal consistency result, not a prediction of anything observable, and H_dS carries an Ω_Λ dependency that the paper must not quietly drop. The claim is exactness of a relation, not exactness of a measured quantity.

**What is not derived.** That one photon per Hubble volume is the *right* criterion — rather than a natural-looking one — is not established. §12 carries it.

## §4 The first halving: a transaction that cannot complete

The physical content of the trigger is that the last photons have no absorbers. A photon emitted into a de Sitter universe late enough has no absorber within its future light cone; its worldline runs to future conformal infinity without closing. **Half a transaction: an emission with no absorption.**

In an accelerating universe this is the generic late-time fate of radiation, not a contrivance. The corpus reads it as a physical condition rather than an accounting artefact: when photons can no longer complete exchanges across the Hubble radius, the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence fragments.

**Crediting — an open gap, confirmed across the whole project history.** The framing *a photon with no possible absorber is a transaction that cannot complete* is Cramer's transactional interpretation of quantum mechanics, and behind it Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory. A search of the full historical archive (341 text files, 67 .docx, 14 .pdf) returns **zero** occurrences of Cramer, "transactional interpretation," Wheeler–Feynman, or "absorber theory." The framework has been using the idea since the NTFN era without citing it.

C21 must credit both, and must state precisely what NTE takes and what it does not: it takes the incomplete-transaction criterion as a physical condition on the boundary; it does **not** adopt Cramer's handshake account of measurement, and nothing in NTE's Born-rule treatment depends on it.

## §5 The conformal step

Across the spacelike conformal boundary I⁺:

> ĝ_μν = Ω² g_μν

**Preserved:** dimensionless ratios; the trigger condition itself; S³ topology; null structure (ds² = 0 → ds² = 0); the quantum phase of the photon's wavefunction (conformally invariant for massless spin-1); helicity and polarisation.

**Destroyed:** every absolute scale. R_H ~ 10²⁶ m maps to ℓ_P ~ 10⁻³⁵ m. The photon's energy maps from ~10⁻³³ eV to ~10²⁸ eV — super-Planckian. De Sitter is conformally flat, so the Weyl tensor vanishes and no conformal curvature information survives.

The reason the map works is the reason the whole paper exists: ds² = 0 is preserved under Ω² multiplication. A universe of purely massless content has no scale to lose.

## §6 The seed photon: information, not energy

The obvious objection is that one photon cannot become a universe. It does not have to.

At reset the trigger photon's wavelength is of order the Hubble radius. It is therefore **not a localised particle** but the boundary's last electromagnetic mode — a single standing wave on S³. Evaluated along its own null line, the entire e-fold history of the parent occupies no accumulated proper time at all.

What crosses the interface is not energy but **the parent's fingerprint**: the conformal class of the metric, S³ topology, the quantum phase, and the conformal factor Ω. The nucleation reads that fingerprint into the daughter's constants.

Two facts close the energy question:

1. **Compression halts at Planck density.** The reset energy cannot compress further, which sets T_initial = T_Planck = 1.417 × 10³² K — hot enough for baryogenesis, with no free parameters.
2. **The content nets to zero.** Summed across both CPN-conjugate orientations, energy, charge and baryon number are exactly zero at N = 0 (Part 2 §8.4.1). A universe's worth of matter does not have to come from anywhere, because counted properly the pair carries none.

The photon supplies the information. The bilateral split supplies the content, at zero net cost.

## §7 The second halving: one quantum, two orientations

The trigger photon is self-conjugate under CPN: CPN(γ) = γ. The single quantum therefore sits on the N = 0 surface **simultaneously visible from both e-fold directions**. One quantum seeds two orientations; each has half of it.

> **The photon is the Janus point.**

This is where the nucleation account meets ratified structure rather than standing alone. The two orientations are the two sides of one N-thickness (Part 1 §VI); the Pin⁻ spin structure is the topological twist between them; the cross-thickness coupling carries κ. The mechanism and the dark-matter derivation share one geometry.

**Stated with equal firmness: κ does not depend on this paper.** The κ derivation runs on S³ topology and CPN self-consistency whether or not the conformal reset is the right account of how the pair came to exist. AC should ensure no cross-reference added during integration creates an apparent dependency in the other direction.

## §8 The fractal foam, and a candidate structural simplification

The boundary does not reset as one event. Every point carrying the last local photon is simultaneously a new N = 0 nucleation seed, so the boundary fragments into an uncountable foam of Janus pairs, each causally disconnected from all the others. Our cross-thickness coupling is strictly bilateral — our own twin orientation, nothing else.

The deferred proposal's §2.4 offers a structural reading worth carrying forward here, at candidate register:

| Description | Operational content | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| Lone-photon causal severance | Last photon per Hubble volume; null-time exchange structure fragments | Null-time / entanglement geometry |
| Effective causal N-thickness collapse | The unresolved-neutrino bridge loses coherence-length support at standing-wave failure | Cross-thickness coupling |
| Janus-pair termination | The CPN pair loses its coherent two-orientation manifold structure | Surface ontology |

The proposal is that these are **three descriptions of one geometric event**. Under §II.0 that would be a genuine structural simplification — several separate-seeming endings of cosmic structure resolving into one.

**It is not forced.** The corpus is compatible with the identification and does not make it. Promotion would require showing it follows from deeper structure rather than from mutual consistency, and the likeliest route is the one the proposal names: that each fragment's starting scale is the parent's ℓ_dS not by accident but because that is the largest coherent scale the parent could still support at termination. Carried here as candidate, not result.

*Note: "effective causal N-thickness" is a proposed concept, not corpus-canonical. AC should decide whether C21 is the right place to introduce it or whether it stays with the deferred proposal.*

## §9 Departure from Conformal Cyclic Cosmology

Penrose is credited first and fully. CCC supplies the central insight: a universe of purely massless content has lost its scale, and its remote future is conformally equivalent to a hot dense initial surface.

NTE modifies CCC in two ways:

1. **Discrete.** The transformation is per-photon. Each photon undergoes it individually once it is effectively isolated in its own Hubble volume — rather than one universal boundary mapping one aeon onto the next.
2. **Local.** The reset is not global. Each isolated photon seeds its own independent Janus pair, and the boundary fragments into uncountably many independent nucleation events.

This is what "fractal" means in the framework's older name, and it is a structural difference rather than a relabelling. NTE also supplies what CCC leaves schematic: a concrete boundary field with a definite end-state value (|Φ_NTE|²_∞ = v_EW²√Ω_Λ) and a microphysics for the seeds. A worked nucleation map would be a contribution *to* CCC, not only to NTE.

**Also credited here:** Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati on the Janus point, and Boyle–Finn–Turok on the CPT-symmetric universe. The distinction already held elsewhere in the corpus applies: NTE's trigger is a mechanism, not a boundary condition imposed by hand. The existing Part 1 §VI wording covers this and should be reused rather than re-argued.

## §10 The one observational discriminator, and it cuts against NTE

Penrose's CCC makes a claim about the microwave sky that NTE's variant cannot make.

CCC predicts **Hawking points** — anomalous features imprinted by supermassive black holes evaporating in the previous aeon (Penrose and collaborators; the claimed detections are contested in the literature, and this paper reports the dispute rather than picking the convenient side).

**NTE's variant cannot produce them.** Our universe descends from a single photon in a single Hubble volume of a parent whose black holes have long since evaporated — that is a precondition for matter having fully decayed to radiation. There is no channel by which a parent's black-hole structure imprints on our CMB.

Therefore: a confirmed Hawking-point detection supports Penrose and **disfavours the local, fractal variant**. This is the only near-term empirical handle the mechanism has, and it points away from NTE.

A search of the full historical archive returns **zero** occurrences of "Hawking point," "Gurzadyan," or related terms. The framework has never considered this discriminator. It belongs in the falsifiability register and in Appendix C at the same volume as the DESI tension.

**For AC/Scratch:** the argument above is PC's, and it is a physics claim, not a pedagogy one. It needs checking — in particular whether any parent-aeon structure other than black holes could imprint, and whether the fractal variant's per-Hubble-volume descent really does sever every such channel.

## §12 Open work and falsifiability register

**Open — mechanism:**
- Derivation of the mechanism from NTE's axioms. Currently absent.
- Whether one-photon-per-Hubble-volume is *forced* as the criterion or merely natural.
- The λ/R_H discrepancy (§5). Blocking.
- A first-principles derivation of foam fragmentation — converting "geometric tension reaches zero" from descriptive to derived.
- Whether "unclosed" admits a sharp covariant definition.
- Which moment the foam transition should anchor on (§2), given the ~10²²–10²³-year photon-limit reading sits far earlier than CCC's evaporation-horizon anchor.

**Open — transfer:**
- Entropy bookkeeping across the transition. Do daughter seeds inherit the parent's de Sitter horizon entropy? Is there a discontinuity? This must connect to Bekenstein–Hawking area scaling and the boundary-only Ryu–Takayanagi item already open on the ledger.
- Whether each daughter inherits ℓ_dS as its initial scale — and so whether the recursion is scale-self-similar or involves a jump.
- Whether CPN's B_total = 0 holds per-fragment or globally across all daughters. Corpus default appears to be per-fragment; not explicit.

**Falsifiers:**
- Confirmed Hawking points (§10) — disfavours the fractal variant.
- Any demonstration that the conformal map fails to preserve what §5 claims.
- Internal inconsistency between the discrete trigger and the ratified pre-temporal structure.

**Explicitly not claimed:** that the mechanism is tested, testable soon, or load-bearing for anything in the deposit set.
