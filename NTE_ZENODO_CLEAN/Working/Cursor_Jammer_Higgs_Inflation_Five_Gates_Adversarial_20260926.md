## G-2 — Unitarity. State of the dispute (do not pick the convenient side)

### What the literature actually says

1. **Burgess–Lee–Trott (2009, 2010).** Large ξ induces a tree-level unitarity / power-counting cutoff **Λ_vac ∼ M_Pl/ξ** already in the electroweak vacuum. Semiclassical inflation at or above that scale is not justified inside the EFT; UV physics that unitarizes can spoil the potential shape.

2. **Barbón–Espinosa (2009).** Same cutoff. The Higgs values needed for inflation satisfy |H| ≳ M_Pl/ξ ≫ Λ_vac, so extrapolating the pure SM potential into the inflationary region is **unwarranted without a UV completion**. Naturalness of “just the SM” is lost.

3. **Bezrukov–Magnin–Shaposhnikov–Sibiryakov (2010/11).** Cutoff is **background-dependent**. At large Higgs background it rises to **Λ_inf ∼ M_Pl/√ξ** (Jordan) / ∼ M_Pl (Einstein-frame statement of the same regime). They argue dynamical scales through inflation and (claimed) reheating stay below this rising cutoff.

4. **Later literature (through ~2022–2023).** Field-redefinition / S-matrix analyses (e.g. JHEP 06 (2022) 132) and background-dependent tree-unitarity (JHEP 06 (2022) 164) **largely confirm** Λ_inf ∼ M_Pl/√ξ during inflation and treat the vacuum M_Pl/ξ appearance as often an artifact of bad field variables for the inflationary background. **Separately**, violent preheating (longitudinal gauge-boson production with momenta ∼ √λ M_Pl) and collective effects **reopen** unitarity during/after reheating — often harder than the slow-roll epoch. R² / Higgs–R² unitarization is a common proposed cure (adds a scalaron — not free for NTE’s “zero new fields” pitch).

**State of dispute: LIVE and unresolved as a single verdict.** Consensus has shifted toward “inflationary EFT OK on background-dependent cutoff,” but Barbón-style UV-control of the potential and reheating unitarity remain open external objections. Picking only Bezrukov soft-pass would be convenient. Picking only Burgess kill would ignore a decade of amplitude/redefinition work. Both stand.
