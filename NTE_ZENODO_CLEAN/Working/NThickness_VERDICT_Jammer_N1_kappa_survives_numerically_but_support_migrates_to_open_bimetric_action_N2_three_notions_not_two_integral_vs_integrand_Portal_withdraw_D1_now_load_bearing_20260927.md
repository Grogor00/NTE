**2.2 T against ℓ is an integral-versus-integrand conflation, inside Part 1.**
- T = ∫dN/H and ℓ = 1/H agree only up to O(1) factors, and only in decelerating eras (standard FRW):

- **In the de Sitter future they diverge without bound:** ℓ → constant while T grows linearly.
- That is exactly the regime where Part 1 §V's reset trigger operates, and the trigger uses **ℓ** (V_H ≡ R_H³).
- §2.1.2 says the thickness is "accumulated proper time toward the CPN mirror — which is why it runs to ℓ(N) = 1/H(N)". That sentence equates T with ℓ. **It is false in the regime the conformal reset lives in.**
- AC's directive treats "thickness = ∫dt" as the only geometric notion. **The deposit also uses the integrand, for a different job.**

**2.3 δ against T, which is AC's collision.** It is real, and it has a clean resolution, because δ and T are *related*, not rival:
- §VII compares δ against N_I, "the imaginary extent".

- δ is **how much of that extent a species spans**, set by rest energy.
- A photon spans none of it.
- **Zero embedding depth ≠ zero extent at the photon's location.** That is the distinction the corpus needs to state.
