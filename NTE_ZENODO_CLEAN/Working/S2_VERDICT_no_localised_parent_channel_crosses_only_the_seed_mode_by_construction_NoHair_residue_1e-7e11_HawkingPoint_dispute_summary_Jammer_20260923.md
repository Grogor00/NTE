## Verdict

**Greg's position holds.** No localised parent structure crosses: not black holes, not their Hawking radiation, not anything else. There are two precision points, and C21 §10 should state both so a referee cannot find them first.

### 1. "Nothing crosses" is too strong. Exactly one thing crosses, by construction: the seed mode.

- **What crosses.** The single Hubble-scale photon mode crosses, and with it the global conformal data it carries: conformal class, S³ topology, phase, Ω. That transfer is the mechanism itself. Part 1 §V says so, and C21 §6 says so.
- **Wording for §10.** Replace "nothing crosses" with: **"nothing localised crosses; the only inheritance is one horizon-scale mode"**.
- **Why this matters.** The phrase "one horizon-scale mode" is itself the no-Hawking-point argument. A Hawking point needs energy concentrated at a point on the crossover surface. A single mode of wavelength ∼ R_H has no localisation to give.

### 2. "Permanently disconnected" is exact for signals. The pre-existing field is erased exponentially, not exactly.

- **Signals.** A black hole beyond the event horizon can send nothing new, ever. This is exact.
- **Frozen geometry.** Anything that left the horizon earlier leaves static Weyl curvature behind. Under cosmic no-hair (Wald 1983) that residue decays roughly as e^(−3Ht).
- **Size of the residue.** At the trigger epoch Ht ≈ 5.7 × 10¹¹ to 5.7 × 10¹² (for 10²²–10²³ yr), so the residue is about **10^(−7×10¹¹)** or smaller.
- **Wording for §10.** Call it **"erased to one part in 10^(10¹¹)"** rather than "zero". It is in-principle unobservable, and saying so blocks the "effectively is not actually" objection AC flagged.
- **Weyl curvature is not the Hawking-point channel anyway.** In CCC the channel is concentrated Hawking radiation, as AC's amendment already notes. So this residue is a completeness remark only.

### 3. Consistency check that passes: the trigger sits above the horizon's own thermal floor

- **The comparison.** The de Sitter horizon radiates at T_GH = ħH/2πk. The trigger temperature is T_reset/T_GH = 2π × 1.601 = **10.06** (n_γR_H³ = 1 convention, per the S1 ratification).
- **The floor's contribution.** The Gibbons–Hawking bath supplies only about **0.001 photons per Hubble cube**. It cannot pre-empt or fake the one-photon trigger.
- **What would have gone wrong.** If T_reset had come out below T_GH, the trigger could never be reached. It does not, and nothing in the archive checks this. The bath is thermal and featureless, so it carries no information about anything outside the horizon.
- **Register.** Internal consistency, same tier as §3. **New.**

## Hawking points: the literature dispute (for §10 framing; no side taken)

- **The claim.** An, Meissner, Nurowski and Penrose report an excess of anomalous circular spots in Planck and WMAP data, around 99.98% significance against ΛCDM simulations: "Apparent evidence for Hawking points in the CMB sky", *MNRAS* 495, 3403 (2020), arXiv:1808.01740.
- **The re-analysis.** Jow and Scott repeated the analysis. They found the signal consistent with ΛCDM, with no significant excess, and noted that the significance depends on analysis choices: "Re-evaluating evidence for Hawking points in the CMB", *JCAP* 03 (2020) 021, arXiv:1909.09672.
- **Status.** Contested, and unconfirmed as of the last reading. **Check for post-2020 work before deposit.**
- **For §10.** The falsifier stands as PC wrote it: a *confirmed* detection supports Penrose's global crossover and disfavours NTE's local variant. It should be stated as conditional on confirmation. It must not be framed as currently favouring either side.
