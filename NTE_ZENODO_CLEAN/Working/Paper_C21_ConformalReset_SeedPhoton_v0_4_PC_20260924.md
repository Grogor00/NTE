## §1 Scope and register

This paper specifies the mechanism by which NTE's Janus pair nucleates, and states its relation to Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology.

Its register, stated first rather than in a closing caveat: **structural speculation**. The mechanism is internally specified — it has a trigger condition with no free parameters, an explicit account of what the conformal map preserves and destroys, and a structural answer to where a universe's content comes from. It is **not derived from NTE's axioms**, and it makes no observational prediction of its own except the one in §10, which cuts against the framework. Discipline §8 applies: no standalone claims a tier above what the corpus grants the underlying scale.

**Nothing in the deposit set may be made to lean on this paper.** §7 states the κ case explicitly.

### Temporal language, and two distinct reasons for it

The parent aeon is **causally disconnected** from this one. It has its own e-fold coordinate; we have ours; no shared coordinate relates them. "Before" does not appear in this paper in that sense, because there is no relation for it to name.

This is **not** the corpus's existing pre-temporal rule, and the two must not be collapsed:

- **Pre-temporal.** "Before" has no referent because time has not emerged. N < N_lock, within one universe's own history. This is the standing rule (Manifest §6).
- **Inter-aeon.** "Before" has no referent because two *emerged* times share no coordinate. Both aeons are post-lock throughout.

Same failure of language, different cause. The distinction matters because a reader who takes C21's usage as deference to the pre-temporal rule will conclude that the parent aeon *is* the pre-temporal epoch. It is not: the parent is fully Lorentzian — photons, black holes, de Sitter expansion — post-lock from end to end. Its relation to this universe's own Euclidean section is an open question, recorded in §12.1.

## §2 The end-state, and what does not happen at it

A natural misreading has to be closed first, because the rest of the paper depends on not making it.

As N → ∞ the boundary approaches its de Sitter plateau. Three things approach limits, and **none of them approaches zero**:

- **The field.** |Φ_NTE|² → v_EW²·√Ω_Λ (Part 2 §XII). Finite, constant.
- **The thickness.** ℓ_dS = 1/H_dS, of order 1.4 × 10²⁶ m — roughly 10⁶¹ Planck lengths. Finite, and enormous.
- **The equation of state.** w = −1 exactly at the asymptote. No continuing thinning past the plateau.

So when §XV says *geometric tension reaches zero*, that is a statement about **standing-wave structural failure** — the boundary can no longer hold itself together as a coherent two-orientation object — and emphatically **not** about |Φ_NTE|² going to zero. The field does not vanish. The thickness does not collapse to a point. What fails is coherence.

**Timescale, and an open item that is not load-bearing.** The one-photon-per-Hubble-cube crossing sits at roughly 10²²–10²³ years (Adams & Laughlin 1997; Krauss & Scherrer 2007), about 200 e-folds past today — much earlier than the ~10¹⁰⁰-year black-hole-evaporation horizon that anchors Penrose's version. Which moment NTE's foam transition should anchor on is open work (§12.2).

This does **not** bear on §10. An earlier draft argued that the parent's black holes had evaporated by the reset, which is false at the photon-limit anchor and unnecessary at either. §10's argument is **causal isolation**, which holds at any anchor.

## §3 The trigger: one photon per Hubble cube

Matter decays to photons; photon density thins; at one photon per Hubble volume the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence can no longer close.

**Convention, stated because it matters.** The trigger is *one photon per Hubble cube*:

> n_γ · V_H = 1, with **V_H ≡ R_H³ = (c/H_dS)³**, and n_γ = (2ζ(3)/π²)(kT/ħc)³

This is the convention canonical Part 1 §V now carries explicitly (Greg-ratified 2026-09-23), and it is the one the formula below has always used. It is **not** V_H = (4π/3)R_H³; earlier working text carried that definition alongside this formula, which is inconsistent by (4π/3)^(1/3) = 1.612. The factor is a convention, not a derivation — "one photon per Hubble volume" is an order-of-magnitude criterion — but the convention and the formula must match, and here they do.

Solving:

> x ≡ kT_reset·R_H/(ħc) = (π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) = **1.60121**
>
> **kT_reset = ħH_dS(π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3)** → **T_reset = 2.213 × 10⁻²⁹ K**

**Register.** Zero free parameters *given H_dS*. This is an internal consistency result, not a prediction of anything observable. **H_dS carries an Ω_Λ dependency**, and with Λ's value open on the ledger, T_reset is exactly as determinate as Ω_Λ is and no more. That qualification travels with the number wherever it appears.

**A consistency check that passes, and could have failed.** The de Sitter horizon radiates at the Gibbons–Hawking temperature T_GH = ħH/2πk. The trigger temperature sits above it:

> T_reset / T_GH = 2π × 1.601 = **10.06**

and the Gibbons–Hawking bath supplies only about **0.001 photons per Hubble cube** — three orders below the trigger. Had T_reset fallen below T_GH, the horizon's own thermal floor would have kept the volume permanently above one photon and the trigger could never have been reached. It does not. The bath is thermal and featureless, so it carries no information about anything beyond the horizon. Register: internal consistency. *(Jammer, S2 verdict §3.)*

**What is not derived.** That one photon per Hubble cube is the *right* criterion, rather than a natural-looking one, is not established. §12.2 carries it.

## §4 The first halving: a transaction that cannot complete

The physical content of the trigger is that the last photons have no absorbers. A photon emitted into a de Sitter universe late enough has no absorber within its future light cone; its worldline runs to future conformal infinity without closing. **Half a transaction: an emission with no absorption.**

In an accelerating universe this is the generic late-time fate of radiation, not a contrivance. The corpus reads it as a physical condition rather than an accounting artefact: when photons can no longer complete exchanges across the Hubble radius, the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence fragments.

**Crediting.** The framing *a photon with no possible absorber is a transaction that cannot complete* is **Cramer's transactional interpretation of quantum mechanics**, and behind it **Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory**. A search of the full historical archive (341 text files, 67 .docx, 14 .pdf) returned **zero** occurrences of either; the framework has used the criterion since the NTFN era without citing it. Both are credited here.

- J. G. Cramer, *The transactional interpretation of quantum mechanics*, Rev. Mod. Phys. **58**, 647 (1986).
- J. A. Wheeler & R. P. Feynman, *Interaction with the absorber as the mechanism of radiation*, Rev. Mod. Phys. **17**, 157 (1945).

**What NTE takes and what it does not.** NTE takes the **incomplete-transaction criterion** as a physical condition on the boundary: an emission with no available absorber is a structure that has not closed, and at the one-photon limit that failure is universal. NTE does **not** adopt Cramer's handshake account of measurement, and **nothing in NTE's Born-rule treatment depends on it**. That clause is not decoration: Part 1 §II.0 already grades the Born rule a *reformulation* rather than an earned simplification, and an uncredited borrowing sitting beside it would read as a second undeclared import.

## §5 The conformal step

Across the spacelike conformal boundary I⁺:

> ĝ_μν = Ω² g_μν

**Preserved:** dimensionless ratios; the trigger condition itself; S³ topology; null structure (ds² = 0 → ds² = 0); the quantum phase of the photon's wavefunction (conformally invariant for massless spin-1); helicity and polarisation.

**Destroyed:** every absolute scale. R_H ~ 10²⁶ m maps to ℓ_P ~ 10⁻³⁵ m. De Sitter is conformally flat, so the Weyl tensor vanishes and no conformal curvature information survives.

The reason the map works is the reason the whole paper exists: ds² = 0 is preserved under Ω² multiplication. A universe of purely massless content has no scale to lose.

### The seed's wavelength is of order R_H, and the ratio is universal

The corpus has carried two figures, λ/R_H = 0.625 and λ = 3.92 R_H. **They are one relation**, reduced and unreduced: ħc/(kT_reset·R_H) = 1/x = 0.62453, and hc/(kT_reset·R_H) = 2π/x = 3.92402. Neither is a mistake; what was missing was a label.

Neither is the wavelength of a typical photon either — at T_reset the Wien peak sits at 0.790 R_H and a mean-energy photon at 1.453 R_H. **The defensible statement is λ ∼ R_H**, which is what canonical Part 1 §V says and what holds under any convention. Where a number is wanted it travels with its label: *the reduced thermal wavelength ħc/kT_reset is 0.62 R_H under the n_γR_H³ = 1 convention.*

What survives intact is the **universality**: x = (π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) is a pure number, so the ratio is the same for every parent aeon whatever its β_NTE.

### The mapped seed arrives at Planck scale — by construction, not by coincidence

Taking the conformal factor at its geometric value, Ω ≡ R_H/ℓ_P ≈ 10⁶¹:

> E′ = kT_reset × Ω = (x·ħc/R_H)(R_H/ℓ_P) = x·ħc/ℓ_P = **x·E_Pl ≈ 1.95 × 10²⁸ eV**

with E_Pl = ħc/ℓ_P = 1.22 × 10²⁸ eV exactly. R_H cancels identically, so the result carries no residual scale dependence.

**This is a consistency of construction and must not be read as more.** Ω ≡ R_H/ℓ_P *is* the definition of the map — the parent's horizon scale is carried to the daughter's Planck scale. The trigger condition makes the seed's energy horizon-scale by construction, since n_γ ∝ (kT/ħc)³ against a volume R_H³ forces kT ∝ ħc/R_H on dimensional grounds alone. Multiply a horizon-scale energy by a horizon-to-Planck ratio and a Planck-scale energy is **forced**. §5's Ω and §6's Planck-density halt are not two independently fixed facts that meet; they are one assumption seen twice, and the cancellation of R_H is the map doing what it was defined to do.

**How little survives, stated exactly.** What the definition does not fix is the coefficient, which comes out at x = 1.601. That residue is thinner than it first looks: x is the cube root of π²/2ζ(3), a ratio of two O(1) constants from Planck statistics, so an O(1) coefficient was itself close to forced. The honest content is that the map is dimensionally coherent and delivers a coefficient of order unity rather than an absurd one.

> **Provenance.** The overstatement corrected here — that the trigger and the Planck-density halt "were not tuned to meet" — originated in AC's first-pass review, was carried into v0.2 by PC without being tested, was identified by AC, and was then tightened further by PC when AC's replacement wording still implied a space of outcomes the construction never had. ## §6 The seed photon: information, not energy

The obvious objection is that one photon cannot become a universe. It does not have to.

At reset the trigger photon's wavelength is of order the Hubble radius. It is therefore **not a localised particle** but the boundary's last electromagnetic mode — a single standing wave on S³. Evaluated along its own null line, the entire e-fold history of the parent occupies no accumulated proper time at all.

What crosses the interface is not energy but **the parent's fingerprint**: the conformal class of the metric, S³ topology, the quantum phase, and the conformal factor Ω. The nucleation reads that fingerprint into the daughter's constants.

Two facts close the energy question:

1. **Compression halts at Planck density**, setting T_initial = T_Pl = 1.417 × 10³² K — hot enough for baryogenesis, with no free parameters. This and §5's arrival at x·E_Pl are **the same statement in two vocabularies, not two independent results**.
2. **The content nets to zero.** Summed across both CPN-conjugate orientations, energy, charge and baryon number are exactly zero at N = 0 (Part 2 §8.4.1). A universe's worth of matter does not have to come from anywhere, because counted properly the pair carries none.

The photon supplies the information. The bilateral split supplies the content, at zero net cost. §12.1 records why the *information* reading is not merely preferable but forced.

## §9 Departure from Conformal Cyclic Cosmology

Penrose is credited first and fully. CCC supplies the central insight: a universe of purely massless content has lost its scale, and its remote future is conformally equivalent to a hot dense initial surface.

NTE modifies CCC in two ways:

1. **Discrete.** The transformation is per-photon. Each photon undergoes it individually once it is effectively isolated in its own Hubble volume — rather than one universal boundary mapping one aeon onto the next.
2. **Local.** The reset is not global. Each isolated photon seeds its own independent Janus pair, and the boundary fragments into uncountably many independent nucleation events.

This is what "fractal" meant in the framework's older name, and it is a structural difference rather than a relabelling. NTE also supplies what CCC leaves schematic: a concrete boundary field with a definite end-state value (|Φ_NTE|²_∞ = v_EW²√Ω_Λ) and a microphysics for the seeds. A worked nucleation map would be a contribution *to* CCC, not only to NTE.

**Also credited:** Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati on the Janus point, and Boyle–Finn–Turok on the CPT-symmetric universe. NTE's trigger is a mechanism, not a boundary condition imposed by hand.

## §10 The one observational discriminator, and it cuts against NTE

Penrose's CCC makes a claim about the microwave sky that NTE's variant cannot make: **Hawking points**, anomalous features imprinted by supermassive black holes evaporating in the previous aeon.

**The reason NTE cannot produce them is the modification in §9, not any fact about when black holes evaporate.** Penrose's crossover is a *single global surface*, so every evaporation event anywhere in the parent has somewhere on it to imprint. NTE's crossover is *per-Hubble-volume*, and each volume inherits exactly one mode. The discrete-and-local modification **is** the no-Hawking-points result.

Three supports, in descending order of strength.

**1. Causal severance — decisive, and in principle rather than in practice.** At the trigger the photon is alone in its observable universe. In de Sitter, anything beyond the event horizon at ~c/H is *permanently* causally disconnected: not distant, but unreachable for the remainder of that aeon. A supermassive black hole immediately outside the Hubble boundary has no channel to the photon and none to the daughter. How many black holes the parent contains is therefore irrelevant to what a causally severed patch inherits.

One qualification, stated so a referee does not find it first: what left the horizon earlier leaves static Weyl curvature behind, and cosmic no-hair (Wald 1983) erases it as roughly e^(−3Ht) rather than exactly. At the trigger epoch Ht ≈ 5.7 × 10¹¹, so the residue is of order one part in 10^(10¹¹). **Erased, not zero** — and in-principle unobservable, which blocks the objection that "effectively" is not "actually". A completeness remark only: in CCC the Hawking-point channel is concentrated Hawking radiation, not Weyl curvature.

**2. The trigger condition excludes black holes from the patch.** A black hole radiates at T_H ≈ 6.2 × 10⁻⁸ K × (M_☉/M). Setting T_H = T_reset ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻²⁹ K gives M ≈ 3 × 10²¹ M_☉. Any black hole lighter than that is *hotter than the reset bath* and would flood its Hubble volume with photons, violating n_γ·V_H = 1. **A volume containing a black hole cannot satisfy the trigger.** The condition enforces the isolation rather than coinciding with it. Register: internal consistency.

**3. The seed carries no localisation.** Whatever lay in the reset event's causal past has already been diluted to one photon — that is what the trigger states. A Hawking point is a *localised* feature requiring concentrated energy at a point on the crossover surface. NTE's seed is a single standing wave of Hubble-radius wavelength (§6). A one-mode, horizon-scale inheritance has no localisation to give.

**Precision on what crosses.** It is too strong to say nothing crosses. Exactly one thing does, by construction: the seed mode, carrying the global conformal data of §6. The accurate statement is **nothing localised crosses; the only inheritance is one horizon-scale mode** — and that phrase is itself the argument.

### The falsifier

**A confirmed Hawking-point detection would support Penrose's global crossover and disfavour NTE's local variant.** This is the only near-term empirical handle the mechanism has, and it points away from the framework. It belongs in the falsifiability register and in the Open Ledger at the same volume as the DESI tension.

The claim is contested and this paper takes no side. An, Meissner, Nurowski and Penrose report an excess of anomalous circular spots in Planck and WMAP data at around 99.98% significance against ΛCDM simulations (*MNRAS* **495**, 3403, 2020; arXiv:1808.01740). Jow and Scott repeated the analysis, found the signal consistent with ΛCDM with no significant excess, and noted the significance depends on analysis choices (*JCAP* **03** (2020) 021; arXiv:1909.09672). Unconfirmed as of last reading. **The falsifier is conditional on confirmation and must not be framed as currently favouring either side.**

### A test that sounds applicable and is not

Searches for matched circle pairs in the CMB constrain non-trivial spatial topology, and a closed-universe framework invites the question. **NTE's S³ is simply connected, and a simply connected S³ produces no matched circles at any radius.** The null results therefore exclude nothing NTE predicts. The reason is topology, not a curvature radius large compared with the horizon — an argument from radius would be the wrong one and is not made here.

## §12 Open work and falsifiability register

### 12.1 The pre-temporal interface — the reading is forced, the mechanism is open

C21's seed photon arrives at the daughter's origin. NTE says a universe's origin is the **Hartle–Hawking Euclidean section at N < N_lock**, where time has not emerged. Two accounts of an origin sit in one corpus and have not been related. Either the seed arrives *after* the daughter's gauge birth — and then what occupies the interval to N_lock? — or the CCC variant *bypasses* the Euclidean section, in which case the corpus holds two origin stories.

**What the research round settled.** The seed cannot be read as **matter content** of the daughter: taken that way it recollapses the closed universe, requiring ρa⁴ > 10¹⁰. **Boundary data is therefore the only surviving reading — forced by elimination, not chosen among alternatives.** Both streams reached this independently.

The supporting observation is that the seed photon has **zero embedding depth** — no Higgs coupling, no rest energy, nothing that accumulates — which makes it the one object indifferent to whether time exists, because it gains nothing either way. It is not that the photon *is* the Euclidean section: Part 1 §III blocks that, since null-time is null *from* time rather than existence in its absence, and a Euclidean signature is positive-definite and admits no null vectors at all. The surviving form is that a null object of zero embedding depth is the kind of thing a **boundary can carry**, and under the boundary-only architecture the daughter's Euclidean section needs a boundary condition rather than an interior to contain a photon.

Concretely: the lowest photon mode on S³ is **n = 2, six-fold degenerate, with λ = πa** — not the naive n = 1. The datum's entropy is therefore at most k ln 6.

**What remains open is the mechanism.** Nobody has checked whether S³ boundary data of this kind is consistent with the no-boundary construction. The interface question above is not resolved by the elimination; only the choice of reading is.

**The null, stated once.** The round asked whether the boundary-data reading buys testable content. **It does not.** The reading is internally consistent and forced over its alternative, and it adds nothing observable beyond what the corpus already predicts. The seed sits *upstream* of inflation and is inflated away, which is independently why the searches came back empty.

**One narrowing, so nothing downstream promotes it.** The reading gives a low-entropy, Weyl-free start, which satisfies Penrose's Weyl Curvature Hypothesis. But the vanishing Weyl curvature comes from the **parent's de Sitter state being conformally flat**, not from the seed — so it is a property of any conformally-flat-parent scheme, Penrose's own included, and **does not distinguish NTE from CCC**. Register: explanatory, permanently. Not a test, not a distinguishing feature.

A stronger reading — that the Euclidean section is a shared container for all aeons — was considered and set aside: a shared manifold carries a metric and therefore distances between universes, which is the relation causal severance denies and on which §10 rests.
