## 1. Ruling: Fork 2, strengthened. Both routes fail, and the premise is sign-inverted.

**Q1 — the bilateral zero-energy condition does not imply flatness.** Two quantities are conflated: the Janus sum E₁ + E₂ = 0 over the CPN pair, and the single-sector FRW energy where E = 0 ↔ k = 0. The bilateral sum vanishes perfectly well with each sector closed.

**And Scratch's sharpening is correct — AC verified it independently.** Read as a genuine statement about the manifold's total energy, *"the total energy vanishes"* is a property of a **closed** universe specifically: a spatially compact universe has identically zero total energy, because there is no asymptotic region in which to define a non-zero one (the Hamiltonian constraint vanishes identically). Flat and open universes have infinite or ill-defined total energy. **So Companion 5's own premise supports Ω_k < 0 — the opposite of the conclusion it draws.**

**Q2 — the Euler identity is decoration here.** e^{iπ} = −1 as the antipodal map in §5 is correct and stays. In §4, "e^{iπ} + 1 = 0 encodes the zero-sum energy constraint" is a pun on *zero* with no physics between premise and conclusion. Same pattern as the six-note chord, the β_NTE √6/π characterisation, θ₁₃ and Cabibbo.

**Q3 — the dynamical route fails independently.** H = β_NTE|Φ|² fixes the expansion rate, not the curvature term −k/a². Part 1 §2.3 already grades the constraint a relabelling of the expansion history, with no curvature content. **Fork 3 refuted**, so nothing survives by that path either.

**Q4 — what survives is what Part 3 and item 11 already say:** closed S³ ⇒ Ω_k < 0, with |Ω_k| driven toward zero by the e-fold run. Sign fixed, magnitude unpinned. This **replaces** the derivation rather than rescuing it.

**Q5 — §9's falsifier is sign-inverted and must go.** It currently says any non-zero curvature falsifies; item 11 says non-zero curvature is *required*.

**No structural content is lost.** Observed near-flatness is still accounted for — better, in fact, since it becomes a consequence of the expansion history rather than a boundary condition asserted by wordplay.
