## 2. The five gates

### G-1 — Existence. *Does it actually stop the recollapse?*
Verify the recollapse bound independently, then show that a Higgs-inflation birth satisfies **ρ + 3p < 0** at the no-boundary handover with **a′ = 0**. **This is the gate the whole programme exists to pass** — if the accelerated epoch cannot start at the HH junction, nothing downstream matters.

### G-2 — **Unitarity. The substantive external objection.**
At ξ_H ~ 10⁴ the effective cutoff sits near **M_Pl/ξ_H**, plausibly *below* the inflationary scale — the model may operate outside its own domain of validity. **This is a long-running, unresolved dispute in the literature, not a footnote.**

Survey it against current sources: the original Burgess–Lee–Trott and Barbón–Espinosa objections, the Bezrukov–Magnin–Shaposhnikov–Sibiryakov background-dependent-cutoff response, and whatever has appeared since. **Report the state of the dispute; do not pick the convenient side.** State plainly whether NTE's inflationary scale sits above or below the cutoff on each reading.

### G-3 — **ξ_H ≫ 1 beside ξ = 1/6. The internal consistency gate.**
NTE ratified **ξ = 1/6** for Φ_NTE on conformal-origin grounds. Assigning the Higgs a coupling four orders larger requires an account.

**Two ways this can fail, and they fail differently:**
- If the framework has a principle fixing ξ = 1/6, **silence on ξ_H is a gap** the corpus must close.
- If it has **no such principle**, that **weakens the ξ = 1/6 ratification retroactively** — and Companion 17's K(Φ) closure rests on it.

**Either outcome is a finding. Report which.**

### G-4 — **Does it fit? Reality's gate.**
Compute (n_s, r) and H_inf for **NTE's actual parameters**, not generic Higgs inflation. Scratch's (0.965, 0.0037) at H_inf ≈ 1.5 × 10¹³ GeV is **unverified by AC** — treat it as a claim to check, not an input.

Confront both anchors: **Planck 2018 (0.9649 ± 0.0042)** and **P-ACT-LB (0.9743 ± 0.0034)**. Note that ≈0.965 sits near Planck and about **−2.7σ from P-ACT-LB** — the same tension the corpus spent 2026-09-25 on. **Print both; pick neither.**

**The good news, and state it:** r ≈ 0.0037 is inside CMB-S4 and LiteBIRD reach (σ(r) ~ 0.001–0.003). **This is testable within a few years, not merely consistent.** That is an argument for adopting it properly rather than quickly.

### G-5 — Interaction with existing NTE structure.
What does ξ_H ≫ 1 do to the **Higgs portal that already sets embedding depth** (Part 1 §III; Part 2 §8.2–8.3)? Does a large non-minimal coupling disturb the mass-acquisition ontology, the locking sequence, or the κ derivation? **And does it reopen the reheating question favourably** — Higgs decay to SM is automatic, so "not inflaton decay" would withdraw. Confirm that, rather than assuming it.
