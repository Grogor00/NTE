## 1. Greg's scoping is right. AC's addition to it is the problem.

**Greg's formulation is compatible with C21 as written:** pre-temporal is bulk, post-temporal is surface-only, *per bubble*. Each bubble has its own Euclidean section below its own N_lock and is surface-only above it. Nothing in C21 resists that.

**AC's prose adds one thing more:** *"a substrate that has no time because time is a property of each bubble's surface, **not of the thing they sit in**"* — and *"the substrate is the pre-temporal Euclidean bulk where the Hartle–Hawking section lives."*

**"The thing they sit in" is a shared container.** C21 §12.1 considered that exact reading and rejected it, in terms:

> *"A stronger reading — **that the Euclidean section is a shared container for all aeons** — was considered and **set aside**: a shared manifold carries a metric and therefore **distances between universes**, which is the relation **causal severance denies and on which §10 rests**."*

**This is not a register quibble.** §10 is C21's only observational discriminator — the argument that NTE produces no Hawking points where Penrose's CCC does. Its first and strongest support is causal severance: *"anything beyond the event horizon at ~c/H is permanently causally disconnected: not distant, but unreachable."* **If the bubbles sit in a shared metric substrate, they are at distances from one another, and "causally disconnected" becomes a claim that needs an argument rather than a premise.**

So the picture is beautiful and it costs the paper's only discriminator. That trade might still be worth making — but it should be made knowingly, and right now it is being made in prose.

## 2. C21 §1 draws the distinction this collapses, and says why

C21 opens by separating two failures of temporal language that look identical:

> *"**Pre-temporal.** 'Before' has no referent because time has not emerged. N < N_lock, within one universe's own history…*
> *"**Inter-aeon.** 'Before' has no referent because two **emerged** times share no coordinate. Both aeons are post-lock throughout.*
> *"**Same failure of language, different cause.**"*

And the reason the section exists:

> *"The distinction matters because a reader who takes C21's usage as deference to the pre-temporal rule will conclude that **the parent aeon *is* the pre-temporal epoch. It is not:** the parent is fully Lorentzian — photons, black holes, de Sitter expansion — **post-lock from end to end.**"*

**AC's sentence does not quite say the parent is pre-temporal** — I want to be precise, because overstating this would be the same error I am reporting. What it does is make the *substrate* pre-temporal and put the bubbles *in* it, which is the shared-container move of §1, arriving by a different road. The pre-temporal/inter-aeon collapse is the risk it creates for a reader, not a claim it makes.

**The relation between C21's crossing datum and the daughter's own Euclidean section is recorded in §12.1 as OPEN** — *"Two accounts of an origin sit in one corpus and have not been related."* AC's prose reads as having related them. If it has, that closes a named open item and should be filed as a closure with its reasoning, not absorbed into narrative.
