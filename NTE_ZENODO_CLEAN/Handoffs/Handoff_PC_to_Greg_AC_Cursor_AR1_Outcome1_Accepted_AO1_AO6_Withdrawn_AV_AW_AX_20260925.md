## 1. **AO1 and AO6 are withdrawn. The error was mine and it was not a small one.**

AC ruled AH1 this morning: the Born rule is a **reformulation**, not a derivation from less — *"Gleason's theorem read on an imported qubit geometry."*

I took that ruling and extended it. AO1 ended with *"the geometry it is selected from is **imported**, not derived here."* AO6 proposed adding to Chapter 6: *"the framework does not derive the qubit's geometry. It takes it from quantum mechanics, as everyone does … That is a claim about kinship, not about provenance."*

**That extension was mine, not AH1's, and three deposited companions contradict it.** Per AC's reads today:

- **Companion 2 §7.2:** *"S³ ≅ SU(2) appears as the quaternionic submanifold of S⁷ ≅ unit ð, providing **both the SU(2)_L gauge factor and the spin-½ state-space topology used here**."*
- **Companion 6 §5:** *"every element (the S³ geometry, the spinor harmonics, the Hopf projection) is a property of **the cosmological boundary**, not an abstract postulate. **This is not metaphorical**."*
- **Companion 6 §6:** *"**The Hopf fibre is the gauge fibre** — the U(1)_Y of hypercharge."*

The geometry is not imported. It is the framework's central claim about what that geometry *is*.

**The mistake has a shape worth naming, because it is the second time I have made it.** AH1 answered one question — *is the Born rule derived from less?* — and I let the answer travel to a second question it never addressed: *whose geometry is it?* Companion 6 §8 separates them in terms, per AC: **activation is closed, form-from-less is open.** I fused them. This is the same failure as the Chapter 4 rest-energy patch — taking a ruling and extending it past its scope — and it is why AR3 went to AC as a question instead.

**What survives:** AO2, AO3, AO4, AO5 — the register downgrades. AH1 is now triple-confirmed from Companion 2's own abstract (*"a reformulation rather than a derivation from less"*), so those are firmer than when proposed. **AN5 is also discharged** — AC has now read the source that owns the claim, which is better than the §II.0 read AN5 asked for.

**And Chapter 6 turns out to have it backwards twice, in opposite directions.** It overstates the derivation *and* understates the geometry. The corrected chapter is a smaller claim about the rule and a much larger one about the sphere.
