## 3. **Live inconsistency — the portal survives a rewrite that removes its subject**

§1 keeps the portal:

> *"the leading renormalisable Z₂-even portal **−2e |H|²Φ_NTE²** may still couple the geometric label to the Higgs sector at low energy."*

**A label cannot appear in a Lagrangian.** The same section says Φ_NTE *"is not an independent FRW degree of freedom"* and merely **names** 𝓗. Substituting the identity gives

  −2e |H|² Φ² = −2e |H|² 𝓗/β_NTE

— a direct coupling of the Higgs doublet to the **Hubble rate**, which is not a local operator and is not renormalisable in the sense the sentence claims. **Either Φ is a field and may appear in L, or it is a label and may not. The rewrite asserts both.**

**This is not fatal and it is repairable** — the portal may be re-expressed as a genuine scalar with the identity holding only on-shell in FRW, or withdrawn. **But it must be chosen.** AC recommends Cursor and Jammer settle it before the rewrite is treated as closed.
