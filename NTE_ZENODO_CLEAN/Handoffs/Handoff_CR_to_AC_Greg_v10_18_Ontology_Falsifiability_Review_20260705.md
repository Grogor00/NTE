## Ontological weaknesses (ranked)

1. **Central relation is a definition (HIGH).** §2.3 sets |Φ_NTE|² ≡ v_EW²(H/H₀), so Φ_NTE = √H relabelled and H = β_NTE|Φ|² is an identity (β_NTE = H₀/v_EW²). §2.4 concedes self-consistency selects no β_NTE across nine orders of magnitude. So the "boundary expansion field" is not an independent DOF, and "the addition is forced" (§2.3) is circular. Consequence: NTE's cosmological sector carries no dynamics beyond FRW — which is why Part 3 lists H₀/w/Ω_k/BBN as construction/inherited. New vocabulary over ΛCDM, not a new mechanism.

2. **"N fundamental, t emergent" is untestable in principle (HIGH).** Post-lock, §2.1.1 grants t = ∫dN/(β|Φ|²) invertible and standard physics "proceeds normally" → N-primary and t-primary make identical predictions everywhere observable. The axiom only bites pre-lock (Euclidean HH section), which is causally inaccessible. The one observational tether was n_s/r — now demoted to conditional. **The framework's defining commitment currently has no observational handle.** Empirically inert where testable; untestable where not.

3. **"Surface-only S³" contradicted by required machinery (HIGH).** §2.1.2 needs a Euclidean 4-bulk "bulk-dominated" pre-temporally; §VI gives the boundary an N-thickness and identifies it as ℓ(N) = 1/H(N) — the Hubble radius. A "surface" as thick as the observable universe has collapsed the surface/bulk distinction. Ω_k = 0 is derived "from the boundary-only ontology" (Part 3) while the ontology isn't boundary-only.

4. **The founding photon-paradox is trivial-or-false (HIGH, and §III admits the break).** §I's "exact correlation" (zero proper time ⟺ zero Higgs coupling) is just "massless ⟺ null worldline" (SR) ∘ "mass ⟺ Higgs" (SM) — no new ontology demanded — and it *fails* for hadrons: §III concedes ~99% of proton mass is QCD binding (zero Higgs coupling) yet the proton accumulates proper time. The real correlation is proper-time ⟺ energy ⟺ sub-luminal = relativity alone. The trilogy's opening argument doesn't survive its own §III.

5. **Two "earned simplifications" are re-descriptions by §II.0's own test (MED-HIGH).** Born rule "as Hopf projection" (§IV) = Gleason's theorem on an imported (Mosseri–Dandoloff) geometry, no postulate reduction, no new prediction. t = iΔN / Euclidean-saddle (§2.1.2) = Wick rotation + Hartle–Hawking no-boundary in NTE vocabulary, wholly within the unobservable regime. §II.0 says re-description without distinguishing prediction "fails the test"; apply it evenly.

6. **Ontologically central neutrino sector misfits data (MED-HIGH).** The "unresolved neutrino" forces Majorana (good, falsifiable) but sits on a sector with θ₁₂ = 1/3 at 2.9σ and atmospheric mass ratio overshooting ~1.5× (both Part 3 "open"). Signature ontology on the worst-fitting sector.

If independently forced → fine; if S³ chosen and the involution reverse-fitted → circular. §II.0 records S³ was *selected* on convergent grounds; state in Part 2 whether this is one of them or independent.
