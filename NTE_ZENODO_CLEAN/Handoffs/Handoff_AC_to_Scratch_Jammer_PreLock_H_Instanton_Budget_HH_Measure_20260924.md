## Framing (AC's reading, to be tested not assumed)

The Hartle–Hawking section is a **fixed 4-geometry** — for the de Sitter instanton, a 4-sphere foliated by S³ of varying radius, a(τ_E) = H⁻¹ sin(Hτ_E), zero at the pole, maximal at the equator where the Lorentzian section joins.

So **a varies and ΔN = Δ ln a is well defined as a geometric interval — but nothing expands.** Expansion is a rate, da/dt, and there is no t. The varying radius is like circles of latitude on a sphere: varying circumference, nothing growing. Pre-lock ΔN measures geometric separation, not elapsed anything.

If that reading is right, it has three consequences worth checking.

## P1 — What is H before the lock? *(completeness check; probably a gap)*

The corpus has **H = β_NTE|Φ|²**, and H ≡ dN/dt. **Pre-lock there is no t.** So either H means something else in the Euclidean section, or the constraint μ(N)[H − β_NTE|Φ|²] is doing something structurally different either side of N_lock.

The natural move is a Euclidean rate **dN/dτ_E**, with the Wick rotation relating the two — but AC has not found a deposited paper that says so.

**Check:** does any file in `Core_Papers\` state what H means at N < N_lock? Grep Part 1 §2.4 and §V, Part 2, Companions 8, 9, 15, 17 for the constraint's pre-lock reading.

**If absent, it is an open item, not a defect** — the corpus can legitimately have left it unstated. But it should be *registered* as unstated rather than left for a referee to notice. AC expects absent; report either way.

Related, and worth stating precisely in whatever verdict lands: **pre-lock ΔN is an ordering, not a time.** It is a total order on a coordinate with nothing propagating along it and no rate in it. This is the sharpest form of Greg's own standing objection — that "whatever N provides is already the operational content of time, renamed" — and it is why t = iΔN is graded a motivating frame rather than a mechanism. If the computation supports a cleaner statement of the distinction, the corpus would benefit.

## P2 — **Does the instanton join supply enough inflation?** *(consequential; AC believes this is new)*

If the section is a fixed instanton, the Lorentzian branch begins at a **determinate join**: the equator, ȧ = 0, with a specific φ. Given a fixed potential, that fixes the **total inflationary e-folds available** from the join to the end of inflation.

That is not a prediction of N_*. N_* is horizon matching — e-folds before the *end*. It is a **consistency requirement**:

> **N_inflation available from the instanton join must be ≥ N_\* ≈ 55–56.**

**Compute** on the corpus's own α = 2 attractor (Companion 17's K(Φ) closure, ξ = 1/6): what φ does the instanton join fix, and how many e-folds does the potential deliver from there?

**Pre-registered fork, to be stated before computing:**
- Comfortably ≥ 56 → consistency holds; the surplus is unobservable N_pre and nothing follows. Report and close.
- Marginal, or < 56 → **a genuine problem**, independent of the Companion 17 register question, and it would need its own item.
- The join does not fix φ determinately (e.g. the instanton admits a family) → say so; the question then dissolves and that is the result.

## P3 — **Does NTE inherit the Hartle–Hawking measure problem?** *(consequential)*

The no-boundary weighting is long accused of favouring **minimal** inflation — the standard measure-problem criticism of the HH proposal. If NTE adopts the HH section, it may inherit that preference, and the tension runs the wrong way: the measure pushes toward few e-folds precisely where the data needs ~56.

**Assess:** does NTE's construction inherit the standard HH weighting, or does the boundary-only/trace-free structure, the μ(N) constraint, or the Φ_NTE sector alter it? Survey the literature's responses (including Hartle–Hawking–Hertog and later developments) and report the state of the dispute **without picking the convenient side**.

**Note the interaction with a known result:** the raw-trace saddle was killed in 2026-08 (Tr f monotone on homothety families; no scale extremum), so NTE's nucleation saddle, if any, must come from the constraint plus the Φ sector. That may mean NTE's weighting is *not* the textbook HH one — in which case P3 may resolve favourably, or may simply become undefined until the saddle question closes. Either is a legitimate answer.

**If NTE does inherit it and has not addressed it,** that belongs on the open list. Adopting a construction with a known standing criticism and not naming the criticism is the same class of omission as an uncredited import.
