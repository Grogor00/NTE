12. **Doubt about a measurement must be pre-registered or external.** The only loop connecting this framework to physical reality runs through observation; every other discipline in this manifest improves self-consistency, which a wholly wrong model can also have. That loop is therefore protected as follows. **(a) A reason for discounting a measurement must be stated before its result is known, or must come from the experimental literature rather than from us.** Noticing that a result disfavours NTE and then finding grounds to doubt it is forbidden, however sound the grounds appear. Legitimate examples already on the record: the DES-Dovekie reassessment of SN-driven support, and the ACT-versus-Planck n_s discrepancy — both the field's own disputes, neither ours. **(b) Agreement inside an error band is reported with the band's width.** Fitting inside a wide band is weak evidence, because most wrong models fit too; the confidence comes from the narrowness of the claim, not from the agreement. **(c) Tune the process, never the model to the data.** No quantity is adjusted to match an observation, and where a magnitude cannot be derived the corpus says so rather than choosing one that works — as Companion 11 does for Λ ("no quantity in this paper is adjusted to match the observed Λ") and the Dark Sector draft does for |Φ_∞|.
