## 2. C11-1's two substitutions invert or undo their own intent

The OLD anchors are substrings of two sentences in §3. Substituted in place, they give:

- **(a)** "NTE **adopts** this structure, **because its substrate *is* a spectral triple of the Chamseddine–Connes type**…". The because-clause re-asserts inheritance-by-type, which is the claim being withdrawn.
- **(b)** "The unimodular projection **is not an adopted formulation, credited below** — and not, as earlier drafts stated, a property inherited … to fix Λ; **it is a property the framework already carries by virtue of how its gravitational action is defined.**"
  - This **inverts** the intent ("is not an adopted formulation").
  - It **keeps** the withdrawn claim in the tail.
  - It is ungrammatical ("…triple. to fix Λ").
- **Fix:** replace the **whole two sentences**, not substrings.
  - Suggested: "NTE **adopts** this structure: unimodular / trace-free gravity (Buchmüller–Dragon; Henneaux–Teitelboim). Unimodularity is not generic to spectral-triple gravity — the spectral action cited carries the catastrophic term — and the only Chamseddine–Connes source of it, the volume quantisation of Chamseddine–Connes–Mukhanov (2014), requires the Clifford algebras M₂(ℍ) ⊕ M₄(ℂ); whether NTE's 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆 triple admits it is open."
- **Also:** "as earlier drafts stated" is process metadata in a decontaminated deposit (Part 1's header says so). **Drop it.**
