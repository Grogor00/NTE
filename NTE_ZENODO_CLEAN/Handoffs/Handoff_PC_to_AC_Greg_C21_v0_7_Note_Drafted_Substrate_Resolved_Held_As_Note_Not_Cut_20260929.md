## 2. The note — replaces the *"Routed 2026-09-29, open"* block at the end of §12.1

> **Resolved 2026-09-29, and it strengthens the paragraph above rather than qualifying it.** Prose circulating in the working streams describes the foam's substrate as *"the thing they sit in"* — a pre-temporal Euclidean bulk in which the bubbles nucleate. **That is not the reading set aside above, and the difference is one of epoch.** Part 2 §XVI: *"Pre-temporally the Hartle–Hawking epoch is bulk-dominated; the surface-only statement applies after gauge birth."* The substrate is real and it is pre-temporal. What this paper rejects is a **post-birth** container — a shared manifold relating emerged universes, carrying a metric and therefore distances between them. The corpus scopes the two apart, and once they are apart both statements hold at once: the bulk exists below each bubble's gauge birth, and above it each bubble is surface-only and severed.
>
> **Within a single Janus pair the bulk is not absent**, and that is the right reading of §VI's N-thickness: Part 2 §IX describes the conjugate twin as *"the opposite surface with only residual pre-temporal bulk between them."* Two orientations of **one** pair are separated by residual bulk. Two **different** pairs are not separated by anything, because §XIV–XV has the foam's new Janus pairs *"each causally disconnected."*
>
> **This is why the substrate strengthens §10 rather than threatening it.** The objection the rejection above was built against is that a shared manifold gives distances between universes and so undercuts causal severance. A pre-temporal bulk cannot do that: **it carries no post-birth causal relation between bubbles**, and severance is a post-birth statement about what a causally isolated patch can inherit. The bulk is what each bubble emerges *from*, not a channel between bubbles that have emerged.
