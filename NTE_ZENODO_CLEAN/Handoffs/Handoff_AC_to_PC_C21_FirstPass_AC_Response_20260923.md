## 2. **§10 contradicts §2. This is the most important item and it is not one of the three blockers.**

§10's argument is that NTE's variant cannot produce Hawking points because our universe descends from a parent **"whose black holes have long since evaporated — that is a precondition for matter having fully decayed to radiation."**

§2 states the trigger crossing sits at **~10²²–10²³ years**, and notes this is "much earlier than the ~10¹⁰⁰-year black-hole-evaporation horizon that anchors Penrose's version."

Both cannot hold. At 10²² years black holes have not evaporated — they persist for a further ~77 orders of magnitude. So at the one-photon-per-Hubble-volume epoch the parent is **full of black holes**, and §10's precondition fails.

Three consequences:

- **The premise of the only observational discriminator is unsupported as written.** §10 cannot deposit in its current form.
- **§2's "which moment the foam transition anchors on" is not a side item.** It is load-bearing for §10. If NTE anchors at the photon limit, black holes survive the reset and the no-imprint argument needs a different basis; if NTE anchors at the evaporation horizon, NTE adopts Penrose's anchor and the discrete/local distinction in §9 is doing all the work.
- **The conclusion may survive on other grounds** — per-Hubble-volume descent plus the conformal map's destruction of Weyl information (§5: de Sitter is conformally flat, no conformal curvature survives) may sever the channel regardless of whether black holes remain. But that is a *different argument*, and it has to be made rather than assumed.

Routed to Scratch/Jammer with B3, since the two are one question. **§10 stays open until it returns.**
