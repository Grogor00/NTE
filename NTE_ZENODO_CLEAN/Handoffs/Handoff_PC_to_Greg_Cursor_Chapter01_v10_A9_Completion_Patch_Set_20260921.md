## Why v10

Reading v9 whole showed the A9 correction applied at the patched sites but not at the chapter's structural joints. Three problems:

1. **An internal contradiction introduced by v9.** P7's OLD block stopped at "Not just one of them." The next two sentences survived — *The photon does not see… It sees zero of both.* — so the paragraph now says there is no vantage along the line and then describes what the photon sees. Patch-set error (PC's), fixed at R5.
2. **An untouched section.** The opening of "The Forty-Million-Year Instant, Revisited" — the paragraph that restates the chapter's answer — still carries *From its frame*, *From your frame*, *taken from two vantages*, *the photon's frame does not register*. Fixed at R10–R11.
3. **The refrain.** *One object with two readings, real from both vantages* is a deliberate refrain at four section joints. v9 corrected it in two places (P4, P10) and left it standing in four, so the chapter argues both positions at different boundaries.

## The refrain decision

The refrain is retired and replaced at all four joints (R7, R8, R11, R13) by one idea stated consistently: **the line has length to anyone measuring it from outside, and no ruler along its own length.** This does two jobs. It carries P4/P10's measurement-versus-property distinction, so the joints now agree with the body. And it repeats P8's phrase — *the line has no ruler along it*, which P8 told the reader to hold on to — so the refrain now pays that instruction off at every section boundary instead of contradicting it.

Greg's standing ruling is untouched: *a photon experiences no time* stays as the Preamble's seed and is paid back at P8. Nothing here edits either.

## One further correction found in the pass

**"Same event, same place, same moment, same point"** occurs four times (R2, R3, R4, R5). It is stronger than the geometry. Emission and absorption are two distinct events a null interval apart; zero interval does not make them one point. Under A9 the accurate statement is that *nothing along the line separates them* — which is the no-ruler claim again, so the fix costs the prose nothing and strengthens the refrain. The Preamble's *It does not travel. It simply connects.* is kept; it is true as stated.

### R5 — The contradiction v9 introduced

**OLD**

**NEW**

### R6 — "From the photon's vantage"

**OLD**
From the photon's vantage, they were never separate to begin with. From our vantage, they look separated because we are reading the line from outside, where the line has length, and so the events that anchor it appear apart. The distance and the duration are not gaps the photon must traverse; they are how the connection appears to a vantage outside of it.

**NEW**
Along the line, nothing separates them. From our vantage — the only one there is — they are separated, because we read the line from outside, where it has length. The distance and the duration are not gaps the photon must traverse; they are what the connection measures as, to anyone positioned to measure it.

*"Appear apart" is dropped deliberately: they are apart, measurably. The measurement is not an illusion; it is the only reading there is.*

**NEW**

### R10 — Revisited, the restated puzzle

**NEW**

### R11 — Revisited, the answer (refrain, joint 3)

**OLD**
Both readings of it are real, taken from two vantages. From outside, the connection has length in time and space, because the manifold has unfolded between its two ends. From along the connection, both extents vanish together, because the photon's frame does not register the expansion at all. The puzzle dissolves not by reconciling two facts that looked in tension, but by recognising they were never two facts — they were two readings of one object.

**NEW**
Two true things can be said of it, and only one of them is a reading anybody takes. From outside, the connection has length in time and space, because the manifold has unfolded between its two ends; that is what you measure. Along the connection, both extents vanish together, because nothing on it registers the expansion at all; that is what the line is. The puzzle dissolves not by reconciling two viewpoints that looked in tension, but by recognising there was only ever one viewpoint — yours — looking at one object that has no ruler along its own length.

**NEW**
Along its path there is no *t* to count, though it rides the same expansion of the same manifold. Both facts describe the same unfolding.

**NEW**
