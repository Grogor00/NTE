## The gap being closed

Clause (c) as first drafted forbids adjusting **quantities** to match data. **It does not forbid choosing which structure to adopt on the same grounds** — and that is the failure mode most likely to get past us, because it does not look like tuning.

**Nobody would write "set N_e = 63.4 to match Planck."** But *"adopt exit (a′) rather than exit (a)"* is a structural choice with different observational consequences, and if made on those grounds it is hand-tuning in a structural costume.

**Door B is the clean test case and it passes.** It was adopted on an internal inconsistency — the 5 × 10²⁰ mismatch — and its immediate consequence was to **cost the corpus a prediction**. No fit improved. **That is what a legitimate system change looks like: it can cost you.**

## MF-D12c — replacement clause (c)

```
NEW (clause c, in full):
**(c) Tune the system to produce a better model; never tune the model to replicate the data.** No quantity is adjusted to match an observation, and where a magnitude cannot be derived the corpus says so rather than choosing one that works — as Companion 11 does for Λ ("no quantity in this paper is adjusted to match the observed Λ") and the Dark Sector draft does for |Φ_∞|. **This extends to structural choices.** Legitimate system changes are: improving the process; choosing what to derive next; and revising a structural commitment **on structural grounds**. Door B is the reference case — adopted because an internal inconsistency forced it, and its immediate effect was to lose the corpus a prediction. **Illegitimate: adopting a structural option because of where it lands the numbers.** Choosing among mechanisms, formulations or exits **after** seeing which fits best is hand-tuning in structural form, and is forbidden whether or not any parameter is touched. **The protection is procedural: outcomes are pre-registered before the computation that selects among them** (see the forks in every directive since 2026-09-24). A mechanism must never be adopted after its fit is known.
```

## The methodology, locked — for any future session

**The corpus's epistemic position in five statements:**

1. **Internal consistency is necessary and not sufficient.** Every discipline except 12 improves self-consistency, which a wholly wrong model can also have.
2. **Observation is the only link to physical reality**, and the corpus has **three** channels: κ ≈ 5.50 (CMB-S4), w = −1 (DESI and successors), forced Majorana (0νββ). It had four; native (n_s, r) was withdrawn 2026-09-26.
3. **Doubt about a measurement is pre-registered or external** (12a). Two of the three handles face anchors in mutual tension, so a defensible excuse will always be available.
4. **Agreement is reported with the band width** (12b). Confidence comes from the narrowness of the claim, not from the agreement.
5. **Tune the system, never the model — including structurally** (12c, above).

**The corpus's own best statement of this is Companion 11's**, and no paraphrase improves on it:

> *"Empty space is quiet because the geometry forbids the loud term — not because large terms finely cancel, and not as a tuned coincidence."*

**The claim is the prohibition; the magnitude is explicitly not claimed. Every result should have that shape.**

## Note on scope

**These disciplines govern how claims are made, not what may be investigated.** Nothing here forbids exploring a mechanism, computing its consequences, or finding that it fits well. **What is forbidden is selecting it because it fits.** The distinction is the pre-registered fork, and it is procedural rather than a matter of judgement — which is the point, since judgement is what fails under the temptation these rules exist for.
