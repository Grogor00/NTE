## The register problem, and why it comes first

This is the most speculative construction in the corpus, and it is also the framework's origin story. Those two facts pull in opposite directions, and the paper fails if it does not settle the tension on page one.

The discipline that applies is §8: *no standalone claims a tier above what the corpus grants the underlying scale.* The nucleation mechanism is **structural speculation** — internally specified, not derived from the axioms, and with no near-term observational test of its own. The Companion must say that in its abstract, not bury it in a closing caveat. A paper that opens by narrating the fractal foam and admits the tier in §9 is doing the thing the corpus forbids elsewhere.

The compensating honesty: the mechanism is *specified*, not gestured at. It has a zero-free-parameter trigger condition, an explicit statement of what the conformal map preserves and destroys, and a structural account of where a universe's content comes from. That is worth depositing at its honest tier. It is not worth depositing at a tier it has not earned.

## §1 — Scope and register

States the tier before any content. What this paper does: specifies a nucleation mechanism and its relation to Penrose's CCC. What it does not do: derive that mechanism from NTE's axioms, or offer a test that distinguishes it from CCC in the near term (with one exception, §8).

Also fixes the temporal language, since this paper is the hardest case in the corpus for it. The parent is **causally disconnected**, not *earlier*. It has its own N; we have ours; no shared coordinate connects them. "Before" does not appear in this paper — not as a concession to the standing rule, but because there is no relation for it to name.

## §2 — The trigger: a transaction that cannot complete

The physical condition. Matter decays to photons; photon density thins; at one photon per Hubble volume the null-time exchange structure that sustains causal coherence can no longer close.

- n_γ × V_H = 1, with n_γ = (2ζ(3)/π²)(kT/ℏc)³ and V_H = (4π/3)R_H³
- kT_reset = ℏH_dS(π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) → T_reset = 2.213 × 10⁻²⁹ K
- Verifies back to N_γ = 1.00000000 per Hubble volume

**Register:** zero free parameters *given H_dS*. This is an internal consistency result, not a prediction of anything observable. H_dS carries an Ω_Λ dependency, and the paper states it.

**Crediting gap to close here.** "A photon with no possible absorber is a transaction that cannot complete" is Cramer's transactional interpretation, and behind it Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory. Neither is cited in any NTFN- or NTE-era text; grep returns zero. Same class as the BFT and Chamseddine–Connes items already closed book-wide. The paper credits both and states what NTE takes (the incomplete-transaction criterion) and what it does not (Cramer's handshake account of measurement).

## §3 — The first halving

The unclosed photon is half a transaction: an emission with no absorption. In an accelerating universe this is the generic late-time fate, not a contrivance — a photon emitted late enough has no absorber inside its future light cone, and its worldline runs to I⁺ without closing.

This section makes precise what "unclosed" means geometrically and why the framework treats it as a physical condition rather than an accounting artefact.

## §5 — The seed photon as information, not energy

The reader's obvious objection: one photon cannot become a universe. The answer is that it does not have to.

- Wavelength ~R_H — not a localised particle but the last standing electromagnetic mode on the boundary
- Carries the conformal class, S³ topology, phase, and Ω — the parent's fingerprint, which nucleation reads into the daughter's constants
- Compression halts at Planck density: T_initial = T_Planck = 1.417 × 10³² K, which makes baryogenesis viable
- The content comes from the bilateral split, not the photon: summed across both CPN-conjugate orientations, energy, charge and baryon number are exactly zero at N = 0

This is the section that answers "where does a universe's worth of stuff come from," and the answer is *zero, counted properly*.

## §6 — The second halving: one quantum, two orientations

CPN(γ) = γ. The trigger photon is self-conjugate, so the single quantum sits on the N = 0 surface visible from both e-fold directions. One quantum seeds two orientations; each has half of it. **The photon is the Janus point.**

This is where the paper connects to ratified structure rather than standing alone: the N-thickness (§6.1–6.3 of the earlier corpus), the Pin⁻ spin structure, and the cross-thickness coupling that carries κ. The nucleation account and the dark-matter derivation share a geometry, and the Companion should say so precisely — while being explicit that **κ does not depend on this paper.** The κ derivation stands on S³ topology and CPN self-consistency whether or not the conformal reset is right. Nothing in the deposit set may be made to lean on Companion 21.

## §7 — Departure from CCC

Penrose credited fully and first. Then the two modifications:

1. **Discrete** — each photon transforms individually once isolated in its own Hubble volume, rather than one universal boundary mapping one aeon to the next
2. **Local** — the boundary fragments; each isolated photon seeds its own Janus pair; the result is an uncountable foam of causally disconnected pairs

This is what "fractal" means in the framework's name, and it is the substantive difference from CCC rather than a relabelling of it.

Also credited here: Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati on the Janus point, and Boyle–Finn–Turok, with the distinction already held elsewhere in the corpus — NTE's trigger is a mechanism, not a boundary condition imposed by hand.

## §8 — The one observational discriminator, and it cuts against us

Penrose's CCC makes a CMB claim: Hawking points, from supermassive black holes evaporating in the previous aeon, appearing as anomalous features in the microwave sky.

**NTE's variant cannot produce them.** Our universe descends from a single photon in a single Hubble volume, and the parent's black holes have long since evaporated by the time matter has fully decayed to radiation. There is no channel by which a parent's black-hole structure imprints on our CMB.

So: a confirmed Hawking-point detection supports Penrose and **disfavours the fractal variant**. This is the only near-term empirical handle the mechanism has, and it points the wrong way for NTE. It belongs in the falsifiability register and in Appendix C, stated as plainly as the DESI tension is.

(Status of the claimed detections is contested in the literature. The Companion reports the dispute rather than picking the side convenient to NTE.)

## §9 — α inheritance and the recursion

α is inherited, not derived — established negatively, and that negative is a real result: the boundary tension μ(N) is well-behaved for all tested α across nine orders of magnitude, so self-consistency does not select it. §2.4.1's "closed question with a negative answer" framing is correct and should be preserved.

The recursion α_{n+1} = F(Ω(α_n)), with our universe possibly at a fixed point, is the speculative edge of a speculative paper. v10.5.9 already flags it as structural speculation. The Companion keeps that flag and does not upgrade it.

## §10 — Falsifiability register and open work

**Open:** derivation of the mechanism from the axioms; the λ/R_H discrepancy (§4); whether the one-photon criterion is forced or merely natural; the recursion's fixed-point structure; whether "unclosed" admits a sharp covariant definition.

**Falsifiers:** confirmed Hawking points (§8, disfavours); any demonstration that the conformal map fails to preserve what §4 claims; internal inconsistency between the discrete trigger and the ratified pre-temporal structure.

**Explicitly not claimed:** that this is tested, testable soon, or load-bearing for anything in the deposit set.
