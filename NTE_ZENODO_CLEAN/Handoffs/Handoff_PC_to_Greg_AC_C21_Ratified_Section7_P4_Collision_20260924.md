If the Z₂ acts freely or CPN is global, the sign is a phase and the reading survives — a sign distinct from δ_J.
>
> *(Earlier corpus text carried the line "CPN(γ) = γ", which conflates self-conjugate with C-even. The photon is C-odd. That formulation is not used here.)*

Two consequential words in that draft: **proposes** rather than states, and **excluded** rather than disfavoured. Both are what the verdict supports.
