## The heat-kernel expansion

A heat-kernel expansion is a standard mathematical technique for organising quantum-field calculations on curved backgrounds. You compute the trace of the operator exp(−tD²) for small t, and the result expands as a series in t, with coefficients a₀, a₂, a₄, … that are integrals of curvature invariants over the manifold (plus internal-algebra trace contributions).

For the framework's setting, the leading coefficients are:

a₀ ∝ Vol(M) × N_F — the volume contribution, weighted by the finite-algebra trace dimension N_F = 180. This is the leading term and gives the bare cosmological-constant scale before structural cancellation.

a₂ ∝ ∫_M R √g d⁴x × N_F — the scalar-curvature contribution. This is the Einstein-Hilbert term in the resulting effective action.

a₄ ∝ ∫_M (5R² − 8R_μν² − 7R_μνρσ²) √g d⁴x × N_F — the curvature-squared combination. This is the standard 4D conformal trace anomaly for Dirac fermions.

The a₄ combination is where a structural identity that organises the chord lives. On the framework's canonical substrate S³ × S¹, this combination decomposes algebraically into the Weyl-squared part and the Euler density: F₁ = 5R² − 8R_μν² − 7R_μνρσ² = −18 C² + 11 E₄. Both C² and E₄ evaluate to zero pointwise on S³ × S¹: the Weyl tensor vanishes because the curvature is isotropic in the S³ directions, and the Euler density vanishes because of the product with a flat factor. The companion note on F₁ uniqueness establishes that S³ × S¹ is the unique member of the product-of-spheres family {S^p × F^q : p ∈ {2,3,4}} where both vanish simultaneously (uniqueness is not claimed within the broader class of closed 4-manifolds: flat T⁴ and closed-hyperbolic N³ × S¹ also have Weyl-flat / Euler-zero structure outside this family).

The result is that the a₄ coefficient on the canonical substrate **vanishes**: the simultaneous Weyl-flat and Euler-zero structure of S³ × S¹, combined with the contribution from the untwisted product Dirac operator, sends the full F₁ combination to zero. This vanishing is a structural consequence of the substrate's geometry, not a tuned cancellation.

This is what reduces the chord to five notes. The original framework presentation listed six notes, and two of those notes relied on a₄-route arithmetic that the vanishing closes off. The inflation-sector predictions (n_s and r) would have entered the chord via the kinetic-term default K(Φ) = 1 inferred from a₄ structure; with a₄ vanishing, that route is unavailable, and K(Φ) becomes an open structural question. The cosmological-constant numerical residual would have entered via a non-zero a₄ contribution; with a₄ vanishing, the framework's Λ account is structural-only rather than numerical at the chord's present register. The five remaining notes — those that emerge from a₀ and a₂ heat-kernel orders or from cross-cutting structural identifications — organise the chord at its current scope.

## What would break it

The five-note chord has multiple paths to falsification, distributed across the notes.

If the heat-kernel computation on the canonical substrate yields a coefficient different from the framework's prediction — most directly visible in the κ₀ extraction — the chord breaks at the computational step. Independent verification by other groups is the most direct test.

If the simultaneous Weyl-flat + Euler-zero structure of S³ × S¹ turns out to be wrong (the C² and E₄ vanishing fails by direct calculation), the chord's organising identity fails. The companion note *The Shape Where the Anomaly Vanishes* covers this within the product-of-spheres family; the same algebraic fact has to hold for the chord to organise.

If precision measurements of any individual chord note — the dark-matter ratio, the gauge-birth thermal scale (via the bare coupling's downstream dependencies), the neutrino mass scales, the structural Λ commitment — diverge substantially from the framework's predictions, the corresponding note fails. Because the notes are structurally connected, a single decisive miss propagates into the chord's collective standing.

If the bilateral-mirror closure step that produces κ = 5.50 from κ₀ = 0.846 fails (the framework's identification of dark matter with the gravitational echo of the other orientation is wrong), Note 1's downstream physical reading fails — though the bare coupling κ₀ from heat-kernel alone would still stand at the math-physics level.

The two inflation-sector predictions reported separately — n_s and r — have their own falsifier paths consistent with their honest registers: a future CMB measurement shifting n_s outside the slow-roll range invalidates the framework's slow-roll reading without breaking a chord note directly; a future r detection at the conditional value confirms the K(Φ) = 1 default reading or, alternatively, a detection at substantially different r value invalidates that default. These tests sit at the honest registers the framework reports them at.

The collective signal is whether the pattern across notes holds together under precision improvements (CMB-S4 in the 2030s; DUNE / Hyper-K; next-generation 0νββ searches). The chord either tightens (matches stay clean or move toward exact agreement) or breaks (one or more notes diverge under improved measurement).
