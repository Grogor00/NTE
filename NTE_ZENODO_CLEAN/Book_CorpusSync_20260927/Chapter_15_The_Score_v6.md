## §1 What This Chapter Does

This is the line in the book where the framework puts its money down.

The previous chapters built one picture: closed boundary, two scalars, Hopf geometry, bilateral thickness, the unresolved neutrino, the instrument-and-chord arc in Chapters 11–13. The technical papers organise that picture at the gauge-birth boundary; here it's enough to say the pieces lock together — pull on one thread and the others move.

This chapter lists the framework's claims and what each one would cost NTE if it failed. Some already match observation within the quoted errors. Others wait on measurements due in the next decade. A few are bets on *absence*: no WIMP, no fourth generation, no W_R — and so far the experiments keep finding nothing.

The list is not a menu of independent guesses, and it is not a list of equals. The framework grades its own claims, and the scorecard keeps the grades visible. Three claims sit in the strictest tier — *genuine zero-parameter predictions*, numbers or structures forced by the geometry with no observational input anywhere in their chains, made about observables that were never used to build the framework: the dark-matter ratio κ, the maximal CP phase, and the maximal atmospheric mixing angle. Below them sit the chord's structural results at their own registers; an open early-universe sector whose pathway is not yet chosen (§3); consequences that follow by construction and are honestly not tests at all; the claims the framework has withdrawn; and the open tensions — the places where it is currently *losing* — which have their own catalogue in Appendix C, because a scorecard that lists only wins is advertising, not accounting.

On scope, one correction this edition makes explicit: the chord of Chapter 12 does not include the CMB spectral index or the tensor-to-scalar ratio. Earlier presentations seated native inflationary numbers among the chord's notes or in a conditional tier wired to Φ_NTE; under Door B those native (n_s, r) values are **withdrawn** as framework predictions — Φ_NTE is not the inflaton, and GR is restored. The early-universe sector itself is open (Option D), with live pathways listed in §3. Λ likewise stands as structural quietness — the framework's account of why empty space is not catastrophically loud — without a numerical value at the framework's current state; Chapter 13 develops what the structural account claims and does not claim.

The rest of the chapter is the scorecard.

## §2 The Chord — Notes From One Source

These lines are the chord from Chapter 12 — one heat-kernel reading on the spectral triple, each note at its honest register. What each line claims, and where observation stands:

**κ = 5.50 (dark matter to ordinary matter) — zero-parameter, passed.** Chapter 9 derived this in full. Bare coupling κ₀ = 3/√(4π) ≈ 0.846 from the Hopf monopole projection on S³; algebraic closure κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) ≈ 5.50 — topologically demonstrated, with no thickness-magnitude input. Planck: 5.38 ± 0.15 (0.83σ). Ontological support for the ratio has migrated to the open bilateral two-metric action: the averaged-sector equations already reproduce the same resummation, while the full nonlinear ghost-free completion remains open work. The corpus does not claim that completion is guaranteed to leave the averaged value untouched; that question stays on the ledger. This is the first of the framework's three genuine zero-parameter predictions. CMB-S4 in the early 2030s will tighten the measurement by roughly a factor of two; a shift beyond ~3σ would break the bilateral reading.

**T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) (gauge-birth temperature) — conditional.** When gauge symmetry and clocks switched on, the boundary field locked and post-temporal dynamics began. Standard cosmology treats that temperature as an initial condition; the framework places T_GB ≈ 4.9 × 10¹⁸ GeV at the width of the Hartle–Hawking Gaussian saddle on the Hopf fibre — the same single relation that fixes the temporal circle's size, with √(2π) the Gaussian normalisation rather than a number anyone chose. The register is the honest part. The raw-trace route to that saddle is ruled out, and the route that survives rests on identifying the fibre modulus from the spectral side — a step the framework has not independently grounded. The corpus therefore carries T_GB as *conditional*, and so does this chapter. No thermometer reaches the birth event; the commitment is structural, and its failure mode is structural too — the fibre relation threads the whole computation, so a wrong T_GB does not fail quietly.

**M₁ ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV (leptogenesis scale) — cross-check, passed at order one.** Why matter won over antimatter is the leptogenesis story: the lightest heavy neutrino's mass sets the decay channel, and standard work leaves that mass free. The framework's geometry places it: M₁ = (8π)^(1/3)(M_Pl² H_GB)^(1/3) ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV, landing within a factor of about 1.1 of what the standard mechanism requires for the observed asymmetry. The corpus grades this a *cross-check* — a consistency between a geometric output and an external requirement, neither adjusted toward the other — and deliberately not a prediction.

**M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV (heavy seesaw scale) — pathway identified, not derived.** Type-I seesaw models need a heavy partner so light neutrino masses go like v_EW²/M_R; standard treatments fit M_R by hand. The framework has identified a structural pathway — three powers of the Planck mass against one of the Higgs scale, M_R = M_Pl^(3/4) v_EW^(1/4) ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, the canonical seesaw size for sub-eV neutrinos — but the derivation is not closed, and the scorecard carries it at exactly that register: pathway, not theorem. No collider will reach M_R directly; the downstream checks run through the visible masses and the double-beta-decay window (§5).

**m_ν₂ ≈ 0.0086 eV (second-generation neutrino mass) — cross-check, branch-contingent.** On the same flux line as the Majorana scale, the Dirac coupling Y_ν₂ = (8π)^(−1/3) feeds the seesaw and delivers a solar-sector mass within 0.7% of the observed oscillation scale (0.993×), with no observational input in the chain. The full caveat travels with the number: the chain contains an open normalisation fork — 8π or 4π — and the match lands on one branch; the other branch fits the ratio between the two oscillation scales instead, and no single choice currently fits both legs. The atmospheric leg meanwhile overshoots by ~1.5× — a live tension carried in Appendix C. Striking, honest, and graded as a cross-check.

**The 2π² volume audit — internal consistency, passed.** One geometric number — the volume of the unit three-sphere — recurs at three separate stages of the computation, doing three different jobs. No telescope measures it; its role is the instrument confirming it is one object. Listed for completeness, weighted accordingly.

**Λ — structural quietness; numerical value open.** Chapter 13: quantum field theory overshoots the vacuum energy by ~10¹²⁰; the framework's boundary-only ontology plus the trace-free treatment of gravity removes the catastrophic term rather than cancelling it. The mechanism is the claim; the number is not — a previously circulated value was withdrawn when its route was found to be closed, and the numerical Λ sits in the open-work ledger. Nor is the mechanism NTE's alone: boundary-only ontologies and trace-free treatments of gravity remove the catastrophic term in other hands too. What the framework claims here is the combination and the company it keeps, not the discovery. Success in deriving it would promote this note to the chord's numerical surface; nothing about the structural mechanism waits on that.

Notes from one calculation, none tuned to fit the sky — and no fixed tally: the count reflects where the mathematics has reached, and the accounting has moved notes in both directions as the work matured. It withdrew the native spectral index and tensor ratio as framework predictions (§3). It promoted another note (T_GB) and then demoted it again, when the route to the saddle that carried it turned out to be conditional on the fibre-modulus grounding. A tally that only ever grows is a sales figure; this one is allowed to shrink. What cannot change without collective failure is the joint origin of what remains.

## §3 The Open Early Universe — Option D

The early-universe sector is open. That is the deposit stance, and this section states it without selling a finished inflationary story.

**Door B first.** Φ_NTE is the expansion-history field — the geometric quantity in H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² — not an inflaton. General relativity is restored at all epochs; the old non-minimal F(Φ)R reading that once wired native (n_s, r) numbers to Φ_NTE is gone. So the numbers that used to sit here as a "conditional pair" — n_s = 0.9692, r = 0.0053, tied to a closed K(Φ) at ξ = 1/6 — are **withdrawn as framework predictions**. They are audit-trail residues of a route the corpus no longer takes. They are not fuses waiting to become zero-parameter tests. CMB-S4 and LiteBIRD will still measure n_s and r; those measurements will discriminate among whatever open-sector pathway is eventually selected. They do not score a native NTE hilltop that no longer exists.

**Option D.** Without further observational evidence pointing clearly at one mechanism, the framework leaves the cosmological-origin sector open. Four pathways remain live for the reader; none is narrated as settled NTE physics:

- **A — Narrow §8.1.** A Higgs field may pre-exist; locking and mass are restaged at gauge birth. This is a sequencing and scope option, not a claim that SM Higgs inflation is NTE's chosen exit.
- **B — Move the Hartle–Hawking handover.** Place the Euclidean-to-Lorentzian handover after gauge birth, re-sequencing the early dynamics relative to the birth event.
- **C — Credited Starobinsky R².** Adopt R² inflation as external, credited content *if* selected — clears one existence-gate reading, carries its own observational and route costs, and is not sold here as deposited NTE.
- **D — Leave open.** The current deposit stance: no early-universe exit selected; prefer an honest open register over choosing an inflaton or reheating channel before the sky forces the choice.

Do not read A or C as the framework having adopted Bezrukov–Shaposhnikov Higgs inflation or Starobinsky as its own. They are live options on an open ledger. D is what the deposit currently holds.

What still belongs in the conditional tier of *this* chapter is narrower: **T_GB**, pending the Companion-12 fibre-modulus grounding (§2). Everything else in the early sector waits on pathway choice, not on lighting a fuse under withdrawn numbers.

## §4 Cosmology Beyond the Chord

Further cosmological claims follow from the framework's broader structural commitments — the closed three-sphere and the relation H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² — with their grades stated.

**The Hubble parameter H₀ = 67.6 km/s/Mpc — by construction, not a test.** The framework's cosmic-mean expansion rate follows once β_NTE is fixed from Planck-era inputs; the sub-percent closeness to the CMB-derived value (Planck, ACT, SPT: ≈ 67–68) is structural bookkeeping, and the scorecard declines to count it as a passed prediction. On the Hubble *tension*, the framework does not own a distinctive void mechanism. Directional scatter of local H₀ with line-of-sight density is the standard language of inhomogeneous cosmology — underdense regions expand slightly faster; low-redshift distance indicators preferentially sample them — and NTE sits inside that reading rather than inventing a private anatomy. Supernova catalogues and Rubin's void mapping can still test whether local scatter correlates with density as that shared picture expects; the framework does not claim the correlation as an NTE-only signature.

**The dark-energy equation of state w = −1, exactly — naked prediction, under real but carefully stated pressure.** A clean cosmological constant: structural quietness, not a dynamical field, and no dial anywhere in the framework to turn. What DESI actually says needs stating without theatre. A frequentist DESI+CMB model comparison often quotes a ~3.1σ preference for an evolving w₀wₐ dark-energy sector over ΛCDM; that is a preference between models, not a direct exclusion of w = −1. Bayesian evidence on the same combinations modestly favours ΛCDM. So w = −1 is **not withdrawn**. The tension is live and uncomfortable; it is not "naked at 3.1σ" in the exclusion sense, and "supernova-independent" must not be glued to that 3.1σ figure as though it were. Standard void/inhomogeneity bias remains signed the wrong way for DESI: it cannot rescue the preference for evolving dark energy, and the framework says so rather than borrowing an escape it does not have. Test: DESI's full survey and what follows it. If evolving dark energy firms up after the statistics are read honestly, w = −1 fails in the open, and the reading of Λ as structural quietness rather than dynamics fails with it.

**The dark-matter variance scaling σ(M) ∝ M^(−1/2).** Above the coherence mass, system-to-system scatter in the dark-to-ordinary ratio must fall off as the square root of mass — a central-limit consequence whose NTE-specific content is its combination with κ = 5.50 and the coherence scale itself. Chapter 10's companion claim: the transition to tight convergence above the neutrino free-streaming coherence mass, which is cluster-scale — of order 10¹⁴ solar masses — and which the framework adopts rather than derives. It is also not a fixed threshold: free-streaming scales inversely with the neutrino mass, so the transition moves with a quantity the framework has not pinned. What the framework claims is the combination of that scale with the scaling law and the mean, not the scale itself. Rubin's Legacy Survey, Euclid, and the spectroscopic surveys map dark-matter fractions across hundreds of millions of systems; absence of the scaling, or a transition at a substantially different scale, breaks the bilateral-coupling mechanism's empirical signature.

## §5 Particle Physics

The particle-physics lines carry two of the framework's three zero-parameter predictions, its hardest near-term falsifiers, and its bets on absence.

**The CP phase |δ_CP| = π/2 — zero-parameter, under active test.** The framework's Janus geometry forces maximal CP violation, and the μ–τ reflection symmetry of the neutrino sector carries the same value into the mixing matrix. This is the second of the three genuine zero-parameter predictions, and it is being measured right now: DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande are built to pin the leptonic CP phase within the decade. A measured phase well away from maximal breaks it — no adjustment available. What the geometry fixes is the magnitude, and only the magnitude: |δ_J| = π/2. The sign is free — nothing in the Janus construction selects it. So the framework underwrites maximal CP violation, and with it the *existence* of a baryon asymmetry; it does not underwrite the direction. That matter won rather than antimatter is a fact the framework accommodates, not one it predicts, and the distinction is worth keeping because the opposite sign would have been just as comfortable. The asymmetry's magnitude it carries only at order-one consistency (Appendix C).

**The atmospheric mixing angle θ₂₃ = π/4 — zero-parameter, inherited and credited.** Maximal atmospheric mixing follows from the μ–τ reflection symmetry — a result the framework *inherits from the established μ–τ symmetry literature and credits accordingly*, its own contribution being the geometric seat that symmetry occupies (the residual twin-degeneracy of the eigenmodes transverse to the Higgs direction, Chapter 8). Global fits: 45.0° ± 1.0° — consistent. The third of the three. What the reflection does *not* fix belongs in the same breath, because it is the reason the framework uses reflection rather than the simpler μ–τ permutation: reflection leaves θ₁₃ free, and θ₁₃ is measured nonzero, near 8.6°. A permutation symmetry would have forced θ₁₃ = 0 and been dead before it started. A symmetry that declines to fix an angle is doing work too. Tightening octant data will eventually settle whether exact maximality holds.

**Normal ordering — required, verdict imminent.** The eigenmode geometry of Chapter 8 forces one heavier neutrino above a near-degenerate light pair. Inverted ordering has no home in the framework — not a stretched fit, no fit. JUNO, and DUNE after it, settle this within the decade; present data lean normal at modest significance. This is the framework's sharpest near-term falsifier.

**No fourth light neutrino.** Exactly three su(2) directions, exactly three eigenmodes; there is no fourth slot. A confirmed fourth light mass state falsifies the framework outright.

**Neutrinoless double beta decay — expected, in the few-meV window.** The unresolved conjugation axis permits the process; a genuinely Dirac neutrino forbids it. Because the framework requires normal ordering, its prediction lives where normal ordering puts it: an effective Majorana mass in the narrow few-meV window, at next-generation reach. KamLAND-Zen, LEGEND, and successors are pushing into exactly that range. Observation there confirms the ontology's central claim; a firm null across the motivated window rules it out. (Earlier presentations placed this test in the inverted-hierarchy band; that band is where the framework's own required ordering says the signal is *not*, and this edition corrects the framing.)

**Exactly three generations of fermions — imported, credited; the absence bet stands.** The three-generation count and the Standard Model gauge group come to the framework from the established octonionic and exceptional-Jordan-algebra programme — Günaydin–Gürsey, Furey, Todorov–Dubois-Violette — and the framework claims them as *adopted structure, credited*, not as NTE derivations; NTE's own contribution is the cross-consistency of its 90-species Majorana accounting with that structure. The commitment, however, is fully owned: the algebra permits three generations and forbids a fourth. High-luminosity LHC continues to push fourth-generation bounds higher; an unambiguous fourth-generation fermion breaks the algebraic reading. As of 2026, none has been seen.

**No right-handed W boson (W_R).** The framework's reading places SU(2)_R in the mirror sector — the other orientation of the bilateral manifold. At gauge birth, the electroweak vacuum selects a direction in the imaginary octonions, and CPN combined with parity assigns SU(2)_R to the mirror sector; the observable gauge group in our orientation is SU(3)_C × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y, and parity violation is built into the boundary. SU(2)_R is not a gauge symmetry of our observable sector; therefore W_R is not an observable particle in our orientation. Test: LHC searches for W_R signatures continue to push the lower mass bound higher. If a W_R is unambiguously detected at any mass, the framework's assignment of SU(2)_R to the mirror orientation is wrong. As of 2026, no W_R has been seen.

**No WIMP direct detection.** The framework's dark matter is the gravitational register of the mirror sector's matter through the bilateral coupling — no particle in our orientation to find. XENON, LUX-ZEPLIN, PandaX and successors keep finding nothing, with the standard WIMP parameter space now definitively ruled out and the remaining theory space progressively less natural. Each null tightens the framework's bet; one unambiguous detection breaks it.

## §6 Reckoning

The catalogue pulls together, with the grades kept visible.

**Passed, at the strictest tier:** κ within 0.83σ of Planck, zero parameters, algebraically demonstrated, with support now carried by the open bilateral action. **Consistent, at the strictest tier:** θ₂₃ maximal within current precision (inherited, credited); |δ_J| = π/2, with the sign free and the asymmetry's direction therefore not claimed. **Cross-checks passed:** M₁ within a factor 1.1 of the leptogenesis requirement; m_ν₂ within 0.7% of the solar scale on one branch of an open fork — with the branch and the atmospheric overshoot stated in the same breath. **Conditional:** T_GB, pending the fibre-modulus grounding. **Open sector (Option D):** native (n_s, r) withdrawn under Door B; pathways A–D live; D the deposit stance — no fuse under 0.9692 / 0.0053. **By construction, not counted:** H₀ at the cosmic mean; standard BBN recovered (N_eff = 3.044, Y_p = 0.247). **Absences holding:** no WIMP, no fourth generation, no W_R, decade after decade. **Currently under pressure:** w = −1 against a frequentist DESI+CMB preference for evolving w₀wₐ often quoted near 3.1σ — model comparison, not a direct exclusion — while Bayesian evidence modestly favours ΛCDM; w = −1 not withdrawn. Also live: the atmospheric mass ratio; lithium-7; the asymmetry's magnitude. **Withdrawn — gaps, not defeats:** the leading-order solar angle, and the native inflationary pair. The framework once offered sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3 and no longer does; the algebra does not choose between the candidate values, and on the present reading it does not determine the solar angle at all. That is a hole in the derivation, not a failed test, and a ledger that blurs the two is no use to anyone. All of it sits in Appendix C, at full volume, alongside the open gaps and the pathways under investigation.

Pending tests cluster in the next ten years, and they are not gentle. JUNO rules on the mass ordering the framework requires. DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande measure the CP phase the framework fixes at maximal. CMB-S4 tightens κ by a factor of two and will bound whatever early-universe pathway the open sector eventually selects — not a withdrawn native pair. DESI's full survey rules on w = −1 and the variance scaling. Rubin and Euclid map the coherence transition and test whether local H₀ scatter tracks density in the ordinary inhomogeneous sense. KamLAND-Zen and LEGEND push into the few-meV double-beta window. The high-luminosity LHC keeps pressing the absences.

What would break NTE, specifically: a κ measurement well away from 5.50; confirmed inverted mass ordering; a confirmed fourth light neutrino or fourth generation; a W_R at any mass; an unambiguous WIMP; genuinely time-varying dark energy that survives an honest reading of the DESI statistics; a 0νββ null across the few-meV window; the variance scaling absent. Early-universe r and n_s will break *pathways*, not a native NTE hilltop the corpus no longer claims. Local H₀ scatter uncorrelated with density would stress the shared inhomogeneous reading, not a framework-private void anatomy. Each break is a specific structural failure, and the commitments are tied tightly enough that some failures propagate: a κ break compromises the bilateral picture; the bilateral picture supports the closed manifold; the closed manifold supports the spectral triple. The catalogue's claims are not independent test cases. They are pressure points on the same architecture.

If the passed lines survive tighter measurement and the pending tests land where NTE places them, the recurring Hopf fibration, the three of su(2), the closed S³ cease to look like coincidences picked up in separate chapters. Observation is the only court. The next decade is when the case gets heard.
