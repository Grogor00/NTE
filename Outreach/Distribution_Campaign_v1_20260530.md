## 1. Campaign rules

1. **One paper per email.** Never bundle four standalones in one message.
2. **One concrete question** tied to the recipient’s published work — not a generic “please read my paper.”
3. **Under 180 words** in the body; link once at the end.
4. **Honest prior art** — especially F1 (Chamseddine–Connes 2008; Mistry–Pinzul–Rachwał 2020). Ask for pointers, not novelty claims.
5. **No paywall language** — corpus is for humanity; ask for **technical criticism**, not endorsement.
6. **Track everything** in §8 before the master-corpus wave.
7. **One file link only.** Send the **direct PDF URL** for that paper (§2). Do **not** link the [Zenodo record landing page](https://zenodo.org/records/19440068) unless the email is explicitly about the full deposit index.

**Tone:** Colleague-to-colleague. “Would you point out where this breaks?” beats “Revolutionary new theory.”
