## §1 What This Chapter Does

This is the line in the book where the framework puts its money down.

The previous chapters built one picture: closed boundary, two scalars, Hopf geometry, bilateral thickness, the unresolved neutrino, the instrument-and-chord arc in Chapters 11–13. The technical papers organise that picture at the gauge-birth boundary; here it's enough to say the pieces lock together — pull on one thread and the others move.

This chapter lists the framework's claims and what each one would cost NTE if it failed. Some already match observation within the quoted errors. Others wait on measurements due in the next decade. A few are bets on *absence*: no fourth generation, no W_R — and so far the experiments keep finding nothing. One former bet on absence, the WIMP, is withdrawn in this edition, and §5 explains why a framework can lose an absence without the experiments changing their answer.

The list is not a menu of independent guesses, and it is not a list of equals. The framework grades its own claims, and the scorecard keeps the grades visible. Two claims sit in the strictest tier — *genuine zero-parameter predictions*, numbers or structures forced by the geometry with no observational input anywhere in their chains, made about observables that were never used to build the framework: the maximal CP phase and the maximal atmospheric mixing angle. Until this edition there were three. The dark-matter ratio κ was the first of them, and it has left the tier twice over: first because the framework can no longer say what the number is a ratio *of*, and then because the amplifier that produced 5.50 is itself withdrawn — **5.50 is not a framework quantity**. What remains deposited is the bare coupling κ₀. §2 tells that story at length, because it is the most instructive entry in the chapter. Below them sit the chord's structural results at their own registers; an open early-universe sector where native (n_s, r) once sat; consequences that follow by construction and are honestly not tests at all; the claims the framework has withdrawn; and the open tensions — the places where it is currently *losing* — which have their own catalogue in Appendix C, because a scorecard that lists only wins is advertising, not accounting.

On scope, one correction this edition makes explicit: the chord of Chapter 12 does not include the CMB spectral index, and native (n_s, r) are **withdrawn** as predictions — |Φ_NTE| is not an inflaton (Door B), and the cosmological origin sector is open (Option D). Earlier presentations seated the inflationary tilt among the chord's notes and then in a conditional fuse; both filings overclaim what the deposit holds. Λ likewise stands as structural quietness — the framework's account of why empty space is not catastrophically loud — without a numerical value at the framework's current state; Chapter 13 develops what the structural account claims and does not claim.

The rest of the chapter is the scorecard.

## §2 The Chord — Notes From One Source

These lines are the chord from Chapter 12 — one heat-kernel reading on the spectral triple, each note at its honest register. What each line claims, and where observation stands:

**κ₀ = 3/√(4π) ≈ 0.846 — zero-parameter, derived. The shape of this loss is unlike anything else on the scorecard, and a reader who follows only one withdrawal should follow this one. **κ₀** is still forced, still zero-parameter, still what the Hopf geometry produces — and it still forces Majorana through κ₀ < 1. The identification failure is structural rather than repairable: the bilateral symmetry that once seemed to give an amplified κ its footing — equal masses on the two sides — is the same symmetry that forces the equal temperatures that make the other sector un-cold. So from this edition forward the deposit carries **κ₀ alone**. No amplified ratio, no Ω_DM/Ω_b identification, and no CMB-S4 test aimed at 5.50. The framework's account of the cold dark matter inferred from the microwave background is **open**; Appendix C carries it as such, with the three findings that closed the identification. **T_GB = M_Pl/√(2π) (gauge-birth temperature) — conditional.** When gauge symmetry and clocks switched on, the boundary field locked and post-temporal dynamics began. **M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV (heavy seesaw scale) — pathway identified, not derived.** Type-I seesaw models need a heavy partner so light neutrino masses go like v_EW²/M_R; standard treatments fit M_R by hand. The framework has identified a structural pathway — three powers of the Planck mass against one of the Higgs scale, M_R = M_Pl^(3/4) v_EW^(1/4) ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV, the canonical seesaw size for sub-eV neutrinos — but the derivation is not closed, and the scorecard carries it at exactly that register: pathway, not theorem. No collider will reach M_R directly; the downstream checks run through the visible masses and the double-beta-decay window (§5).

**m_ν₂ ≈ 0.0086 eV (second-generation neutrino mass) — cross-check, branch-contingent.** On the same flux line as the Majorana scale, the Dirac coupling Y_ν₂ = (8π)^(−1/3) feeds the seesaw and delivers a solar-sector mass within 0.7% of the observed oscillation scale (0.993×), with no observational input in the chain. **Measurement band (12(b)):** NuFIT 6.0 gives Δm²₂₁ = (7.49 ± 0.19) × 10⁻⁵ eV² (1σ), so √(Δm²₂₁) ≈ 0.00866 ± 0.00011 eV (~1.3% relative); the 0.7% residual is inside that band. **The 2π² volume audit — internal consistency, passed.** One geometric number — the volume of the unit three-sphere — recurs at three separate stages of the computation, doing three different jobs. No telescope measures it; its role is the instrument confirming it is one object. Listed for completeness, weighted accordingly.

**Λ — structural quietness; numerical value open.** Chapter 13: quantum field theory overshoots the vacuum energy by ~10¹²⁰; the framework's boundary-only ontology plus the trace-free treatment of gravity removes the catastrophic term rather than cancelling it. The mechanism is the claim; the number is not — a previously circulated value was withdrawn when its route was found to be closed, and the numerical Λ sits in the open-work ledger. Nor is the mechanism NTE's alone: boundary-only ontologies and trace-free treatments of gravity remove the catastrophic term in other hands too. What the framework claims here is the combination and the company it keeps, not the discovery. Success in deriving it would promote this note to the chord's numerical surface; nothing about the structural mechanism waits on that.

Notes from one calculation, none tuned to fit the sky — and no fixed tally: the count reflects where the mathematics has reached, and the accounting has moved notes in both directions as the work matured. A tally that only ever grows is a sales figure; this one is allowed to shrink. What cannot change without collective failure is the joint origin.

## §4 Cosmology Beyond the Chord

Further cosmological claims follow from the framework's broader structural commitments — the closed three-sphere and the relation H = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² — with their grades stated.

**The Hubble parameter H₀ = 67.6 km/s/Mpc — by construction, not a test.** The framework's cosmic-mean expansion rate follows once β_NTE is fixed from Planck-era inputs; the sub-percent closeness to the CMB-derived value (Planck, ACT, SPT: ≈ 67–68) is structural bookkeeping, and the scorecard declines to count it as a passed prediction. The *genuine* prediction in this sector is the tension's anatomy: late-universe direct measurements (SH0ES: ≈ 73) should show a **directional scatter of σ(H₀) ~ 2–4 km/s/Mpc correlated with line-of-sight void fraction** — void enhancement, not new physics. Supernova catalogues and the Rubin Observatory's void mapping test this directly: scatter that correlates with density as predicted supports the reading; local values that fail to converge toward the cosmic mean as systematics are controlled, or scatter with no density correlation, break it.

**The dark-energy equation of state w = −1, exactly — naked, and under pressure.** A clean cosmological constant: structural quietness, not a dynamical field, and no dial anywhere in the framework to turn. The pressure is real, and it is worth stating at its actual strength, because earlier editions of this scorecard overstated it against the framework's own interest. DESI's second data release taken on its own is well described by a flat universe with a constant equation of state, and does not exclude w = −1. The 3.1σ figure that circulates is narrower than it sounds: it is a preference for a two-parameter evolving equation of state over the constant one, when DESI's baryon acoustic oscillations are combined with the microwave background — a comparison between models, not a measurement of w. On that same combination the Bayesian evidence modestly favours the constant. And the supernova-driven part of the case softened in 2026, when a recalibration of the DES sample brought it from about 4.2σ to 3.2σ. **What is left is a real and unresolved tension rather than a refutation**: the data prefer a shape the framework cannot produce, and the framework has no internal adjustment available. The void mechanism that carries the Hubble tension is signed the wrong way here: it works *against* the DESI preference rather than explaining it, so it cannot be borrowed to soften this. w = −1 is exposed exactly as a prediction should be. Test: DESI's full survey and what follows it. If the preference firms up, w = −1 fails in the open, and the reading of Λ as structural quietness rather than dynamics fails with it.

**The dark-matter variance scaling σ(M) ∝ M^(−1/2) — bare CLT only.** Above the coherence mass, scatter should fall off as the square root of mass — a generic central-limit consequence. **Its former combination with a mean of 5.50 is withdrawn with the amplifier**, so this is not a prediction about the measured dark-to-ordinary ratio; what survives is the narrower statement that a conjugate-sector contribution should average down as M^(−1/2) above the coherence scale. Chapter 10's companion claim: the transition to tight convergence above the neutrino free-streaming coherence mass, which is cluster-scale — of order 10¹⁴ solar masses — and which the framework adopts rather than derives. It is also not a fixed threshold: free-streaming scales inversely with the neutrino mass, so the transition moves with a quantity the framework has not pinned. The framework no longer claims a three-legged test of scale + scaling + mean; the mean is gone twice over. Rubin's Legacy Survey, Euclid, and the spectroscopic surveys map dark-matter fractions across hundreds of millions of systems; the surveys will map that scatter either way, and the framework states plainly that what a result there would test is held open until the footprint's referent is established.

## §5 Particle Physics

The particle-physics lines now carry both of the framework's zero-parameter predictions, its hardest near-term falsifiers, and its bets on absence.

**The CP phase |δ_CP| = π/2 — zero-parameter, under active test.** The framework's Janus geometry forces maximal CP violation, and the μ–τ reflection symmetry of the neutrino sector carries the same value into the mixing matrix. This is one of the framework's two genuine zero-parameter predictions, and it is being measured right now: DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande are built to pin the leptonic CP phase within the decade. A measured phase well away from maximal breaks it — no adjustment available. What the geometry fixes is the magnitude, and only the magnitude: |δ_J| = π/2. The sign is free — nothing in the Janus construction selects it. So the framework underwrites maximal CP violation, and with it the *existence* of a baryon asymmetry; it does not underwrite the direction. That matter won rather than antimatter is a fact the framework accommodates, not one it predicts, and the distinction is worth keeping because the opposite sign would have been just as comfortable. The asymmetry's magnitude it carries only at order-one consistency (Appendix C).

**The atmospheric mixing angle θ₂₃ = π/4 — zero-parameter, inherited and credited.** Maximal atmospheric mixing follows from the μ–τ reflection symmetry — a result the framework *inherits from the established μ–τ symmetry literature and credits accordingly*, its own contribution being the geometric seat that symmetry occupies (the residual twin-degeneracy of the eigenmodes transverse to the Higgs direction, Chapter 8). Global fits: 45.0° ± 1.0° — consistent. The other of the two. What the reflection does *not* fix belongs in the same breath, because it is the reason the framework uses reflection rather than the simpler μ–τ permutation: reflection leaves θ₁₃ free, and θ₁₃ is measured nonzero, near 8.6°. A permutation symmetry would have forced θ₁₃ = 0 and been dead before it started. A symmetry that declines to fix an angle is doing work too. Tightening octant data will eventually settle whether exact maximality holds.

**Normal ordering — required, verdict imminent.** The eigenmode geometry of Chapter 8 forces one heavier neutrino above a near-degenerate light pair. Inverted ordering has no home in the framework — not a stretched fit, no fit. JUNO, and DUNE after it, settle this within the decade; present data lean normal at modest significance. This is the framework's sharpest near-term falsifier.

**No fourth light neutrino.** Exactly three su(2) directions, exactly three eigenmodes; there is no fourth slot. A confirmed fourth light mass state falsifies the framework outright.

**Neutrinoless double beta decay — expected, in the few-meV window.** The unresolved conjugation axis permits the process; a genuinely Dirac neutrino forbids it. Because the framework requires normal ordering, its prediction lives where normal ordering puts it: an effective Majorana mass in the narrow few-meV window, at next-generation reach. KamLAND-Zen, LEGEND, and successors are pushing into exactly that range. Observation there confirms the ontology's central claim; a firm null across the motivated window rules it out. (Earlier presentations placed this test in the inverted-hierarchy band; that band is where the framework's own required ordering says the signal is *not*, and this edition corrects the framing.)

**Exactly three generations of fermions — imported, credited; the absence bet stands.** The three-generation count and the Standard Model gauge group come to the framework from the established octonionic and exceptional-Jordan-algebra programme — Günaydin–Gürsey, Furey, Todorov–Dubois-Violette — and the framework claims them as *adopted structure, credited*, not as NTE derivations; NTE's own contribution is the cross-consistency of its 90-species Majorana accounting with that structure. The commitment, however, is fully owned: the algebra permits three generations and forbids a fourth. High-luminosity LHC continues to push fourth-generation bounds higher; an unambiguous fourth-generation fermion breaks the algebraic reading. As of 2026, none has been seen.

**No right-handed W boson (W_R).** The framework's reading places SU(2)_R in the mirror sector — the other orientation of the bilateral manifold. At gauge birth, the electroweak vacuum selects a direction in the imaginary octonions, and CPN combined with parity assigns SU(2)_R to the mirror sector; the observable gauge group in our orientation is SU(3)_C × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y, and parity violation is built into the boundary. SU(2)_R is not a gauge symmetry of our observable sector; therefore W_R is not an observable particle in our orientation. Test: LHC searches for W_R signatures continue to push the lower mass bound higher. If a W_R is unambiguously detected at any mass, the framework's assignment of SU(2)_R to the mirror orientation is wrong. As of 2026, no W_R has been seen.

**No WIMP direct detection — withdrawn as a framework bet.** Earlier editions listed this among the absences, and the reasoning was clean: if the framework's dark matter is the mirror sector's matter registering gravitationally, there is no particle in our orientation for a detector to find, and every null result is a correct result. That reasoning rested entirely on the identification §2 has withdrawn. With the framework's account of the observed dark matter open, it has no licence to say that dark matter is not a particle — it has no account either way, and an absence asserted from a withdrawn premise is not an absence the framework has earned.

The experimental situation has not changed and deserves stating on its own terms: XENON, LUX-ZEPLIN, PandaX and successors keep finding nothing, the original WIMP parameter space is ruled out, and the surviving theory space is progressively less natural. That is a fact about the search rather than a score for this framework. A WIMP detection would no longer break NTE; it would fill a gap the framework has left open, and the framework would have to say what relation, if any, such a particle bears to the bilateral geometry. Giving up a bet one was winning is an uncomfortable thing to write down, which is the reason to write it down.

## §6 Reckoning

The catalogue pulls together, with the grades kept visible.

**Passed, at the strictest tier:** nothing, this edition. The entry that stood here — κ within 0.83σ of Planck — is withdrawn as a current claim, and the tier has no passed line until one of the two remaining predictions is measured. **Consistent, at the strictest tier:** θ₂₃ maximal within current precision (inherited, credited); |δ_J| = π/2, with the sign free and the asymmetry's direction therefore not claimed. **Cross-checks passed:** M₁ within a factor 1.1 of the leptogenesis requirement; m_ν₂ within 0.7% of the solar scale on one branch of an open fork — with the branch and the atmospheric overshoot stated in the same breath. **Conditional, not a fuse for n_s/r:** T_GB, pending the fibre-modulus grounding. Native (n_s, r) are withdrawn (§3). **By construction, not counted:** H₀ at the cosmic mean. The line that sat beside it — standard big-bang nucleosynthesis recovered, with N_eff = 3.044 and Y_p = 0.247 — is withdrawn: the framework's bilateral structure does not deliver the standard radiation budget for free, and claiming recovery by construction assumed exactly the thing §2's withdrawal unsettled. **Absences holding:** no fourth generation, no W_R, decade after decade — two, where this edition's predecessor counted three. **Currently losing:** w = −1 against the DESI-plus-microwave-background combination, which prefers an evolving equation of state over a constant one, with nothing internal to absorb it — §4 states the figure at its proper strength; the atmospheric mass ratio; lithium-7; the asymmetry's magnitude. **Withdrawn, in several shapes:** the amplifier κ ≈ 5.50 as a framework quantity; its identification with the measured dark-matter ratio; native (n_s, r); and the leading-order solar angle. They fail differently, and a ledger that blurs them is no use to anyone. κ₀ survives as deposited Hopf structure; 5.50 does not. The solar angle is the reverse pattern: the observable is in no doubt, and the derivation is what the framework no longer has. The framework once offered sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3 and no longer does; the algebra does not choose between the candidate values, and on the present reading it does not determine the solar angle at all. All of it sits in Appendix C, at full volume, alongside the open gaps and the pathways under investigation.

Pending tests cluster in the next ten years, and they are not gentle. JUNO rules on the mass ordering the framework requires. DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande measure the CP phase the framework fixes at maximal. DESI's full survey rules on w = −1. CMB-S4 / LiteBIRD bound the early-universe sector for whatever pathway Option D eventually selects — but no longer test κ, and no longer test a native Φ_NTE (n_s, r) pair. Rubin and Euclid map the coherence transition and the void anatomy of the Hubble tension. KamLAND-Zen and LEGEND push into the few-meV double-beta window. The high-luminosity LHC keeps pressing the absences.

What would break NTE, specifically: confirmed inverted mass ordering; a confirmed fourth light neutrino or fourth generation; a W_R at any mass; genuinely time-varying dark energy after void corrections; a 0νββ null across the few-meV window; H₀ scatter uncorrelated with density. Two items left this list in this edition and their departure is a loss, not a relief. A dark-matter-ratio measurement away from 5.50 no longer breaks anything, because 5.50 is not a framework quantity; and an unambiguous WIMP no longer breaks anything, because the framework no longer says there is nothing to find. A falsifier that cannot fire is worse than no falsifier, and the scorecard says so rather than quietly keeping the line. Each break is a specific structural failure, and the commitments are tied tightly enough that some failures propagate: the Majorana condition carries the convergence requirement κ₀ < 1 and so the three-eigenmode count; the bilateral picture supports the closed manifold; the closed manifold supports the spectral triple. The catalogue's claims are not independent test cases. They are pressure points on the same architecture.

If the passed lines survive tighter measurement and the pending tests land where NTE places them, the recurring Hopf fibration, the three of su(2), the closed S³ cease to look like coincidences picked up in separate chapters. Observation is the only court. The next decade is when the case gets heard.
