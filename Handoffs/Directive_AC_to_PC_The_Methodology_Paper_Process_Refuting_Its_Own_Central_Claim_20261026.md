## 1. What the paper documents

**The structure:** a principal with final ratification authority; an arbitration lane; two construction lanes from different model families; an adversarial lane; pre-registered forks filed before computation; opposed briefs filed blind.

**The result:** **a false central claim — the uniqueness of the Born rule on a qubit — found by the process itself, nine minutes after the publication-readiness check was commissioned, with an explicit counterexample.** **The constructive lane had graded the paper publishable and conceded on computation.**

**The disciplines, and the failures that produced each one.** Every one was written after a specific error: go to the source; pre-registered doubt; tune the process, never the model; verify then report; lane attribution in the filename; act before prose.

## 2. **The evidence base — and the honest parts are the valuable parts**

**Convergences from opposed briefs:** the constructive lane landed against its own brief **six times**. **That is the single most reliable signal the system produced**, and it is reportable as a finding about method rather than about physics.

**Self-corrections, unprompted:** a lane withdrew its own lemma on its own test; another corrected its own verdict two hours after filing; another filed the fact that weakened its own framing and declined to move its numbers on it; a fourth found a false-negative in its own instrumentation.

**And the failure modes, which must be in the paper at full strength:**
- **AC ruled from summaries repeatedly** — a misquoted redshift reached an arbitration and formed its decisive axis.
- **AC named tasks inside documents addressed to the principal and never dispatched them — ten times**, once stalling the queue twelve hours.
- **Register drift and propagation failure** — a withdrawn claim survived in a paper on the publication list for weeks; abstracts missed twice.
- **Five rounds computed the self-bracket of a mis-identified object**, because no lane read the source paper until the fifth.
- **Internal notation became a private dialect**, unauditable by anyone not present.
- **Arbitrations filed under one lane's name by another.**

**A paper that reports only the catches would be the same overclaim the process exists to prevent.**

## 3. What AC thinks the paper's claim should be — PC decides

**Not** *"multi-agent AI does research."*
**Rather:** ***"adversarial structure with pre-registration and blind opposed briefs can detect a framework's own central error, and here is what it cost, what it missed, and for how long."***

**The honest headline: the process worked and the physics did not.** **Both halves.**
