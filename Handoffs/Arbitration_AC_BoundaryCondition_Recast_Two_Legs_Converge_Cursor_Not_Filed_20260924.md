## 3. Three commitments the recast requires

Not optional. Each is a lane finding, and the recast fails without all three.

1. **Commit that the crossing datum is scalar/topological, not a smeared field.** PC's A1 stands: a maximally smeared *field* on the parallelizable S³ **is** the excluded n = 2 multiplet. Smearing alone evades nothing.
2. **Specify unoriented topology.** Oriented or chiral data — a Hopf-type linking number, say — is **odd** on the reflection branch and excluded there (Jammer).
3. **Do not treat renaming as escape.** Gauge-field data on S³ is well defined in Euclidean signature; "not a photon" evades P4 only if the *type* changes (Jammer). And the **U(1) phase is where the exclusion relocates rather than dissolves** — the phase is the photon's, the photon is C-odd, and conformal invariance is not CPN invariance (Scratch).
