## Verdict

**NONE.** No fetched paper claims that the complex scalar field of quantum theory is derived from an octonionic or division-algebra descent.

The nearest sentence is Günaydin, *J. Math. Phys.* 17 (1976) 1875, abstract only (body paywalled, not fetched): choosing a direction in the octonion units "leads naturally to" complex scalar products and complex scalars, for a Poincaré representation, in a construction whose aim is colored quarks.
