# Name tasks by content, never by label

## The rule

**Never use an internal register label as a task name, a directive heading, or a filename.**

Labels like **R3, D2, S1, M1, Gate 0, W-2, option D** mean different things in different contexts. **The heading wins over the body**: a lane reading a directive titled "R3" will resolve that token its own way, even when the loci are listed correctly underneath.

**Name the task by what it is**: `neutrino-regrounding`, `secondary-constraint-bracket`, `kappa-as-geometric-identity`.

## Why

**"R3" meant two things.** In one context the unresolved-neutrino re-grounding; in another, option D / Door B. A directive used the label in the heading and listed the correct loci in the body. **The lane flagged the ambiguity honestly, resolved it the other way, and produced a report of the wrong sector. One work cycle lost.**

**The lane did nothing wrong.** It said so explicitly: *"the register label does not appear as a string in the quoted tips."* **The defect was the naming.**

## Procedure

1. **Filenames and headings carry content words only.** Labels may appear in the body, defined at first use.
2. **Define any label the first time it appears in a document.** Do not assume a reader shares the context in which it was coined.
3. **If a label must appear in a title, pair it with its content**: `Stage1p1_NEUTRINO_REGROUNDING`, not `Stage1p1_R3`.

## The wider failure this belongs to — private dialect

Sustained multi-agent work generates its own notation, and it compounds fast: `B1–B9`, `OF-BAR-MSS`, `P4`, `F1–F16`, `G2`, `soft-P1`, `K0′`, `D2′`.

**Artefacts written in that dialect become unauditable by anyone who was not present — including the principal, and including later sessions of the same agent.** The register discipline exists so claims can be checked; a document needing a glossary defeats it.

**Standing requirement: every synthesis carries one plain-language line a stranger could check.** One sentence, no internal labels, stating what was found.

## Corollary — attribution belongs in the filename

A listing shows only the filename. **Output from a clone, a secondary lane or a different model family is named for its author** — `Arbitration_CursorAC_…`, not `Arbitration_AC_…`. **An unqualified name is a claim of authorship**, and a ruling must be traceable to the party answerable for it.
