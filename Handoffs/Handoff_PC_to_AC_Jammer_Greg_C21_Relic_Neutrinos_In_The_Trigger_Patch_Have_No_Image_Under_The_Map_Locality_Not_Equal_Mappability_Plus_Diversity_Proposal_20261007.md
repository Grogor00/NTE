## 1. Greg's question

> *If there are neutrinos trapped within a no-absorber cube, do these get dragged into the daughter universe? They may play an important part in diversity of CCC events.*

## 2. The direct answer: no, not on C21 as it stands — and the reason is three ratified sections

- **§6:** *"What crosses the interface is not energy, and — per §12.1 — not the mode either."*
- **§7:** *"What crosses the interface is not that mode but the scalar and topological datum it carries"* — Ω, the topological class, the conformal class. The v0.5 boundary-condition recast exists precisely to change the crossing object's **type** from field to datum.
- **§12.1:** *"The conformal class and the topology carry zero information; at most one real number remains, Ω."*

A massive fermion crossing would carry energy, lepton number and a mass scale. It also disturbs **§6's zero-net-cost accounting** — *"the content nets to zero … summed across both CPN-conjugate orientations, energy, charge and baryon number are exactly zero at N = 0"* — which is what lets a universe's worth of matter not come from anywhere. Importing relics from the parent breaks that, and §6 item 1's Planck-density baryogenesis start assumes nothing is imported.

**On the parenthetical — absorber or emitter?** §4's criterion is the **absence of an absorber** in the photon's future light cone (Cramer; Wheeler–Feynman). It is not an emitter condition. A relic neutrino is neutral and is neither an emitter nor an absorber of photons at any relevant order, which is exactly why it is still sitting there when the trigger fires. Jammer's point stands.

## 3. **The finding: locality is not mappability, and today's settlement conflated them**

Jammer closed the locality question on the ground that neutrinos are not absorbers, so they do not block the trigger — and concluded *"PC should not look for the difficulty in locality; it is entirely in (c)."* AC adopted that, and I applied it to §3 an hour ago.

**Blocking the trigger and having an image under the conformal map are different questions, and only the first was answered.** The trigger fires with of order one relic neutrino in the patch, and the conformal step then has to do *something* with it. A massive worldline carries proper time, which Ω² rescaling does not preserve — so the relic is precisely the content the map has no image for. It is not an obstruction to the trigger; it is an obstruction to the step the trigger initiates.

**So item (c) has a local component after all**, and it is sharper than §5's version. §5 as I wrote it today says scales are present in the *laws* — a neutrino mass, a finite |Φ_∞|, a Planck length. Greg's question says something harder: there is **actual massive content inside the patch being mapped**. "No scale in the physics" fails not only in the abstract but in the specific region the map acts on.

**This is a scoping defect in text I shipped today, not merely a new idea.** §3 now states that the whole-boundary reading is excluded and the paper is committed to patch-locality. True, and still true. But a reader will take "locality settled" to cover the patch's massive content, and it does not. Per the standing rule — *a shipped patch found wanting is a defect report, not a withdrawal* — this is filed as a defect against C21 v0.8 §3 and §5, mine.

**Proposed scoping sentence for §3**, not applied pending AC:

> *What the locality argument settles is that massive content does not **block** the trigger. Whether massive content inside the triggering patch has an image under §5's conformal map is a separate question and is open (§12.2) — a relic neutrino carries proper time, which Ω² rescaling does not preserve.*

## 4. **Greg's diversity instinct identifies something real about C21: there is currently almost no diversity to be had**

This deserves stating because it is not obvious from the paper. Follow §12.1 through: the crossing datum reduces to **at most one real number, Ω, built from the parent's β_NTE**. §5 adds that x = (π²/2ζ(3))^(1/3) is a pure number, *"the same for every parent aeon whatever its β_NTE"*, and that R_H cancels identically in the mapped seed energy.

**A single parent has one β_NTE.** So every one of the foam's daughters — a large, growing, finite number of them, one per Hubble cube — inherits the **same** Ω. On the present reading the foam is an enormous ensemble of causally disconnected universes that are **identical in everything the mechanism transmits**. C21 has no per-event variable at all, and no mechanism for β to drift between generations.

**Greg's suggestion is the only per-event variable the mechanism naturally has.** The relic content of the triggering cube genuinely does vary cube to cube — at the reset epoch about 45% of cubes hold no relic neutrino and the rest hold one or a few — and that variation is physical, not a modelling choice.

## 5. But the carrier cannot be gravitational. The numbers.

If the relic's influence on the daughter were to run through its gravitational imprint on the patch, it would have to beat the residue §10 already dismisses. It does not come close.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| ρ_Λ at the plateau (3H²/8πG, H = 2.141 × 10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹) | 8.20 × 10⁻²⁷ kg m⁻³ |
| one 0.05 eV relic in V_H = R_H³ | 3.25 × 10⁻¹¹⁶ kg m⁻³ |
| **ρ_ν / ρ_Λ** | **≈ 4 × 10⁻⁹⁰** (10⁻⁹⁰ at 8.6 meV, 10⁻⁸⁸ at 0.5 eV) |
| no-hair Weyl residue at first occurrence, N ≈ 65.4 | ≈ 10⁻⁸⁵ |

**The relic's fractional contribution to the local energy density is four to five orders of magnitude *below* the Weyl residue §10 calls erased.** These are not the same kind of quantity and the comparison is an order-of-magnitude framing rather than a calculation — I overstated a residue claim once already today and will not do it twice. But the conclusion is robust: **gravitationally the relics are beneath the noise floor the paper has already argued away.**

**A cross-check that passed, worth recording.** From the standard relic density, 336 cm⁻³ today diluted as e⁻³ᴺ, the mean relic count per Hubble cube at N = 67 comes out of order 0.5, and at N ≈ 65.4 of order 50. That is independently consistent with Jammer's λ ≈ 1 photon and ≈ 0.8 λ neutrinos at the nominal epoch, and with his λ ≈ 115 at first occurrence. **His estimate reproduces from a different starting point.**

## 6. Where the proposal could live, and the condition it must meet

If the relics are to matter, the carrier has to be **discrete information** — the integer count in the cube, or flavour and helicity content — and not an amplitude. **That is the right *type*.** §7's crossing datum is already scalar and topological, so a count is type-compatible in a way most proposals are not, and this is more than a passing resemblance: the recast's whole gain was that only a change of type did any work.

**The cost is explicit.** It adds a **second datum** beside Ω, which contradicts §12.1's *"at most one real number remains"* — a ratified section, cited in the deposit ratification. That is AC's and Greg's to move, not mine.

**And one discipline-12c condition, stated before anyone gets attached to it.** If a relic count is admitted *because* it supplies the diversity the mechanism lacks, that is structural hand-tuning — adopting a structural option for where it lands the result. The order has to run the other way: **is there a type-consistent reason a relic count crosses the interface at all?** If yes, diversity is a consequence and a welcome one. If the only argument for it is that C21 needs diversity, it should not be adopted, however attractive the consequence. **The question Greg has actually opened is whether C21's near-total uniformity across the foam is a problem the paper should own** — and that is worth an entry whether or not the relic route works.
