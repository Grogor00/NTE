## AC's §2/§10 objection is withdrawn as a blocker. Greg ruled 2026-09-23.

AC raised that C21 §10's stated premise — a parent "whose black holes have long since evaporated" — contradicts §2's ~10²²–10²³-year trigger epoch, since evaporation runs to ~10¹⁰⁰ years.

**The arithmetic stands; the objection does not reach the conclusion.** Greg's ruling: the mechanism is **causal isolation**, not cosmic chronology. At the trigger the photon is alone in its observable universe. In de Sitter, anything beyond the event horizon at ~c/H is **permanently** causally disconnected — not distant, but unreachable in principle for the remainder of that aeon. A supermassive black hole immediately outside the Hubble boundary has no channel to the photon and none to the daughter. How many black holes the parent contains is irrelevant to what a causally severed patch inherits. This is the CCC mechanism applied per-volume, and it is the whole premise of the paper.

**§10's conclusion is correct. Only its supporting sentence is wrong** — and that sentence is unnecessary as well as false at the photon-limit anchor.

**Consequence for §2:** the open question of which moment the foam anchors on is **not load-bearing for §10**. Causal isolation holds at either anchor. §2 keeps the item as ordinary open work.

## For PC — §10 is unblocked. Rewrite, do not re-argue.

Drop the evaporation premise and argue from §9, which already contains the result:

> Penrose's crossover is a **single global surface**, so every evaporation event anywhere in the parent has somewhere on it to imprint. NTE's crossover is **per-Hubble-volume**, and each volume inherits exactly one mode. The discrete/local modification *is* the no-Hawking-points result.

Three supports, in descending order of strength:

1. **Causal severance (decisive).** The de Sitter event horizon makes the isolation permanent and in-principle, not practical. State it this way — "effectively infinitely far away" invites the objection that effectively is not actually.
2. **The trigger excludes black holes from the patch.** T_H ≈ 6.2 × 10⁻⁸ K × (M_☉/M); setting T_H = T_reset ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻²⁹ K gives M ≈ 3 × 10²¹ M_☉. Any black hole below that is hotter than the reset bath and would flood its Hubble volume with photons, violating n_γ·V_H = 1. **The trigger condition cannot be satisfied in a volume containing a black hole** — it enforces the isolation rather than coinciding with it. AC believes this is new; it is not in the archive. Register: internal consistency, same tier as §3.
3. **The seed cannot carry a localised feature.** Whatever lay in the reset event's causal past has already been diluted to one photon — that is what the trigger states. Hawking points are *localised* features requiring concentrated energy at a point on the crossover surface. NTE's seed is a single standing wave of Hubble-radius wavelength (§6). A one-mode, horizon-scale inheritance has no localisation available. Nothing point-like can cross.

The Weyl/conformal-flatness line AC floated is **not** needed and should not be used: in CCC the Hawking-point channel is concentrated Hawking radiation, not Weyl curvature, so it argues past the actual mechanism.

**§10's falsifier survives intact and still cuts against NTE** — a confirmed Hawking-point detection supports Penrose and disfavours the local variant. That is the point of the section and none of the above weakens it.
