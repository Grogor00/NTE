## 1. What the corpus set out to do, and what it achieved

NTE proposed that the quantum state space and physical space are **one S³**, and attempted to derive from that geometry both the structure of quantum mechanics and a cosmology.

**The quantum-geometric half stands.** The Born rule as the measure induced by the Hopf projection; quantisation from the S³ Laplacian spectrum; entanglement as zero-proper-time geometry; and the identification of state space with physical space. **None of these was touched by any finding in this campaign.** Their register was *tightened* — the Born rule is a **reformulation, not a derivation**, as Companion 2 states of itself three times — and they are more accurate for it.

**The cosmological half did not close.** The sections below record why.

## 2. The withdrawals, with the reason in kind

### 2.1 Native (n_s, r) — withdrawn 2026-09-26

**Claimed:** spectral index and tensor ratio derived from Φ_NTE's inflationary trajectory.
**Defeated by:** Φ_NTE cannot inflate under the framework's own constraint H = β_NTE|Φ|². At φ* ≈ 16 M_Pl the constraint gives H ≈ 3.6 × 10⁻⁸ GeV; the map's own r ≈ 0.0068 requires H ≈ 1.9 × 10¹³ GeV. **Mismatch 5 × 10²⁰.**
**Why, in kind:** *the framework's defining constraint and an inflationary potential cannot both hold.* A field whose Hubble rate is fixed by its own amplitude is not free to roll.
**What would reopen it:** a second scalar, or an inflaton that is not Φ_NTE. The deposit has neither, and adding one is new content.

### 2.2 The cosmological origin sector — open since 2026-09-27

**Three computed obstructions**, each independently sufficient: a no-boundary birth hands over at ȧ = 0, so content with ρ + 3p > 0 recollapses in ~1 Planck time; T_GB ≈ 0.4 M_Pl cannot be a post-inflationary reheating temperature (cap T_max ≈ 2.2 × 10¹⁵ GeV, ~2,200× lower); and the deposit does not state whether gauge birth precedes or follows the handover, while containing both readings.
**Why, in kind:** *existence requires an accelerated epoch the framework cannot supply from its own content.* Four candidate mechanisms were gated and all failed at the junction.
**What would reopen it:** a deposited mechanism giving ρ + 3p < 0 at ȧ = 0 — **and note that exit (a′), Starobinsky R², clears §8.1 but not the junction density.**

### 2.3 κ as dark matter — withdrawn 2026-10-06

**Claimed:** κ = 5.50 is Ω_DM/Ω_b, matching Planck's 5.38 ± 0.15 at 0.83σ.
**Defeated by three independent closures.** CPN forces T′ = T, so the conjugate sector recombines at z′ ≈ 1090 against equality at z ≈ 3490 — baryon-like, not cold; contrast 2–12% of the CDM reference, σ₈ ratio ≈ 0.27, x = 1 against a required x ≲ 0.3. The hot reading gives ΔN_eff ≥ 3.73 (41 on the framework's own formula) against N_eff = 2.86 ± 0.13. And the coupling transmits *magnitude, not location*, while the measured ratio is defined against a co-located adiabatic component.
**Why, in kind — and this is the sharpest lesson in the corpus:** ***CPN symmetry gives κ its footing through M_A = M_B, and CPN forces the equal temperatures that make the sector un-cold. The symmetry that produces the number denies its interpretation.*** They cannot be separated. **This is not a defect awaiting repair.**
**What would reopen it:** a deposited reason for T′ ≠ T that is not sized to reach x ≲ 0.3. **A cooling mechanism tuned to the threshold is fitting, whatever its narrative.**

### 2.6 The unresolved-neutrino ontology — re-grounded 2026-10-12

**Claimed:** the neutrino is unresolved on the conjugation axis *because it is minimally Higgs-coupled* (y_ν ~ 10⁻¹²), and this forces Majorana character.
**Defeated by:** the two couplings are different objects — the light–light coupling is ~m_ν/v, the Dirac Yukawa is Y_ν₂ ≈ 0.34 — and the deposit never says which governs. **On the effective-coupling reading the argument is circular** (the coupling is small *because* M_R is a large Majorana mass); **on the Yukawa reading it fails** (with Y₃ = 1 the neutrino is the *most* strongly Higgs-coupled lepton). Part 1 §VII's override is also anachronistic: it invokes at the pre-EWSB locking epoch a non-resolution that C7 §3 places at EWSB.
**Why, in kind:** ***no coupling magnitude sets the conjugation axis. Dirac-versus-Majorana is a lepton-number-violation fact.*** An argument from coupling smallness cannot reach it, and one that tries is downstream of the seesaw it means to explain.
**Consequence:** **"forced Majorana" does not survive as a topological or geometric result.** The Pin⁻/ℤ₂ twist stands and grounds CPN, baryogenesis and the three-eigenmode count — **the no-fourth-light-neutrino falsifier survives** — but no deposited text ties the twist to Dirac-versus-Majorana. That forcing lived only in the κ₀ counting, withdrawn with 1c. **Majorana character reverts to standard type-I seesaw: NTE-distinctive in statement only.**
**What would reopen it:** **Pin⁻ spin structures ↔ Majorana fermions is a genuine theme in the mathematical-physics literature.** A real Pin⁻ → Majorana argument **might** be constructible. **It is not in the deposit.** This is the most concrete reopening path the corpus has.
