## 2. **Chapter 4 v10 carries two claims the N-thickness arbitration has just removed. It is Greg-ratified.**

### 2.1 The owned/inherited split on time dilation — **now both inherited**

**Chapter 4 v10, in terms:**

> *"A boundary worth marking while we are here. This is the **gravitational** dilation, and it is the one the framework offers a structural reading of. The other dilation — the one that comes from relative motion — the framework does not derive and does not claim; it inherits it from special relativity intact… **Two dilations, one owned and one inherited, and the book will keep them apart.**"*

**AC's N-3 ruling:** gravitational time dilation **regrades derived → inherited (GR)**, because *"β|Φ(x)|² is a local expansion rate, **not** the lapse √g₀₀ that sources Schwarzschild; a bound well has no local Hubble flow"* — and AC calls it *"a real content loss, and the one a careless simplification would have hidden."*

**So the split the chapter builds is gone: both dilations are inherited.** This is not a register softening — the chapter's *structure* at that point is the contrast, and the contrast no longer exists.

**Two things partly cushion it, and I flag them so the repair is not over-scoped.** The chapter already hedges the derivation: *"the framework's account is consistent with the observed time dilation; it does not yet **derive** it from first principles."* And its |Φ_NTE|-varies-where-the-Higgs-does-not observation is a separate point that survives. **What cannot survive is "one owned and one inherited."**

### 2.2 The three-names identification — **AC's N-2 says these are three notions, and one identity is false**

**Chapter 4 v10:**

> *"the boundary's local **thickness** in the e-fold direction, the local **Hubble radius**, and the inverse of β_NTE|Φ_NTE|² are **one and the same quantity** — a single geometric object carrying three names — so a gravity well is a place where the manifold's thickness varies, and gravitational time dilation is the time-side measure of that thickness variation."*

**AC's N-2:** three distinct notions — **T = ∫dN/H** (cosmic time), **ℓ = 1/H** (Hubble length), **δ** (embedding depth) — and *"Part 1 §2.1.2 asserts T = ℓ. That is **integral versus integrand**, and it is **false in the de Sitter future** (ℓ → const, T → ∞)."*

**Chapter 4 makes the same collapse Part 1 §2.1.2 makes**, and adds a third name to it. AC owns the Part 1 repair; **the Book carries the same defect and should be repaired from the same wording** (Jammer's §2.4 minimal form, per AC).
