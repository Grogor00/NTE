## Verification 3 — the no-boundary measure literature. **Genuinely unresolved, and NTE sits in the contested configuration.**

The dispute is real, active, and was never settled:

- **Feldbrugge, Lehners & Turok** (PRD 95, 103508, 2017; PRL 119, 171301, 2017) — Picard–Lefschetz treatment of the Lorentzian path integral; the no-boundary condition yields **unsuppressed perturbations**.
- **Diaz Dorronsoro, Halliwell, Hartle, Hertog & Janssen** (PRD 96, 043505, 2017; PRL 121, 081302, 2018, *Damped perturbations in the no-boundary state*) — counter-proposal via a complex lapse contour.
- **FLT** (PRD 97, 023509, 2018, *No rescue for the no boundary proposal*) — **prove no contour in N avoids the unsuppressed contributions**; and *Inconsistencies of the New No-Boundary Proposal* (Universe 4, 100, 2018).
- **Di Tucci & Lehners** (PRL 122, 201302, 2019) — the partial resolution: **Robin boundary conditions** give manifestly convergent path integrals approximated by **stable** Hartle–Hawking saddles.

**The exposure, and it is specific.** The Robin rescue works at a stated price: *"the off-shell geometries do not start at a zero size."*

**Companion 9 §1 states NTE's saddle closes regularly at τ = 0 — "the no-boundary condition: a → 0 without conical singularity."** That is exactly the configuration FLT attacked and exactly what the Robin-condition rescue gives up.

So NTE's deposited saddle description sits in the contested case, and the known route to a stable no-boundary saddle would require abandoning the zero-size closure Companion 9 asserts. **Scratch's framing — NTE does not *inherit* the textbook problem because its own saddle is unbuilt — stands. But the pre-lock node now has a named, specific obstruction to clear, not a general caveat.**

Neither AC nor the corpus adjudicates FLT vs the Hartle–Hertog line. **Both are recorded; neither side is picked.**
