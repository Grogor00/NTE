## Grades
- **C2: NOT PUBLISHABLE.** The qubit uniqueness claim is false.
  - Counterexample: P = sin²(π|⟨φ|ψ⟩|²/2). It is smooth, U(1)-invariant, invariant under joint SU(2) rotation, and normalised in every qubit basis. I checked it numerically.
  - The paper names this function itself and then misstates why it fails.
  - The exponent is fixed only at n≥3, by non-contextual additivity. That input is not in M1–M3.
  - §6's "non-contextuality emerges" is false.
  - "Topological signature of Hopf" is wrong: the quadratic comes from the Hermitian norm.
  - "Axiom without derivation since 1926" contradicts the paper's own Gleason citation.
  - The Layer-1 plus Layer-2 reduction to f(|z₁|²) is correct and is the only part that survives.
- **C1: NOT PUBLISHABLE.**
  - There is no photon frame, and the two detection events are spacelike. The "one zero-interval object" does not exist geometrically.
  - The paper contradicts itself on hidden variables: outcomes are "fixed at creation" plus settings, yet it claims "no hidden variables".
  - Its own flagship test (Hensen 2015, massive electron spins) refutes the zero-proper-time selection rule.
  - The ontology is photonic, but the formula given is the spin-½ cos²(θ/2).
- **C3: NOT PUBLISHABLE.** My PF prior is overturned.
  - The thesis is a category error. Spectra belong to operators, not to the unit sphere of the Hilbert space. Hydrogen and a free particle share one Hilbert space, and an infinite-dimensional unit sphere is not compact.
  - The CCR [x̂,p̂]=iℏ has no representation on a compact phase space, because the trace of a commutator is zero.
  - "Hopf exists only for S³" is false, and C2 §4 itself uses S^{4j+1}→ℂP^{2j}.
  - §6's uniqueness criteria forbid every state space that is not a qubit.
  - Known defects (a)–(c) are confirmed. (b) has three errors in one sentence.
- **C6: NOT PUBLISHABLE.**
  - It identifies spin-rotation SU(2) with weak-isospin SU(2)_L.
  - It identifies global phase (M2) with gauge U(1)_Y. Neutral states and the post-EWSB era break that identification.
  - The §8 claim that G₂ forces the SM group is false.
  - Known defect (d), κ=5.50 and "the dark-matter ratio", is confirmed in §6 and §9.
  - The paper depends on A1/A5 throughout.
