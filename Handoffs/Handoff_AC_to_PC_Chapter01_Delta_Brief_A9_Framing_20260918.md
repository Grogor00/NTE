## The headline, so PC can calibrate scope

**The chapter's geometry is correct and survives.** The line on the map, the extent read from outside, the zero interval along the line, the photon-as-connection, the endpoints drawn apart by expansion — all of it holds and none of it needs rebuilding.

**What breaks is the epistemic symmetry.** The chapter's spine is *two readings of one object, both real, neither more fundamental*. Part 1 §I now says the photon's side is **not a reading at all** — there is no inertial frame at c, so "from the photon's perspective" is **undefined, not degenerate**. The limit does not converge; it fails to exist.

So the work is a **vocabulary and register change, not a demolition**. Everywhere the chapter says "what the photon sees / the photon's frame / the photon's vantage," the honest statement is "what the geometry assigns along the line." The picture is the same. The speaker changes.

One encouraging thing: the chapter already hedges in one place — *"from the photon's own perspective, granting that 'perspective' is loose here for a thing that does not experience time the way creatures of time do."* That instinct was right. The corpus has now gone further than the hedge, and the hedge is the natural place to build from.

## D1. "The photon's frame" — the central delta

**Loci (§ Preamble; § The Forty-Million-Year Instant; § Stepping Outside Time; § The Forty-Million-Year Instant, Revisited):**

- Preamble: *"from its own frame, the moment it's emitted … are the same event"*
- *"Two events on a photon's path are, from the photon's frame, the same event"*
- *"From the photon's frame, there is no journey"*
- *"while the photon itself, measured along its own existence, finds zero of both"*
- *"And from the photon's perspective, both the temporal extent and the spatial extent vanish"*
- *"From the photon's vantage along the line, the geometry assigns zero to both"*
- *"From its frame, no time has passed"*
- *"because the photon's frame does not register the expansion at all"*

**Canonical position.** ds² = 0 is an invariant *we* compute about the worldline, not a description of anything the photon undergoes. No inertial frame moves at c. Statements about what the photon experiences are undefined rather than zero.

**Suggested direction.** The asymmetry PC needs is: *one vantage is an observer's measurement; the other is not a vantage but a property of the line.* The chapter's own map does this work already — the line has extent when measured from outside, and the metric assigns it zero internal length. That second fact needs no observer sitting on the line. PC may find the reframing actually *simplifies* the passage, because it removes the awkward business of granting the photon a loose perspective and then apologising for it.

**Watch:** the phrase *"Both readings real; neither more fundamental than the other"* is the load-bearing sentence of the section and is the one that now misstates the corpus.

## D2. "Experiences" as a verb

**Loci (§ Preamble, twice, one in bold):** *"the photon experiences null-time"*, *"If a photon experiences no time"*.

Also the chapter title conceit and the Prelude title.

**Canonical position.** §III: null-time is null *from* time — a saturated value of an existing proper-time measure — **not existence in the absence of time**, and not something undergone.

**Suggested direction.** "Experiences" is the single word doing the most damage, because it's in the opening sentence in bold and sets the reader's whole frame. Candidate replacements that stay vivid: the photon *accumulates* no proper time; the geometry *assigns* it none; it is *null from* time. PC's call on which carries the voice.

## D3. The strongest new tool the chapter doesn't have yet

**Canonical (new today, §I):** the photon has **no four-velocity at all** — u^μ = dx^μ/dτ is 0/0 when dτ = 0 — and its four-momentum is **null**, k·k = 0. It is not the extreme case of proper-time accumulation; **it is the case outside the construction.**

The chapter currently frames the photon as *the limit case*: *"The photon is the limit case of this. At exactly the speed of light, proper time stops accumulating entirely and proper distances contract to zero."*

**Suggested direction.** "Limit case" is exactly the framing §I now rejects — it implies a smooth approach to a defined endpoint. The correction is that the sequence has no limit point: massive things get arbitrarily close, and the photon isn't at the end of that road, it's off it. This is the sharpest available argument against "the photon's frame" and it will do more work than any amount of hedging. Worth its own short passage.

## D4. Null-time as reparameterisation freedom — the positive replacement

**Canonical (new today, §III):** a massive worldline carries an intrinsic scale along it; a null worldline carries **only an affine parameter, fixed up to affine rescaling**, so nothing along it is metrically marked. This is the relativistic reading of zero e-fold embedding depth — no embedding, no intrinsic parameter. One fact, two vocabularies.

**Suggested direction.** This is the constructive replacement for "what the photon sees," and it's more pedagogically tractable than it sounds: *the line has no ruler along it*. Not "the ruler reads zero" — there is no ruler, and nothing to mark. It also connects the chapter's two halves for free, because "no ruler along the line" and "no Higgs coupling, no embedding" become the same statement in different languages, which is precisely the structural move the chapter says it makes repeatedly.

## D5. The one-dial framing understates the corpus

**Locus (§ The Forty-Million-Year Instant, Revisited):** *"How deeply a given particle is embedded is set by the same coupling that gives it mass — the Higgs coupling, one dial read in two ledgers"*; and in § Where This Leads, *"set by its Higgs coupling, the same coupling that gives it mass"*.

**Canonical (§I, restated 2026-07-06 — after the Book freeze).** The Higgs-only framing is clean for *fundamental* particles but **misses the composite case**. The exception-free statement is the relativistic one: an entity accumulates proper time **if and only if it carries rest energy and moves sub-luminally**. A proton accumulates proper time although ~99% of its mass is QCD binding energy of massless gluons. Embedding depth is sourced by rest energy **from whatever mechanism** — Higgs coupling for fundamental fermions, confinement for hadrons.

**Suggested direction.** The chapter's "one dial" image is good and worth keeping, but the dial is *rest energy*, not Higgs coupling. Ch 4 is flagged to carry the full version; Ch 1 should at minimum not state the narrow version as complete. This is a correction the chapter could not have known about — the restatement shipped one day after the freeze.

## D6. Proper time vs cosmic time, and the missing half of dilation

**Locus (§ Time, As the Expansion Unfolds):** *"the rate at which proper time accumulates is parameterised by |Φ_NTE|"*; *"less t accumulates per unit of expansion in the well than in flat space"*.

**Canonical (new today, §2.1).** β_NTE|Φ|² is a **lapse** on the N-foliation. t(N) is the proper time of an observer **at rest in the comoving frame at x** — cosmic time — not proper time simpliciter. The Schwarzschild recovery is the **static-observer** statement.

**And the gap:** velocity-dependent dilation is the ordinary tilt of a worldline relative to the foliation, dτ/dt = √(1−v²/c²), and is **inherited, not derived**. NTE's derived content is the gravitational piece alone. Two observers at the same x with different velocities share a local |Φ| and are separated by the tilt, not by the field.

The chapter currently attributes *all* time dilation to |Φ| variation. A reader who knows that muons in an accelerator are time-dilated will notice, correctly, that no |Φ| gradient is involved — and the chapter has no answer.

**Suggested direction.** A short passage distinguishing the two dilations, with the honest register attached: the gravitational one is the framework's; the velocity one is ordinary special relativity, which the framework inherits unmodified once t exists. This is a *credibility gain*, not a loss — it shows the framework knows which half it owns.

## D7. Watch-item, not an error: the surfer

**Locus (§ The Forty-Million-Year Instant, Revisited):** the compound-trajectory passage and the surfer analogy — carried at c by the expansion, traversing along the wavefront.

This is a **spatial** decomposition and is consistent with §2.2 (the photon sits on the boundary, co-moving at c). **No change needed.**

**But:** §2.2 now explicitly disowns the "spacetime speed budget" — the popular framing in which motion through space is traded against motion through time. A reader arriving with the budget picture in mind will map the surfer's two components onto it. PC should ensure nothing in the surrounding prose invites that reading, and consider a one-line disclaimer. The canonical wording is available: the budget's trade-off is velocity-dependent and frame-dependent, whereas embedding depth is set by rest energy and is **frame-invariant**. That contrast is the cleanest inoculation.
