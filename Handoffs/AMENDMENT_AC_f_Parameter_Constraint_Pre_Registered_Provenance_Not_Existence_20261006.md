## 1. The hazard, named before it arrives

**κ cannot currently be tuned.** κ₀ = 3/√(4π), closed to κ₀/(1−κ₀) = **5.50**. No free parameter, nothing to adjust. **That is precisely why 0.8σ against Planck's 5.38 ± 0.15 is worth something.**

**But fork N1 — the only survivable one — would introduce one.** If matter crosses into our Friedmann equation and radiation does not, *something* mediates that crossing. **The moment that mechanism has a strength, a fraction or a mixing angle, there is a dial.**

Call it **f**. Then the observed ratio is **κ_eff = f × 5.50**, and **f ≈ 0.978 lands exactly on 5.38.**

**That would be indistinguishable from fitting.** A zero-parameter result at 0.8σ is evidence. **A one-parameter exact match is nothing** — and it would cost the corpus its best handle while appearing to improve it.

**The pressure runs one way, which is what makes this dangerous:** ΔN_eff wants *less* conjugate energy crossing; κ wants a specific amount. **A lane solving one will be pulled toward adjusting the other.** Nobody will experience this as fitting.

## 2. **Greg's ruling, and it is the right distinction**

> ***"We will not number fit, but f may have a legitimate reason/pathway/physics for inclusion — 'may' being speculative, we would have to find a real mechanism."***

**So f is not forbidden. Fitting f is forbidden.** The test is **provenance, not existence**:

**ADMISSIBLE** — f arises from a structural feature of the deposit, with its value **determined by that structure** before any comparison with 5.38. Then κ_eff is still a zero-parameter prediction; it simply has a different zero-parameter value, and whatever it lands on is the result.

**INADMISSIBLE** — f is introduced with a free or fitted value, or a range, or is "constrained by requiring agreement with the observed ratio." **Then κ is no longer a prediction and its Planck agreement re-registers as *fitted*, losing the handle regardless of how well it matches.**

## 3. **The procedural test — state f's value before comparing**

**Any lane proposing an f must, in this order:**

1. **Name the mechanism** and show it is in the deposit, or state plainly that it is new content requiring its own justification.
2. **Derive f's value from that mechanism**, with the derivation written down.
3. **Only then** compute κ_eff = f × 5.50 and compare with 5.38 ± 0.15.

**A value obtained in the reverse order is fitted, whatever the accompanying narrative.** AC will return any filing where the comparison precedes the derivation.

**AC's stated preference, recorded now rather than after the result is known:** a mechanism that is **all-or-nothing** — matter crosses, radiation does not, by a structural distinction with **no tunable strength** — is the clean outcome. **A continuous knob is not, even if it works perfectly.** *Especially* if it works perfectly.
