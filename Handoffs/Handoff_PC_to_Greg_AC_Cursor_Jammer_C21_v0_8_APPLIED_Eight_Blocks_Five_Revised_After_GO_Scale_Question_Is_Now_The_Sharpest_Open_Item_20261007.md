## 2. What the new evidence changed — four corrections, three of them to me

**(a) The spread's mechanism was wrong in my draft.** I attributed the distribution of trigger times to inhomogeneity and "local density", following AC's wording. **Jammer's correction is right and it reverses the picture:** the relic photon bath is uniform to parts in 10⁵, and voids are empty of *matter*, not of *photons*, so the photon condition is met at nearly the same time everywhere. The spread is not at the front — it is in the **tail**, from cubes holding bound charged matter that keep producing both photons and absorbers. §3 now says that.

**(b) I said no first-occurrence estimate could be made. One can.** Jammer's: **N ≈ 65.4**, about **1.5 e-folds** before the nominal 67, driven by the number of Hubble cubes and the chance one holds no relic neutrino rather than by inhomogeneity. **PC reproduced it independently to order of magnitude** — at N = 65.4 the expected number of qualifying cubes is of order unity; at N = 67 the probability a cube holds no relic neutrino is 0.46, against his 45%. It is in the paper as an order-of-magnitude estimate with his name and his own "unreviewed, not pre-registered" label on it, and §12.2 keeps it as open work.

**(c) My §1a was overstated, and this is the correction that matters most.** I wrote that the residue became indeterminate — "bounded below by 10⁻⁸⁷ and above by nothing the paper derives" — and framed it as running against §10. At 65 e-folds the residue is **10⁻⁸⁵**, two orders from the nominal figure, not an open-ended blow-up. The direction was right and the magnitude was rhetoric. §10's text now gives both figures and rests the conclusion squarely on causal severance, which is where it always belonged.

**(d) Jammer found a second textual ground I missed, and it is better than mine.** I argued from §3's criterion being a photon count. **§4 already states the trigger's physical content as the absence of absorbers** — and a relic neutrino is neutral and absorbs nothing, so on §4's own reading it does not even block the trigger. Charged matter does. That is the paper answering the question in its own voice, one section earlier than I looked.

**And the third reading is excluded, not open.** My Block 6 would have filed it in §12.2 as an open question. Jammer closed it from §10: if massive content elsewhere on S³ could spoil the conformal step, black holes elsewhere would too, and §10's decisive support would fail — the paper cannot require a mass-free S³ and keep §10. **That item is now stated as a settled exclusion in §3, not parked in §12.2.**
