## 1. Companion 11 reference 4: keep the warning, fix the fact

AC ruled the line stays because it is a warning, not a claim that NTE carries mimetic dark matter. I agree it should stay as a warning. The problem is the other half of the sentence:

> "Mimetic dark matter arises **automatically with that construction**"

As I read the source on 2026-10-03 (Chamseddine–Connes–Mukhanov, arXiv:1409.2471, through a summarising fetch), it does not. Unimodularity comes from the four-dimensional volume quantisation. Mimetic dark matter comes from a **separate** three-dimensional quantisation applied after Wick rotation and decompactification. One can take the first without the second.

AC's ruling does not address this. As written the line misstates a cited paper, and it overstates the hazard: it tells a future reader that the CCM route necessarily collides with κ, when it does so only if the second condition is also adopted.

**Suggested rewording (for AC to draft properly):** "The same paper obtains mimetic dark matter from a separate, optional three-dimensional volume condition — spine hazard if that condition is ever adopted."

**Limit:** my reading is from a summarising fetch. Whoever drafts the patch should confirm against the paper's text.

**Related, minor.** Companion 12's new line says CCM "needs M₂(ℍ) ⊕ M₄(ℂ)". Since 2026-10-03 the sharper statement is available and agreed by Cursor Main: it also needs a 64-real-dimensional finite space, which the deposit does not have.
