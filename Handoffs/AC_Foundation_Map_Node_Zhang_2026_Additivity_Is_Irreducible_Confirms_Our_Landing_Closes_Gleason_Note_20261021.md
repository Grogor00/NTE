## 1. **The node — for `Working\foundation_map\`, schema §3**

**claim:** The additivity postulate is **indispensable** in all five major derivations of the Born rule, and **additivity and noncontextuality are provably independent** — neither can substitute for the other.

**type:** theorem (two no-go results plus a survey of five derivations)

**establishes:**
- **Additivity ⇎ noncontextuality.** Proved by two counterexamples: μ(A) = Tr[A]/d² satisfies noncontextuality but not additivity; the "Equal rule" satisfies additivity without either form of noncontextuality.
- **Additivity ⇎ normalization.** *"Strong normalization"* (Σμ(A_i) = 1) is **normalization *plus* additivity**, and conflating the two is what produced the literature's false equivalence claims.
- **All five derivations require countable additivity** — Gleason, Busch, Deutsch–Wallace, Zurek's envariance, Finkelstein–Hartle — **either explicitly or as a hidden assumption.** Zurek's "weak additivity" yields additivity only for *rational* probabilities and **cannot establish continuity**; Hartle's lacks it and is incoherent on mixed states without it.
- **Conclusion:** *"The Born rule cannot be derived solely from the nonprobabilistic postulates of quantum mechanics,"* and **additional postulates with intrinsic probabilistic character are required.**

**does not establish:** that no derivation of the Born rule is possible — only that every known route needs a probabilistic input. It does not address operational-reconstruction routes (Hardy, CDP, Masanes–Müller) directly; it is about derivations *within* the standard formalism.

**status:** established (peer-reviewed, PRA, Editors' Suggestion, October 2026)

## 2. **What it means for this project — three things, and they are all clarifying**

**(a) It confirms the landing Jammer and Scratch reached.** Their finding was that **the Born exponent is fixed only at d ≥ 3 by non-contextual additivity**, and that additivity is **not** in M1–M3. **Zhang proves exactly that additivity is a separate, irreducibly probabilistic postulate** — not derivable from noncontextuality or normalization. **Two lanes reached by computation what this paper proves in general.**

**(b) It closes the Gleason-note novelty question, and closes it negatively.** Zhang notes Gleason *"has an exception for two-dimensional Hilbert spaces"* as known background, and that **Busch's extension is valid at d = 2 precisely because it assumes a stronger (POVM) additivity.** **So our sin²(πr/2) counterexample demonstrates a documented exception, not a new one** — consistent with Cursor's literature finding that the Hopf–Born identification is classic (Mosseri–Dandoloff; Urbantke). **The note has no novelty claim available.**
