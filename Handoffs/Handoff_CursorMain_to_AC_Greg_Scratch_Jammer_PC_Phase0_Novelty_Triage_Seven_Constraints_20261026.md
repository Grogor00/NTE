| 1. \(G_2\) cannot contain the Standard Model group | **PUBLISHED.** Todorov–Dubois-Violette, arXiv:1806.09450, eq. | 2. ℂ-selection / Hurwitz | **PARTIAL.** Hurwitz's classification, given a composition algebra, is published (Gałecka 2021; Baez 2002 reviews it). Furey–Hughes, arXiv:2210.10126, state the other direction: complex structures derived from \(\mathbb{O}\), then \(\mathbb{H}\), then \(\mathbb{C}\). |
| 3. \(6.14x^4\) and the \(x\) bounds | **PARTIAL.** Formula: Berezhiani–Comelli–Villante, hep-ph/0008105, eq. (9), \(\Delta N_\nu=6.14\cdot x^4\), with \(x<0.64\) and \(x<0.70\). Ciarcelluti–Wallemacq, arXiv:1401.4763: \(x\lesssim 0.7\) from BBN; CMB-only 95% \(x<0.456\) (pure) and \(x<0.479\) (mixed); CMB+LSS \(x<0.297\) and \(x<0.315\). "\(x<0.46\)" is \(0.456\) rounded, and it is not the CMB+LSS number. The same paper says identical Lagrangians do **not** force \(T'=T\). \(x\) is free. |
| 4. Escapes closed by the symmetry | **NOT FOUND.** Fetched papers keep asymmetric reheating under an exact Lagrangian \(Z_2\) (hep-ph/0008105; arXiv:2107.04045; Ireland–Koren, PRD 109, 103537). Boyle–Finn–Turok, arXiv:1803.08928, use one scale factor. They do not forbid a second. |
| 5. Phase-stabiliser reduction | **NOT FOUND** in Gleason, Busch quant-ph/0306179, or Zhang. |
| 6. \(d=2\) underdetermined | **PARTIAL.** True for one-dimensional projectors (quant-ph/0306179). An explicit continuous family, any odd \(f\) with \(f(1)=1\), is arXiv:2603.07745. The two formulas \(\sin^2(\pi r/2)\) and \(r+\varepsilon\sin 2\pi r\) were not found. The same Busch paper recovers uniqueness for a qubit once the rule is defined on effects. |
| 7. Additivity | **PUBLISHED.** Zhang, Phys. Rev. A 114, 042208 (2026). Additivity is not noncontextuality, and the five named derivations need it. Cite Zhang. |
