## 1. **THE PRIMARY DISCRIMINATOR — read this before designing your approach**

The obvious framing is *"the boundary ontology dissolves the horizon problem, so inflation is unnecessary."* **That framing is a trap, and it will produce a wrong answer if left unexamined.**

**Inflation's principal job in modern cosmology is not solving the horizon problem. It is generating the primordial perturbation spectrum.** Any no-inflation account of NTE must produce:

- a **near scale-invariant** spectrum, n_s ≈ 0.965;
- amplitude **A_s ≈ 2.1 × 10⁻⁹** at k = 0.05 Mpc⁻¹;
- **adiabatic**, **Gaussian**, **coherent** acoustic peaks — the phase coherence that produced the observed peak structure and killed the topological-defect models in the 1990s.

**Horizon and flatness are the easy half. The spectrum is the hard half, and it is where every non-inflationary alternative in the literature has historically failed.**

**So: do not report "NTE does not need inflation" on horizon-problem grounds alone.** If the spectrum has no NTE-native generator, the answer is that NTE *does* need inflation and cannot currently supply it — which is a worse position than the one we are in, and must be reported as such.
