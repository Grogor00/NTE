**(b) The ghost mechanism looks specific to second-order fields.**

**A spinor's action is first order in time derivatives.** The Dirac time-derivative term is **√γ·ψ†ψ̇**: **the lapse cancels between the volume element and the inverse tetrad.** Therefore the spinor's conjugate momentum **does not depend on either lapse**; with kinetic terms on both metrics it depends on the two spatial volume factors and the relative boost, **but not on N or L**. **If this holds, the specific mechanism by which 1408.0487 revives the ghost does not arise for a spinor at all** — massive or massless, conformal metrics or not.
