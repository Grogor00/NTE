## The descent, step by step (C16 §1–§3, verbatim anchors)

**Step 1: "no time-ordering ⇒ non-associative". A posit, not an inference.**
C16 §1: *"With no time coordinate there is no time-ordering operator, hence no mechanism enforcing associativity. The pre-temporal algebra is therefore **generically** non-associative."* C16 credits the reading to Gogberashvili and says *"NTE adopts that reading; it does not claim it."*
- Associativity is an identity of the composition law, not something an operator "enforces". Plenty of algebras with no temporal structure are associative (any matrix algebra).
- The absence of an ordering operator therefore licenses *nothing* about associativity. "Generically" is doing the work.
- **Grade: adopted posit.**

**Step 2: Hurwitz ⇒ 𝕆. Circular at the norm.**
Hurwitz needs a **normed division algebra**. C16 supplies the norm thus: *"…while still inheriting a norm from the S³ geometry and **Hilbert-space structure**."* Its premise (ii) is *"a closed spatial S³ geometry carrying a norm (Companions 3, 5)."*
- C3's S³ is the **state space S³ ⊂ ℂ²**, i.e. M1 itself. The "Hilbert-space structure" is a **complex** inner-product space.
- **So the descent that is meant to explain why amplitudes are complex takes its norm from the complex Hilbert structure.** That is the circularity the pre-registration expected. Even granting the step, the premise is at least as strong as M1: **W-2 at best**.

**Step 3: 𝕆 → ℍ → ℂ, where ℂ is selected. A vacuum choice, placed at two different epochs.**
- ℍ carries a 2-sphere of complex structures, one for each unit imaginary quaternion. Picking ℂ ⊂ ℍ means picking a direction. C16 §3.2 does this with the electroweak vacuum: *"At gauge birth the electroweak vacuum selects e₁ … The complex line ℂ = span{1, e₁} gives U(1)_Y."*
- Any direction gives an isomorphic ℂ, so the *existence* of a complex structure is natural. **But it is selected by a vacuum direction; it is not derived.**
- **And the deposit places this step at two different epochs:**
  - C16's table: *"ℍ → ℂ | commutativity | **EWSB** (U(1)_EM selected)"*;
  - C16 §3.2: ℂ = span{1, e₁} gives U(1)_Y **at gauge birth**;
  - C6, which C2 §7.2 relies on, has the Born rule (which needs ℂ) **activate at gauge birth**.

  If ℂ only arrives at EWSB, the state space between gauge birth and EWSB is quaternionic and C6's activation story fails. If ℂ arrives at gauge birth, the table is wrong. **This is a deposit inconsistency, independent of the derivation question.**
- C16 grades the whole epoch mapping itself: *"a framework-specific **structural correspondence**, not a dynamical derivation."*
- **Conflation, flagged:** the table says the ℍ → ℂ step gains *"commutativity"*. But quantum mechanics needs a *complex scalar field*, not a commutative algebra of observables, which stays non-commutative. The descent tracks the algebra of observables and reads off the scalar field.

**Step 4: the qubit, "the basic system is ℂ²". Not derived.**
- The descent reaches ℂ as a scalar field. It reaches "S³ ⊂ ℂ²" only by **reading unit ℍ (≅ S³) as a state space** (C3 §6: *"the embedding S³ ≅ unit ℍ ⊂ unit 𝕆 = S⁷ places the post-temporal QM state space as the quaternionic submanifold"*). **That reading is the A5-type identification**, which the audit has just graded interpretive.
- **And the object is wrong in general** (C2 §4–§5): spin-j states live on S^{4j+1}, and a general system on S^∞. A derivation of "the state space is S³" derives one qubit's state space. **It must say why the basic system is a qubit. The descent says this only by identification.**

- **That is a non-quantum premise set that yields the complex formalism and the qubit's Bloch ball**, which is exactly the object M1 names (S³ → S² is the Hopf map onto the Bloch sphere).
