## 4. The constructive case for P1, built and then broken — two independent axes

I built the strongest honest P1 case I could: "the conjugate footprint behaves as cold collisionless matter by the epoch Planck fixes the ratio, so κ's comparison is like-with-like." It requires the conjugate matter to be **cold** (pressureless, not oscillating) and **co-located / adiabatic** (tracing the same potential wells our baryons and photons do) at z ~ 1100. Each requirement fails on an independent axis.

### Axis A — temperature / thermal history (kills "cold")

- **CPN symmetry forces T′ = T.** Companion 5 §5 fixes the conjugate sector as the antipodal image with M_A = M_B and coincident field values — i.e. an **exact** mirror, not a cooled one. (Directive §2's own framing: "under the shared-neutrino reading … coupled to its own photons until its own recombination.")
- **Same temperature + same atomic physics → same recombination redshift.** Saha with T′ = T gives z′_rec ≈ z_rec ≈ 1100. The conjugate sector does **not** recombine early.
- **Directive §2 specific — "its own recombination occur early enough for its matter to be effectively cold by the epoch Planck's ratio is fixed?"** No. At T′ = T the conjugate baryons are **still tightly coupled to conjugate photons at z ~ 1100**, so at the epoch Planck's ratio is fixed they are a **radiation-pressure-supported, acoustically oscillating** plasma — the definition of baryon-like, the negation of cold.
- **Directive §2 specific — acoustic oscillations vs cold collapse; footprint CDM or baryon?** Baryon. An oscillating plasma contributes an **oscillating** gravitational potential, not the monotonic pressureless growth that drives the CDM envelope of the acoustic peaks. 84% of the matter budget behaving this way is not a perturbation of ΛCDM; it is a different model.
- **Directive §2 specific — Silk damping in the conjugate sector.** At T′ = T the conjugate Silk scale ≈ ours, so the small-scale conjugate footprint is **damped like baryons**, not preserved like cold matter. It cannot supply the small-scale power / early-equality driving that Planck attributes to CDM.

**P1 on Axis A would need T′/T ≲ 0.3** (so mirror recombination at z′ ≳ 3000–4000, mirror baryons already neutral and pressureless through our acoustic epoch — the standard mirror-DM construction, Cursor Main's lane). **NTE does not derive this; CPN symmetry forces the opposite (T′ = T); and No-f bars introducing T′/T as a knob tuned toward 5.38.** This is a P4 commitment, and it is both undeposited and unfittable.

### Axis B — perturbation structure (kills "co-located / adiabatic") — and this is where the κ mechanism turns on itself

- **Directive §2 specific — "visible in the acoustic peak structure of our CMB, and already excluded?"** Yes, excluded, under either admissible reading of an 84%-of-matter conjugate component:
  - *As oscillating mirror-baryons:* it acts as a massive extra baryon loading of the acoustic oscillations — peak heights and positions shift by an amount equivalent to Ω_b several times its measured value. Planck excludes this at overwhelming significance.
  - *As a spatially decorrelated component:* it is **isocurvature**, and Planck bounds CDM-type isocurvature to the few-percent level. An 84% isocurvature component is excluded by more than an order of magnitude even on a generous reading of the bound.
- **The structural kicker — the κ derivation selects the excluded reading.** κ = 5.50 is derived in Companion 5 §6 / Part 2 §IX from mirror feedback on a sector whose binding "**couples gravitational magnitude, not spatial coordinates … not their locations**," which Part 2 §IX makes quantitative: the two sectors "**decorrelate to ~10⁻³ by z~1100**." That decorrelation is precisely the **isocurvature condition.** To make the conjugate footprint comparable to Planck's 5.38 you would need the opposite — a **co-located, adiabatic** component tracing our wells at z ~ 1100. **You cannot have both.** The property that yields κ = 5.50 (and that yields the σ(M) ∝ M^{−1/2} prediction §6 calls the framework's strongest falsifiable claim) is the same property that makes the component fail to be the CDM Planck measures.

**P1 on Axis B would need co-located adiabatic transmission at z ~ 1100.** This is not merely undeposited — it **contradicts** Companion 5 §6 and Part 2 §IX. A second P4 commitment, and this one is anti-deposited.
