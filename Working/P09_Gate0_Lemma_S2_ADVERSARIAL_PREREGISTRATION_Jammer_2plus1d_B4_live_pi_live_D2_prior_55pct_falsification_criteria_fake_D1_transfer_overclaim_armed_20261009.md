## 0. What changed from S1 — why 2+1d is a different test

### 0a. B4 is live (unlike 1+1d)

In **2+1d**, spatial dimension = 2. The square-root matrix

\[
S = \sqrt{\gamma_g^{-1}\gamma_f}
\]

is a genuine **\(2\times 2\)** matrix:

| Object | Count in 2+1d | Count in 1+1d (S1) | Count in 3+1d |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent matrix elements \(S^i_j\) | **4** | 1 (scalar) | 9 |
| Elementary symmetric polynomials \(e_n(S)\) | **2** (\(e_1=\mathrm{tr}\,S\), \(e_2=\det S\)) | 1 (\(e_1=S\)) | 3 |

**The polynomial-class mismatch (banked B4) no longer evaporates identically.** The HR potential still depends on \(e_n(S)\); the Dirac spatial-kinetic term still depends on the matrix elements \(S^i_j\) via the SVC-eliminated triad. S1’s central lesson stands and is **not** re-litigated: 1+1d was non-decisive because \(S=e_1(S)\). S2 is the first authorized setting where that excuse is unavailable.

### 0b. Term IV / π-channel is live (unlike 1+1d)

Per AC R2 / Directive: in 1+1d, Term IV vanished as a **dimensional artifact** (topological EH; no DeWitt \(\pi\)-kinetics). In 2+1d, nondegenerate gravitational kinetics are present — the \(\pi\)-channel that feeds Term IV of \(\Delta\mathcal{S}\) is **retained** by design.

A vanishing Term IV in S2 is therefore **not** automatic. If Scratch reports \(\Delta_{\mathrm{IV}}=0\), that must be earned by algebra on the constraint surface — not inherited from 1+1d specialisation.

### 0c. What 2+1d still is not

2+1d is **not** 3+1d:

- Clifford algebra \(\mathrm{Cl}(2,1)\): typically 2-component spinors; gamma-space dimension **4**, vs \(\mathrm{Cl}(1,3)\) with **16** structures in 3+1d.

- Ghost / BD dof counting is not the 3+1d count (C3 / S1 K3 analogue).
- A clean 2+1d pattern can still fail to transfer.

### Opening E — Term IV left dark
