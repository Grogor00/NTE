## Landing: **N-1. E4 stands.** Greg's reading is about the daughter side, which the deposit already settles. E4 is about the parent side, and nothing in the reading touches it.

### 1. C21 does claim a stranded photon, in four places, all on the parent side
- **§4 (verbatim):** *"A photon emitted into a de Sitter universe late enough has no absorber within its future light cone; its worldline runs to future conformal infinity without closing. **Half a transaction: an emission with no absorption.**"*
- **§3:** the trigger is a photon count, n_γ·V_H = 1, with thermal n_γ = (2ζ(3)/π²)(kT/ħc)³, *"where a Hubble volume holds one photon and no absorber for it."*
- **§6:** *"the parent's **last unabsorbed photon** has a wavelength of order the Hubble radius … that boundary's last electromagnetic mode — a single standing wave."*
- **§7:** *"At the reset the parent's last unabsorbed photon is a single horizon-scale mode."*

**This is the object E4 concerns:** a photon that was emitted, exists, propagates, and has no absorber. It is the trigger's own content, not a stray sentence. Removing it removes the trigger.

### 2. Greg's reading concerns what happens *after* the conformal step, and the deposit already says it
- **§6:** *"What crosses the interface is not energy, and — per §12.1 — not the mode either."*
- **§7:** *"The photon language belongs to the parent side and is not carried over."* Also: *"'survives', like 'continues' and 'persists', is a temporal predicate, and at N < N_lock nothing has a duration to endure through."*

So "nothing persists" (the photon is not a photon in the daughter; the daughter is what the datum seeds) is **already deposited**. Greg's reading supplies a better *reason* for §7's "the photon is the Janus point". AC is right about that, and it is worth recording as an interpretive gloss.

It does not change what §4 says about the photon **before** the step.

### 3. Could the reading make the nucleation the photon's absorber, closing the transaction? **No; it collides with three deposited commitments.**
For E4 to reverse, the daughter's nucleation would have to be the unclosed transaction's far end, i.e. its absorber. In Wheeler–Feynman an absorber is what responds to the emitted field and **receives its energy**, and in Cramer it is what returns the confirmation wave. Either version:
- **contradicts §6:** *"What crosses the interface is not energy."*
- **contradicts §12.1:** the seed cannot be the daughter's matter content (*"it recollapses the closed universe, requiring ρa⁴ > 10¹⁰"*).
- **needs a mode crossing.** A confirmation wave from the daughter back across I⁺ is the object my 2026-10-08 E1 analysis excluded, *"what the recast exists to stop"*.

**So the attractive version is not available,** and AC's caution holds: it is prettier, and it is excluded by text the paper ratified.

### 4. Ω survives, as AC's §3 requires
"Nothing persists" must be read as *no mode, object or energy crosses*, **not** *nothing crosses*. {Ω, [S³], [g]} crosses (§6, §7, §12.1), and §12.1's "at most one real number remains, Ω" is unaffected. Greg's "scale is upended and meaningless" should be stated as **scale is rescaled by Ω**, which is AC's correction.

### 5. E4 is now partly sourced. AC's ruling required sourcing before §4 is narrowed.
From arXiv:0808.0767 (secondary, summarising Hogarth 1962), as returned by the fetch tool:
- *"in a totally absorbing cosmos, time-symmetric interaction is equivalent to retarded interaction."*
- Hogarth *"pointed out that the Wheeler–Feynman calculation did not apply to an evolving universe"*. With future and past absorber response factors f and p, the consistency conditions for retarded radiation are **f = 1, p ≠ 1**. Advanced radiation is consistent for p = 1, f ≠ 1.
- *"f = 1 holds for models with a final singularity, or those which expand slower than the Dirac model"* (also argued for steady state). Big-bang models give p = 1, a perfect *past* absorber.

**Applied to C21:** C21's own premise is that late photons reach I⁺ unabsorbed, so its parent's future absorber is incomplete (f ≠ 1). **In the credited absorber theory, as Hogarth reformulates it, retarded emission into that future is not supported.** That is E4: the theory C21 credits does not support an emission with no absorption in exactly C21's setting.
