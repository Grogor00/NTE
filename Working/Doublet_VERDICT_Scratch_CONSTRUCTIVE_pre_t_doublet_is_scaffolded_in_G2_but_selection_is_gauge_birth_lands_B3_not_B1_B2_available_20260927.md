## 3. The strongest constructive case (built to its honest limit)

**Step 1 — the right distinction: representation *label* vs gauged *dynamics*.** §8.1 blocks the gauge *connection* A_μ (it needs a timelike index → needs *t*). It does **not**, by that argument, block an object from carrying a *representation label* under a subgroup of the pre-*t* automorphism group. So "can a pre-*t* object be a doublet?" is a question about labels, and §8.1 does not settle it — correctly leaving it to §8.5.

**Step 2 — the scaffolding for a doublet genuinely exists pre-*t*.** The pre-*t* algebra is 𝕆 with automorphism group G₂ (§8.5). G₂ has a subgroup **SO(4) = SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R** — the stabiliser of a quaternionic ℍ ⊂ 𝕆 — and under it the fundamental **7 splits as (2,2) + (3,1)**. The **(2,2) is a genuine SU(2)_L doublet.** Crucially, **the deposit itself invokes this structure**: §8.6 says the observable S³ "is the unit sphere of the quaternionic subalgebra ℍ ⊂ ð … the same ℍ, stabilised by SO(4) ⊂ G₂, whose SU(2) factor is SU(2)_L." So the SU(2)_L is not conjured at gauge birth from nothing — it is a subgroup of the pre-*t* automorphism group, and a doublet is expressible within the pre-*t* 7.

**This is the strongest case, and it is real: the group-theoretic scaffolding for an SU(2)_L doublet is present pre-*t*.**

**Step 3 — where the case stops, honestly.** A doublet is a label *relative to a chosen* SO(4) ⊂ G₂, i.e. relative to a chosen ℍ ⊂ 𝕆. Under the **full, unbroken G₂** the 7 is **irreducible** — "doublet" is *not* a G₂-invariant statement. And §8.5–8.6 place the **selection of ℍ at gauge birth**: §8.6 says the ℍ is "the quaternionic subalgebra that **survives the ð → ℍ associativity transition**," and that transition (the first Cayley–Dickson step, 𝕆→ℍ) **is** gauge birth per §8.5. §8.5 also has the electroweak vacuum "select a direction e₁" at gauge birth. So *as written*, pre-*t* there is no chosen ℍ, hence no distinguished SU(2)_L, hence an object is a **G₂ 7 (or singlet)** — not a doublet.

**Step 4 — the minimal commitment that would rescue a pre-*t* doublet.** Break G₂ → SO(4) (select ℍ ⊂ 𝕆) **pre-*t***, earlier than the ð→ℍ transition §8.6 ties to gauge birth. That is a genuine, nameable commitment — but it has costs that are not free rewording: it front-loads the Cayley–Dickson cascade, and the deposit ties to gauge birth both the CPN+parity assignment of SU(2)_R to the mirror (§8.5) and baryogenesis (§8.4). Selecting ℍ pre-*t* pulls the parity/mirror structure earlier and must be reconciled with those.
