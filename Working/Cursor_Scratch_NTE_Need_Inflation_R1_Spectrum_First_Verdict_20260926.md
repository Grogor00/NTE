## R3 — Horizon / isotropy (after R1)

**Strongest constructive case:** at the signature change / Janus section, every point of the S³ shares one Euclidean origin with no Lorentzian time between them (Part 1 §2.1.2; C8 F2; C9). That is a real structural difference from a hot Big Bang of disconnected causal patches.

**Test, do not assume:** standard Hartle–Hawking is normally paired with inflation precisely because the no-boundary proposal **does not by itself** deliver the observed large-scale isotropy / horizon solution. NTE’s shared-origin claim softens the classical horizon *puzzle* but has **not** been shown to produce (i) the observed homogeneity of the last-scattering surface or (ii) the **phase-coherent** acoustic peaks. Coherence is an R1 requirement, not an R3 free lunch.

**R3 constructive grade:** promising partial; **not** a completed dissolution that licenses “no inflation.”

## Fork result

**Primary: G3.**

Inflation (or an inflation-equivalent quasi-de Sitter map that generates the coherent adiabatic spectrum) **is required**. The corpus has no second scalar that can play that role. Φ_NTE is ruled out as inflaton. Constructive salvage is to **admit new content**: a second field (call it χ) that:

1. Sources a quasi-de Sitter epoch long enough for the observed pivot (horizon-matched N_* band, not necessarily N_HH≈63.4);
2. Yields **n_s ≈ 0.965**, **A_s ≈ 2.1×10⁻⁹**, adiabatic + Gaussian + **coherent acoustic phases**;
3. Reheats, or is compatible with gauge-birth thermalisation at T_GB, without relying on Φ_NTE’s **ξ=1/6** geometric/Ricci channels (see § below);
4. Does not re-identify with Φ_NTE under H=β_NTE\|Φ\|².

**Collapse alternate: G4** — if Greg refuses a second field, the cosmological sector that carried inflationary (n_s,r), the Ω_k *explanation*, and post-inflation reheating **is broken** as a package. Scratch does not soft-pedal that collapse; G3 is the constructive fork, not a denial of G4’s severity.

**Not G1** — R1 absent.  
**Not G2** — horizon not shown dissolved; even if partially softened, R1 still fails.  
**Not G5** — the missing commitment can be named (second inflaton-like field); that is G3, not indeterminacy.

## §5 owed check — does ξ=1/6 suppress geometric / Ricci reheating?

**Verdict: AC’s inference holds**, with the scope below. Geometric reheating is **not** restored as a free escape for Φ_NTE.

| Source | What it requires | Relation to ξ=1/6 |

| **Bassett & Liberati** (hep-ph/9709417) | Geometric reheating strong for **ξ<0** (negative-coupling instability) and **ξ≫1** (broad resonance). Conformal UV fixed point (m=0, ξ=1/6) flagged as a *different*, not efficient, regime. | **Operating window excludes ξ=1/6.** |

**Caveats (honest):** (i) A *different* field with ξ≠1/6 could still use geometric/Ricci channels — that is G3-shaped, not a Φ_NTE rescue. (iii) AC correctly marked the NTE-case conclusion as inference; Scratch’s source check **banks the suppression for Φ_NTE / conformal massless channels**, not a universal theorem for every imaginable extension.
