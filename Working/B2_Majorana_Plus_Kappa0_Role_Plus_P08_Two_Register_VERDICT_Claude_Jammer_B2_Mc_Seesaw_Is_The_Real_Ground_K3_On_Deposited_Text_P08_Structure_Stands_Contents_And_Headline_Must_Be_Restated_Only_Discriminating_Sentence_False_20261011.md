## Block 1 — B2: forced Majorana

### Route 1 — convergence (κ₀ < 1). **Dead.**
- **Part 2 §IX** (verbatim): "The convergence condition κ₀ < 1 requires exactly three eigenmodes (a fourth gives κ₀ > 1, divergent), and on the Z₂ orbifold Dirac neutrinos would give κ₀ = 6/√(4π) > 1 — so the Majorana condition is topologically forced, not assumed."
- **Part 3, derived tier**: "the Majorana condition (forced by orbifold convergence κ₀ < 1)".
- **Companion 5 §6**: "a fourth species would give κ₀ = 4/√(4π) > 1, with the geometric series diverging."

The only series in the corpus whose convergence needs κ₀ < 1 is G_A = M + κ₀G_B → κ₀/(1−κ₀). The same paragraphs withdraw it ("no deposited action"; Part 2 §IX, C5 §6). With no series there is no convergence condition. Route 1 is gone, as the pre-registration expected.

**Companion 7 §4 already concedes the point.** It grounds the fourth-neutrino exclusion on the count of three su(2) generators, and calls the κ₀ < 1 argument "a consistency check on the same fact, not an independent argument." So C7 has already demoted the convergence condition that Part 2 and Part 3 still use as the forcing ground.

### Route 2 — ontology (C7 §1, §7; Part 1 §III; C5 §6). **Independent of κ₀, but not sufficient.**
C7 §7: "a genuinely Dirac neutrino, with an independent right-handed partner carrying distinct quantum numbers, would be a resolved particle." None of this uses κ₀ or the series. On that point the pre-registration holds.

**The gap is sharper than the one I pre-registered** ("what forces the neutrino to be unresolved?"). The deposit's stated answer contradicts its own mass sector:
- **C7 §2** grounds non-resolution on weak Higgs coupling: "The electron (y_e ∼ 3 × 10⁻⁶) is fully resolved … The neutrino (y_ν ∼ 10⁻¹²), six orders of magnitude more weakly coupled, is not pinned." Part 1 §III says the same.
- **y_ν ∼ 10⁻¹² is the Yukawa a *Dirac* neutrino would need.** My arithmetic: m_ν ≈ 0.05 eV gives y = √2·m/v ≈ 3 × 10⁻¹³. So the coupling cited as the reason the neutrino is unresolved is the value it has under the hypothesis C7 §7 says the ontology excludes.
- **Under the deposit's own seesaw** (C7 §5; C12 §3), the light neutrino is light because v²Y²/M_R is small, not because Y is. C12 §3 gives the solar-leg Dirac Yukawa as Y_ν₂ = (8π)^(−1/3) ≈ 0.34. That is about 10⁵ times larger than y_e. If resolution tracks Higgs coupling, as C7 §2 says it does, the deposit's own numbers make the neutrino the more resolved of the two. *(This uses C12 §3's statement of the Companion 13 value; Companion 13 itself not read.)*
- **Part 1 §VII registers the tension without fixing it:** "Neutrinos formally exceed the locking threshold but their unresolved configuration prevents full locking." So the coupling that is supposed to cause non-resolution is overridden by the non-resolution. As written, that is circular.

So C7 §7 holds as a **posit**: a definite ontological commitment with a clean falsifier. What it does not show is that the structure forces it.

### Route 3 — the 90-species count (Part 2 §8.6). **Not independent.**
- Part 2 §8.6 grounds the Majorana condition as "required independently by the dark-matter mechanism, §IX". That points back to Route 1, and to a dark-matter mechanism the deposit now holds open.
- Part 3 grades the 90 as "imported, credited", with "the Majorana species reduction to 90" as a cross-consistency.
- My count: 3 generations × 15 Weyl fields × 2 (particle + antiparticle) = 90. That is the Standard Model **without ν_R**. A Dirac neutrino adds ν_R and gives 96. But a deposit with three heavy right-handed Majorana states (C12 §3: M₁, M_R) also gives 96 above M_R. **So 90 records whether ν_R is counted, not whether the light neutrino is Majorana.** *(My arithmetic. Whether the J₃(𝕆) count is meant to include the heavy states is for Part 2's author; Companion 16 not read.)*

### Route 4 — the type-I seesaw. **Not pre-registered. This is the actual ground.**
C7 §5 and C12 §3 put the absolute neutrino mass scale on a type-I seesaw with a heavy right-handed **Majorana** mass M_R. In a standard type-I seesaw, a Majorana M_R makes the light neutrinos Majorana. **So the deposit already contains a sufficient ground for light-neutrino Majorana character, and it is standard physics, adopted.** It is only as strong as the commitment to a Majorana M_R. Whether Companion 10 forces that or adopts it, I have not checked.

### B2 landing: **M-c**, with a repair available
- **Strike** "forced by orbifold convergence κ₀ < 1" (Part 3, derived tier), "topologically forced, not assumed" (Part 2 §IX), and the series-divergence sentence (C5 §6).
- **Restate** as: *Majorana character of the light neutrino follows from the deposit's type-I seesaw with heavy Majorana M_R (standard, adopted), and is a required commitment of the unresolved-neutrino ontology (C7 §7). A firm Dirac determination falsifies NTE.* On Part 1 §II.0's scale, that is **consistent and committed, not forced**.
- **Separately, open:** C7 §2's coupling ground for non-resolution conflicts with the seesaw Yukawa (C12 §3) and with Part 1 §VII. Either the ground for non-resolution is restated, or the tension is named in the ledger.

### Is Majorana discriminating? **No.**
Standard type-I seesaw predicts it, and so does the deposit's own seesaw. A positive 0νββ signal cannot tell NTE from standard seesaw models. A firm Dirac result falsifies both. **It is a falsifier, not a discriminator.** (Taken up under P08 test (c).)

## Block 3 — P08: the two-register split

### (a) Does (A) contain things that belong in (B)? **Yes: three items, plus a category problem.**
- **κ₀.** Under K-3 it has no job in either register. Move it to a note ("computable Hopf number, no current framework role"), or put it in (B) with the bilateral programme. It does not belong in (A) "with the bilateral geometry". The bilateral geometry's cosmology is (B) by P08's own text.
- **Forced Majorana as worded.** Its stated ground (κ₀ < 1) is (B) material, so (A)'s headline item cites (B) support. Under the B2 restatement (seesaw plus ontology commitment) it **stays in (A)**, renamed and regraded.
- **w = −1.** Its support is the **adopted** unimodular treatment (Part 2 §XIII: "its support is adopted, not derived"). Adoption alone does not disqualify it, since (A) already holds adopted-and-credited items. **The category does.** (A) is "quantum-geometric / particle core"; w = −1 and closed S³ with Ω_k < 0 are cosmological statements. **And see (c): w = −1 is ΛCDM's value, so it cannot distinguish NTE from the cosmology P04 adopts.**
- **Closed S³, Ω_k < 0.** Cosmological, and by Part 3 falsifier 11's own text "not NTE-distinguishing" and one-sided. It is a legitimate ontological falsifier. It sits in (A) as an ontology commitment, not as a particle-core result. A label fixes this.

### (b) Does (B) contain things that belong in (A)? **No. But (A) is missing two particle commitments.**
Nothing in (B) moves up. **(A) omits the deposit's two most near-term particle falsifiers:**
- **Normal mass ordering is required** (C7 §3 "This is a prediction … confirmed inverted ordering would falsify it"; C7 §7; C5 §9). It is **absent from (A) and absent from Part 3's falsifiability register.** Register item 8 covers octant and θ₁₂, not ordering.
- **No fourth light neutrino** (C7 §4, §7; C5 §6, §9). Also absent from (A) and from Part 3's register.

### (c) "The framework's only discriminating near-term particle prediction." **False on all three counts.**
1. **Not discriminating.** Standard type-I seesaw predicts Majorana neutrinos, and the deposit's own mass sector is a type-I seesaw (Block 1, Route 4).
2. **Not near-term, on NTE's own other commitments.** NTE requires normal ordering. Part 3 falsifier 7 states the 0νββ window as "a few meV; next-generation reach". LEGEND-1000's discovery sensitivity is **9–21 meV** (arXiv:2107.11462), so a few meV is beyond it. In the other direction, under normal ordering m_ββ can cancel toward zero, so a null result does not establish Dirac neutrinos. **Neither the confirming nor the falsifying outcome is reachable in the near term, given the ordering NTE itself requires.** Falsifier 7's "next-generation reach" should be corrected.
3. **Not the only one.** Normal ordering (JUNO, 3σ median after ≈ 6.5 yr, arXiv:2405.18008; data taking since 2025) is nearer-term than Majorana and is a sharper NTE commitment: standard frameworks accommodate either ordering, while NTE forbids one. No fourth light neutrino is also near-term (short-baseline programme). |δ| = π/2 is tested by DUNE and Hyper-K, but it is **inherited** from the credited μ–τ reflection (Part 1 §III; C7 §5), as θ₂₃ is.

**A related defect in the |δ| entry.** Part 2 §X derives |δ_J| = π/2 as a Janus-boundary Bogoliubov phase for a **leptogenesis-scale** Majorana fermion. Part 1 §III and C7 §5 derive |δ_CP| = π/2 for the **light PMNS** phase from the μ–τ reflection. Part 3 lists "|δ_J| = π/2 … DUNE/Hyper-K test the magnitude", which attaches the experiment that measures the PMNS phase to the Janus phase. In Parts 1–3, C5, C7 and C12 I found no statement identifying the two. Either the identification is stated and grounded, or the DUNE/Hyper-K line goes with the μ–τ (inherited) entry.

**Part 3's "Empirical distinctiveness" paragraph does not survive.** Its "two handles … distinguishing it from SM + ΛCDM" are (i) w = −1 exactly and (ii) forced Majorana. **(i) is ΛCDM's own equation of state**, the very cosmology P04 adopts for comparison, so it distinguishes nothing observationally. What is distinctive is NTE's *reading* of Λ (the dissolved vacuum-energy problem), which the same paragraph says is explanatory and makes no number. **(ii) does not distinguish NTE from seesaw.** Both handles fail. What survives as NTE-committed and near-term-falsifiable is normal ordering and no fourth light neutrino. Neither is unique to NTE, but each is a definite exposure.

AC's stated goal ("careful derivations [should not] sit at the same apparent register as four open sectors") **is not met yet.** It is reachable with the restatements in (a) and (c) plus register markers in Part 2 §IX and C5/C7/C12. After the B2 restatement and κ₀'s move, none does, as far as Parts 1–3, C5, C7 and C12 show. So a single deposit is sound **after** the repair, not before.
