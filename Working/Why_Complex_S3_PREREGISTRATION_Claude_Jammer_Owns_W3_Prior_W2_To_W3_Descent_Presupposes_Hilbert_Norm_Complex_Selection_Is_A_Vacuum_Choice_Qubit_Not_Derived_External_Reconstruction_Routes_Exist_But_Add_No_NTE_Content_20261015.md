## Attack points, declared before re-reading
1. **"No time-ordering ⇒ non-associative" is a posit, not an inference.** Associativity is an algebraic identity of composition; the absence of a time-ordering operator does not produce its failure.
2. **Hurwitz needs a normed division algebra.** If the norm is supplied by "Hilbert-space structure", the descent presupposes the complex inner-product structure it is meant to derive (circularity).
3. **The ℂ-selection step.** ℍ carries a 2-sphere of complex structures. Selecting one is selecting a direction, which C16 assigns to the electroweak vacuum. That is a choice by a vacuum: isomorphic for any direction, but still a selection, not a derivation. **I also expect a timing inconsistency:** ℂ is placed at EWSB in C16's table but at gauge birth in C16 §3.2 and C6.
4. **The qubit.** Even if ℂ is derived, "the basic system is a qubit (ℂ²)" needs its own ground. The descent would supply it only via unit ℍ ≅ S³ ⊂ ℂ² *read as a state space*, which is the A5-type identification again.
5. **External routes.** The reconstruction literature (Hardy; Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti; Masanes–Müller) derives complex quantum mechanics from operational premises. **So W-3's second clause ("no external route") probably fails.** But adopting such a route grounds M1 as *inherited-and-credited* and adds no NTE content.
