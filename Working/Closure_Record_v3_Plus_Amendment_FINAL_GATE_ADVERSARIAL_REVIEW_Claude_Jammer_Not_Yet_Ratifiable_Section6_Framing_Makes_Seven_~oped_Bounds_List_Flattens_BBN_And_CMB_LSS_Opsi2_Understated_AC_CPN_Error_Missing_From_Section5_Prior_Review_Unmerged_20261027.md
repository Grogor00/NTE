## 2. The bounds list conflates data sets and epochs (directive §3)

The amendment's §1 and §3 list three "published bounds" for x:

| Bound | Source | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| x < 0.64 | BCV hep-ph/0008105, eq. 9 | ΔN_ν < 1, a **BBN** criterion |
| T′ < 0.64 T | Foot, astro-ph/0407623 | Also a BBN bound. Same value, almost certainly the **same criterion** (ΔN_ν < 1), so it is **not independent**. Not verified: I have not read Foot. |

**Arithmetic check (mine):** (1/6.14)^{1/4} = 0.635, consistent with 0.64. ΔN_ν < 1 is the BCV-era criterion. With present N_eff uncertainty (about ±0.17), the same formula gives x ≲ (0.3/6.14)^{1/4} ≈ 0.47 for ΔN ≲ 0.3. That is presumably where the earlier "0.456" came from. **It is my arithmetic with an assumed ΔN_max, not a cited bound.**

**Fix:** present two kinds of bound:
- *radiation-content* bounds: x < 0.64 for ΔN_ν < 1 (BCV; Foot), tighter with current N_eff;
- *structure-formation* bounds: x < 0.297 / 0.315 (CMB+LSS, CW).

Do not list them as one quantity. The conclusion is unaffected: x = 1 fails every one.
