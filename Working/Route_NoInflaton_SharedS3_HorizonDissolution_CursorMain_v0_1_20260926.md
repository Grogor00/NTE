## 3. Why the horizon problem may be ill-posed in NTE

### Standard statement (what inflation solves)

In FRW with a hot Big Bang start and finite particle horizon, causally disconnected patches at last scattering should not share temperature to ~10⁻⁵ unless they shared a past light cone — hence inflation (or equivalent).

### NTE-native counter-reading

1. **One S³, not many patches waiting to meet.** Spatial ontology is a single compact 3-manifold from the start. “Disconnected causal diamonds on an open chart” presupposes a different ontology (local light cones on a large nearly-flat 3-space with a finite past).
2. **Null-time / entanglement geometry (Part 1 IV; Companion 2 family).** Correlations are not required to be assembled by post-lock light travel; the framework already denies that Bell-type correlation needs signalling across the manifold chart.
3. **Pre-temporal Euclidean section.** The HH cap is not a Lorentzian FRW with a particle horizon problem; asking for causal contact in the Euclidean section is a category error (same class of error as treating Euclidean 63 e-folds as slow-roll — Scratch’s geometric point, reused here for horizon rather than observables).
4. **Shared Janus seed (C5 §5).** Both sectors nucleate from one CPN-symmetric boundary; large-scale uniformity is a statement about common origin on one S³, not about post-lock thermalisation of independent Hubble volumes.

**Conclusion (provisional, needs Scratch/Jammer stress):** If (1)–(4) hold, **NTE does not inherit the standard horizon problem**, so it does not inherit the obligation to solve it with ~60 Lorentzian e-folds of accelerated expansion.

**Honesty gate:** This dissolves *horizon*, not *CMB acoustic peaks / near-scale-invariant spectrum*. Those remain open if inflation is dropped (see §6).
