## 1. K4-A — parity permit vs monopole-only force

### Deposit text (operative)

Companion 5 §5:

> even-\(l\) harmonics are symmetric and couple to their antipodal images, odd-\(l\) harmonics are antisymmetric and decouple. **This rule governs which modes participate in cross-boundary gravitational coupling.**

Companion 5 §6:

> The bare coupling is a monopole (\(l = 0\)) harmonic — the isotropic, **lowest-mode contribution selected by** the parity rule \((-1)^l\).

Part 2 §IX deposits \(\kappa_0 = 3/\sqrt{4\pi}\) from \(Y_{00}\) and \(\dim\mathfrak{su}(2)=3\), and states the bilateral action as bi-Einstein–Hilbert + shared neutrino + **Hopf–\(Y_{00}\) bridge**. Spatial decoupling (“magnitude, not location”) is stated as the property of **that** binding.

### Construction score

1. **Parity is a filter, not a monopole ceiling.** Even \(\ell\) **couple**; odd \(\ell\) **decouple**. The rule **permits** even \(\ell\geq 2\) and **forbids** odd \(\ell\). Nothing in the deposited parity sentence says “only \(\ell=0\) may enter a stress/bridge operator.”  
2. **Monopole language is selection of Channel M, not force of truncation.** “Lowest-mode contribution selected by” the parity rule means: among modes the rule allows, the deposit **chose** the isotropic \(\ell=0\) piece for \(\kappa_0\). That choice is register-(A)-adjacent Hopf structure for Channel M; it does **not** logically entail that higher even multipoles are forbidden.  
3. **Spatial decoupling is channel-dependent.** Under pure Channel M (monopole), magnitude-only coupling ⇒ structures decorrelate (deposit). That consequence **does not** survive unchanged if the coupling operator is enlarged to even \(\ell\geq 2\). Changing the operator changes the story — it is not a second, independent ban on Channel L.  
4. **What deposit does force:** odd-\(\ell\) leakage remains forbidden by the same parity rule; \(\kappa_0\) monopole footing must remain intact as Channel M (must not be destroyed / renormalised into a free dial). Channel L, if added, sits **beside** Channel M, not instead of it.

### Fake-P1 check

Claiming deposit *already supplies* \(\ell\geq 2\) coefficients, or that parity *forces* a unique Bullet-ready Channel L, would be fake P1. Claiming parity *forbids* \(\ell\geq 2\) would be a false kill (over-read of monopole truncation as sacred ceiling).

### K4-A sublabel

# **K4-A: PERMIT**

Parity **permits** even \(\ell\geq 2\) stress/bridge coupling. Deposit language **does not force** monopole-only truncation. Monopole = deposited Channel M truncation / footing, not a parity ceiling.
