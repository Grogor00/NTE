## 1. Order as written

**Part 2 §VIII** places **gauge birth** as the Euclidean→Lorentzian signature change / locking event, and identifies that event with the **hot Big Bang**. Inflation appears only as *conditional observables* attached to the pre-temporal Euclidean section — not as a named post-GB epoch.

- Pre-temporal = Euclidean; “$t$ — together with conventional notions like equation of state and slow-roll — acquires meaning only post-locking.” (§8.1)
- “Slow-roll quantities applied to the Euclidean section are therefore artefactual and profile-contingent; any inflationary observable computed this way is conditional…” (§8.1)
- At locking: “$t$, the operative $S^3$ slice, the constraint…, the gauge/Hopf structure, and the Lorentzian continuation” switch on together; radiated gauge bosons = hot Big Bang: “$T_{\mathrm{GB}} \sim 0.4\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}$, **not inflaton decay**.” (§8.2–8.3)
- §IX prior-art note: “NTE retains a Hartle–Hawking Euclidean sub-phase (**its inflationary observables conditional**, Part 3).”

Implied Part-2 order: **Hartle–Hawking / Euclidean sub-phase → gauge birth (= hot BB at $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$)**. Inflation is not sequenced as a separate Lorentzian stage after GB; inflationary numbers are flagged as Euclidean-section / conditional.

**Companion 9** sequences the **Hartle–Hawking Euclidean sub-phase first**, then **gauge birth when that sub-phase ends**, and ties the **Lorentzian continuation** to an **inflationary trajectory**.

- “In the Hartle-Hawking sub-phase … **gauge birth occurs after it ends**…” (§1)
- “its Lorentzian continuation **gives the inflationary trajectory**.” (§1)
- “At the equator the geometry continues from Euclidean to Lorentzian; this is the signature change whose geometric signature is the C¹ kink **at gauge birth**.” (§1)

So C9’s geometric order is: **Euclidean HH saddle → equator / signature change = gauge birth → Lorentzian inflationary trajectory**. Separately, §4–§5 compute $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$ and $(n_s, N_e)$ from saddle / near-saddle slow-roll structure (Euclidean-side machinery), and §6 points to C17’s conditional $(n_s,r)$ at $N_e=63.4$.

**Companion 17 §3** does **not** order gauge birth vs inflation. It evaluates conditional $(n_s,r)=(0.9692,\,0.0053)$ at $N_e=63.4$ and keeps the flag: “**Euclidean-section / $\mu(N)$ applicability of the Lorentzian slow-roll formulae remains a caveat.**”

**Cross-text summary:** Part 2: GB = hot BB; inflationary observables = Euclidean / conditional. C9: GB after HH ends; Lorentzian continuation = inflationary trajectory. C17: inflationary numbers only, with Euclidean-section caveat. The three texts do **not** state one shared chronological order for “inflation” relative to “gauge birth.”

## 2. Physical consistency as written?

**Partially consistent on ontology; not consistent as a single thermal history.**

What coheres:
- Signature change / Wick rotation as the locking event (Part 2 §8.1; C9 §1).
- $T_{\mathrm{GB}}\sim 0.4\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ as nucleation / saddle-scale temperature (Part 2 §8.2–8.3; C9 §4).
- Inflationary observables treated as conditional / Euclidean-caveated (Part 2 §8.1; C9 §5–§6; C17 §3 flag 1).

What does **not** cohere without further statements:
- Part 2’s hot Big Bang is GB radiation at Planck-scale $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$, “**not inflaton decay**” (§8.2–8.3). C9’s wording places an **inflationary trajectory after** the Euclidean→Lorentzian continuation at GB (§1). A post-GB Lorentzian inflation at $N_e\sim 63$ would dilute a $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$ plasma by $\sim e^{3N_e}$; **none of the three texts mention reheating, dilution, or a second heating after inflation.**
- Part 2 §8.1 says slow-roll “acquires meaning **only post-locking**,” while also saying inflationary observables computed on the **Euclidean** section are conditional. C9 §5 and C17 §3 use slow-roll / e-fold formulae for $(n_s,r)$ under Euclidean / saddle framing. The texts flag conditionality but do **not** resolve whether the e-folds that set $(n_s,r)$ accumulate before locking, at locking, or in the post-GB Lorentzian trajectory C9 names.
- C9’s “Lorentzian continuation gives the inflationary trajectory” (§1) sits next to Part 2’s identification of post-locking radiation with the hot BB and rejection of inflaton-decay reheating (§8.2–8.3). As written, those claims are not joined into one temperature history.

**Verdict on consistency:** The geometric HH → signature-change/GB story is shared. The inflationary *numbers* are consistently labeled conditional. The **thermal/dilution sequence** (when heat is produced, whether inflation follows or precedes that heat, how $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$ relates to CMB-era radiation) is **not** physically consistent as a single written chronology — because the texts never supply reheating or dilution bookkeeping to reconcile “GB = hot BB, not inflaton decay” with “Lorentzian inflationary trajectory” after the equator.

## 3. What remains unstated

- An explicit single timeline: does inflation **precede**, **coincide with**, or **follow** gauge birth?
- Whether $N_e\simeq 63.4$ is Euclidean-profile depth, post-locking Lorentzian expansion, or both under different readings.
- Any reheating mechanism (or explicit denial that one is needed) linking $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$ to the radiation era that feeds BBN (Part 2 §XI assumes standard BBN post-temporally but does not connect it to inflation).
- Dilution / entropy accounting if Lorentzian inflation follows a $T_{\mathrm{GB}}\sim 0.4\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ plasma.
- How “slow-roll only post-locking” (Part 2 §8.1) and “Euclidean-section slow-roll formulae” (C17 §3) are to be used in the same prediction chain.
- Whether the hot Big Bang radiation of §8.2–8.3 is the *same* radiation bath that survives to BBN, or an intermediate product overwritten by later dynamics (unstated either way).

## Bottom line

On this read set alone: **gauge birth is placed at the Euclidean→Lorentzian locking / signature change; inflation is either (a) conditional Euclidean-section observables (Part 2 / C17 caveat) or (b) the post-equator Lorentzian trajectory (C9 §1) — not one agreed order.** Thermal consistency (reheating, $T_{\mathrm{GB}}$ survival vs dilution) is **not established in these three texts.**
