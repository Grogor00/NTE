## 1. The crux: **Euclidean.** The Lorentzian-upstream option is excluded by definition

### 1a. There is no Lorentzian epoch upstream of gauge birth (J-a answered: no)

- **Part 2 §8.1:** gauge birth *is* the Euclidean→Lorentzian signature change, and before it "there is no time coordinate". "Lorentzian" and "upstream of gauge birth" are therefore mutually exclusive in this corpus.
- **Part 2 §8.2–8.3** lists "the Lorentzian continuation" among the things that switch on at gauge birth.
- **Consequence:** AC's second horn ("after the equator, Lorentzian de Sitter, while still upstream of gauge birth") describes an epoch the corpus's own definitions do not allow. Only two placements remain: **pre-lock and Euclidean**, or **post-lock and Lorentzian**.

### 1b. The deposit places the 63.4-e-fold sub-phase before gauge birth, in so many words

- **Companion 9 §1:** "In the Hartle-Hawking sub-phase the Higgs has not yet acquired its VEV (the sub-phase is pre-electroweak-symmetry-breaking; **gauge birth occurs after it ends**)." This is an explicit ordering statement. AC's premise that "the deposit does not state the ordering anywhere AC has read" is therefore incorrect for Companion 9.
- **Companion 8 §2** files the sub-phase under "**Two pre-temporal epochs**": the 63.4-e-fold HH sub-phase, followed by the ~0.1-e-fold locking transition, and gauge birth at y = N_lock.
- **Companion 8 F2**: "The Hartle-Hawking framework NTE adopts for the pre-temporal regime is Euclidean-signature." **Companion 9 §5**: "The pre-temporal regime is Euclidean … the post-locking regime is Lorentzian."
- **"De Sitter" in Companion 8 §2 names the H ≈ M_Pl instanton, not a Lorentzian phase.** Companion 9 §3 calls the same object "the natural Planck-scale instanton, H ≈ M_Pl", and an instanton is Euclidean by construction.

### 1c. The sub-phase as deposited is internally inconsistent regardless (adversarial, sourced)

- 63.4 itself is defined as the energy-scale ratio −ln(β_NTE M_Pl) (Companion 8 §2). The item (a) N_HH gloss issue I flagged on 2026-09-25 is this same defect, one level up.

## 2. Second, independent failure: the inflaton's field value contradicts the constraint in every epoch where the constraint holds
