## A1 — The e-fold count N is fundamental; time is emergent (t = i·ΔN)
- **Says** (Part 1 §2.1, §2.1.1–2.1.2): *"N = ln a … requires no clock. All dynamics live in how |Φ_NTE(N)| evolves with N."* t emerges at gauge birth; pre-temporal = Euclidean Hartle–Hawking section.
- **Licenses:** t(N) = ∫dN/(β|Φ|²); the Euclidean→Lorentzian signature change at gauge birth (Part 2 §8.1); the Wick-rotation / no-boundary identification.
- **Does NOT license:** any observable distinguishing N-primary from t-primary — Part 3 states the time-emergence sub-claim *"makes no post-lock observable distinct from a t-primary reading,"* and §II.0 reclassifies t = i·ΔN as a **reformulation, not a postulate reduction.**
- **Class: S (over-strong).** The ontology ("N is fundamental") claims strictly more than its derivations invoke (an N↔t relabelling with no distinguishing observable). The closure record: it *"steered every result toward defending a mechanism that did not survive."* Repair is not a scope line — it is to **demote the claim to what it licenses** (a relabelling) unless/until it yields a distinguishing observable. This is the axiom whose name is being retired, and the demotion is the structural reason why.

## A2 — The core relation H(N) = β_NTE|Φ_NTE|²
- **Says** (Part 1 §2.1): *"a constraint … not a causal claim."* §2.3: under |Φ|² = v²(H/H₀) it is *"an identity with β = H₀/v²"* — a relabelling of the expansion history.
- **Licenses:** H₀ = βv² by construction; w and Ω_k→0 as structural consequences; the tie of the boundary-expansion sector to v_EW.
- **Does NOT license:** native inflation — a field whose Hubble rate is fixed by its own amplitude cannot roll (closure record's 5×10²⁰ mismatch); independent FRW dynamics (§2.3: *"introduces no independent dynamics"*); gravitational time dilation (inherited GR).
- **Class: U.** The scope *(this is an identity, not a dynamical law that can drive inflation)* IS deposited — but in §2.3, not where §I/§2.1 state the relation. Native (n_s, r) was attempted in the gap. Underspecified at point of statement; the missing line is nameable.

## A3 — β_NTE is a fundamental input constant, not derived
- **Says** (§2.4): self-consistent across nine orders of magnitude; *"inherited … not derived."* The closed-form "characterisation" is withdrawn as number-fitting.
- **Licenses:** H₀ once β is fixed. **Nothing else downstream derives from it.**
- **Does NOT license:** any derivation of β from measured inputs (that was the withdrawn characterisation).
- **Class: C (clean) — and a model for the others.** It states its own non-derivation scope at the point of use, which is exactly the field A2/A4 lack. **Task B flag:** near-zero downstream dependents — hand to Jammer as a redundancy candidate (it is an input, not a generative axiom).

## A4 — Boundary-only (surface) ontology
- **Says** (Part 2 §XVI): *"Post-temporally there is only the boundary,"* with scope *"the surface-only statement applies after gauge birth."* Part 1 §2.1.2: *"a statement about the spatial ontology."*
- **Licenses:** entropy = area; the RT-analogue S = A/4G; the Λ-problem reframing (a volume-scaling vacuum energy is the wrong object for surface dof).
- **Does NOT license:** *"there is no four-volume"* — **S³ × N is four-dimensional and ∫√g R integrates over it** (closure record §5). It denies a 4-ball interior, not a four-volume.
- **Class: U (the canonical case).** The scope was deposited (§XVI parenthetical, §2.1.2) yet unnoticed for months; the Dark Sector §4.1 dead end came from the over-reading. Sound axiom, scope stated in the wrong place. *(This is the one AC read twice and missed — confirms the "stated without scope where a user meets it" pattern.)*

## A5 — The quantum state space IS the physical S³ (one manifold)
- **Says** (Part 1 §I, §IV; **Companion 5 — VERIFY, not re-read this session**): state space and physical space are one S³.
- **Licenses:** the Born rule as the Hopf-projection measure; quantisation from the S³ Laplacian spectrum; entanglement as zero-proper-time geometry. Closure record: *"the ontological claim the rest hangs on."*
- **Does NOT license:** the bilateral/cosmological architecture (that rests on A6, not A5); by its own terms it identifies the *state space* with S³, nothing more.
- **Class: C candidate (VERIFY C5).** This is the surviving core and reads clean; confirm against Companion 5 before final.

## A6 — CPN symmetry / Janus two-orientation N-thickness
- **Says** (Part 1 §VI): CPN = *"charge, parity, e-fold-direction reversal"*; the map is *"z → −z, multiplication by e^{iπ}"*; *"one manifold viewed from two CPN-conjugate orientations."*
- **Asked to license:** M_A = M_B (**C5 — VERIFY**); baryon sorting to the mirror (Part 2 §8.4); SU(2)_R to the mirror (§8.5–8.6); κ₀'s bilateral footing; the Z₂-even neutrino.
- **Does NOT license (by its own form):** the map z → −z is a **Hopf-fibre half-turn — identity on the base, orientation-preserving, connected to the identity** — so it contains **no C, no P, no N**, and on resolved matter it is a gauge transformation. **A map of this form performs none of the three jobs.** *(Independent confirmation of AC's flag: I checked the map's stated form against each job; the mismatch is in the form, not a missing qualifier.)*
- **Class: W (over-loaded).** Not underspecification — the gap is between a map and three symmetry jobs no map of that form can do. **Repair = splitting:** state the conjugation operation, then state separately the symmetry each job actually requires (a genuine C and P and e-fold reversal; a mass-equality symmetry; an SU(2)_R embedding). **This is the single most load-bearing axiom defect in the deposit** and the one most likely to decide whether a rebuilt cosmology is even possible.

## A8 — Rest energy = e-fold embedding depth (three coupling regimes)
- **Says** (Part 1 §I, §III, §VII): proper-time accumulation = embedding depth, sourced by rest energy; δ ∝ rest mass; locking criterion y_eff²/(β|Φ|) ≥ 1.

- **Class: U-core, with one WRONG sub-clause (S).** The embedding∝rest-energy axiom is sound for the proper-time/arrow content. But Part 1 §I's clause *"rest mass **is** Higgs (Yukawa) coupling … neutrinos barely, with y_ν ∼ 10⁻¹²"* is **false for seesaw neutrinos** (m_ν = Y²v²/M_R ≠ y·v) and seeded the June–October confusion. That clause is wrong, not underspecified — **flag for correction**, distinct from the axiom it sits in.
