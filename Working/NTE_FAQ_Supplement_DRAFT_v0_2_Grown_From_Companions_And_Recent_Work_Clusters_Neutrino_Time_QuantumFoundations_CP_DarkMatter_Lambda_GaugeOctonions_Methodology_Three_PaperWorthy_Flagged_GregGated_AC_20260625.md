## A. Neutrino identity & the mass sector

**A1. Is the NTE neutrino "its own antiparticle" in the usual Majorana sense?**
No — it is *unresolved*, not collapsed. The neutrino has not undergone the Hopf projection (S³→S², engaging the S¹ fibre) that *creates* the particle/antiparticle binary, so it is **neither particle nor antiparticle — the category never applied**, rather than the textbook "its own antiparticle." It has bilateral support across both CPN-conjugate orientations of the N-thickness.
**See:** Part 1 §III; Companion 7 (Neutrino); Part 1 §VI.

**A2. Isn't the neutrino a LH/RH chirality superposition ("both and neither")? How does that square with the seesaw retaining handedness?**
Both hold, because chirality (the grading γ) and particle/antiparticle-resolution (the real structure J) are independent axes. The "both and neither" lives on the J/mass axis (ν_R = ν_L^c for a Majorana neutrino); handedness is retained on the γ axis (the SU(2)_L embedding). §III removes the J-resolution, not γ-chirality.
**See:** Part 1 §III; Companion 7 (absorbing the depreciated 7a "Chirality Superposition"); Paper P5 §1; Paper P4D.

**A3. Does the LH/RH superposition extend to a 4π/8π superposition?**
No. 4π and 8π are operator *entries*, not *states*: the neutrino mass **factorises through both** (seesaw — 4π Dirac × 8π Majorana), a product, not a blend. Branch-superposition is a category slip; only an off-diagonal particle↔J entry in a faithful D_F could mix them, and that would give a single *derived, shifted* value, not a superposition.
**See:** Paper P5; `Working/…Chirality_Superposition_Does_Not_Extend_To_4pi_8pi…20260625`; P4D-sequel descent verdict.

**A4. Why does the Dirac neutrino Yukawa carry 4π, not 8π?**
The Dirac entry connects LH↔RH *within* the particle sector and does not pass through the antiparticle (J) block that doubles the Majorana mass — so it takes the single, un-doubled Hopf flux (4π), while M_R takes 8π. Now **triple-convergent**: P5 real-structure ⊕ Farnsworth associator ⊕ Scratch block-structure.
**See:** Paper P5; Companion 13 (ν2 Yukawa, D5-inverse); 4π convergence-ratification (2026-06-21); descent verdict.

**A5. Why does the neutrino mass sit *between* the two branches?**
The solar level and the atmospheric ratio **anti-correlate in the flux**: 8π fits solar / overshoots ratio ~1.5×; 4π fits ratio / overshoots solar ~1.5×; an intermediate value makes *both* mediocre. It is an honestly-flagged structural tension (the (flux)^(2/3) *shape* is the fallible content), not a hidden interpolation; magnitude is not dialled. **¶**
**See:** Paper P5; Companion 13; `Working/…Offset_Pattern_CHASE_RESULT…20260624`.

**A6. Is the neutrino mass a zero-parameter prediction?**
No — and the paper says so plainly. The *shape* m(ν₃)/m(ν₂) = (flux)^(2/3) is the genuine fallible content; the magnitude is a structural confluence-tier input, not fixed by a Lagrangian term, with M_R set independently by the heaviest mass. What remains open: Y_ν1, the charged-lepton Yukawas, the full Dirac matrix, and first-principles fixing of the branch.
**See:** Paper P5 (abstract, five falsifiers); Companion 10 (M₁ pathways).

**A7. What is the atmospheric "2/3" question?**

## B. Time & emergence

**B1. Why is there "no time" before gauge birth? Isn't that contradictory?**
There is no *proper-time coordinate* t pre-temporally, but there *is* the primitive e-fold ordering N — succession without metric duration. "Before" is the wrong word (oxymoronic in the pre-temporal context); the correct phrasing is "at N < N_lock." t emerges only once dt/dN stabilises at locking.
**See:** Part 1 §2.1.1 (Axiom 1.1); Part 1 §VII (locking); Reader Glossary ("pre-temporal", "null-time").

**B2. What does t = iΔN mean — is time imaginary?**
The "i" is geometric, not mystical: it encodes that emergent proper time is the metric distance *perpendicular* to the e-fold direction, between the two CPN-conjugate orientations of the N-thickness. This is the same content as the Hartle–Hawking no-boundary section (pre-temporal = Euclidean, gauge birth = signature-change saddle) — a *consequence* of the axioms, not a separate postulate.
**See:** Part 1 §2.1.2; Companion 6 (gauge birth).

**B3. Is NTE a "block universe" / is the future fixed?**
The arrow is a real, irreversible commitment, not a convention: a species locks to one e-fold direction once it accumulates >1 Higgs interaction per e-fold. The arrow is emergent at locking, not imposed.
**See:** Part 1 §VII (temporal locking and the arrow).

## G. Methodology — what NTE claims and how

**G1. Does NTE "fit numbers" / is this numerology?**
The no-numerology rule governs *ratified additions*: nothing enters the corpus as a result by numerical fit. Investigating numerical patterns (e.g. offset structures) is open and encouraged, but a pattern only becomes a claim when a physical mechanism derives it — otherwise it stays flagged as investigation or is discarded.
**See:** `Working/…NoNumerology_Scope_Clarification…20260623`; rev10 manifest (no-numerology, three-tier taxonomy).

**G2. What's the difference between a prediction, a cross-check, and an input in NTE?**
A *prediction* is forced by the structure (e.g. (flux)^(2/3) shape, n_s, r); a *cross-check* is a known result the framework reproduces; an *input* is inherited and disclosed (e.g. β_NTE, M_R magnitude). The corpus tiers these explicitly and withdraws claims that turn out to be inputs dressed as predictions (e.g. the β_NTE characterisation, the Λ numeric chain). **¶ (strongest paper candidate)**
**See:** rev10 manifest (New / Re-derived / Imported map); Part 1 §2.4; the sunshine-audit reframes (F1, κ₀, P5, P4).

**G3. Why do some NTE papers present "conditional" results?**
Because the discipline is to state exactly what is derived and openly bracket what isn't (e.g. P4D's unification is conditional, n_s/r are conditional on the open K(Φ)). A conditional result with the open step flagged is preferred to an overclaimed closed one.
**See:** Paper P4D v0.3; Part 1 (n_s/r, K(Φ) open); programme-level honest-scope standard.
