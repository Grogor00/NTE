## Findings

### 1. The deposit has two different mode rules, and neither is "ℓ = 0 only"

- C5 §5: "even-l harmonics are symmetric and couple to their antipodal images, odd-l harmonics are antisymmetric and decouple. This rule governs which modes participate in cross-boundary gravitational coupling." This is a deposited filter — **all even ℓ on S³**.
- C5 §6: "The bare coupling is a monopole (l = 0) harmonic — the isotropic, lowest-mode contribution selected by the parity rule (−1)^l — read on the S² base of the Hopf-projected bridge cross-section."

"Lowest-mode contribution" names ℓ = 0 as the first member of a family, not the sole member. The deposited rule passes ℓ = 2, 4, … The deposit's own parity rule therefore contradicts a monopole-only filter rather than supporting it.

### 2. Which-sphere ambiguity (the M4 component)

- C5 §5 states the parity rule on S³, which C5 identifies with physical space.
- C5 §6 reads the monopole "on the S² base of the Hopf-projected bridge cross-section".
- C6 §6 (read 2026-10-06) says the Y₀₀(S²) "lives on the same S² the Born rule projects onto". Companion 2 makes that S² the Bloch sphere — an internal state space.
- C7 §4 (read 2026-10-06): gravity "couples to this residual geometry through its isotropic component, the s-wave harmonic Y₀₀(S²)".

If the S² is internal, ℓ = 0 on it says nothing about spatial multipoles of the conjugate density, and F2+ cannot be read off it at all. If it is spatial, finding 1 applies. Either way F2+ is not forced. The deposit should say which sphere it means; that is a wording-level defect with a claim-level consequence.

### 3. "Mode-independent" in Part 2 §IX is about the neutrino, not about matter

Part 2 §IX: "exact and mode-independent (the monopole projection of any normalised density is Y₀₀, a mathematical identity), and structurally grounded by the neutrino's angular-direction-free delocalisation". "Mode" here is the neutrino eigenmode; the identity is about a *normalised* density. It fixes the coupling *coefficient*. It does not project the *source* (conjugate matter) onto its spatial monopole. Reading it as a source filter is a category slip.

### 4. The deposit elsewhere requires non-monopole content to cross

- C5 §6: "each sector feels the magnitude of the other's gravitational wells, not their locations". Wells are localised structure. A strict spatial monopole carries no wells, only a mean.
- The σ(M) scatter mechanism (Part 3 derived tier still lists σ(κ;M) ∝ M^{−1/2}) needs per-object variation of what crosses. A homogeneous monopole has none.

So monopole-only removes deposited content. Adopting it is a claim change, not a clarification.
