## Landing: **B4-b.** The edge as worded is wrong. The two literatures already meet, and they meet at a **no-go**. That no-go is the nameable obstruction AC asked for under B-3. B-3 and B-4 collapse into one finding.

## 1. The operational side already reaches J₃(𝕆)

Barnum–Müller–Ududec (2014) use three single-system postulates: classical decomposability, strong symmetry, and no higher-order interference.
- From these they obtain the irreducible formally real (Euclidean) Jordan algebras: "real, complex, and quaternionic quantum theory, **together with 3-level octonionic quantum theory** and ball state spaces" (quoted).
- So an operational reconstruction lands on **J₃(𝕆)**, which is the exceptional Jordan algebra the division-algebra Standard Model uses.
- Adding a fourth postulate, **observability of energy**, leaves complex quantum theory as "the unique solution" (quoted). That relates "the emergence of the complex numbers to the possibility of Hamiltonian dynamics" (quoted).

**So the reconstruction programme is not silent on the octonions.** It meets J₃(𝕆) explicitly, and then excludes it.

## 2. The composition side excludes J₃(𝕆) outright

Barnum–Graydon–Wilce (Quantum, 2020) study non-signalling, dynamically reasonable composites of Euclidean Jordan algebras.
- "No such composite has the exceptional Jordan algebra as a direct summand" (abstract, as extracted).
- Prop. 4.14, as extracted: if A has an exceptional ideal and B has a nontrivial ideal, no composite AB exists. The only exception is when B is essentially classical.
- The reason: composites of simple, non-exceptional algebras are **special** (Thm 4.12), and J₃(𝕆) is not special.

## 3. The division-algebra side does treat J₃(𝕆) as a quantum system

Todorov (2019): the Euclidean Jordan algebra "gives room not only to the observables of a quantum theory, **it also contains its states**" (quoted). States are defined as positive normalised functionals, with pure states as primitive idempotents. J₃(𝕆) is presented as a "finite quantum geometry", an internal observable algebra.
- That is exactly the object Barnum–Müller–Ududec and Barnum–Graydon–Wilce reason about.
- The two programmes therefore **share a category**, the order-unit and Euclidean-Jordan-algebra framework. AC's B-3 lean ("no common category") is wrong.

## 4. The obstruction, named

Combining §§1–3:

> **If the Standard Model's internal space is a Jordan-algebraic quantum system J₃(𝕆), as the division-algebra programme states, then:**
> - (i) it is excluded by any reconstruction that includes Hamiltonian dynamics ("observability of energy", BMU);
> - (ii) it cannot be composed, as a genuine subsystem, with any non-classical system (BGW Prop. 4.14), and that includes the spacetime and field degrees of freedom it would have to couple to.

This is the precise reason no bridge was built. **The reconstruction programme reached the octonions and ruled them out on composition and on dynamics.**

## 5. Escape routes, which are where the real open question now sits

- **E1: J₃(𝕆) is a label algebra, not a subsystem.** It would be an internal superselection or charge algebra that is never tensored with anything. Todorov's own construction realises the internal observables inside complex Clifford algebras and complex 16×16 Hermitian matrices, i.e. inside special, composable algebras.
  - If the physics only ever uses the special sub-structure (for example the one-generation subalgebra that gives G_SM), then the composition no-go may be **evaded**, not violated.
  - **Test:** is every element of the Standard Model derivation realisable inside a special Jordan subalgebra? If so, J₃(𝕆) is scaffolding and the "octonionic" claim weakens. If not, the no-go bites.
- **E2: leave the Euclidean Jordan algebra class.**
  - Baez–Bokor–Boyle (July 2026) note that "there is no Euclidean Jordan algebra underlying" the bi-Cayley structure (quoted). They move to positive Hermitian Jordan **triples**, from which G_SM arises via two colinear minimal tripotents in 𝕆²_ℂ.
  - They explicitly leave "mixed-state quantum theory to future work" (quoted), and they discuss **no** GPT reconstruction and **no** composites.
  - **This is the genuinely open bridge:** does a GPT or operational reconstruction exist for Jordan-triple quantum theory, and do its composites survive?
  - It is fresh (three months old), it sits exactly between the two literatures, and nobody has done it. That is a better research target than the gap as originally worded.
- **E3: a dynamics postulate weaker than observability of energy** that keeps 3-level octonionic theory. That is possible but contrived; BMU tie the complex numbers to Hamiltonian dynamics.
