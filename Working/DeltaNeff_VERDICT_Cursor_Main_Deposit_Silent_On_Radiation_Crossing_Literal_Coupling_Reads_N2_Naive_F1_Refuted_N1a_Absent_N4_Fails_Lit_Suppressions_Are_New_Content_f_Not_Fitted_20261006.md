## 3. Computations (declared order)

### 3.1 ΔN_eff at equal temperature, unit gravitational weight

Shared neutrinos counted **once** (one Z₂-even field; Companion 7’s three light states).

After e± annihilation, a twin photon bath at T′ = T contributes
\[\Delta N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(\gamma')} = \frac{8}{7}\left(\frac{11}{4}\right)^{4/3} \approx 4.40.\]
A full twin SM with its own three neutrinos would give ≈ 7.40; under shared-ν that extra neutrino copy is **not** added.

**Against ΔN_eff ≲ 0.17 (95%, fetched):** exclusion factor ≈ **26×** (photons alone). Against AC’s 0.14 figure ≈ **31×**. AC’s ≈6 estimate (full doubling including twin neutrinos) is the right order for a twin SM; with shared ν the cleaner number is **≈ 4.4**, still refuted by tens, not “in tension.”

### 3.2 Same at deposit cross-coupling weight

If the fixed-point rule applies to radiation as to matter:
- bare: κ₀ ≈ 0.846 → effective ΔN_eff ≈ 4.40 × 0.846 ≈ **3.7** (still ≫ 0.17)
- resummed: κ ≈ 5.50 → ≈ **24** (worse)

So **weighting by κ does not rescue**; it aggravates if radiation participates in the same algebraic closure.

Order-of-magnitude: Γ ~ G_F² T⁵ vs H ~ 1.66√g_* T²/M_Pl. Γ/H ∼ 1 near **T ≈ 1.5 MeV** (standard weak decoupling scale). That is **not** early enough to freeze in a large T′/T hierarchy before BBN/CMB without an additional entropy dump into our sector only.

**Deposit contains no asymmetric reheating, no late reheaton, no stated T′/T ≪ 1.** Under CPN-symmetric content and a shared weak neutrino, **N4 fails** as a deposit-supported escape.
