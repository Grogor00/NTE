The correct statement is the one already deposited twice:
- Part 2 §XII: the 10¹²⁰ catastrophe "is computing a **volume-scaling vacuum energy for a system with only surface degrees of freedom**."

$$\boxed{\rho_\Lambda = 3\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2 H_{\mathrm{dS}}^2}, \qquad \text{coefficient } 3 \text{ given } S = A/4G.$$
