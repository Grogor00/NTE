## Defect check (from source)
- **(b) CONFIRMED — mathematical error.** C3 §4: *"Ĥ=(p̂²+ω²x̂²)/2 is unitarily equivalent (via the Bargmann transform) to the Laplacian on a circle S¹, whose eigenvalues n² give the oscillator levels."* Wrong twice: (i) the S¹ Laplacian eigenvalues n² = 0,1,4,9,… are **not equally spaced**, so they cannot give the equally-spaced oscillator levels E_n = ℏω(n+½); equal spacing is the **number operator** a†a (eigenvalues n), not any Laplacian. (ii) The Bargmann transform maps to the **Segal–Bargmann/Fock space of holomorphic functions**, not to the S¹ Laplacian. The sentence is a referee-fatal error in a would-be-published paper. I concur with Jammer and AC, computed independently.
- **(c) CONFIRMED heuristic.** C3's headline "quantisation is compactness" is false for energy: bound states live on non-compact L²(ℝ³); §8 itself concedes "needs the potential and boundary conditions."
- **(a) CONFIRMED circular.** C3 §6/§8's "why S³/complex" cites the octonion descent; C16 §1 premise (ii) draws its Hurwitz norm from "the S³ geometry and Hilbert-space structure," citing C3/C5 — whose S³ is M1. Load-bearing for C3's "why S³" §6 claim 3 ("associative in the post-temporal regime"), not for the compactness⇒discreteness core.
- **(d) CONFIRMED verbatim.** C6 §6: *"the bare coupling there is κ₀ = 3/√(4π)… closed by CPN-symmetric self-consistency to κ = κ₀/(1 − κ₀) = 5.50 (Part 2 §IX)… The measurement-probability rule and the dark-matter ratio share a common geometric origin."* The broad κ withdrawal (2026-10-09) never reached C6 (header: ratified 2026-09-28).
