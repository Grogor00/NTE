## Facts verified at canonical source this session
Barnum–Graydon–Wilce, *Composites and Categories of Euclidean Jordan Algebras*, arXiv:1606.09331:
- **"no nontrivial composite exists if either factor is M₃(O)"** (§4.3). A composite with the exceptional algebra requires the other factor to be essentially classical (abstract/intro).
- **"a composite of simple, nontrivial Jordan algebras is always a special Jordan algebra"** (Thm 4.12); M₃(O) is not special (§3.1/4.3) → excluded.
- **"the only EJAs having locally tomographic composites with a qubit are the complex quantum systems"** (§3.4, via Hanche-Olsen Prop 3.10). Real quantum composites are not locally tomographic (§2.3).

Local-tomography / quantum-logic paper, arXiv:2001.11421:
- **Prop 7.2: the quantum logics of H_k(ℝ), H_k(ℍ) (k≥3) and H₃(𝕆) do NOT satisfy local tomography (LT).** For H₃(𝕆): n_{A²}=729 cannot equal any of dim H₉(ℝ/ℂ/ℍ) = 45/81/153 → impossible.
- Caveat (honest): with spin factors present, LT does not UNIQUELY pick ℂ (spin factors mimic H₂(ℂ)) — so "LT ⇒ ℂ" is not clean in every case, but the k≥3 real/quaternionic and the octonionic matrix algebras are definitively excluded.
