## 0. Bottom line

**The premise in its current form does not hold.** The current form is: a second, populated, CPN-conjugate universe, coexisting in the same forward proper time, at equal temperature, coupled to ours by a neutrino that sits in both Einstein equations. It fails on three independent counts:

1. **Data.** The equal-temperature twin is excluded by N_eff by a very large margin.
2. **Dynamics.** The only proposed bridge between the two worlds is matter with kinetic terms on both metrics. That is exactly the coupling known to revive the Boulware–Deser ghost.
3. **Earnings.** Every observable once attributed to the second world has been withdrawn, or is not action-backed, or is supplied by something inside our world.

**Recommended course: keep the symmetry, drop the second populated world.**

**On the nature of the symmetry:** the evidence says that, if nature has this symmetry, it is a **symmetry of the laws** (and possibly of the state's boundary condition), **not a duplication of worlds.** "Nature is symmetric, so there must be a mirror twin" conflates symmetric laws with symmetric states. Nature's usual pattern is the opposite: symmetric laws, asymmetric world (§5).

## 5. What is the nature of the symmetry?

Three possibilities. Each permits different things.

**(a) A symmetry of the laws.** The Lagrangian is invariant under CPN, but the actual state need not be.
- This is how nature behaves wherever we have checked:
  - the laws are rotation-invariant, but a magnet points one way;
  - the electroweak laws are SU(2)×U(1)-symmetric, but the vacuum is not;
  - the laws are CPT-invariant, but the universe is matter-dominated.
- **CPT is already exact in any local Lorentz-invariant QFT inside one universe.** It relates particles to antiparticles here. It needs no twin.
- Under (a), a mirror sector *may* exist, but nothing *forces* it to be populated, hot or symmetric. NTE's "CPN forces T′ = T" is exactly a confusion of (a) with (b) below.
- **This is the reading I recommend.**

**(b) A symmetry of the state or boundary condition** (Boyle–Turok 2109.06204).
- The maximal analytic extension of a radiation→Λ universe is "a single two-sheeted universe", exchanged by a reflection isometry about the bang. The isometry "picks a preferred (CPT-symmetric) vacuum state for quantum fields."
- The "anti-universe" is **the same spacetime's other sheet**, on the *other side of the bang in time*. It is not co-present, so it contributes nothing to N_eff today.
- It also gives a reason for the arrow of time: there is a boundary condition at the bang and none at de Sitter.
- **NTE departed from BFT exactly here.** NTE chose a shared forward t (Part 2 §IX (i)), which makes the twin co-present, and co-presence is what the data kill (§2).
- The BFT form of the symmetry is coherent and observationally safe. It is also prior art, so NTE cannot claim it.

**(c) A gauge redundancy.** The two "orientations" are two descriptions of one state.
- Physically there is then one universe. Orientation is a choice of description, like choosing which way a normal vector points.
- The post-temporal "boundary-only" ontology (Part 2 §XVI: "post-temporally there is *only* the boundary") actually **pushes toward this reading**. A surface with no bulk has no physical "other side" on which to put a second population.
- The two-population reading needs two surfaces with residual bulk between them (§IX (iii)). That sits in tension with §XVI.
- Greg's 2026-09-25 ruling rejected (c). This verdict notes that §XVI argues for it.

**Consistency flag (minor).** Part 2 speaks of "the Z₂ orbifold", while the 2026-09-25 ruling says the Z₂ "acts freely". A freely acting Z₂ gives a smooth quotient manifold, not an orbifold. An orbifold has a fixed locus, which here would plausibly be the N = 0 Janus surface. Which is meant matters for the Dirac-vs-Majorana counting quoted above.

**Direct answer to "nature seems symmetric":** the symmetric thing we actually observe is the **laws** (CPT exactly; CP and P approximately, in specific sectors). The **world** is asymmetric: matter over antimatter, left over right, past over future. A symmetric law does not need a mirror world to "balance" an asymmetric state. Spontaneous or initial-condition breaking is the standard and sufficient account. The intuition that symmetry requires a twin world is the one step the evidence does not support.
