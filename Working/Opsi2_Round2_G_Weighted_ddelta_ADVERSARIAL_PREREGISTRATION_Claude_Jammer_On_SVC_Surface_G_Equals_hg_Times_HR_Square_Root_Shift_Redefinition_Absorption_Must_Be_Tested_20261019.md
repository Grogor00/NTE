## 1. A structural fact the round must use, and my own round-1 framing it qualifies
I declare this before the lanes compute. It is elementary algebra; I am not asserting a fork.

**On the symmetric-vielbein (SVC) surface, G is not foreign to HR.**
- Write the inverse triads as matrices E, F and set F = E·X.
- Then h_g = EᵀE, h_f = Xᵀ h_g X, and G = EᵀF = h_g X.
- The SVC (G symmetric) makes X h_g-self-adjoint. It follows that h_f = h_g X², so X = √(h_g⁻¹h_f).
- **That is the HR square-root matrix, in inverse-metric form.**
- So, at the order where the relative Lorentz sits at Λ₀ (S3a: Λ* = Λ₀ + O(ψ²)), **G = h_g · X, with X the same square root that builds the HR potential and the HR shift redefinition** (N^i = L^i + L D^i_j n^j + N n^i, D built from X).

**Consequence for my round-1 synthesis.** "Pure HR never produces G" remains true for the ∂δ sector of pure HR, which carries only h_g and h_f; the potential is ultralocal. But G is built from the HR square root. The live absorption candidate for the G-weighted part is therefore **the D-matrix shift redefinition**, which mixes shifts and lapse through the same X. That is a mechanism a G-1 result could use, and a G-2 result must exclude.
