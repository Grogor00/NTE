# NODE_hassan_rosen_bimetric

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **id** | `hassan_rosen_bimetric` |
| **claim** | There exists a ghost-free nonlinear bimetric theory of gravity (Hassan–Rosen) with two dynamical metrics interacting through a carefully chosen potential; the theory propagates the healthy massive + massless graviton degrees of freedom at the nonlinear level (within its stated analysis). |
| **source** | Hassan, S. F. & Rosen, R. A., “Bimetric Gravity from Ghost-free Massive Gravity,” arXiv:**1109.3515** (*JHEP* **02** (2012) 126). **Fetched** Cursor Main 2026-10-18 (arXiv abs+body). Interaction parameters appear as $\beta_n$ in the mass/potential sum. |
| **type** | framework |
| **assumes** | Two metrics; specific interaction potential built from elementary symmetric polynomials of √(g⁻¹f); vacuum / matter coupling as specified. |
| **establishes** | A consistent classical bimetric framework free of the BD ghost in the vacuum sector as claimed. |
| **does not establish** | Values of the **β coefficients** or the graviton mass scale (they are **free parameters**). That matter may couple to both metrics (see `doubly_coupled_bd_ghost`). Uniqueness. Cosmological data fits. |
| **status** | established as a framework; phenomenology open |
| **verified** | Cursor Main, 2026-10-18 — arXiv:1109.3515 fetched; ghost-free bimetric construction confirmed at abstract/intro strength; $\beta_n$ are free model parameters. `verified: YES`. |

**Edges:** `is-compatible-with` single-metric GR limit. `assumes`-constraint from `doubly_coupled_bd_ghost` for matter.
