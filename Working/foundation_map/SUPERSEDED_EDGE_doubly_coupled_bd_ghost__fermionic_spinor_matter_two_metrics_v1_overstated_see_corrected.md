## Node A — doubly-coupled matter revives the BD ghost
- `doubly_coupled_bd_ghost` (Yamashita–De Felice–Tanaka, [arXiv:1408.0487](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.0487); Cursor-verified 2026-10-18): matter coupled to **more than one** metric generically **reintroduces the Boulware–Deser ghost**; ghost-freeness **strongly restricts** kinetically doubly-coupled matter, with controlled exceptions.
- Companion result `hassan_rosen_bimetric` ([arXiv:1109.3515](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3515)): ghost-free vacuum bimetric framework; β coefficients free.
**What they establish:** the ghost cost and the permitted exceptions for matter sourcing two metrics — **analysed for scalar fields, perfect fluids, and the de Rham–Heisenberg–Tolley effective composite metric g_eff.**

## Node B — fermionic/spinor matter coupled to two metrics
**What is established:** *essentially nothing specific to fermions.* Fermions couple to a **vierbein/tetrad**, not the metric directly, so a bimetric fermion sector requires coupling a Dirac field to **two vierbeins** — a different object from the scalar/effective-metric analyses of Node A. The first-order (linear-in-derivative) structure of the Dirac action interacts with the ADM lapse differently from a scalar's quadratic kinetic term, which is the precise place the BD-ghost mechanism of Node A operates.

## The object they share
Both bear on **the Boulware–Deser ghost in the matter sector of ghost-free bimetric gravity.** Node A settles it for bosonic/scalar matter and the effective-metric coupling; Node B — the fermionic case — is where the shared object has no established treatment.

## What would be needed to bridge (NOT proposed here)
A Hamiltonian (ADM) constraint analysis of a **Dirac field with two independent kinetic couplings to two vierbeins**, determining whether the secondary constraint that removes the BD ghost survives for first-order fermionic matter, or whether the ghost returns as it does for scalars. **That** computation does not appear in the literature. (Dirac on a **single effective** vielbein \(\tilde{E}(E_g,E_f)\) **is** partially done: Melville–Noller 1511.01485, mini-superspace only.)
