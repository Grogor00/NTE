## Argument that photons on −N are **required** (A2-force)

1. From P2+P3, both faces are post-temporal after locking ⇒ both admit gauge bosons.  
2. From P5+P6, −N has a full fermion sector (not a ghost label). Charged fermions under a unbroken Abelian after EWSB need a photon *or* an alternative massless gauge field *or* confinement of all charge — the SM pattern on +N uses U(1)_EM.  
3. CPN is a symmetry of the boundary: the image of our photon under CPN is a null-time gauge excitation on the conjugate orientation (P4+P5).  
4. Orientation confinement says that excitation **does not cross** electromagnetically to +N; it does **not** delete it.  
5. Therefore a conjugate null-time photon plasma is the CPN-symmetric completion.

**Force grade if this holds:** A2 ≈ **forced**; A1 ≈ incomplete CPN.

---

## Argument that photons on −N are **not** required (A1-force)

1. From P7, the octonion route draws **one** complex line → one U(1)_Y → one U(1)_EM after EWSB on the **observable** sector.  
2. SU(2)_R is the *only* gauge factor explicitly relocated to −N (C16 §3.3). Colour and hypercharge are not duplicated in the text.  
3. Adding mirror QED is importing Foot/Berezhiani completeness, which Part 2 cites as prior art NTE does **not** simply adopt.  
4. Conjugate charged fermions could couple through SU(2)_R-heavy dynamics without a light Abelian (charges confined / screened / massive vectors only).  
5. Therefore twin photons are an **extra** assumption.

**Force grade if this holds:** A1 ≈ **forced by constructional minimalism**; A2 ≈ optional maximality.
