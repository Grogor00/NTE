## 1. Was / now / next recalled

Chapter 4 drew three words the book keeps using.

**Was** is settled in the effects of expansion already traced. E-folds that have passed leave fixed consequences — photon paths taken, lockings committed, orientations chosen. That is not the claim that the past is still present as a stored reel. It is the claim that once expansion has committed structure, the effects do not un-happen.

**Now** is where expansion runs and proper time accumulates. It is not a sharp classical edge — no clockwork frame advancing along a frontier. The chapter kept that fence on purpose.

**Next** is what has not yet unfolded. This chapter is about that third word, and about what the framework may honestly say without inventing a deposit it does not have.

Deposit fences that stay closed here: expansion rate \(c\); photons at null depth; **no manifold ahead** of the wavefront as a pre-drawn script (Part 1). This chapter does not reopen those.

## 2. Quantum openness from the Born rule (Ch 6 bridge)

Chapter 6 reads the Born rule as geometry of outcomes on the sphere after the Hopf quotient — not as a second substance poured into an otherwise classical world.

That reading matters for **next**. If outcomes are geometric possibilities weighted on a sphere, then the future is not a single film already on the reel. Probabilistic **next** is not a block of spacetime waiting offstage. It is structure that has not yet been selected by the world's actual course of expansion and measurement.

Explicit refusals (brief's "not in scope"):

- This chapter does **not** prove moral free will.  
- It does **not** import consciousness-collapse mysticism.  
- It does **not** claim that NTE has measured how much openness remains after decoherence and chaos.

What it claims is narrower: the framework's deposited reading of quantum probability is compatible with an open **next**, and incompatible with a purely eternalist story in which every outcome is already geometrically present in the same sense as **was**.

## 3. Chaos and sensitivity

Even before quantum language, classical chaos and measurement sensitivity mean that fine detail on a slice — which weather, which molecular dance — is not fixed by the deposited geometric skeleton alone.

The framework's structural constraints still bind: expansion, gauge structure after locking, deposited lapse rate. Within those rails, which macroscopic history realises on a given \(N\)-slice is underdetermined at the level this chapter can honestly support.

That underdetermination is not a loophole for arbitrary miracles. It is a statement about grain: the deposit fixes the instrument and the tempo; it does not write every note of every performance in advance.

## 5. Contrast: eternalism vs clockwork frontier

Two pictures the framework refuses.

**Block eternalism** (as a total story of time): past, present, and future equally real in one frozen four-dimensional object, with "now" as illusion. The framework allows **was** to be settled in effects; it does not license treating **next** as already present in the same way.

**Clockwork frontier:** a sharp expansion edge advancing like film through a projector. Chapter 4 already rejected that mechanistic edge. This chapter keeps the rejection when it talks about openness.

What remains is **becoming**: expansion + commitment at locking where the deposit earns it + an open outcome layer the deposit has not closed. Not retrieving a stored script.
