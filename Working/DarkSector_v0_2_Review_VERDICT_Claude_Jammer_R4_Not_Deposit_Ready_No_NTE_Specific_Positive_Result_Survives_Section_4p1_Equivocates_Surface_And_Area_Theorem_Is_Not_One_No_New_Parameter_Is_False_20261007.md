## (a) §4.1 — the degree-of-freedom grounding fails. Claim; defective when written; and the defect is upstream in Part 2

AC's third diagnosis does not land, and neither did the first two, for one reason: **the argument equivocates between "surface" and "area".**

- NTE's "surface" is S³. It is three-dimensional. It is all of space. Companion 5 §2: both GR and the Standard Model "are field theories on a 4D manifold — but the manifold is (N, x_{S³})… all equations of motion, conservation laws, and symmetries are preserved."
- The vacuum-energy catastrophe is an energy **per unit 3-volume**: zero-point energy summed over modes of fields living in three-dimensional space. M_Pl⁴ is one Planck energy per Planck 3-cell. It never used a fourth spatial dimension or a 4-ball interior.
- So a field theory on S³ × N has exactly the standard mode sum. Removing the 4-ball interior removes nothing the calculation used.
- "Area-scaling" degrees of freedom means scaling with a two-dimensional horizon inside S³. That is the holographic principle. It is a far stronger statement than "there is no 4-ball interior", and it is not NTE's: the draft itself credits 't Hooft, Susskind and Cohen–Kaplan–Nelson for it in §4.2 and §8.

The draft's sentence "A system whose degrees of freedom are the boundary's (area-scaling, Part 2 §XVI)" slides from the first meaning to the second inside one parenthesis. Part 2 §XVI does the same: "Entropy scales with area because there is only area" — where the "only" thing is a 3-volume.

Consequence: the item the ledger calls "**the NTE-specific dark-energy result**" is the holographic bound, inherited. The surface-only ontology contributes nothing to it. My pre-registered expectation was "insufficient"; the actual result is stronger than I expected.

Second problem, same section. The draft holds two mechanisms for one job: §4.1 says the volume term is "not in the degree-of-freedom count in the first place"; the ledger and Companion 12 say the unimodular formulation "removes the extensive vacuum term". If the term is absent, there is nothing for unimodular to remove; if unimodular removes it, it was present. The draft's own parenthesis in §4.1 concedes unimodular removes only the constant mode. Claim; defective when written.

**Upstream flag:** Part 2 §XII ("computing a volume-scaling vacuum energy for a system with only surface degrees of freedom") and §XVI carry the same equivocation in the deposit.

## (b) "A theorem" — it is not. Claim; defective when written (at AC's instruction)

Pre-registered test: a theorem needs a stated class of routes and an argument that the class is exhaustive. §4.4 has neither. It lists routes that were tried — spectral, CosMIn, reset, total e-folds — and shows each fails. That is "four examined routes closed". The abstract's "provably-underivable" and the ledger's "structural impossibility" go beyond the evidence. AC asked whether this was his over-read of Scratch's verdict; on the draft's text, yes.

Three further points:

1. **The spectral route.** Companion 12 §2 says "a₀ ∝ Vol(M) (the cosmological/volume term)" and then §3 says a numeric Λ "would have required a derivation through a nonzero a₄". Both sentences are in the same paper. If a₀ is the cosmological term and a₀ ≠ 0 (Vol(M) = 2π²β_GB), then a₄ = 0 is not what closes the route; the adopted unimodular formulation is. The draft inherits this unexamined. I have not recomputed anything; this is a conflict between two sentences of Companion 12, for AC.
2. **The real reason is simpler and is not NTE's.** In the adopted unimodular formulation Λ is an integration constant — a free initial datum by construction. "Not derivable" follows from the adoption. That is a property of the borrowed framework, and the honest register is: *not derived; examined routes closed; no derivation expected within the adopted formulation without new content.*
3. "no future tuning within this substrate can manufacture ρ_Λ — consistent with No-f" misuses the rule. No-f forbids fitted strengths. It does not turn an absence of derivation into a result.

## New — §4.2's boxed result and its "band width" are identities. Claim; defective when written

- ρ_Λ = 3M_Pl²H_dS² is the Friedmann equation for de Sitter space. It defines H_dS. It contains no information about the size of ρ_Λ.
- "(H₀/M_Pl)² = 3.5×10⁻¹²¹ against the observed ρ_Λ/M_Pl⁴ = 7.2×10⁻¹²¹, the residual ratio being 3Ω_Λ". The ratio is 3Ω_Λ because ρ_Λ = 3Ω_Λ M_Pl²H₀² by definition. I checked: 7.2/3.5 ≈ 2.06 ≈ 3 × 0.69. This is arithmetic on the observed H₀ and Ω_Λ, presented under a "band widths" label as though it were a comparison. Discipline 12(b) is for comparisons of a prediction with a measurement; there is no prediction here.
- "The entire 120-order gap is the single replacement M_Pl⁴ → M_Pl²H²" inserts the measured H.

The draft does grade §4.2 "inherited". But the abstract's item (ii) still presents it as something the surface-only ontology "delivers".
