# The neutrino-condensate bridge route: closed, and why

**Register.** Honest negative. This note closes a route rather than opening one. It exists so the route is not re-proposed from scratch in six months — the failure mode PC's archive triage of this same morning (`Handoff_PC_to_Greg_Archive_Working_Triage_20260922.md`) documents twice over, and whose proposed standing rule ("a deferral no one can see is indistinguishable from a deletion") this note is written in compliance with.

## §1 Provenance — how the question arose

Greg raised, in a live session on 2026-09-22, a chain of reasoning that began from a Space.com report of Gogilashvili & Tamborra (Phys. Rev. D, September 2026) on neutrino flavour conversion in core collapse, and proceeded through: whether "antineutrino" is a justified category; whether a Majorana neutrino is *neither* particle nor antiparticle (it is *both* — self-conjugate, a populated category containing the photon, Z, π⁰ and Higgs); whether helicity is the operative distinction; whether a neutrino could exist in both sheets of a CPN-conjugate pair; and whether that bilateral neutrino could source dark matter.

The reasoning arrived, unprompted and without reference to the corpus, at an electrical analogy: **reflected impedance in a transformer — the load felt across the gap, seen from both sides, generating a series recursion.**

That analogy is the subject of §2, which is the most important finding in this note.

The chain then terminated on a specific proposal: that the linking field is not the neutrino but the **neutrino condensate** ⟨ν̄ν⟩, following Dvali & Funcke (Phys. Rev. D **93**, 113002 (2016), arXiv:1602.03191). Greg directed that this be pursued against observation. §§3–5 are that assessment.

## §3 The condensate assessed — three independent blockers

Each of the following is individually fatal to using the Dvali–Funcke condensate as either the bridge realisation or the dark sector.

### §3.1 Epoch — decisive

Dvali–Funcke require Λ_G ≲ 256 meV to evade neutrino free-streaming at photon decoupling and the Majoron-coupling bounds (their §IV A; diagonal couplings ≲ 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ if active before T ∼ 256 meV). Their stated working range is 4 meV ≲ Λ_G ≲ 256 meV.

That ceiling **is recombination**: T = 256 meV ↔ z = 1089. The bottom of the range, 4 meV, is z ≈ 16. Lorenz, Funcke, Calabrese & Hannestad (Phys. Rev. D **99**, 023501 (2019), arXiv:1811.01991) find current data prefer the transition scale factor a_s ∼ 1 — essentially today — and that the Σm_ν bound correspondingly relaxes to < 4.8 eV at 95%.

κ = 5.50 is compared against Planck's 5.38 ± 0.15, which is the acoustic-peak dark-matter-to-baryon ratio **at z ≈ 1100 and earlier**. A condensate forming at or after recombination cannot source the quantity κ is measured against. There is no parameter choice that repairs this: pushing Λ_G early enough to matter at the CMB re-imposes the free-streaming and Majoron constraints the model evades by being late.

### §3.2 Vacuum energy — reintroduces the catastrophe in NTE's one structural win

Computed at Planck-2018 parameters (h = 0.674, Ω_c h² = 0.120, Ω_b h² = 0.0224, Ω_Λ = 0.685):

- ρ_crit = 3.66 × 10⁻¹¹ eV⁴ = (2.460 meV)⁴
- ρ_DM = 9.67 × 10⁻¹² eV⁴ = (1.764 meV)⁴
- ρ_b = 1.81 × 10⁻¹² eV⁴ = (1.159 meV)⁴ — ratio 5.36, the κ target
- ρ_Λ = 2.51 × 10⁻¹¹ eV⁴ = (2.238 meV)⁴

Against Λ_G⁴:

| Λ_G | Λ_G⁴ / ρ_Λ | Λ_G⁴ / ρ_DM |
|---|---|---|
| 4 meV (floor) | 10.2 | 26.5 |
| 50 meV | 2.5 × 10⁵ | 6.5 × 10⁵ |
| 256 meV (ceiling) | 1.7 × 10⁸ | 4.4 × 10⁸ |

Dvali–Funcke's remark that the condensate "provides a vacuum energy at the scale of dark energy" is a *scale* coincidence in the meV⁴ sense, not a fit; it overshoots by an order of magnitude at the very floor of the allowed range and by eight orders at the ceiling.

Part 3 sells the boundary-only / trace-free construction as dissolving the ~10¹²⁰ naturalness catastrophe (while explicitly **not** predicting Λ's value). Importing a condensate with vacuum energy 10–10⁸ × ρ_Λ introduces a new fine-tuning precisely there. This is a net loss on the corpus's own ledger.

### §3.3 Direct collision with Companion 7 — two separate contradictions

**(a) Mass origin.** Dvali–Funcke predict neutrinos are **massless until the late transition**; that prediction is the load-bearing element of their cosmological-bound evasion. `Companion_07_Neutrino_v10_19_0.md` §2–§3 derives neutrino mass as geometric tension at minimal Higgs coupling, and derives **normal ordering** from the Higgs VEV splitting the three σ eigenmodes at EWSB. These are two incompatible mass origins at epochs separated by ~15 orders of magnitude in temperature. Adopting the condensate would forfeit C7's normal-ordering prediction, which is one of C7 §7's four stated falsifiers.

**(b) Conjugation axis.** The Majorana-channel condensate ⟨ν_L^c ν_L⟩ is an order parameter that breaks lepton number — a definite commitment **on the conjugation axis**. C7 §1 states that what is unresolved in NTE is exactly that axis ("the operation that would distinguish them has no resolved target to act on"), chirality being separately definite. A condensate in that channel *resolves* the axis C7 says is unresolved. The two ontologies cannot both stand.

Note that Dvali–Funcke's claimed indifference to Dirac-vs-Majorana does not help here: the indifference is about whether the *mechanism runs*, and it runs in whichever channel exists. The channel NTE needs is the one that collides with C7.

### §3.4 Ancillary: relic neutrinos cannot be the dark matter in any case

Ω_ν h² = Σm_ν / 93.14 eV. At Σm_ν = 0.06 eV this is 6.4 × 10⁻⁴, i.e. **0.54%** of Ω_DM h² = 0.120 — short by a factor of ~190, before any consideration of free-streaming and hot-dark-matter structure suppression. Doubling it across a conjugate sheet does not close a factor of 190.

Dvali & Funcke themselves state (their §IV E) that bound neutrino states, a neutrino superfluid, η_ν particles and topological defects "could be partially responsible" for galaxy rotation curves, but that "still additional cold dark matter is required in order to explain, inter alia, earlier structure formation and CMB data." The originating authors do not claim what this route would need them to claim.

## §4 What survives — one open question, routed, not a result

The deposited κ appendix specifies `S_bridge[g⁺, g⁻, ν]` with ν "the Z₂-even unresolved neutrino, delocalised over the Hopf fibre, the only field carrying stress-energy on both metrics at once," and obtains the cross-source coefficient from the Y₀₀ overlap on the internal S² base.

Read carefully, that is an **overlap/projection** coefficient, not a condensate VEV — which is why the condensate adds nothing to it and, per §3, subtracts. The appendix does not require ⟨ν̄ν⟩ ≠ 0 and should not be read as tacitly assuming one.

But one question raised in session does land on the deposited text, and AC does not adjudicate it here:

> **Q-Scratch-1.** What makes T_bridge a *coherent, classical* transmission across the thickness, rather than a suppressed two-fermion exchange? A single-fermion exchange transfers quantum numbers rather than producing a force; a fermion-pair exchange gives the Feinberg–Sucher potential, which falls as 1/r⁵ and is useless at the kiloparsec scales where κ is tested. The appendix establishes the *coefficient* of the transmission given that it is coherent; the coherence itself is carried by the delocalised-zero-mode reading and is, so far as AC can find in canonical, asserted rather than derived.

**This is a question, not a defect claim.** The likely answer is that the Z₂-even zero mode is not an exchanged quantum at all but a single mode with support on both sheets, in which case "exchange" is the wrong picture and Feinberg–Sucher does not apply. If so, saying that explicitly in the appendix would close the question cheaply and pre-empt a referee raising it. Routed to Scratch; AC to integrate whatever comes back.

## §5 One unexplored adjacency, flagged for the literature sweep

Dvali–Funcke's η_ν couples to RR̃ — the gravitational Chern–Simons / Chern–Pontryagin density (Jackiw & Pi, Phys. Rev. D **68**, 104012 (2003)). NTE carries Pin⁻ on the Z₂ orbifold, |δ_J| = π/2, and a CPN structure with an antilinear character. RR̃ is CP-odd. The proximity is real and, per the archive sweeps, has never been examined in this project.

Two cautions, both of which cut against a warm reading:

1. The Dvali–Funcke mechanism **screens** ⟨RR̃⟩ to zero in vacuum — that is its entire point (their §V: "preventing the manifestation of CP violation by the gravitational vacuum"). Anything NTE wanted to carry on that density in the vacuum is driven to zero by the mechanism it would be borrowing.
2. This is a *register* proximity — both involve a CP-odd gravitational topological density — not a shared structure. Asserting a link would be the failure mode the Manifest records at the 2026-09-18 declined Companion-15 cross-reference, and the same one that produced the retired six-note chord framing.

Recommendation: a one-line entry in the positioning/related-work register, at proximity tier, no more.
