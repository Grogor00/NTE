## 3. The obstruction, stated precisely (why O1 was W-2)

A generic two-metric gravity (two Einstein–Hilbert terms + arbitrary interaction) carries a **Boulware–Deser ghost** — a 6th, negative-energy scalar in the massive sector. Hassan–Rosen is the unique interaction potential that removes it. **But coupling *matter* to both metrics can bring it back.** arXiv:1408.0487, verbatim conclusion:

> "the Boulware–Deser ghost generally revives in the presence of doubly coupled matter and … ghost freeness strongly restricts the model of kinetically doubly coupled matter."

And its exception (§IV): ghost-free coupling is permitted when matter is **kinetically coupled to one metric only**, the second metric entering **only through a non-derivative potential term** — allowed form
$$\mathcal{L}_m = \sqrt{-g}\,P(X,\phi) + \sqrt{-f}\,\tilde P(\phi),$$
kinetic terms on g, the second metric f appearing only through a potential $\tilde P(\phi)$ independent of derivatives. Dual-*kinetic* coupling fails because "the conjugate momentum of the matter field depends on both lapse functions … nonlinear in two lapse functions," defeating the shift redefinition that removes the ghost.

**NTE's Z₂-even neutrino is the doubly-present matter.** So O1's sharp question is exactly: **is the deposited ν coupling K-single (safe) or K-dual (ghost)?**
