## §1. Greg’s editorial law (summary)

1. **Standalone papers must stand alone** — readable without prior corpus; standard physics vocabulary where possible.
2. **Strip internal jargon and unexplained TLAs** — Companions **and** standalones; a motivated mathematician (and ideally a careful non-specialist) should be able to **follow the argument**, not decode acronyms.
3. **Publication tiering (new Greg bands):**
   - **Tier 1 (outward-facing):** Fully realisable from **standard physics**; **no** reliance on Janus architecture, twin-universe / mirror-cosmology framing, or other mainstream-challenging ontology as load-bearing premises.
   - **Tier 2:** More progressive NTE structure (spectral triple programme, bulk–boundary crystallisation, etc.) — still disciplined prose.
   - **Tier 3:** Full corpus ontology where needed (master-consistent).
4. **Explicit allowance (Tier 1):** **Chirality superposition** as the mechanism explaining Majorana neutrinos — framed with **accepted** quantum-field ideas (not as corpus axiom dump).
5. **Naming:** Internal codes like **“E1”** are **not** publication names. File names and register rows use **descriptive titles** (AC + Cursor liaison).

## §4. Tier 1 prose rules (checklist per paper)

| Rule | Example violation → fix |
|------|-------------------------|
| **No unexplained TLA** | “CPN”, “LB-6”, “Tier 2g” → spell out once, then short form |
| **No Janus / twin universe in Tier 1 body** | Kappa closure → “geometric resummation of cross-sector coupling” (Tier 2 paper) or separate Tier 2 appendix |
| **σ modes explained in words** | “the neutrino mass matrix can be organised in three polarisation directions (σ₁, σ₂, σ₃); we identify …” |
| **Chirality superposition allowed** | Companion 7a mechanism: plain-language Majorana bridge + accepted QFT |
| **One “reader map” paragraph** | §0 or §1: what is assumed, what is derived, what is compared to PDG/cosmology |
| **No “the corpus”** | “prior NTE technical work” or citation to companion — outward papers don’t self-reference as closed club |
| **Math before ontology** | Derive identity (F₁), then optional one-sentence motivation |

## §5. Companion papers — expository pass (parallel track)

Companions are **not** all Tier 1, but Greg requires the same **clarity discipline**:

- **Lead paragraph per section:** what question this section answers.
- **Glossary sidebar** (or §0): κ₀, M₁, M_R, β_NTE-chain caveat — in English.
- **Janus / Pathway α** confined to sections explicitly tagged **corpus architecture**; empirical predictions stated in laboratory/cosmology language first.

**PC** leads accessible framing on Popular + book; **AC** owns companion expository audit queue; **Cursor** applies OLD/NEW patch blocks when AC ships instructions.
