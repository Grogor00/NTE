## Point

Inflation’s hard empirical job is a **passive, nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic** curvature spectrum with **phase-coherent** acoustic oscillations. Active, causal sources (topological defects, textures, other “seeds”) generically produce **incoherent** contributions and wrong peak-structure / isocurvature signatures. Once precision CMB data required coherent acoustic peaks, defect-dominated / pure causal-seed scenarios were ruled out as the primary structure-formation engine (1990s literature → WMAP confirmation).

## Implication for NTE

Any no-inflaton NTE story that only “puts fluctuations on the S³” without a frozen super-horizon ζ with coherent phases is structurally in the causal-seed failure class. The corpus currently has no closed transfer from boundary/Dirac/gauge-birth language to that ζ.
