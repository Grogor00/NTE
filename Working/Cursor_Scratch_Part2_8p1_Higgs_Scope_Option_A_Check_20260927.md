## Deposit under test (verbatim structure)

**Part 2 §8.1 (v10.19.0):**

1. A gauge connection \(A_\mu(x,t)\) is a 1-form; its index runs over the time coordinate.  
2. The local gauge principle presupposes a 4-manifold with a timelike direction.  
3. Pre-temporally the universe is parameterised by \((N,x)\), not \((t,x)\): no time coordinate → fibre bundle lacks its full base → **no gauge symmetry can be formulated**.  
4. *Thus* every SM gauge boson — photon, gluons, W/Z — **and the Higgs** come into existence only with \(t\).

## Verdict: over-extension (not entailed)

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| No gauge *connection* / no formulable local gauge symmetry pre-\(t\) | **Entailed** by (1)–(3) as written |
| SM gauge bosons (photon, gluons, W/Z) exist only with \(t\) | **Entailed** (same: they *are* the connection degrees of freedom) |
| *"and the Higgs"* exists only with \(t\) | **Over-extension** — not entailed by the 1-form / timelike-index argument |

**Why.** The SM Higgs is a scalar multiplet \(H(x)\), not a connection 1-form. It does not carry a spacetime index that must include a timelike slot for the field to be defined. Premises (1)–(3) license a conclusion about *gauge connections and gauge symmetry*, not about every field that later participates in the SM. A scalar can be defined on a base without a Lorentzian timelike direction (the corpus already treats \(\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}\) as a scalar on the \(S^3\) boundary parameterised by \(N\) alone — Part 1 Abstract / §2.3). Saying the Higgs "needs no timelike index" is the correct scope remark for *existence of the field*, not a claim that Yukawa / VEV / EWSB are pre-temporal.

**What would entail Higgs-with-\(t\)?** A *different* argument — e.g. that a gauge *representation* cannot exist without a formulated gauge group, or that the Higgs potential / covariant derivative cannot be written without \(A_\mu\). §8.1 does not make that argument. Even those stronger routes are ambiguous: representation labels and a scalar kinetic term can be posed on a Euclidean or \((N,x)\) section without identifying the field's birth with the birth of \(A_\mu\). The deposit leaps from "no gauge symmetry" to "no Higgs" without intermediate premises.

**Option (a) honest?** **Yes** — as repair of over-generalisation. Narrowing §8.1 to gauge fields / gauge bosons restores the *Thus*-clause to what the stated argument supports. AC's lean ("honest *if* Higgs is genuinely outside §8.1's argument") is confirmed on this check: Higgs is outside the argument as written.

## (1) Is (a) an honest repair?

**Yes.** Cheapest wording fix; does not invent new physics; matches the logical scope of the 1-form argument. Honesty caveat (not a veto): allowing a pre-\(t\) Higgs *field* then forces a re-read of mass-acquisition / locking prose (§8.2–8.3, §VII) so that "Higgs exists" is not silently equated with "mass, Yukawa, and EWSB already on." That is a **downstream ontology cost**, not a reason to keep the over-extension.
