> At gauge birth the electroweak vacuum selects a direction \(e_1 \in \mathrm{Im}(\mathbb{O})\) whose \(G_2\)-stabiliser is \(SU(3)\) … while the quaternionic \(S^3 \cong SU(2) \subset S^7\) gives \(SU(2)_L\) and the complex line gives \(U(1)_Y\).

Field existence without SM quantum numbers is **not** the Higgs.
