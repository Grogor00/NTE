## Finding 1 — §2 retains the neutrino the paper's own record excludes. Claim; defective when written

§2.1: "The Z₂-even unresolved neutrino is bound to both CPN orientations; its stress-energy sources both Einstein equations." §8 item 1: "Retain … one-manifold bilateral ontology." §10 lists κ₀ as deposited "bilateral geometry".

Against that, from the corpus:
- **N_eff.** Scratch, 2026-10-08, read in full: a symmetric doubly coupled Z₂-even neutrino gives our orientation N_eff ≈ 1.52 — about 10σ below ACT's 2.86 ± 0.13 — "excluded, parameter-free, and independent of the ghost question." Companion 22 never mentions it. Its only N_eff sentence (§2.3) is about equal-temperature thermal contact, a different count.
- **The ghost.** Companion 22's own §7.3 reports, from the 2026-10-11 S1b round: "JP3-Majorana ΔS^(JP3) ≉ 0 at O(ψ²) **proven (D2)** — kinetic JP3 secondary earn **killed**." JP3 is defined in §7.7 as double coupling of the bridge sector (the shared Majorana neutrino). D2 is "the secondary constraint is lost; the ghost returns."

So, as the paper itself reports it, the doubly coupled neutrino of §2.1 is excluded on N_eff and carries a ghost. §2 presents it as retained structure; §7.3 records its failure in a sub-bullet under "Sol-A next S1b+THR2". A reader of §2 and §8 would not learn either fact.

This is the most important finding. It is not a wording issue: it is whether NTE's central dark-sector object is consistent and allowed. On the paper's own reporting the answer is no on both counts, and the deposit should say so where the object is introduced. (I have not read the S1b round; if the D2 result there is narrower than §7.3 states, §7.3 overstates it, which is also a defect.)

## Finding 2 — forced Majorana still rests on the withdrawn series. Claim; stale since 2026-10-07

§2.1: "Convergence κ₀ < 1 requires exactly three modes — forcing Majorana (Dirac would give κ₀ = 6/√(4π) > 1)." §11 falsifier: "firm Dirac establishment falsifies the **κ₀ < 1 bridge mechanism**."

κ₀ < 1 is the convergence condition of the geometric series κ₀/(1 − κ₀). §2.2, three paragraphs later, withdraws that series: "no deposited action; not a framework quantity." Without the series, κ₀ = 6/√(4π) is just a number larger than one; nothing diverges. The argument in §2.1 uses what §2.2 withdraws.

I flagged this dependency to AC on 2026-10-07. Forced Majorana is the corpus's named discriminating particle prediction (register A in §1). It has a separate ground in Companion 7 §7 (the unresolved ontology implies Majorana). Companion 22 should cite that ground and drop the convergence one, and the §11 falsifier should be restated against the ontology, not the "κ₀ < 1 bridge mechanism".
