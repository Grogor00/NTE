## 1. Step-by-step audit against C16's own status language

**Step 1 — P (no time-ordering) ⇒ non-associative algebra. CONCEDED ADOPTION, and a converse.**
C16 §1 verbatim: *"The converse — that non-associativity corresponds to the absence of fixed causal/temporal ordering — is Gogberashvili (2004)… NTE adopts that reading; it does not claim it."* Two problems:
- It is **adopted prior art, explicitly not claimed** — by my decision rule, not a derivation.
- As stated it is the **converse** of "time-ordering ⇒ associative": asserting ¬(ordering) ⇒ ¬(associative) does not follow from ordering ⇒ associative without a separate argument, which the deposit does not give.
→ **Step 1 is not a derivation.** (This is exactly the thread Jammer's Task B flagged as a possible axiom-free assertion; it is one.)

**Step 2 — non-associative + normed division algebra ⇒ 𝕆 (Hurwitz). THEOREM, but on a premise as strong as M1.**
Hurwitz is a genuine theorem. But it fires only given C16 §1 premise (ii): the algebra is **normed**, *"inheriting a norm from the S³ geometry and Hilbert-space structure,"* and is a **division** algebra. "Normed division algebra" already presupposes a norm/Hilbert structure — i.e. most of what M1 asserts. And "division algebra" (every nonzero element invertible) is a strong extra assumption P does not motivate. → **Step 2 is W-2:** a theorem whose premises are as strong as the conclusion. Nothing non-quantum is doing the work.

**Step 3 — descent 𝕆→ℍ→ℂ ⇒ S³ ⊂ ℂ². CONCEDED NON-DERIVATION.**
C16 §2 verbatim: *"This epoch mapping is a **framework-specific structural correspondence, not a dynamical derivation** of transition temperatures."* The descent's stopping point (ℂ², giving the complex Hopf S¹→S³→S², the Born structure) is selected by the **post-EWSB epoch**, not by a necessity — and even that mapping is conceded non-derivational. → **Step 3 is not a derivation.** This is the point AC and Jammer flagged ("where a choice hides as a consequence"); C16 states plainly it is a correspondence.

## 2. The qubit gate — unmet
M1's content is *"the state space of a spin-½ system is S³ ⊂ ℂ²."* The chain delivers S³ as **unit-ℍ**, the quaternionic subalgebra surviving 𝕆→ℍ, whose SU(2) is **SU(2)_L** (C16 §3.3: *"S³ is forced… the unit sphere of the quaternionic subalgebra ℍ ⊂ 𝕆… whose SU(2) factor is SU(2)_L"*). That is S³ as a **gauge manifold**, not S³ as the **state space of the basic quantum system**. Identifying the two is a further posit. Moreover the *complex* content of M1 (ℂ², the Born/Hopf structure) comes from the separate ℂ step, not the ℍ step — so "S³ ⊂ ℂ²" requires both, and their joint selection is the conceded-correspondence epoch map. And Jammer's structural point stands: *"the state space is S³"* holds only for a **single qubit** (spin-j → S^{4j+1}); the descent gives no reason the basic system is a qubit. → **The chain does not reach M1's actual object even if Steps 1–3 were granted.**
