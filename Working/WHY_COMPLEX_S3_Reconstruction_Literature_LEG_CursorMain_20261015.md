## Headline (for the directive)

**Yes — published reconstructions derive complex Hilbert-space QM from operational / GPT-style axioms** (Hardy; CDP; Masanes–Müller).  
**Those axioms are themselves quantum-*like* / information-theoretic** (probabilities, reversible continuous transformations, purification, tomographic locality, etc.) — **not** NTE’s pre-temporal non-associativity / octonion descent.  
**They do not hand NTE a free derivation of M1 from the deposit’s A9.** They show that *if* one accepts a package of operational postulates, complex QM follows. **Complex amplitudes are outputs of those programmes, not inputs — but the programmes’ inputs are not NTE’s.**

Scratch’s prior (operational axioms → complex-over-real/quaternionic) is **broadly confirmed**: the reconstruction literature derives the complex structure from **operational** premises, not from a non-quantum geometric ontology of the NTE type.

## 1. Hardy — *Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms* (arXiv:quant-ph/0101012)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Assumes** | Operational framework: systems, states as probability lists, measurements, composition; finite/countable distinguishable outcomes; “simplicity” (smallest K compatible with other axioms). |
| **Derives** | With continuous reversible transformations between pure states → K = N² (vs classical K = N) → finite-dimensional **complex** quantum theory (density matrices, Born trace rule). Drop “continuous” → classical probability. |
| **Complex amplitudes?** | **Derived** (as part of recovering QT), not postulated as Hilbert-space axioms. |
| **Caveat** | “Simplicity” axiom criticised later (leaves room for infinite ladder of theories); later reconstructions replace it. |
| **NTE relevance** | Does **not** start from octonions, non-associativity, or physical S³. |

## 2. Chiribella–D’Ariano–Perinotti — *Informational derivation of quantum theory* (Phys. Rev. A 84, 012311, 2011; arXiv:1011.6451)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Assumes** | Five informational axioms (causality, perfect distinguishability, ideal compression, local distinguishability, pure conditioning) + **purification** postulate; operational circuit / GPT setting. |
| **Derives** | Finite-dimensional quantum theory including qubit Bloch ball, spectral theorem analogue, superposition principle — i.e. the **complex** Hilbert-space package — **without** assuming Hilbert space at the start. |
| **Complex amplitudes?** | **Derived** as the theory singled out by purification within the standard informational class. |
| **NTE relevance** | Inputs are informational, not geometric-cosmological. No bridge to A9. |

## 3. Masanes–Müller — *A derivation of quantum theory from physical requirements* (New J. Phys. 13, 063001, 2011; arXiv:1004.1483)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Assumes** | GPT / convex state spaces; finite capacity; local tomography; subspace equivalence; continuous reversible transformations between pure states; all measurements allowed (no-restriction-type). |
| **Derives** | Full finite-dimensional QT: density matrices on **complex** Hilbert space; unitary reversible dynamics; Bloch ball for the qubit (group-theoretic 3D explanation). |
| **Complex amplitudes?** | **Derived** (QT as the unique theory satisfying the requirements vs classical). |
| **NTE relevance** | Jammer already flags adopt-and-credit as external route for register reformulation — **correct**: this is the published route that *does* derive complex structure, from **operational** requirements, not from NTE’s deposit. |

## 4. Quaternionic / hypercomplex exclusion results

| Work | What it shows | What it does **not** show |
|---|---|---|
| **Adler** (quaternionic QM book / scattering results; e.g. Phys. Rev. D 37, 3654) | In standard quaternionic QM, asymptotic **S-matrix is complex** (no j,k dependence for suitable rays); far-zone looks complex. | Does not derive “ℂ from nothing”; shows quaternionic theories often **reduce asymptotically to complex**. |
| **Peres** (Phys. Rev. Lett. 42, 683, 1979) and later optical tests | Proposed experimental distinctions; Adler/later analyses: radiation-zone tests often **cannot** see hypercomplex effects. | Not a derivation of complex QM from non-quantum premises. |
| **Stueckelberg / real QM** | Real Hilbert space possible under extra structure; typically needs restrictions. | Real ≠ NTE’s target; doesn’t give octonion→ℂ². |

**For M1:** Quaternionic-exclusion literature **constrains alternatives** inside already-quantum Hilbert-space generalisations; it is **not** a derivation of complex amplitudes from pre-quantum geometry.

## 5. Answer to AC’s outside question

> *If complex amplitudes are routinely an axiom rather than a result, say so.*

**Nuanced:**
- **In textbook QM:** complex Hilbert space is an **axiom / postulate**.
- **In the reconstruction literature named above:** complex structure is a **result** of operational axioms.
- **For NTE’s question (can M1 be derived from the deposit / A9?):** the reconstruction literature **does not supply that derivation**. It supplies an **external** adopt-and-credit path (Masanes–Müller / CDP / Hardy class) whose premises are **not** deposited in NTE. That path supports **register = reformulation + credited reconstruction**, not “NTE derives M1 from octonions.”

**Scratch deferred “in principle” gap:** confirmed — reconstruction programmes derive complex-over-alternatives from **operational (quantum-like)** axioms, not from non-quantum geometric premises of the NTE type.
