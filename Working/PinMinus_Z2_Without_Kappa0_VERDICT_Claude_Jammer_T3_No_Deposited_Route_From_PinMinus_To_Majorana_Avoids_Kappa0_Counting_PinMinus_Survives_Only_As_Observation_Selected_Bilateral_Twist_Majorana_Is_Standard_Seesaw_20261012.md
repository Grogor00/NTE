1. **A selection, not a derivation.** Part 1 §VI selects the twisted structure because it "produces baryogenesis and **matches observation**". That is selection by data. **Part 3 nonetheless lists "CPN selection (Pin⁻ on the ℤ₂ orbifold)" in its *derived* tier**, which is a register mismatch.

3. **The orbifold premise is itself open.** C21 records that whether the Janus ℤ₂ is a gauged orbifold or acts freely is unsettled.
4. **A wording defect, flagged but not ruled on.** "Exactly two spin structures; the twisted one (Pin⁻)" mixes two different notions. As I understand standard topology, an orientation-reversing ℤ₂ quotient needs **pin** structures. Pin⁺ and Pin⁻ are the two **types** of pin structure, and how many of a given type exist is a cohomology count, not "exactly two". *I did not source this today; I am flagging it for whoever drafts, not making a ruling.*
