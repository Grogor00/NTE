## G-1 — Existence (lead gate)

### Recollapse under hot bath (independent check)

Companion 9 §1: at the equator the Euclidean geometry continues to Lorentzian; the no-boundary handover has **a′ = 0** (turning point). Closed FRW (k=+1), reduced Planck mass M:

\[
H^2 = \frac{\rho}{3M^2} - \frac{1}{a^2},\qquad
\frac{\ddot a}{a} = -\frac{\rho+3p}{6M^2}.
\]

At H=0: \(a_b^2 = 3M^2/\rho\). Radiation / hot gauge bath: \(p=\rho/3\) ⇒ **ρ+3p = 2ρ > 0** ⇒ **ä < 0**. Birth is at **maximum** radius; the closed S³ recollapses on a timescale ∼ a_b ∼ O(0.1–1) t_Pl (banked G4 figure ∼0.073 t_Pl is the same order).  

**Verified:** a hot-bath HH handover does not yield an expanding classical universe. An accelerated content with **ρ+3p < 0 at a′=0** is required for existence.

### Higgs-plateau birth

Proposal (imported, credited): add ξ_H \|H\|² R with ξ_H ≫ 1. At large field the Einstein-frame potential plateaus,

\[
V_E \simeq \frac{\lambda M^4}{4\xi_H^2},\qquad w\simeq -1,\qquad \rho+3p = -2\rho < 0.
\]

At the HH equator with plateau vacuum energy: **ä > 0** — the universe **accelerates out** of the turning point. Birth radius is the inflationary horizon \(a \sim 1/H_{\mathrm{inf}}\), not the Planck-density hot-bath scale.

With H_inf ≈ 1.5×10¹³ GeV (from G-4 amplitude, below):  
\(a_{\mathrm{birth}} \sim M/H_{\mathrm{inf}} \sim 1.6\times 10^5\,\ell_{\mathrm{Pl,red}}\) — vastly larger than the hot-bath recollapse radius, and expanding.

**G-1 result: PASS. D1 does not fire.**

Ordering that closes existence: Higgs-plateau no-boundary cap → Lorentzian slow-roll (ρ+3p<0) → end of inflation → Higgs→SM reheating → hot Big Bang. The T_GB plasma of Part 2 §8.2–8.3 cannot sit at the a′=0 handover (it recollapses). That narrative rework is a **priced cost**, not a patch in this filing.
