## §1. Canonical identity (one sentence)

**Non-Temporal Emergence (NTE) is the Null-Time Extension to the Standard Model.**

- **NTE** = acronym; always expand once per document as **Non-Temporal Emergence**.
- **Null-Time Extension** = what NTE *is* in relation to the SM (outward-facing role phrase). **Not** a second expansion of the letters N-T-E.

---

## §2. Why both phrases (not arbitrary)

| Phrase | Emphasis |
|--------|----------|
| **Non-Temporal Emergence** | Fundamental ontology — pre-temporal geometry, emergence of proper time, gauge birth, e-fold substrate |
| **Null-Time Extension to the SM** | Outward positioning — geometric extension beneath/around Standard Model physics; null-time entities (photon, gluon); $|\Phi_{\mathrm{NTE}}|$ distinct from Higgs |

Same framework; **Emergence** names the deep structure; **Extension** names the relationship to the SM.
