- **Mandatory caveat to record in verdict:** 1+1d specialises \(S\) (scalar vs matrix). Note how that affects transfer to 3+1d. Also note 2D Einstein–Hilbert bulk topology and what that does to gravitational kinetic structure.

**Step 1 — S1a (modified SVC).**
