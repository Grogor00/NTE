## 3. Greg's attachment (stable keV sterile neutrinos): assessed

**What it gets right:**
- A sterile neutrino with exactly zero active mixing does not decay radiatively, so it evades X-ray searches.
- If it is not made by oscillation (Dodelson–Widrow), something else must make it.
- Production by singlet-scalar decay gives a colder spectrum than oscillation production (Petraki–Kusenko, as cited; not fetched by me).

**Where it fails on its own terms:**
- **It contradicts itself.** Perfect stability needs mixing θ = 0, but its proposed laboratory test (a kink in the tritium β spectrum at KATRIN/TRISTAN) **requires** non-zero mixing. A θ = 0 state is not emitted in β decay.
- **θ = 0 is not free.** It needs a symmetry forbidding the state's Yukawa coupling (in practice a ℤ₂), and that state then plays **no part in the seesaw**.
- **Mass and abundance stay dials.** With scalar-decay production the abundance is set by the scalar's couplings. "Perfectly balances the scales" is advocacy.
- **Sources** include a secondary-school journal and video.

**Within NTE, each production route the attachment names fails or needs new content:**
- **Singlet-scalar decay.** Φ_NTE cannot be the scalar: under Door B it is an identity relabelling H (Part 1 §2.3), with no Yukawa couplings. The nearest structural parallel, the spectral-action σ field that sets the right-handed Majorana mass in the Connes model, is **explicitly not inherited** (C5 §3; C8 §8). A scalar would be new, adopted content, and its couplings are dials.
- **Twin / mirror sector.** This is NTE's own architecture, and it falls under D1: at CPN-enforced T′ = T, twin sterile states are thermal and the twin's radiation alone breaks N_eff. **Excluded.**

**A deposit defect this exposes (for Stage 1 / the closure record):**

## 4. The starting candidate: Boyle–Finn–Turok (BFT), and why it suits an NTE rebuild

**What BFT is** (arXiv:1803.08930, fetched):
- The universe before the bang is the CPT image of the universe after it.
- The Standard Model is extended by three right-handed neutrinos.
- A **ℤ₂ acting on one right-handed neutrino only** (ν_R,1 → −ν_R,1, implemented as a **vanishing first row of the Yukawa matrix**) makes it stable. It "only interacts with gravity".
- It is produced **gravitationally**: the CPT-invariant vacuum differs from late observers' "out" vacuum by a Bogoliubov offset, so the stable neutrino has a non-zero abundance.
- Its mass is set **by matching the observed dark-matter density: ≈ 4.8 × 10⁸ GeV.**
- The other two right-handed neutrinos decay and drive standard thermal leptogenesis.
- The background is radiation-dominated, a ∝ τ, continued analytically through τ = 0. **No inflation.**

**BFT's predictions:**
1. The light neutrinos are Majorana.
2. **One light neutrino is exactly massless.**
3. No primordial long-wavelength gravitational waves.

**BFT's assumptions:**
- the vacuum is chosen by minimising particle and energy density;
- a non-minimal ξRh†h term is present;
- Weyl/dilaton unit choice;
- backreaction deferred.

**Why it is the right place to start:**
- **Architecture.** Part 2 §IX already names BFT as NTE's closest predecessor: a universe–antiuniverse pair from the bang, the asymmetry sorted rather than annihilated, a ℤ₂ on the neutrino sector stabilising dark matter. The rebuild would be **crediting the model NTE was always nearest to**.
- **It needs nothing NTE lacks.** Three right-handed Majorana neutrinos are already deposited (C10, C13). Leptogenesis by the unstable ones is already NTE's account (Part 2 §X; C10 §5).
- **It is co-located, adiabatic matter**, so it clears D1's location-blindness failure (the Bullet Cluster class) by construction.
- **It is falsifiable on a near-term channel:** one massless light neutrino, hence the minimal normal-ordering mass sum. *(That cosmological Σm_ν bounds are approaching that minimum is my recollection; not checked today.)*

**Where it collides with what NTE kept: the forks the rebuild must face, stated plainly.**
- **(i) The mirror is a different symmetry.** BFT's pair is related by **genuine time reversal across τ = 0**. NTE's mirror is the CPN orientation with a **common proper-time arrow** (Part 2 §IX axis (i); C5 §5). The closure record v2 §5 also records that CPN, as deposited (z → −z), is a Hopf fibre half-turn containing no C, P or N. **Adopting BFT's production means adopting BFT's CPT pair, not NTE's CPN pair.**
- **(ii) A different beginning.**
  - BFT runs a radiation bang through a = 0 with no inflation.
  - NTE's origin story is a Hartle–Hawking no-boundary handover at ȧ = 0, which **is exactly the obstruction that blocks NTE's existence** (closure record §2.2: ρ + 3p > 0 at the handover recollapses).
  - **So BFT is attractive partly because it replaces the part of NTE that failed.** That is a real gain, but it should be named as a replacement, not presented as a confirmation.
  - The cost: BFT inherits the horizon and flatness questions that inflation normally answers. Their own treatment of those is not assessed here.
- **(iii) The ℤ₂ is new content.** NTE's own ℤ₂ (Janus/CPN) acts between orientations, not on one neutrino flavour. BFT's ℤ₂ is a separate, flavour-specific symmetry. **It enters as adopted and credited.**
- **(iv) The mass.**
  - BFT's 4.8 × 10⁸ GeV is fixed by matching Ω_DM, i.e. **fitted inside BFT**.
  - NTE fixes its heavy masses geometrically: M₁ = (8π)^{1/3}(M_Pl²H_GB)^{1/3} ≈ 5.4 × 10⁹ GeV and M₂ ≈ M₁(1 + sin²θ₁₃) (C10, flux-quantisation register with an O(1) residue; M_R ≈ 8.2 × 10¹⁴ GeV is a pathway only).
  - The factor of about 11 between them is **exactly the kind of near-match that misled this corpus before** (5.50 against 5.38). **It is recorded as a coincidence, not a lead.**
- **(v) The bookkeeping.**
  - Zeroing one row of the Yukawa matrix removes one heavy state from the seesaw. If it is **N₁**, leptogenesis loses its parent (C10). If it is **N₂**, C10's σ₁/σ₂ pairing and C13's leg structure need rewriting.
  - Either way the lightest active neutrino becomes exactly massless. C13 §5 already treats m_ν₁ ≪ m_ν₂ and makes no claim about Y_ν₁, so **this is compatible**, but the mode assignment must be redone, not assumed.

## 6. The one computation that would make candidate 1 NTE's rather than BFT's (pre-registered)

**Question:** with NTE's own geometrically fixed heavy mass in place of BFT's fitted one, does BFT-style gravitational production give the observed abundance?
- **This is the only route here that turns a fit into a test.** NTE supplies the mass without looking at Ω_DM, BFT supplies the production, and the abundance comes out.
- NTE also already carries a Janus-boundary Bogoliubov treatment of heavy Majorana modes (Part 2 §X, for |δ_J|). **Whether that machinery is the same as BFT's in/out-vacuum offset is itself unknown, and must not be assumed.**

**Forks, declared before any reading of BFT's abundance formula:**
- **A-1:** with M = NTE's M₁ or M₂, the abundance lands within the O(1) residue NTE already carries on M₁ (factor ≈ 1.1). **A genuine, unfitted success.**
- **A-2:** it lands within an order of magnitude but outside the residue. **Consistent, not a success.** Record it and claim nothing.
- **A-3:** it misses by more than an order of magnitude. **Candidate 1 stays adopted-and-credited with BFT's own mass, and NTE's heavy-mass geometry is not the dark-matter mass.** This is a clean negative and goes in the "what doesn't work" list.
- **A-4:** it cannot be computed without importing BFT's background (a ∝ τ through τ = 0) wholesale. **Then the question is really fork (ii) above, the beginning, and Greg rules it first.**

**My prior: A-3 or A-4.**
- If BFT's abundance scales steeply with mass, a factor ≈ 11 in mass moves the abundance a long way.
- The production depends on their bang geometry, which NTE does not share.

**Kill rules:**
- No choice of which N is stable may be made *after* seeing which one fits.
- No O(1) "gauge-birth correction" may be invoked to close a gap that the existing residue does not already cover.
- If the abundance needs any tuned input, it is A-3.
