## Abstract

We report a structural relation between two charged-lepton-flavour-violation (CLFV) amplitudes in a type-I seesaw whose heavy sector contains a pair of quasi-degenerate Majorana states related by a discrete Z₂ exchange symmetry. The Casas–Ibarra orthogonal matrix R — generically a free element of O(2, $\mathbb{C}$) in the relevant block, the reason CLFV rates in seesaw models are usually unconstrained — is **frozen to real O(2)** by the combination of exact degeneracy and reality of the Z₂ swap. The freezing fixes the ratio of squared CLFV amplitudes to the PMNS value

$$\frac{|A(\mu \to e\gamma)|^2}{|A(\tau \to e\gamma)|^2} = \tan^2\theta_{23}.$$

For maximal atmospheric mixing the ratio is unity. Six qualifiers bound it: single relation; conditional on CLFV being observed; amplitude-level (not full branching ratio); preferred over an anarchic seesaw at ~9:1; unobservable at the framework's heavy scale (BR ~ 4×10⁻⁵⁹ vs the MEG II bound ~10⁻¹³); ±2–3% intrinsic width. The maximal angle θ₂₃ = π/4 itself is long established (Lam 2001; Harrison–Perkins–Scott 2002; Mohapatra–Nishiura 2001) and is inherited, not claimed. CLFV ratios as flavour-model discriminators are likewise an established subject (see §1); the contribution here is the *clean amplitude-level* relation that follows specifically from R-matrix freezing.

## 1. Introduction

Maximal atmospheric mixing, θ₂₃ = π/4, follows from a discrete 2–3 (μ–τ) exchange symmetry on the neutrino sector — established work (Lam 2001; Harrison–Perkins–Scott 2002; Mohapatra–Nishiura 2001; Grimus–Lavoura; reviewed in King–Luhn 2013), claimed here only as inherited.

**Prior art on CLFV ratios.** Using ratios of charged-lepton-flavour-violating rates as discriminators between flavour models is also well established. Mohapatra and collaborators (e.g. hep-ph/0211068) studied B(μ→eγ)/B(τ→μγ) as a probe of seesaw structure; μ–τ-symmetric and scaling-ansatz models give characteristic LFV relations (Joshipura–Rindani and others); and CLFV in SUSY seesaw / SO(10) has an extensive literature in which branching-ratio patterns track the neutrino mixing parameters. The present note sits inside that field. What it adds is **not** the idea that a CLFV ratio tracks a mixing angle — that is prior art — but a specific, clean, *amplitude-level* relation, |A(μ→eγ)|²/|A(τ→eγ)|² = tan²θ₂₃, that follows when a discrete symmetry **freezes the Casas–Ibarra R-matrix** and removes the model-dependence that normally makes such ratios free.

The mechanism: if the heavy Majorana sector contains a pair of states that are exactly degenerate and related by a real Z₂ exchange — the same Z₂ that delivers θ₂₃ = π/4 — then R in that block is frozen from O(2, ℂ) to real O(2). With R real, the flavour combinations entering μ→eγ and τ→eγ are fixed by the light masses and the PMNS matrix alone, and their squared-amplitude ratio collapses to tan²θ₂₃. The result is conditional and tightly qualified (§5): a ratio, not a rate; amplitude-level; and at the framework's heavy scale it predicts CLFV far below any foreseeable experiment. It is a discriminator that fires *only if* CLFV is observed.

## 2. Setup: a Z₂-symmetric pair of heavy Majorana states

Type-I seesaw, $m_\nu = -Y_\nu^\top M_R^{-1} Y_\nu v^2$. A pair of heavy states (1,2) carries a discrete exchange symmetry Z₂ — a real swap $N_1 \leftrightarrow N_2$ — enforcing **degeneracy** $M_1 = M_2 \equiv M$ and **reality** of the involution. In the mixing sector this is the 2–3 exchange whose atmospheric-angle consequence is recorded in §3; at that level it carries the same content as the established μ–τ symmetry.

## 3. Inherited consequence: θ₂₃ = π/4

A 2–3 exchange forces θ₂₃ = π/4 and a CP-conserving Dirac phase in the exact-symmetry limit (Lam 2001; HPS 2002; Grimus–Lavoura) — standard, not claimed. Current data: NuFIT 6.0 (Esteban et al. 2024) and recent long-baseline (T2K 2025; NOvA) favour the upper octant, sin²θ₂₃ ≈ 0.55, within a few degrees of π/4, compatible with subleading Z₂ breaking correlated with θ₁₃ ≈ 8.6°. A precise quantification against the global fit is deferred to a data-sensitivity refresh; DUNE/Hyper-K will sharpen. Inherited backdrop, not the load-bearing content.

## 4. The CLFV amplitude ratio from R-matrix freezing

**4.1 Casas–Ibarra freedom.** $Y_\nu = \tfrac1v \sqrt{\hat M_R}\, R\, \sqrt{\hat m}\, U^\dagger$, $R \in O(n,\mathbb{C})$, undetermined by oscillation data. CLFV amplitudes obey $A(\ell_i\to\ell_j\gamma) \propto (Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{ji}$ (Hisano–Moroi–Tobe–Yamaguchi 1996; Casas–Ibarra 2001), and $Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu = \tfrac1{v^2} U\sqrt{\hat m}\,R^\dagger \hat M_R R\,\sqrt{\hat m}\,U^\dagger$ depends on R — so the ratio is generically free.

**4.2 Freezing.** Degeneracy makes $\hat M_R|_{(1,2)} = M\mathbb{1}$, leaving the complex (boost-like) part of $R\in O(2,\mathbb{C})$ as the only freedom; the real Z₂ swap forbids it (a complex $O(2,\mathbb{C})$ rotation between degenerate, real-exchange-related states is not invariant unless real). Hence $R|_{(1,2)} \in O(2,\mathbb{R})$, and $(Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{e\mu}$, $(Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{e\tau}$ are fixed by $\hat m$ and $U$ alone.

**4.3 The ratio.** With the common factors cancelling between channels,

$$\frac{|A(\mu \to e\gamma)|^2}{|A(\tau \to e\gamma)|^2} = \frac{|(Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{e\mu}|^2}{|(Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{e\tau}|^2} = \tan^2\theta_{23},$$

unity at θ₂₃ = π/4. The collapse to tan²θ₂₃ is the real-R evaluation, confirmed by direct numerical computation over the frozen parametrisation [companion numerics]; the explicit channel-by-channel evaluation is carried there. *(Pre-send: this explicit evaluation is to be imported into §4.3 verbatim from the numerics, and the R-freezing → tan²θ₂₃ chain confirmed by independent cross-check, before external dissemination.)*

## 5. Honest scope: the six qualifiers

1. **Single relation** — one ratio, not a full flavour prediction.
2. **Conditional on observation** — testable only if CLFV is detected; it does not predict that it will be (see 5).
3. **Amplitude level** — full branching ratios carry lepton-mass-power factors that distort the simple ratio.
4. **≈ 9:1 vs anarchy** — a Monte Carlo over random Casas–Ibarra matrices reproduces a ratio this close once in ~ten; substantial, not overwhelming.
5. **Unobservable at the preferred scale** — at $M_R \approx 8.2\times10^{14}$ GeV, BR(μ→eγ) ~ 4×10⁻⁵⁹, far below MEG II (~10⁻¹³). A conditional discriminator, not a near-term forecast.
6. **±2–3% width** — subleading degeneracy breaking gives the ratio a few-percent intrinsic width.

---

## 6. Falsifiers and forward observables

1. **The CLFV ratio (conditional).** If CLFV is ever resolved in both channels and the measured ratio departs significantly from tan²θ₂₃ (at the measured θ₂₃), the Z₂-degeneracy mechanism behind *both* θ₂₃ = π/4 and the ratio fails — gated by qualifier 5.
2. **Octant/breaking.** Precision θ₂₃ (NuFIT + T2K + NOvA + DUNE/Hyper-K) against the subleading-breaking prediction.
3. **CP phase.** A δ_CP correlated with the Z₂-breaking parameter distinguishes this realisation from a flavour-sector μ–τ symmetry.
4. **m_ββ.** The same real-R, degenerate structure predicts a narrow 0νββ effective-mass window (a few meV; next-generation reach, e.g. nEXO).

## 7. Discussion

The atmospheric angle θ₂₃ = π/4 is **inherited** — a different structural route to a result two decades old, credited. The CLFV amplitude ratio is the **increment**: a clean amplitude-level relation in a field where CLFV-ratio discriminators are themselves established (§1), made clean by the symmetry that freezes the Casas–Ibarra freedom otherwise responsible for seesaw CLFV model-dependence. The value rests on that increment, bounded by the six qualifiers.

This note establishes the structural mechanism and closed-form ratio; a companion paper develops the laboratory phenomenology at pedagogical length. The two share one structural commitment and cross-reference; neither restates the other.

This note does not predict that CLFV will be observed — at the framework's heavy scale it predicts the opposite. It predicts the **ratio the two amplitudes would stand in if a signal is ever seen** — a structured, falsifiable relation, qualifiers in plain sight.
